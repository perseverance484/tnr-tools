~~~text
Target: Blazing Death Star (jutsu ID y0fmp1QvVcQnBoVZVLFfL). One square 1:1 game icon.

Reference image attached: copy its RENDERING only. Fine pixel clusters, crisp stepped edges, ember
orange and molten gold on a near-black charcoal ground, dark gaps between flame shapes. Do not copy
its fissure shape or its water.

Subject: three four-pointed throwing stars (shuriken), each wreathed in flame, falling in a curved
arc from upper left to lower right like falling stars. Each trails a tapering tail of fire behind it.
The front star is the largest and brightest; the other two are smaller and further back along the
arc. The steel points of each star stay visible inside the flames.

Rendering: high-resolution pixel art, about 256 pixels across. Small 1-2 pixel clusters, crisp
stepped contours, hand-placed highlights. Cel shading with 4-5 flat value bands per colour and no
gradients. Dark outlines between the flames and the steel. Light only as a bright white-gold core in
each flame and lit edges, never a haze.

Composition: the arc spans most of the frame on a diagonal, with clear near-black space above and
below it. Nothing touches the edges. The front star reads clearly at 32 px.

Palette: near-black charcoal, dark steel grey, ember orange, molten gold, a little white-gold at the
hottest points.

Avoid: smooth digital painting, airbrushed gradients, soft blur, bloom, smoke, photorealistic fire,
concept-art lighting. Also avoid chunky 8-bit or retro low-resolution pixels, big square blocks,
dithering noise, scattered specks. No characters, hands, text, symbols, runes, seals, frames or
franchise imagery. Not needles or senbon, not an explosion or burst cluster, not a shield, not a
fissure in the ground.

Generate ONE image. Do not upload or process it.
~~~
