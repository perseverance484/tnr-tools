# Ethereal Rebirth animation starburst: art session prompt

Brief: `docs/handoffs/ETHEREAL_REBIRTH_ANIMATION_STARBURST_ART_REQUEST.md`.

~~~text
You are the art director for The Ninja RPG (repository perseverance484/tnr-tools). Paint two layers for the starburst at
the start of the Ethereal Rebirth cast animation, matching the starburst at the chest of the approved icon. Read
docs/handoffs/ETHEREAL_REBIRTH_ANIMATION_STARBURST_ART_REQUEST.md first.

Attach as references: art/jutsu_icons/ethereal_rebirth/candidates/round3/raw_ethereal_rebirth_final.png (the icon) and the
close-up art/jutsu_icons/ethereal_rebirth/animation/raws/ref_icon_starburst.png. Do not imitate the current animation core
(chunky jagged gold shards with chips around them); that is what we are replacing.

The icon's starburst: a soft, round, white-hot glow; many fine straight rays radiating evenly from it like a lens star; and
cracks of light branching outward like veins or cracks in porcelain, bold at the root, splitting into finer forks, ending in
hair-thin tips. White core #ffffff, cream #fff6c8, pale gold #ffe27a, a thin warm-amber edge #f0a030 on the cracks.

It plays at about 15-20 px on screen, so keep the main forms bold and the fine detail second.

Paint, each on a fully transparent 1254 x 1254 canvas, centred (no grey or tinted panel, no border, no dark fringe):
1. C2 GLOW + RAYS: the soft round white-hot glow (about a third of the canvas across, fading to clear) and 12-16 thin
   straight rays from it, mixed lengths, the longest four reaching near the edge, tapering to points. No shards, no flecks.
2. C3 CRACKS: the cracks alone, no glow in the middle: 6-8 main cracks from the exact centre, each about 24-30 px wide at
   the root and tapering, branching two or three times into finer forks, wandering slightly, reaching about 85% of the
   way to the edge. White-hot inner line, pale-gold body, thin amber edge.

Premium TNR pixel art matching the icon's finish. Non-elemental: cracks of light in porcelain, NOT lightning (no zigzag
bolts, no electric blue, no fire). No text, figures, runes or symbols. Return the raw PNGs only, named
anim_er_v6_C2_glow_rays.png and anim_er_v6_C3_cracks.png.
~~~
