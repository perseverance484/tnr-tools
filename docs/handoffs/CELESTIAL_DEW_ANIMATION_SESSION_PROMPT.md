# Celestial Dew animation layers: art session prompt

Brief: `docs/handoffs/CELESTIAL_DEW_ANIMATION_ART_REQUEST.md`.

~~~text
You are the art director for The Ninja RPG (repository perseverance484/tnr-tools). Paint seven transparent layers for
the Celestial Dew jutsu animations, matching our live icon exactly. Read
docs/handoffs/CELESTIAL_DEW_ANIMATION_ART_REQUEST.md first.

Attach as the style reference: art/jutsu_icons/celestial_dew/refs/ref_cd_live_icon.webp. Its look: a glassy, translucent
white-blue crescent moon with swirling lighter streaks inside, a crisp near-white edge and a soft blue glow; a single drop
falling from its lower tip; a splash with ripples; small thin four-point stars and round glassy bubbles. Palette:
near-white #f4f8ff, ice blue #bcd7ff, sky blue #8ab8ff, deep glow blue #2f5fe0, white highlights; no other hues.

How they will move: the crescent rests on top of the player's round portrait; a drop falls into the portrait's centre and
splashes; ripple rings spread outward and, reaching the portrait's edge, light it up as a ring of celestial light (the
ward), which stays as a soft glow while the shield lasts and breaks into shards when it ends.

Paint each layer alone on a fully transparent canvas (no grey or tinted panel, no border, no dark fringe):
1. M1 CRESCENT (1254 x 1254): the icon's crescent moon alone, a C shape opening to the right, horns tapering to fine points.
2. D1 DROP (600 x 1254): one teardrop of starlight, point up, glassy with a bright highlight and a soft blue glow, a short
   faint sparkle trail above it.
3. S1 SPLASH (1254 x 1254): a small crown splash of glassy water-light seen from the front and slightly above, a few
   droplets thrown up.
4. R1 RIPPLES (1254 x 1254): three concentric ripple rings of light seen face-on, thin bright crests fading inward,
   nothing in the middle.
5. W1 WARD RING (1254 x 1254): a soft glowing ring of celestial light about 80% of the canvas across, the band about 8% of
   the canvas thick, a crisp near-white inner edge, faint swirling streaks inside the band, two or three tiny stars on it;
   the middle completely empty.
6. P1 GLINTS (1254 x 1254): 10-12 separate small pieces, spaced apart: thin four-point stars and round glassy bubbles,
   40-140 px.
7. X1 SHARDS (1254 x 1254): 8-10 separate curved shards of the ward ring's light with glowing edges, spaced apart.

Match the icon's finish. No text, runes, symbols or figures. Return the raw PNGs only, named anim_cd_M1_crescent.png,
anim_cd_D1_drop.png, anim_cd_S1_splash.png, anim_cd_R1_ripples.png, anim_cd_W1_ward_ring.png, anim_cd_P1_glints.png,
anim_cd_X1_shards.png.
~~~
