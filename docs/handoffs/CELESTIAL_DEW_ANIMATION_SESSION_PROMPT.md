# Celestial Dew animation layers: art session prompt

Brief: `docs/handoffs/CELESTIAL_DEW_ANIMATION_ART_REQUEST.md`.

~~~text
You are the art director for The Ninja RPG (repository perseverance484/tnr-tools). Paint six transparent layers for the
Celestial Dew jutsu animations, matching our live icon exactly. Read docs/handoffs/CELESTIAL_DEW_ANIMATION_ART_REQUEST.md
first.

Attach as the style reference: art/jutsu_icons/celestial_dew/refs/ref_cd_live_icon.webp. Its look: a glassy, translucent
white-blue crescent moon with swirling lighter streaks inside, a crisp near-white edge and a soft blue glow; a single drop
falling from its lower tip; a dome-shaped splash below with ripples; small thin four-point stars and round glassy
bubbles. Palette: near-white #f4f8ff, ice blue #bcd7ff, sky blue #8ab8ff, deep glow blue #2f5fe0, white highlights; no
other hues.

Paint each layer alone on a fully transparent canvas (no grey or tinted panel, no border, no dark fringe):
1. M1 CRESCENT (1254 x 1254): the icon's crescent moon alone, a C shape opening to the right, horns tapering to fine points.
2. D1 DROP (600 x 1254): one teardrop of starlight, point up, glassy with a bright highlight and a soft blue glow, a short
   faint sparkle trail above it.
3. S1 SPLASH (1600 x 700): the icon's splash alone, side view: a low dome-shaped crown of glassy water-light, a few
   droplets thrown up, ripple rings on the ground below.
4. W1 WARD (1254 x 1254): a celestial ward from the front: a round dome or sphere shell of light, almost clear in the
   middle, a bright near-white rim, a few faint swirling streaks like the crescent's, two or three tiny stars on it.
5. P1 GLINTS (1254 x 1254): 10-12 separate small pieces, spaced apart: thin four-point stars and round glassy bubbles,
   40-140 px.
6. X1 SHARDS (1254 x 1254): 8-10 separate curved shards of the ward's glassy light with glowing edges, spaced apart.

Match the icon's finish. No text, runes, symbols or figures. Return the raw PNGs only, named
anim_cd_M1_crescent.png, anim_cd_D1_drop.png, anim_cd_S1_splash.png, anim_cd_W1_ward.png, anim_cd_P1_glints.png,
anim_cd_X1_shards.png.
~~~
