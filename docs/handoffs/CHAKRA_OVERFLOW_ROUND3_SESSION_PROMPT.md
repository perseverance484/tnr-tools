# Chakra Overflow: round 3 session prompt (the rising star)

- **Director, 2026-10-23:** favorites from round 2 are R2-2, R2-4 and R2-6; the silhouette is not obvious enough.
  Direction: the **rising star** (a four-point star core with chakra wings swept upward into a chevron beneath it),
  with the chevron **subtle**, suggested by the chakra streams rather than drawn as a graphic badge.
- **Evidence:** `docs/design/chakra_overflow/review/round2_in_context.png` (the six raws in the in-game preview) and
  `review/round2_shape_tests.png` (stencil tests: the rising star read best as one emblem at 32 px).
- **Round-2 numbers that must change:** mean luminance 22-31 against 65-80 for approved icons; only 12-16% of the frame
  lit; hairline rays with no solid mass.
- **Zip:** `chakra_overflow_round3.zip`. **Intake:** raws to `art/jutsu_icons/chakra_overflow/candidates/`, then the
  preview against R2-2, R2-4 and R2-6.

## Paste into a new art-director session

~~~text
You are the art director for The Ninja RPG. This session runs ROUND 3 of the Chakra Overflow icon project, the most
scrutinized piece of art we will make. Chakra Overflow is the most-used jutsu in the game; its icon is the most-seen
icon in the game. Concept (director): BOUNDLESS CHAKRA, energy flowing without limit, no vessel or object.

Attached (chakra_overflow_round3.zip):
- CHAKRA_OVERFLOW_ICON_PROJECT.md: the project brief (criteria in section 3, concept in section 9).
- raw_chakra_overflow_R2-2.png, R2-4.png, R2-6.png: the director's three favorites from round 2. These ARE the
  starting point this round: attach the one named in each brief to its generation as the composition and energy
  reference, and refine it.
- round2_in_context.png: all six round-2 raws in the in-game battle bar and the grayscale test.
- round2_shape_tests.png and C_rising_star.png: crude stencil tests. C_rising_star shows the target SILHOUETTE only
  (star with upward wings); it is not a style, color or rendering reference. Never copy its flat look.
- icon_bee_unholy_enhancement_v2.webp, icon_jutsu_celestial_dew_v1.webp: rendering references only.

What the director liked in round 2: the white-hot four-point star core, the long upward ray, blue chakra turning gold
toward the source, the clean dark space, no fire reading.

What failed: the silhouette is not obvious. In grayscale the raws became soft star and diamond blobs. They are also
far too dark and thin: average brightness 22-31 (approved icons 65-80), only 12-16% of the frame lit, hairline rays
with no solid mass, so at 45 px they look small and dim beside their neighbors.

ROUND 3 DIRECTION: THE RISING STAR (director, 2026-10-23)
- A white-hot four-point star at the heart, its upper ray the longest, rising like a spear of light.
- Beneath and around it, the blue chakra streams gather into two broad WINGS that sweep upward and outward from below
  the star, so the whole emblem suggests an upward chevron (a "rise" / "level up" sign).
- SUBTLE: the chevron must emerge from the flowing chakra itself, never a hard-edged graphic badge or arrow. It should
  read as a chevron at 32 px and as living energy at 112 px.
- Mass and brightness: give every stream real thickness so the emblem reads as ONE solid bright shape. Far fewer stray
  sparks and hairlines. Target two to three times the brightness of round 2 (overall mean luminance about 65-80 at
  256 px; 30-40% of the frame lit). Gold and white-hot remain the brightest values; blue stays deep, never cyan.
- Size: the emblem fills about 75% of the frame width, centered slightly above middle, dark navy-to-black space on
  every side, BOTH bottom corners empty and dark, nothing touching any edge.

Non-negotiables (unchanged): reads as "POWER UP" at 32 px with no label and as one shape in grayscale; house
rendering (detailed pixel-art finish, crisp edges, strong rim light, luminous core, high contrast, opaque dark
background); no fire or flame tongues, no orb or object, no figure, no text, kanji, runes, seals, mandalas or eye
emblems, no frame or UI, no franchise looks (Eight Gates, Rasengan, Super Saiyan aura), no spiral, vortex or ring, no
crescent, dragon, droplet, or pillar from broken stone.

Run these briefs, ONE per isolated generation session, one square candidate each (2 generations per brief, 6 raws):
- R3-a (from R2-2): keep R2-2's crown of rays and tall top spike; gather the lower blue rays into two thick wings
  sweeping up and out beneath the star, and drop the downward rays.
- R3-b (from R2-4): keep R2-4's slender vertical star and veined chakra; thicken the side streams into two upswept
  wings that frame the lower half of the star like a soft chevron.
- R3-c (from R2-6): R2-6 already hints at the wings; make them broad, bright and continuous, sweeping up from beneath
  the star so the chevron reads at 32 px, and enlarge the whole emblem to about 75% of the frame.

Fail-fast every raw: view it at 32 px and in grayscale before accepting it. Reject it if the chevron is not
readable, if it is as thin or dark as round 2, if it looks like a hard graphic arrow or badge, or if it breaks a
non-negotiable; regenerate once.

Return every raw unprocessed, named raw_chakra_overflow_R3-a1.png, R3-a2, R3-b1 and so on, each with one line on how it
reads at 32 px. Do not pick a winner.

Live-game requests/writes: 0. No credentials. Do not upload anything.
~~~
