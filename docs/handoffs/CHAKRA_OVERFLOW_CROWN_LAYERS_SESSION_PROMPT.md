# Chakra Overflow crown layers: art session prompt

Paste into a new art-director session with `chakra_overflow_crown_layers.zip` attached.

~~~text
You are the art director for The Ninja RPG. This session draws two clean layers for the Chakra Overflow cast animation
(the most-used jutsu in the game), in the director's new "crown" layout.

Attached (chakra_overflow_crown_layers.zip):
- CHAKRA_OVERFLOW_CROWN_LAYERS_ART_REQUEST.md: the brief. Read it first (sections 1-3).
- crown_layout_guide.png: the exact layout at 1254 px. Red circle = the character, keep it EMPTY. Gold box = the star.
  Blue = safe area.
- crown_in_battle_preview.gif and crown_in_battle_frames.png: the current scripted version over the director's battle
  recording. The wings look cropped because a window was cut out of them; your layers replace that.
- crown_layout_options.png: the options the director chose from (D1, "star on top of the head").
- anim_co_L1_star.png, anim_co_L2_wings.png: your round-A layers (palette, finish and shapes to keep).
- raw_chakra_overflow_v1.png: the approved icon (the look to match).

Why: in battle the game centers the animation on the character's tile, and the character's round token fills the upper
part of that square. So the wings must frame the character instead of covering it, and the star sits on top of the head,
where it glints and winks out.

Deliver, each 1254 x 1254 on true transparency (or flat pure lime #00FF00), registered exactly to the guide:
- C1 WINGS (crown): the two blue-to-gold wings redrawn to wrap AROUND the empty character window: inner edges follow the
  circle with a clean glowing rim, tips rise beside the head, the wings meet in a V below. Nothing inside the red circle.
- C2 STAR (crown): the four-point star drawn for its small size, about 0.24 of the frame tall, centered at (0.50, 0.075):
  bolder and simpler than L1 (thicker rays, hot white core, crisp gold edges), long upper ray as in the icon, readable at
  50 px.
- C3 PEAK (optional): C1 + C2 composed at full glow, for reference.

Rules: same palette and finish as the approved icon (deep chakra blue, gold, white-hot core); nothing inside the red circle;
the star inside the gold box; everything inside the blue safe area; no fire or flame tongues, no figure, no text or runes, no
spiral or ring, no franchise looks. Check each layer over the guide and at 64 px before returning; regenerate anything that
intrudes on the character window.

Return the files unprocessed as anim_co_C1_wings_crown.png, anim_co_C2_star_crown.png (and anim_co_C3_peak_crown.png),
with one line each on anything notable.

Live-game requests/writes: 0. No credentials. Do not upload anything.
~~~
