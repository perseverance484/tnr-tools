# The Skyglass Project: story bible

Canon for every player-facing line in the Skyglass event: the Wreck dungeon, the five charts and hunts, Crownhold, and
the items. Drafted 2026-10-28 from the director's narrative changes (RUL-2026-10-28-003/-004/-005). Where a surface
disagrees with this file, the surface is rewritten.

## The story in one breath

A generation ago, the mountain fortress of Crownhold learned to call its dead back as afterimages and bind them to
service through a crown of skyglass. Its war-ship sailed the sky for decades with a crew that could not die, could not
rest and could not be relieved. Now the ship has come down broken on the shore, and its crew still stand their watch.
Follow their charts, and the last light out of Crownhold leads you to the man who never let any of them go.

## Canon

- **Skyglass** is a pale cyan crystal that holds chakra the way paper holds ink. A strong enough impression in skyglass keeps a person's habits, voice and skill after the person is gone.
- **Crownhold** is a mountain fortress, the war headquarters of a shinobi command that no longer exists. Its **Skyglass Project** grew skyglass, studied it, and learned to **recall** the dead: to give a stored impression a body of chakra for as long as someone commands it to stand.
- **Afterimages** (also *echoes*, *impressions*) are those recalled shinobi.
  - They remember warmth, food, sleep and home, but need none of them.
  - They repeat what they were: the drill, the ledger, the watch.
  - Most never fully understand what happened. The strongest know exactly how long it has been.
  - They are **never relieved**.
- **Replicant Zero** was the first one Crownhold brought back: a medic, recalled in a sealing jar, "the first to return".
- **The Command Crown** is a ring of blackened iron with seven skyglass blades held in the air above it, in a fan of command. Its wearer commands every afterimage, near or far: the castle's officers and the ship's crew alike.
- **The Crowned Captain** is Crownhold's commander and the crown's wearer.
  - He built the command "so it would outlast us," and it has.
  - He never sailed. He speaks through his echoes: "I control... many echoes."
  - In his own throne room he is an impression too. When the crown breaks, he settles like ash.
- **The war-ship** is Crownhold's. It was built around a skyglass heart (**the Core**) that lifts it over mountains, walls and coastlines.
  - Its crew were recalled to man it, and the Core rebuilds them, and the hull, every time they break.
  - Nothing ever finishes, so nothing ever stops.
- **The Captain** is the ship's captain, and the Crowned Captain's own echo: his face and voice, lent to the ship so it was never out of reach.
  - The crown kept for itself; the Captain was given the watch.
  - Decades at the helm have worn him into his own man: tired, honest, still dutiful.
  - "They built this ship to cross any wall and outlast any siege. No one asked what outlasting everything would cost."
  - This is why the two look alike. Players beat the Captain at the wreck, then meet the face again at Crownhold: "I defeated you once!" / "I control... many echoes."
  - No line refers to the old Ghost Ship quests, which are being retired. The hints are subtle: he touches his bare brow; "I wear his face."
- **The Silence.** A generation ago Crownhold stopped answering. Its living people are gone. Its last directive is not: *preserve command, preserve the crew, hold until the mission is done*. None is relieved. No one living remembers what the mission was.
- **The Ghost Ship.** For decades the war-ship crossed the sky on routes that no longer exist, appearing where no ship belonged, the Wayfinder correcting its charts against a world that kept changing.
- **The Wreck (now).**
  - The ship has come down on the shore with its Core cracked.
  - It still mends itself, and its interior shifts as it does: halls close, stairs move, rooms come and go. A chart taken from the wreck is only good while the ship still holds that shape.
  - The crew still stand their watch.
- **The Lastlight Lantern** holds Crownhold's last signal, caught in skyglass. The crew still obey the order it carries. Its light points home to Crownhold.
- **The end.** Breaking the Command Crown frees every echo it holds: the officers on the mountain, and the crew on the shore. Its blades scatter as the **Fragments of the Skyglass Crown**.
  - The event stays repeatable. Each player's story is their own, and the wreck keeps mending until someone breaks the crown again.

## The Ghost Ship's lore, carried forward

The Ghost Ship quests are retiring, so no line may depend on players having seen them. Their best beats live on as
echoes, which reward veterans without excluding anyone.

| Old beat | Where it lives now |
|---|---|
| The ship appears without approach, a black hull under the moon, "destroyed decades ago" | The Wreck: "The Ghost Ship has finally come down" |
| Deck Warden: young face, uniform generations out of date; the ship restarts him | Foundry intake; the Wreck's endless Wardens |
| Core Horror: the ship remembers a crew member badly | Archive descent |
| Oathkeeper: "Time. We had plenty of that." / "I know that I died, but not how to finish this watch." | The Watch landing: "We have always had time." |
| Wayfinder: "Still no home port." | The Watch chart room. The home port is Crownhold, which the Lantern points to |
| Arsenal Keeper: shore leave crossed out, "crew not relieved" | Foundry ledger; the shared refrain "None is relieved" |
| First Blade: "Finally. Something new." / "That was new." | The Watch deck |
| The Captain: empty ring of white crystal above him; "The ship died once. Skyglass disagreed." | The Watch helm: the empty ring, "The crown disagreed." |
| The Crown completes itself around the Captain: the Crowned Captain | The Crowned Captain reaching through his echo. "I defeated you once!" lands for veterans and for new players who beat the Captain |
| The order: "preserve command, preserve crew, keep the vessel airborne until the mission ended. The mission never ended." | Crownhold: he gave the order; it is carved behind his throne, and the mission line is blank stone |
| The Sealwright's suppressed reports; the Skyglass Reliquary | The Archive: replies sealed with the crown from Crownhold; the reliquary chained behind him |

## Voice

- **Tone:** Edo-era Japanese fantasy, quiet and melancholy, with dry humour from the afterimages. Short sentences at the moments that matter.
- **Never use:** technology words such as system, network, lattice, interface, protocol, terminal, personnel, maintenance, conduit, lift, reactor, data, scan, unit.
- **Use instead:** seal-work, seal channels, the Core, recall, impression, afterimage, echo, watch, post, order, directive, rite, shrine.
- **Characters:**
  - **Deck Warden:** the drill. Counts things. "No one is relieved."
  - **Arsenal Keeper:** the ledger. Has checked the same stores for decades.
  - **Wayfinder:** the charts. Corrects routes against a world that keeps moving.
  - **Oathkeeper:** terms and oaths. Formal. Knows exactly how long it has been.
  - **First Blade:** bored for decades; thrilled by anything new.
  - **Sealwright:** the conscience. Sealed away what command would not let him stop.
  - **Sentinel:** the guard. Says little.
  - **Foundry Warden:** never set down his ladle.
  - **Core Horror:** what the Core builds when the impression is too damaged to follow. It does not speak.
  - **The Captain:** tired, honest, still dutiful.
  - **The Crowned Captain:** certain. He thinks he gave his people a gift.
