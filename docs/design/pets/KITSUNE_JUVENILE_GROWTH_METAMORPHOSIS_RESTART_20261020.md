## The new rule

**Rank 1–4 = visibly growing up. Rank 5–9 = visibly transforming into new forms. Rank 10 = proposed ultimate culmination.** Transformations change the actual silhouette/body architecture, not just the pose, scale, ornament or glow. Keep the same *individual* face, signature markings, player-selected palette and aura as the pet progresses.

Our earlier golden Kitsune and Ember/Moonlit/Jade/Obsidian variants, plus their R2/R3 outputs, are **not the lineage to evolve any further**. Preserve them as *render-quality references only*; they demonstrate premium detail, not valid juvenile progression or locked parentage.

### Proposed juvenile base

Create **one new species reference** resembling a **young fox spirit**, not an already majestic guardian:

- **Fox anatomy:** juvenile quadruped; compact torso, shorter legs, pointed oversized-but-natural ears, shorter rounder muzzle, clear expressive eyes. Cute through natural youth, **not chibi**.
- **Kitsune identity:** **two separate, small bushy tails attached to one body** (proposed; meets seeded "multiple tails" identity), not an enormous five-tail fan. Small cheek ruff; **no huge chest mane or flowing dorsal crest**.
- **Appearance:** visually neutral coat and minimal surface motif, **no fixed aura, jewellery, armour or mature spirit crown**. The species master defines craftsmanship/anatomy, **not** the colours of each player.
- **Rendering:** premium high-definition TNR pixel art, deliberate crisp clusters, defined shadow planes, detailed but controlled fur. Square single creature; full body and tail tips visible; transparent background, no ground/shadow/text.
- **Scale:** keep a similar subject-to-canvas framing throughout the *demonstration*. True physical growth must be visible in silhouette and age proportions, not achieved by zooming the image.

## Reusable generation contract

**Rank 1:** condition on the accepted **juvenile species-base image** and insert saved palette/marking/aura. **Ranks 2–10:** condition on **exactly one image: that individual's preceding accepted rank**. Use identical rank wording across all four colour variants; variation only comes from their unique references and cosmetic traits.

```text
Create a NEW full-body premium pixel-art image of the SAME individual
Kitsune fox spirit in the attached previous-rank reference.

CURRENT GAME RANK: [RANK NAME / NUMBER OF 10].
AGE / FORM STAGE: [GROWING OR METAMORPHOSIS].
NEW REQUIRED SILHOUETTE: [SPECIES-RANK-CONCEPT].
NEW RANK TAIL COUNT (when specified): [COUNT].
This rank must look clearly different from the previous form at 160px.
For R1–4: visibly age/grow the fox through true changes in body
proportions, size-relative head shape, limbs, ears and tail development.
For R5–9: DESIGN A DISTINCT EVOLVED FORM with a different integrated
mane/crest/body/tail architecture; **not** a recolour, pose adjustment,
extra glow or resized copy. Each form is stronger than its predecessor.

IDENTITY TO PRESERVE: One fox head, four natural fox legs, recognizable
eye/muzzle character, the same individual's [PALETTE], [MARKING] and
contained [AURA]; retain previously earned structural features.
When the rank deliberately adds a tail, grow it organically from the
same body. Never duplicate the animal, create detached/extra legs
or copy another player's distinct markings. No humanoid features.

ART: refined crisp 32-bit-inspired pixel art, shaped hard pixel
clusters, precise lineart, controlled cel-shading, clean material
highlights and excellent fur depth. Complete square creature cutout,
three-quarter camera and similar overall framing to parent.
No background, cast shadow, floor, frame, text, broad magical halo,
external particles or unrelated symbols.
```

**Critical fix:** the prior prompt said "preserve existing number of tails" and "only mild changes". **Remove both.** Tail count and major silhouette may change when the **specific approved rank concept requires them**, while ancestry and cosmetics remain locked. Similarly avoid telling the model to retain an identical pose.

## How to prove this works without wasting generations

1. **Director approves ONE new juvenile species master**. Reject any source that already looks like the previous five-tailed evolved artwork.
2. **Generate A Rank 1 from the juvenile master** and approve its styling/individualization; then generate B/C/D Rank 1 using the **same species master** with their saved trait inputs.
3. **Pilot one complete chain** (A Rank 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5) using the preceding image *each time*. **Rank 5 is the key metamorphosis gate:** it must look like a new evolved creature while still being identifiably A.
4. Only then repeat the **same, locked, species-rank concepts** for B/C/D; no switching to someone else's image. Later test Rank 7 and 9 larger transformation gates before scaling.
5. Show **actual saved image files** in aligned rows and columns at 160px, with full-size QC and recorded parent-child hashes. Reject if shapes only differ by coloured aura, larger zoom, added decoration, or a pose shuffle.

The revised juvenile stage therefore **cannot be assumed to work in production merely by editing a species description**. The developer must revise the pet-specific prompt composition in a reviewed implementation. No live pet generation attempts here.
