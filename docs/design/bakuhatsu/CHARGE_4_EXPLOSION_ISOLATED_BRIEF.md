## Proposed target treatment

A chain of **small explosions ignites where charged chakra touches the battlefield**. The technique is **SPREADING EXPLOSIVE CHAKRA**, not firing an explosive projectile, creating a shield or ripping open the entire ground. The name “Charge 4 Explosion” does not prove an exact four-blast count.

The primary silhouette is a **diagonal, ground-hugging ignition cascade** advancing from the lower-left near field into the upper-right far field. Distinct small contact-point detonations rise only a little above a mostly intact dark ground plane, with clear spacing between them. This is a visible *sequential action*, not five equal floating starbursts. Viewed in elevated 3/4 perspective, earlier detonations are dimmer/tighter, while a newer contact-point flare is white-hot and sharply legible. The whole diagonal current/cascade, not any one glowing orb, dominates. Secondary cue: **one slender blue-white electrical chakra filament** supported by the original identity art, visually guiding the blast progression without taking over the Bakuhatsu amber/white palette. No actors or anatomy.
