# Skyglass event — loot tables

**Status:** Structure ruled by RUL-2026-10-25-002 and the balance pass RUL-2026-10-25-004 (supersedes the ladder caches and the shared pick of RUL-2026-10-24-014/-017). Weights and quantities below are proposals unless marked ruled; currency amounts are set by the director in game (RUL-2026-10-24-019).

**Rule: every item has one home.** A reward appears in more than one source only as the same container, never as loose contents. Skyglass items come only from the Skyglass Coffer.

| Container | Owns | Found in |
|---|---|---|

| Sealed Treasury | 100,000 ryo + valuables: Rare Jewels, Imperial Gold, Unobtanium | Wreck clear 3%; every hunt ending at 1% per ladder step (ruled) |

Currency package (director, in game). Independent drops: Chart of the Drowned Foundry **12%**, Chart of the Quarantined Archive **6%**, Chart of the Captain's Watch **6%**, Lastlight **3%**, Sealed Treasury **3%** (ruled).

## 4. Treasure hunts

Every hunt is an isolated Battle Pyramid. **Ending rewards** (always paid, every ending): the 1,000-5,000 exp ladder by final opponent and gating, the hunt's currency (director, in game) and the Sealed Treasury at **1% per ladder step** (I 1% · II 2% · III 3% · IV 4% · V 5% · VI 6%). No caches on endings. **Completion pick** (choose 1) is the hunt's identity:

| Hunt | Pick 1 of |
|---|---|
| Drowned Foundry | Katon / Wave / Ikazuchi / Stonefall / Zephyrfang Tool Pouch |
| Quarantined Archive | Archive Chart Case · Sage's Strongbox · The Records (exp lump) |
| Captain's Watch | Skyglass Coffer · Captain's Purse (ryo lump) · Medicine Inro ×3 |
| Crownhold | Skyglass Coffer ×2 · Crownhold's Honor (village tokens lump; set it large enough to compete with Coffer ×2) · **Crownhold badge** · **Crownhold jutsu** |

The badge and the jutsu grey out once held (the game blocks picking content a player already has), so a player needs at least two Crownhold clears to take both; later clears pick the Coffers or Crownhold's Honor. Badge and jutsu are still to be designed.

Ladder per ending: Foundry V (always fought to the Warden; the leave-early endings are removed, RUL-2026-10-25-004); Archive clean V / alarm III; Watch no terms IV / one-two V / all VI; Crownhold VI. Exp I-VI = 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 / 5,000 / 5,000; Treasury chance = step number %.

The Sealwright's doubled health (push/252) applies to the Archive's final fight too, solo; accepted (RUL-2026-10-25-004).

**Shelved:** Armory Requisition and its Special quest (icon kept in `art/skyglass_event_icons/` unused); Lion Token as a Wreck drop.

## 6. To check before the manifests

- Shop items sit in a cache's Common tier; Dr. Kim's Miracle Potion is the one exception, in Rare. Pill Pack, Small Health Pill, Stamina Pill II, Paper Bombs, Saki, Senbon Needles, Smoke Pellet, Venom Cask, Whetstone Oil, Solider Pill and Mona's Mermaid Song have no capture with a shop flag yet; confirm each before the manifest.
- Forbidden Jutsu Scrolls at 3% of one Strongbox roll: keep, lower or remove.
- Reward packages are built in the game's catalog editor until Forge has a package recipe.
- Closed (RUL-2026-10-24-024): the Skyglass Reliquary, the Mystery Chest and the other old Ghost Ship chests are retired relics; their contents, including the Mystery Chest's Reliquary line, are left as they are.
