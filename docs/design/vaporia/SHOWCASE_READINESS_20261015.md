## Lessons imported from BBE/BEE

- An image-generation tool **must not redraw the final family montage**: BEE's first gallery reinterpreted approved icons and used incorrect file-size labels. Composite the exact image bytes deterministically; fail closed if an original is unavailable.
- Raw visual acceptance, source recoverability, processed WebP QC, family-gallery acceptance, shared alpha preflight and live upload are distinct gates. BEE's history showed that a later director rework makes an earlier faithful showcase **stale**.
- The only transferable family constants are process discipline, thumbnail readability, actual source provenance and correct names/sizes. **Vaporia's artistic palette and subjects must come from Vaporia**, not imported BBE/BEE appearances.
- BBE's director cap **<80,000 bytes** was family-specific. Vaporia uses current general jutsu-ICON target unless the director sets a new tighter limit.
