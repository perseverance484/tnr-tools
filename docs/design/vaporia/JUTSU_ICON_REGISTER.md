## Proposed family visual language — director review before locking

**Vaporia identity:** superheated liquid/Boil chakra expressed through *azure water, hot steam, liquid-ember accents and forceful Taijutsu-like kinetic action*. This is **a fresh family**, not a palette recolor of BBE ice or BEE crimson shadow.

- **Candidate palette:** deep ocean blue/navy for separation; luminous azure/cyan/turquoise as the water/steam anchor; **restrained warm ember/amber-orange** in technique-appropriate places. Near-white glints only for focused steam or pressure highlights. **NOT LOCKED** until the director and actual original icons confirm the palette.
- **Material grammar:** broad translucent water sheets, pressure folds, tight hot vapor gradients, selected glowing liquid-ember seams. Heat and fluidity must both be legible, without random particle clouds or realistic splashes overwhelming the icon. Prefer deliberate cel/pixel-art contours and negative space.
- **Shape and motion discipline:** one main silhouette plus at most one secondary action cue per technique; every jutsu must own a different direction of force and outer contour. No forced ring, human head/eyes, copied splash, duplicated dragon or default chakra-emblem stamp.
- **Universal exclusions:** text, captions, UI, borders, watermarks, franchise emblems, unrelated weapons, cast scenes/portraits, targets/victims, gore. Do not copy BEE's dark-crimson rendering or BBE's faceted ice solely because they are recently accepted bloodlines.
- Pairwise checks as icons arrive: **Azure Dragon Palm vs Surfing Strike** (dragon/wave shape), **Surfing Strike vs Drowning Strike** (moving crest vs engulfing pressure), **Scorch Break vs Liquid Ember Shell** (impact heat vs contained heat), and all five together.
