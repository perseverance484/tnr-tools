## Director art-direction baseline for Vaporia

**Technique first.** Most jutsu icons should be the supernatural effect, never a generic caster/victim. **Actors and hands only when genuinely necessary**, and only after review. Use one prominent primary shape, one optional supporting cue, clean dark/light hierarchy and 125/64/32 thumbnail comparison. Check adjacent techniques against each other before approving their raw files. Do not introduce a ring, sigil, portrait, fake impact point, or character merely to add detail.

**Proposed—not yet director-locked—Vaporia palette:** azure/cobalt/turquoise water and luminous steam, selective warm molten ember accents in heat-bearing techniques. Materials should clearly be **hot fluid/vapor**, not BBE ice or BEE crimson shadows. Respect the actual five source images when available; the family needs its **own** style exemplar, not a recolored BBE/BEE icon.

**Do not claim unverified mechanics:** the historical captured kit has supported/unsupported tags; `Azure Dragon Palm` Pierce and `Surfing Strike` Move were evaluator-unsupported. Neither a dramatic needle nor motion effect should be sold as current mechanical truth just because it looks plausible.
