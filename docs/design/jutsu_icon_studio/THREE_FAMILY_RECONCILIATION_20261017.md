## Transferable, source-supported creative disciplines

- **BBE lesson — differentiation over thematic repetition.** The shared frost/sapphire family used entirely different **Z displacement**, **three parallel cuts**, **X restraint chains**, **guardian armor**, **crescent ice-blade**, **vertical sword resonance**, **lance-volley fan** and **snowflake** silhouettes. Arctic Frost's first candidate lacked contrast, while Blade Resonance required a different visual; family conflicts were solved by targeted replacements. The **<80,000-byte cap required adaptive median-cut quantization**, which is NOT a global standard for BEE/Vaporia.
- **BEE lesson — strong singular metaphors / director can withdraw approval.** Spiral, chalice, skull, scythe, active vein circuit, blast, vitality bubble and needle rain avoid eight lookalike red explosions. Sanguine old corruption blob and Unholy radial/ringlike wave layouts were replaced; do not mark prior versions active. First showcase image-generation attempts altered icons and invented labels, so only **deterministic source-faithful composition** can be final. Family review can reopen *one* icon without discarding unrelated approvals.
- **Vaporia lesson — technique action first, actors only where truly needed.** Electric-azure boiling water and molten orange are rendering identity, but dragon-like motifs, fists and watery turbulence can blur together. The final **Scorch Break** intentionally pivots away from a fist to a dominant hydrothermal rupture, establishing an unambiguous unique silhouette. Vaporia proves **N=5** rather than 8 and **3+2 centered** review layout. A “closeout session” does not automatically approve the exact compiled family.
- **Shared cross-family style contract:** striking technique-specific shapes, visible cause/action or defensive formation, high contrast at small render sizes, controlled meaningful glow, dimensional chakra materials, no unnecessary casters, minimal scene clutter, and a distinct reserved dominant silhouette for every move. There is **no universal color palette or mandatory mandala**.
