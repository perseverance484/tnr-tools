## Proposed immutable P0 visual lock

**Dominant action:** The cave itself **closes in on an unseen target**: large transparent mineral-crystal **stalactites thrust down** from an oppressive upper cavern edge while opposing **stalagmites erupt upward** from below, visibly converging toward the same dark trapped interior. Show the imprisoning/impaling action mid-closure, not a pleasant glowing cavern landscape.

**Primary silhouette:** TWO UNEQUAL JAGGED ROWS / OPPOSING CRYSTAL JAWS with **4–6 dominant crystalline teeth**, framing a strong central **dark compressed pocket**. Distinct from Sphere's single closed, circular protective shell. Keep upper and lower spikes uneven, visibly rooted in rough layered mineral/rock bases; not a symmetrical ornamental mandala.

**Camera:** Shallow three-quarter **sectional cave-mouth view**, as though looking slightly into an active trap; the top and bottom jagged masses should dominate while the central pocket stays legible. Avoid scenic deep vanishing-point tunnel composition.

**Motion axis:** **top-down and bottom-up, converging centrally**, no horizontal shockwave travel, no projectile as the main subject. Align a few opposing points toward imminent collision around the dark center without making a fully closed circular boundary.

**Focal/negative space:** Center-dark crush zone; high-contrast translucent facet edges direct vision inward. Keep a clean gap between opposing spikes wide enough to read as a trap at 32px.

**Exactly one secondary effect:** A few small fractured **mineral chips at the cave floor roots** from the emerging growth. Do not multiply explosions, particles, silhouettes or extra effects.

**Family materials:** premium TNR disciplined pixel-cluster linework, limited cel-shaded planes, transparent teal/jade/turquoise mineral facets with cyan-white hard edges, *restrained* concentrated amber-gold refraction at pressure points, navy-black negative space. Designed opaque background allowed. Material looks like mineral-glass growth, **not frosted sapphire ice**.

**Anatomy exception: NONE.** The old original includes a small human silhouette, but the redesign must communicate a trapped target through **the empty dark trap pocket only**, not by adding an actor.

**Small-format:** At 125/64/32px, read as **opposing cave spikes converging around empty space**. If it reads as a circular dome (Sphere), multi-arc wave fan (Kōsai), vertical split cocoon (Jade), one beam (Prism), or as a static pretty cavern, reject.
