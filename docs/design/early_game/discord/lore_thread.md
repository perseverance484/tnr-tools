## 🗡️ The Kuroganekai Case: Lore Script (Lv 1–70)
**One case runs from level 10 to 70.** Someone is stealing the villages' power to build an engine that can open what the first Kage sealed. The player follows it from a ledger page in an academy errand to the altar at the centre of the world.

**Key**
> **Canon** = already in live quest text or game source. It stays.
> **New** = proposed story.
> *(working)* = placeholder name for review.

Every new beat pays off a hook the live game already planted. Nothing canon is overwritten.

## 🌏 The World As It Stands
**Deep history (Canon)**
• Before Seichi's founding, the first Kage of the five villages sealed a **Fifth Element** (Void Flame / Null Chakra) at a forgotten altar: the **Heart of Balance**.
• The **Great Collapse** nearly ended the world. The **Five Blades of Balance** ended it.
• The **Great Seichi Wars** followed. Large villages absorbed small ones.
• The **Mortal King** watches. He must never reach the **Monarch Powers**.

**How the world runs (New)**
• **Lords own the land.** Villages are military houses paid by contract. Every Mission Hall fee is a lord's or merchant's money.
• **Post roads** link the provinces: waystations, checkpoints, courier relays.
• **Road passes** carry a lord's seal. Forging one is a hanging crime. The Order forges them well.
• **Wayside shrines** to the five patron gods mark each border.
• **Guilds:** smiths, kiln houses, herbalists for Esakanki Hospital, beast-hunters. A shinobi apprentices to one trade at a time.
• **Neutral ground:** Wake Island's free port and the port city of **Kaiyo** (both Canon).

## 🎓 Chapter 1: Academy & Genin (Lv 1–30)
*All Canon. Only the fixes are New.*

**The academy (1–10)** · Getting Started, Road to Genin
Lemu's academy, a lost puppy, a tour of Wake Island. Pick a village, earn Genin.

**The case desk (10–24)** · Genin Trials 1–7
An anonymous **case officer** ("they") stamps each Trial, and each Trial adds a clue:
> 1. Ledgers in three districts are missing the same entries
> 2. A transport vanished; its armour was cut clean
> 3. Stolen conductors for something that "eats power"
> 4. Violet residue at a raid site
> 5. A dark, cold shard: **blacksteel**
> 6. The thefts bend around one guarded region
> 7. The insignia: **Kuroganekai**, the Order of Black Steel
Each Trial unlocks a repeatable Case or Blacksteel Contract.

**The stronghold (24–30)** · Trials 8, GENIN pyramid, Trials 9
Iron Aberrations, Obelisks, bloodline research, a beast behind a door. *"A commander inside needs a name."*

**The free port (24–30)** · Blacksteel Tides
Blacksteel launders through Wake Island. The officer signs your **Chunin recommendation**.

**Fixes (New):** Trials 9 + GENIN pyramid move to 24–30 · unhide Blacksteel Tides · the Chunin Exams acknowledge the recommendation.

## 🛤️ Ch.2 Act I: Waystation (Lv 30–38)
*The Violet Road (working).* The stronghold was only a depot. The blacksteel still moves, and it moves by road.

**1. The field office (30)**
The officer runs a field office out of a mountain waystation. *"A depot means a supply line. Supply lines have schedules. Find me the schedule."*
→ Unlocks **Checkpoint Inspection**

**2. The perfect seal (31–32)**
A clerk flags a road pass whose seal is *too* clean. Real seals wear. The forger's ink has blacksteel filings in it.
→ Unlocks **Pass-Forger Bounty**

**3. The night caravan (33–35)** · Kuroganekai CHUNIN pyramid
A caravan line that only travels after dark. You ride as a hired guard. The drivers talk about "the wolves that don't sleep."
→ Unlocks **Night Caravan Escort**

**4. Capstone: Howling Hills (33–40)**
The wolves are being *driven* off the road so only the caravan crosses. The three branch paths stay (Canon).
**New:** the **Monster of the Moon** has violet veins under its fur. It's an **Iron Aberration**, a stronghold experiment set loose to herd the pack.
**First clear:** a tally in the den: *"high route, Skychain, paid in full."*

## 🔔 Ch.2 Act II: The Sealed Route (Lv 39–47)
The Order isn't fighting the Skychain monks. **It's paying them.**

**1. The closed mountain (39–40)**
**Canon:** the Skychain order "answers to no rank and no village" and has sealed the high courier route.
**New:** village mail takes eleven days around the mountain. One courier line still crosses in two.
→ Unlocks **Mountain Courier**

**2. The split order (41–43)**
**Canon:** some monks want the route open. They meet you at a bell tower. The other faction keeps the seal for a cause they believe in, and a debt they can't pay.
→ Unlocks **Bell-Tower Watch**

**3. The debt (44–45)**
**New:** the monastery borrowed to rebuild after the Great Seichi Wars burned its lower halls. A merchant bought the debt notes. His ink has blacksteel filings in it, the same as the forged passes.
→ Unlocks **Debt-Note Recovery**

**4. Capstone: Skychain Monastery (41–48)**
Climb the Challenge of the Three Bells to the **Abbot of the Broken Sky** (Canon). His grievance: villages send children across storms and call it duty. He yields by the monastery's own law.
**New:** he admits the Order holds the debt, and hands you the courier ledger. Every entry ends at **Kaiyo**.

## 🌊 Ch.2 Act III: Tide at Kaiyo (Lv 48–56)
The freight goes to sea, and the case gets its name.

**1. The neutral port (48–49)**
No village has authority in **Kaiyo**. You work under the harbourmaster's borrowed licence.
→ Unlocks **Harbour Patrol**

**2. The raiders who never sink (50–52)**
**Canon:** the Drowned Fleet are tide-ninja raiders with no origin.
**New:** they're the Order's sea haulers, paid in blacksteel. Their raids cover crates dropped overboard by the caravans' agents.
→ Unlocks **Wreck Salvage**

**3. The manifests (53–54)**
Conductors, blacksteel ingots, and *"one sealed crate, live."* Destination: *"where the old altar was."*
→ Unlocks **Manifest Interdiction**

**4. Capstone: The Drowned Fleet (49–56)**
Skiff → war junk → flagship → **Tidelord Kaisei** (Canon).
**First clear:** the strongroom's sealed orders **name the commander** *(your call)* and reveal the freight is parts of an **engine**, built to draw on something sealed.

**Close (56–57):** the officer takes the file to **Global ANBU**. *"This is past a desk. It needs someone who can lead."* That's your **Jonin Exam** recommendation.

## 🔥 Chapter 3: The Fifth Seal (Lv 57–70)
*Working title.* The engine is real, the commander has a name, and the seal under the world is the target.

**1. The Jonin Exam (57)** · Canon
Haki, the Mortal King, the ironwood altar, the betrayal.
**New bridge:** you recognise violet scoring on the altar. The Order has been here.

**2. The ANBU file (58–60)**
Global ANBU takes over. The officer stays as adviser. You lead squads now.
**The question:** is the commander building the engine *for* the Mortal King, or to stand *against* him?
→ Unlocks **Conductor Recovery**

**3. Capstone: Blacksteel Foundry (60–64)** · new
Blacksteel is ordinary iron soaked in Null Chakra leaking from cracks in the old seal. That's why it's cold, and why it veins violet.
→ Unlocks **Foundry Raid**

**4. The altar (64–66)**
"Where the old altar was" = the **Heart of Balance**. The live crate was the beast behind the stronghold door: a **key bred from altered bloodlines**. The Genin-era rumour, paid off.
→ Unlocks **Engine Survey**

**5. Capstone: Engine Site (66–70)** · new
The engine is built around the altar. The commander is there. You stop the draw.
The seal holds, but it's cracked, and **the Mortal King noticed**. → Level 70 endgame.
