# Event jutsu: story-voice descriptions (drafts)

- **Director, 2026-10-23:** event jutsu are pinnacle, Silver-only jutsu. Descriptions are 3-4 sentences told as a scene
  (how the jutsu plays out in the story), not as combat mechanics; no "offense/defense"; plain colour words ("crimson",
  not "oxblood"). The combat system is an abstraction of what the jutsu really does; the text describes the real thing.
- **Status:** DRAFTS for the director's review. Abyss of Shadows is final in `ABYSS_OF_SHADOWS_TEXT.md`.
- **Faithfulness:** each image maps to a live effect (2026-10-21 census); the mapping is under each draft.
- **Ships:** as text suggestions after the combined icon ticket resolves on each record (one open suggestion per record).

## Tempest Cleave (`Ul83iTziN__Z0i_UOzZTK`)

The user plants their feet and turns once, and the air itself becomes a blade. A storm of razor wind erupts around them, carving through everything within its reach, friend and foe alike, for the tempest knows no allegiance. Those caught in it are left with wounds that will not close, each cut still singing with the wind long after the storm has passed. In its wake, even the hardiest guard lies in ribbons.

*Effects:* area damage (friendlyFire ALL: allies in the area are hit too), afterburn 2 rounds, increased damage taken 2 rounds. Keep the existing red "DON'T TRAIN. ALREADY CAPPED" note (proposed).

## Earthen Bastion (`_zNMEZqIzgjTqXoXQRWz0`)

The user drives a heel into the ground and the earth answers with a roar. Stone surges up and closes around them in jagged plates veined with molten light, a living fortress that moves as they move. Blows that land against it crack and scatter like rain on a cliff face, while every strike the user throws carries the weight of the mountain behind it. For as long as the stone holds, the user stands as immovable as the land itself.

*Effects:* self, damage taken down and damage given up, 2 rounds.

## Drops of Renewal (`-7jaNkJCvt3ByWhPJSw3E`)

The user lifts their face to the sky and a single drop of water falls, then another, then a shimmering rain that gathers around them in slow, glowing spirals. Every wound the enemy inflicts is drawn into the rain and returned as living water, mending flesh even as it is torn. Fed by the current, their chakra swells, and each strike they throw lands with the force of a river in flood. For a short while, the user becomes the eye of a healing storm.

*Effects:* self, absorb (damage into health) and damage given up, 2 rounds.

## Blazing Fang Strike (`l3oXmqSJ7L-r4XI9IBIyh`)

Flame gathers in the user's hands until it can no longer be contained, coiling into a great fire dragon with jaws agape. With a roar that shakes the air, the dragon lunges and sinks its burning fangs straight through the target's guard as if it were paper. The bite does not end with the strike; the fire takes root deep within, flaring anew with every blow that follows. Those who survive the dragon's fangs carry its burn for many breaths after.

*Effects:* pierce (ignores defense), afterburn 3 rounds.

## Celestial Dew (`2vNeMhph6A9Ld--WXa35O`)

The user closes their eyes and the night seems to answer, a pale crescent moon blooming into being above them. From its tip falls a single drop of starlight that splashes across the user and spreads into a dome of soft, silver light. Wounds knit closed beneath its glow, and the dome settles into a quiet ward that swallows the blows meant for its keeper. Within it, for a few heartbeats, the battle seems very far away.

*Effects:* self, heal and shield (2 rounds).

## Hollow Veil (`GAhckNCW8xUES4YGTJPGL`)

With a whisper, the user casts a veil of ash-gray mist that drifts over the target and settles like a funeral shroud. It hollows them out from within, draining the fire from their strikes until even their fiercest blows fall weak and distant. What the veil takes, it gives to its maker, and every wound the target lands on the user returns to them as quiet, restoring breath. Silent and patient, the veil turns the enemy's fury into the user's strength.

*Effects:* target deals less damage (40%, 2 rounds); user absorb (2 rounds). Keep the red "DON'T TRAIN. ALREADY CAPPED" note (proposed).

## Subaku Palace (`jELEiw5W3N4m2R3oJm3Pk`)

Stolen from the cursed halls of Subaku Castle, this forbidden art lets the user tear open the earth and call the demon palace into the waking world. A sigil of burning gold spirals across the ground, and from it rise gates of red sandstone carved with snarling faces, their shadows falling over every enemy nearby. Beneath those gates the enemy's strength drains away, their every strike growing timid, while the palace pours its dark power into the one who summoned it. For a time the battlefield belongs to the demon palace, and to its master.

*Effects:* ground area; user damage given up (2 rounds); enemies in the area deal less (3 rounds).
