# Event and forbidden jutsu: text (APPROVED)

- **Director, 2026-10-23:** approved, training warnings kept. **Voice rule:** the **description** is a short overview of what
  the jutsu is (1-2 sentences); the **battle line** is the jutsu in full bloom, a 3-4 sentence scene in action. Told as the
  story would show it, not as combat mechanics; no "offense/defense"; plain colour words ("crimson", not "oxblood").
- **Battle-line rules:** `%target` only on jutsu aimed at an enemy; area jutsu (Tempest Cleave, Subaku Palace) describe the
  area, never assume someone is in it; self jutsu never use `%target`. No `%jutsu`, no gendered placeholders.

  these texts together. Abyss of Shadows also carries the rename; Scroll of Shadows' description becomes "Learn the jutsu Abyss of

## Abyss of Shadows (was Aegis of Shadows) (`XS08zZhUhK615Dle4j3f3`)

**Description:** Opens an abyss of crimson shadow that swallows the enemy whole. Within its silence their strikes grow slow and weightless and their guard dissolves, for a few precious moments.

**Battle line:** %user's shadow stretches toward %target, and where it falls the world simply ends. An abyss opens in its place, a churning well of crimson darkness that surges up and drags %target down into a silence older than chakra itself. In that dark %target's strikes come slow and weightless, and their guard dissolves like mist before the void. For a few precious moments, the abyss makes even the mightiest shinobi small.

*Effects:* target deals less damage and takes more, 2 rounds.

## Tempest Cleave (`Ul83iTziN__Z0i_UOzZTK`)

**Description:** A spinning storm of razor wind that carves through everything around the user, friend and foe alike. Its cuts keep singing long after it fades.  *(+ training warning)*

**Battle line:** %user plants their feet and turns once, and the air itself becomes a blade. A storm of razor wind erupts outward in a howling ring, carving through everything within its reach, for the tempest knows no allegiance. Those caught in it are left with wounds that will not close, each cut still singing with the wind long after the gale has passed. In its wake, even the hardiest guard lies in ribbons.

*Effects:* area damage (hits allies too), afterburn 2 rounds, increased damage taken 2 rounds.

For as long as the stone holds, %user stands as immovable as the land itself.
