## Direct image-generation brief — POLAR ASCENDANCE

Create **exactly ONE premium square jutsu icon** depicting **ONE GIANT, unmistakably FIVE-POINTED STAR made of condensed magnetic chakra, surging upward**.

### Non-negotiable primary silhouette

- The star is the **single coherent foreground object** — it should occupy roughly **two-thirds of the square** against generous dark negative space.
- **FIVE unmistakable thick points:** **one exceptionally long upward apex**, two distinct broad lateral points, and **two clearly separated lower points**. Visible valleys between the points are crucial; do not merge them into a triangular flame, spear, arrowhead, lightning bolt, or bloom.
- Bold, intentionally sculpted, slightly irregular **three-dimensional star-shaped chakra mass**, not a flat decal, static UI star, astronomical sun, or logo.
- The star reads **instantly as a star in monochrome at 32px**. Point count, shape and negative space must do the work without reliance on glow, debris or color.
- Orient the star vertically, with the elongated top point driving upward and a **short compressed upward wake** only below the lower points — not a full-frame rising energy pillar.

### Magnetic polarity and technique

- **PRIMARY:** electric cyan / deep azure-blue magnetic chakra with concentrated luminous blue-white bevels and a controlled brilliant white heart. Keep the cyan star visually dominant.
- **SECONDARY:** exactly **one small curved scarlet-red returning magnetic strand beneath/beside the lower star points**, hinting at the historically supported SELF REFLECT effect. It should remain subordinate, **not** frame or encircle the star.
- Use tightly layered polarized energy planes **within the star's silhouette** and a few crisp internal tension seams to give magnetic dimensionality. Optional tiny magnetite-dark **internal facets only** — **no floating rocks, black rubble swarm, metal shard storm or chunky debris** around the star.
- Dark midnight-indigo designed background. Keep the silhouette edges legible, with minimal accents beyond the core form. No giant overexposed bloom.

### Separate it from all four other techniques

- **Morning Star (next, approved direction):** a broad **SCARLET eight-ray spiked magnetic star** with a downward diagonal strike. Rising Star must **NOT** have eight rays, a red-dominant star, or a downward impact.
- **Magnetic Pulse Strike:** no horizontal energy wedge, railgun trail, or red-white impact on one side.
- **Magnetic Assignment:** no two opposed jaws, reticle, hourglass or trap structure.
- **Houkyu Dance:** no wide figure-eight swirl, orbit, sphere or concentric magnetic ring.
- No ninja/fist/figure, background terrain, weapon, text, border, emblem, watermark, fire, lava, water, steam, literal star logo, standard lightning bolt, random planet/meteor or franchise symbolism.
