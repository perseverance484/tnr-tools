## Family rendering — DIRECTOR APPROVED (2026-10-16)

**Approved family style: Polar Flux / Ferromagnetic Chakra.** Reinterpret the originals' two opposing glowing poles in a premium physical vocabulary, while respecting their red/blue polarity and not recycling Vaporia's aqueous azure/molten-orange materials.

- **Palette (approved):** cool blue-white **cyan** opposing saturated **coral/scarlet-red**, intense white only at compressed pressure seams; charcoal-gunmetal cores and very dark blue/indigo background. The warm pole should read **red polarity, not orange fire**.
- **Material:** taut high-energy magnetic flux **with dimensional body**, dense black magnetite-like matter, short aligned ferrous particles and crisp conductive edges. Bright energy follows purposeful force paths, not random electrical lightning or transparent decoration.
- **Atmosphere:** tightly localized glow, dark negative space, focused pressure/depth and thick readable shapes. Premium painterly-chakra volume with deliberately clean 32px reduction; no decorative lightshow as the focal point.
- **Family negatives:** no liquid/steam/flames/lava, icy crystal BBE style, BEE shadow-crimson style, franchise markings, generated text, star-logo emblems or generic concentric rings on every icon. No ninja actors, fists, hands or props without specific director acceptance.

The shared ICON spec recommends square 256px lossless WebP, minimum 160px, generic 460,800-byte ceiling. A director-owned BBE-specific <80,000-byte cap does **not** automatically carry to Houkyuken. Deliberately opaque jutsu backgrounds remain visually permitted by ruling, but their **generic ICON alpha preflight remains blocked** pending separate validator/spec review.
