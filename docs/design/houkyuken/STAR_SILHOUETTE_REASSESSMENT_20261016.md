## Proposed correction — STAR FORMS, NOT STAR-THEMED DEBRIS

**Hierarchy:** **1. primary recognizable shape** → **2. coherent direction/action** → **3. polarized color logic** → **4. minimal material detailing**. First judge the art as a flat silhouette against a contrasting field, then in monochrome and at 125/64/32px. A swirl of shards that needs full-resolution color to become meaningful fails the design gate.

**Family treatment to retain:** Polar Flux's electric cyan and scarlet opposition, brilliant concentrated white at high pressure, midnight-indigo ground, premium dimensional finish. **Treatment to reduce:** unbounded angular rock fields, miscellaneous motes, generic red-white impact explosions and many thin concentric loops. Shards can appear as sparse secondary decoration **inside/around an already legible subject**, not define it.

### Rising Star — proposed `Polar Ascendance` (REVIEW / not yet approved)

- One enormous **unmistakably five-pointed, sharp cyan/blue-white chakra star** with a **dramatically elongated UPWARD apex**. Two strong lateral points and a distinct lower pair must remain visible, so it reads as a **star**, not a featureless arrow/spear or triangular pillar.
- One compact lower red/scarlet **returning magnetic countercurve** hints at its historically supported reflect effect; it must not enclose the whole star.
- The star is a **single sculpted coherent high-density magnetic-chakra formation**, luminous edges and restrained gunmetal insets, not a spray of floating rocks. Upward rise is communicated with **one short tail** and posture, not a full-frame explosion.
- **Primary polarity:** cool cyan/blue; warm scarlet subordinate. Use color dominance as artistic coding, not a claim of engine physics.

### Morning Star — proposed `Crimson Starfall` (REVIEW / not yet approved)

- One monumental **scarlet-red, broad EIGHT-RAY spiked magnetic star** (four longer major rays plus four shorter ones), clearly a heavy star-like body, **tilted toward a downward-diagonal strike**. Thick, solid rays avoid fine filigree and keep the silhouette distinct from Rising Star's vertically stretched five-point body.
- **Primary polarity:** scarlet/red. One compact blue/cyan **fractured opposing field plate** beneath the contact edge expresses heightened target vulnerability; avoid a giant white collision bloom.
- Gunmetal geometry may articulate the star's interior; loose shards/particles are sharply limited. The entire composition should read **a red magnetic star crushing**, not rocks descending.
- This is a supernatural star made of force, **not a sun, astronomical meteor, star badge/logo, or literal handheld mace**.

These are **concrete options for director approval**, not a lock on star point count, name, ray treatment, or palette dominance. If a shared five-pointed star vocabulary is preferred to a five/eight contrast, settle that before generating replacements.

### Other family slots — safeguard distinction

- **Magnetic Pulse Strike:** preserve approved exact raw for provenance. Consider cleaning it later if the final 32px set still reads as red/blue debris; do not silently revoke previous acceptance.
- **Magnetic Assignment:** two clean **interlocking/polarized force forms**, not an impact explosion or extra star.
- **Houkyu Dance:** one broad **rhythmic orbital sweep of two substantial magnetic ribbons**, with negative-space loops, not rock swirls, another star or a landscape.
