## Approved direction: ASCENDING REVERSAL

**One dominant silhouette:** a **colossal, vertically ascending, tightly tapered lance of condensed magnetic chakra**, thrusting from a compressed origin near the lower center toward the top. A visual lance of force, **not a literal forged weapon**.

**One supporting effect:** a **small scarlet magnetic return current** curves downward/back toward the origin on **one side near the lower portion**, visually referencing the supported self-reflect effect. This returning stream must remain subordinate and should not become a second equal upward lance, broad orbit or circular halo.

**Composition and physics:**
- A strong bottom-to-top vector occupies most of the square icon: **broad muscular base, coherent taper and decisive leading spearhead high in frame**.
- Irregular **black magnetite plates/ferrous shards** are drawn upward into the interior of the force; only a few large fragments and restrained aligned filings, not a debris snowstorm.
- Thick dimensional **blue-white/cyan polar flux** binds the spear's black physical mass. Local **scarlet/coral magnetic counter-polarity** hugs the base and narrow side return, distinct from flames.
- A tight bright **white pressure seam** connects the magnetic poles near the ascent's compressed root and along a few deliberate stress fractures; not a giant white bloom that obscures the shape.
- The upward thrust itself is the focal point. Clear darker corners and visible negative space around the taper preserve readability at 125/64/32px.
- Mild asymmetry adds motion; no perfectly symmetric column, generic lightning bolt, light pillar without substance, explosion flower or vortex of random thin neon loops.
- Very dark indigo/gunmetal **full-bleed designed background** with controlled luminous edges and no scenery. No character.
