## Dominant visual design — one strong diagonal compression strike

**ONE primary silhouette:** a **large, broad, faceted magnetite hammerhead-like compression mass** driving **from upper-left toward lower-right** at a decisive 35–50-degree diagonal. The mass should occupy most of the image and feel dense, jagged and *weighted*, not pointed like a narrow spear or shaped like a literal handheld hammer/club.

**ONE supporting effect:** beneath the leading edge, a **compact fractured magnetic pressure plane** splits into a few large shards / discontinuous field plates under the blow. Its cracking stress seams hint that the target is more vulnerable to further damage, without drawing a victim, UI vulnerability marker, or full explosion.

**Specific composition:**
- The **descending compressed head** is the clear centerpiece: broad dark layered ferromagnetic plates with an angular, irregular forward face. Not an amorphous particle cloud.
- Thick cyan/blue-white magnetic force **binds and compresses** the mass from one side; vivid scarlet-red counter-polarity is focused along the opposite flank.
- A **short, restrained swept-back field trail** makes the direction **downward diagonal**, not left-to-right or straight upward. Keep dark corners and negative space.
- At its lower-right point of contact, only **one narrow white compression seam / impact crease**. Break the subordinate plane into **two or three major displaced dark plates**, with minimal small filings. No huge white central bloom.
- The contact edge must look like a **crushing collision**, rather than lightning striking the ground or magma erupting.
- Preserve strong dark-vs-light mass and a silhouette recognizable at 125/64/32px. Favor thick readable physical volumes over intricate filaments.

**Palette and materials:**
- high-energy electric cyan / blue-white magnetic flux;
- distinct concentrated scarlet-red opposing polarity (**not orange fire**);
- solid black/gunmetal magnetite with hard edges;
- tiny localized brilliant white pressure seams;
- deep midnight-indigo/charcoal background, deliberately opaque/full-bleed permissible.

**Rendering:** one premium, dramatic supernatural action-icon composition with controlled light and ferromagnetic detail; visibly consistent in fidelity/material with approved Rising Star but **different action and silhouette**.
