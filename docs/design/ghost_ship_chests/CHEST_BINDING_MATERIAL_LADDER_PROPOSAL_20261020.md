## Purpose

The first six visuals were revoked because an unphysical **duplicate metal lid hoop** propagated across the family. During regeneration, the *single central family binding* should add another clear cue of **family identity and named tier**. Texture, width, surface value, and weave/finish matter more than tiny color adjustments. This is a five-step material ladder **within each family**; Hunter always means **one flat leather/hide strap + fang clasp**, Gatherer always means **one visibly round braided rope + tucked botanical sprig**. The other rarity treatments (iron armor, crystal, glow, filigree) remain as already approved.

## Hard geometry / topology — already DIRECTOR APPROVED

- **Exactly one curved metal end hoop at each outside end of the domed lid**; no second independent parallel arch at the right or left.
- **Exactly one central binding per family**, centered and following the curve of the domed lid; a single flat leather strap (Hunter) OR single round natural/fabric rope (Gatherer).
- **No duplicated strap or rope**, no second rope turn that forms its own extra parallel lid arc, no independently mounted metal strap on the center line.
- Material refinements are **within the same binding footprint**. A double-edge stitch, inlaid thread, fine plating, texture or highlight may be *on* the binding; none is a new structural band. Where a rope wraps to make a knot, it is visibly **one continuous length** of rope; the knot may not generate a second full separate arch across the lid.
- Rare reinforced plates, Epic silver edging and Legendary filigree are **surface treatments of the existing two metal hoops**, never a third/fourth hoop. Lid-top Legendary crystals are their separate small mounted feature.
- The Hunter's **front triple claw marks** and the Gatherer's **single leaf front brand, sprig** remain readable at 125px throughout the tiers.
- The wider Ghost Ship palette remains low-value dark wood/iron with pale-cyan skyglass as the deliberate luminous accent; a natural green sprig is a botanical material cue, not a new luminous palette. Color names refer to muted physical dyes/finishes, not additional glow fields.

**Composition-first enforcement:** never rely on the material adjective alone. The generator must visibly distinguish a single **flat leather surface** from a single **rounded braided rope**, and show the intended surface treatment at 125px **without adding geometry**.

## Per-tier checks / constraints

1. **Topology before materials:** reject an extra independent curved lid band on *either end*, an extra middle binding, or metal lines that turn stitching into additional hoops. Inspect full original pixels at near/camera-right angles and at 125px.
2. **Family at 125px:** Hunter = brown/black/blue leather *flat strap* and fang plus triple claws. Gatherer = circular section *twisted or braided rope* and sprig plus single leaf. A rope does not turn into a metal strap at Legendary.
3. **Tier material at 125px:** Rusted fraying, Common plain fiber/hide, Rare dark pebbled hide/slate tight braid, Epic silver stitching/pale silken rope, Legendary embossed silver/cyan-threaded craftsmanship. Fine threads may simplify in small art; *base color, width and texture* still need to read without them.
4. **No unauthorized escalation:** Common no bright rivet/rune/glow escalation; Rare limited cold gunmetal/star details; Epic silver trim and glowing family brand; Legendary silver filigree/lid crystals. Ornate's aged gold and Mystery's chains/mist remain exclusive.
5. **Original-byte preflight still separate:** previous 6 source images are revoked and opaque JPEG-on-black; any new generation needs genuine alpha or proper chroma (magenta for Gatherer's green sprig when native alpha unavailable, flagging the conflict with the workstream's lime fallback). Do not treat visual approval as a finished `ICON` export or `artpreflight.py --type ICON` pass.
