## Main principle — material substitution instead of structural redesign

**The Common chest is the common structural template for every named rarity.** Do not enlarge or add metal arches, redesign lid hoops/corner blocks, add oversized armor plates, move the clasp, or let the central strap/rope continue to the base. Differentiate chiefly through **the finish/material of existing metal, one central leather/rope, one Hunter trophy or Gatherer sprig, and skyglass illumination**. All tiers share the same outline, camera and overall scale; Rare and up may use subtle engraving/trim ON surfaces, not an added plate silhouette.

| Named tier | Exact existing-band metal material | Wear / ornament / crystal ladder |
|---|---|---|
| **Rusted** | Corroded, pitted iron | Dull, weathered; cracked dim crystal; zero external glow. |
| **Common** | Blackened steel | Plain workmanship; small cyan clasp; no seam or brand glow. |
| **Rare** | **Burnished DARK BRONZE** | Warm bronze bands/corners replacing Common steel, brighter cyan crystal, ONE thin restrained cyan lid-seam glow; minor details only (no enlarged riveted plating). |
| **Epic** | **Polished SILVER** | Refined surfaces, enlarged faceted central clasp, glowing family brand, no extra metal hoops. |
| **Legendary** | **Iridescent PALE STARSTEEL** | Finest restrained surface decoration/filigree on SAME hoop shapes; brightest controlled skyglass and previously approved small lid-mounted crystal group as a NONSTRUCTURAL ornament, not a redesigned chest lid/body. |

**Aged gold remains reserved for the separate Ornate Chest.** Bronze is the Rare metal and must read as warm dark bronze, not golden Ornate trim. Mystery retains its independent chains/lock/mist design; neither special chest is assigned Hunter/Gatherer binding rules automatically.

## Gatherer — same metal ladder, evolving compact botanical sprig

Preserve exactly ONE centered round rope binding and ONE carved front leaf emblem, plus ONE small tucked sprig. The rope remains the approved material ladder: Rusted rotten gray hemp; Common honey-tan three-strand jute; **Rare slate-blue waxed flax tight herringbone braid**; Epic pale silver-gray sea-silk with woven silver; Legendary storm-indigo silk with restrained silver/cyan thread. Rope ties off at a compact upper-front keeper with only short tucked ends.

The **botanical accessory changes shape, leaf structure, finish and detail as rarity rises** (withered Rusted; plain fresh Common; sharper/more veined Rare; finer emerald/silver Epic; compact ornate dark-jade/cyan Legendary), with exact leaf silhouettes for each generated icon still director-owned. Green material remains foliage, not a new glowing cyan family brand at Rare. Keep one non-glowing carved leaf emblem until Epic.
