## Stage 1 — composition and action (earlier plan objectives)

**Priority 1: turn beam into a cleave.** Retain the same upper-left-to-lower-right attack trajectory, but reshape the thick even white streak into one **sharpened curved cutting front** with a thin almost-white edge, cooler compressed blue body and a broken charcoal/slate wake. A visible leading edge and taper should announce a *cut through air*, never a lightsaber, generic laser, lightning bolt, water wave, claw set, crescent moon or metal weapon. Preserve a single primary slash; subsidiary wind ribs trail it, not multiple coequal attacks.

**Priority 2: give the caster readable scale.** Bring the figure *approximately* 25–35% optically closer versus source without turning it into a portrait. Increase the mask's screen occupancy to support a distinct pale fox-mask silhouette at 45px; keep the forward arm readable, the torso dark, and the mask clear of the slash. The lower legs may be simplified or partially out of frame. Only one complete, anatomically plausible pair of hands. The figure supports the cleave rather than eclipsing it.

**Priority 3: link cause to effect.** Make the storm bands funnel toward the casting arm, then join the slash through one unmistakable compact wind-transfer ribbon. Tone down or remove repeated wrist-loop decorations, especially the off-hand circle, which presently implies a second competing technique. Keep the storm canopy in the upper portion and lower the brightness of its stripes.

**Priority 4: reduce noise.** Delete random black floating chunks and repeat highlights in favour of a few planned torn-air gaps along the slash. Abstract away the village, torii and fire. Background remains dark, two-tone storm field, not a new scene.

**Framing:** one square full-bleed opaque icon. Eye-line/mask and initiating hand in the safe upper/central area. The dramatic slash may crop *deliberately* at the leading/trailing outer edges as in the approved foundation, but its meaning must remain legible when the 112px tile's two lower-corner badges obscure the corners. Do not let mask, casting gesture or other essential information touch the occlusion zones. ## Stage 4 — **The Executioner's Reap**: cinematic action revision (NEXT; ONE source-conditioned raw)

**Why:** the current raw now has the right oppressive vortex, cold mask, cruel diagonal cleave, navy/blue-white values and premium pixel-art feel. The caster still mostly *poses with open hands*, apparently supervising an attack already in progress. The next change is **cause-and-effect body mechanics**, not more effects, a new scene, a portrait or another storm redraw. The director endorsed this approach as the next implementation direction, not as final art approval.

**Story beat / shutter moment:** capture the end of one violent rotation, precisely as the caster **hooks a clawed hand into compressed wind from the storm and rips it through the atmosphere**. The one foreground cut is the visible leading part of a wider outburst of spinning razor wind. Make it read at a glance as *masked storm-executioner seizing wind → twisting the body → delivering a single merciless cut*.

### Change only the action system

1. **Seize — a forceful claw, not a presentation hand.** Replace the loosely outstretched casting hand with a strong, curved/claw-like silhouette visibly **gripping a taut strand of captured wind**. The wrist and forearm should already be moving through a slash; the arm must not read as pointing, summoning a ball or holding a weapon. At 45px, the **arm direction and hand-to-wind silhouette**, not tiny individual fingers, must carry the gesture.
2. **Turn — make the whole torso throw the cut.** Counter-rotate shoulders against hips; twist/lean the torso forward into the stroke, bend the striking elbow and load the stance with an evident braced leg where visible. The off-hand pulls back toward the body/hip for counterbalance, **without its own glow ring**. Hair, scarf and shredded cloak whip in the **opposite direction** to the cutting arm, visibly recording the speed of the one-turn rotation. No extra/missing limbs, broken joints or implausible spine.
3. **Sever — connect the caster physically to the attack.** Funnel one or two readable, darker wind bands from the canopy into the claw/striking shoulder, then let the **same motion** pull out the already established **single giant upper-left → lower-right curved cleave**. The attack must originate at the attacking hand/arm, not float disconnected across the foreground. Preserve a narrow white cutting edge, dense icy-blue wind body and thin charcoal-black torn-air gouge/pressure wake; neither water-wave, beam, saw nor metal-blade read.
4. **Judgment — cold, not heroic.** Retain the recognizable pale fox mask and cold icy eye treatment; lower the chin/angle the mask to stare **along** the slash. Deepen arm/armor silhouette without hiding the mask. The pose should look deliberate, violent and merciless rather than flamboyant or charging up.
5. **Environmental reaction — subordinate.** At most a few **large** torn fragments and directional pressure tears immediately around the cutting path; do not fill the tile with debris. The vortex remains heavy, near-black and above/behind the caster, not a luminous ornament.

### Non-negotiable carryovers

- **Composition:** same square, same camera, mask in the upper-right/central zone, dark figure behind the diagonal foreground cut, heavy overhead vortex, exactly one primary cleave. Local arm/torso reposing is expected; wholesale re-layout, a new scene, changing the mask, shrinking the figure, or substituting a different pose is not.
- **Motion meaning:** a **360-degree razor-wind ring** is the underlying technique; this icon isolates its cinematic reaping instant. Do not introduce an opponent or silently change the technique into a focused single-target projectile.
- **Color/value:** near-black navy and charcoal storm; slate-blue and icy-blue compressed wind; a **narrowest, brightest icy-white edge**; small electric cyan only where supportive. Do not brighten the canopy, expand all-white coverage, warm-shift, teal-shift or turn the slash into a light beam.
- **Pixel finish:** preserve the source's crisp retro-32-bit look: hard stepped pixel edges, coherent clusters, readable major planes. No soft painterly glow, blur, pseudo-sharpening, broad gradients, random micro-speckle or detailed armor noise.
- **Safe area:** the fox mask, claw/arm-to-cleave origin and sweep's main directional cue must be obvious at **45px**; the jutsu-list tile's two lower corners may be covered by badges at **112px**. The attack may crop naturally at frame boundaries; essential story information must not sit in the badge zones.
- **Hard negatives:** no sword, physical weapon, opponent, extra caster, headband/series insignia, runes, text, UI, water wave, lightning-dominant beam, symmetrical shuriken, decorative wrist loops or unrelated scenery.
