## Creative mission

The current image succeeds at storm doom, mask, blue/navy palette and a huge single diagonal cutting front, but the shinobi looks like they are **standing with their hand outstretched** while the attack happens in front of them. Change the *act of cleaving* into a cinematic, merciless physical gesture:

**"A masked executioner grabs hold of the storm itself, plants into one violent turn, and physically rips the sky into a single blade of wind."**

This is a **refinement of the current image**. Keep the recognizable fox-masked armored ninja, dark upper-frame spiral canopy, one immense foreground **upper-left → lower-right** cleave, placement/camera, square proportions, oppressive navy/charcoal/ice palette and premium TNR pixel-art identity. **Do not redesign the scene or substitute a portrait.**

### The exact frozen moment to illustrate

1. **Claw and capture:** the striking hand is hooked into the compressed wind rather than pointing; strong dark hand/wrist silhouette against a taut blue-grey funnel from the overhead storm. It looks like **grabbing the air** and pulling it through resistance, not conjuring a circular orb.
2. **Rotational body mechanics:** shoulders and hips visibly counter-rotate; torso lunges/twists through the blow; a braced bent leg if visible; dominant elbow drives a wide sweeping slash. Off-hand retracts toward the hip for balance, without a second illuminated ring. Torn cloak, long fabric, hair and scarf **lag opposite** the cutting movement. Maintain anatomically plausible arms, hands, spine and leg geometry.
3. **Contact to cut:** the huge blade is **born at the claw's motion**. One readable storm-to-shoulder/hand-to-slash chain. The single existing diagonal cutting stroke becomes a sharp leading icy-white rim with compressed icy-blue wind body, a narrow deep-charcoal tear and directional pressure wake. Make the air appear severed; **not a beautiful water wave or a laser**.
4. **Executioner's face:** same pale generic fox mask with cold narrow blue eyes, chin angled down to stare along the cleave; no new helmet, face, franchise markings or facial portrait emphasis.
5. **Storm mass:** overhead spiral stays huge, near-black and oppressive. Its bands are drawn into the claw's pull; little unnecessary lighting, a few larger directional fragments rather than scattered debris/confetti.

**Approved text-to-icon fidelity:** the **newly approved battle line** (not yet verified live) describes the user planting their feet, **turning once**, an overhead wheeling vortex, the wind hardening into a **spinning wall of razor air**, lightning crackling through its arms, and a howling **ring** bursting outward indiscriminately. Draw the claw pulling wind as the **physical execution of that wind-hardening/turning moment**, not a separate new power.
