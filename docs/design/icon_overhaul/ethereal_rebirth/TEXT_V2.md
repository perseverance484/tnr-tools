## Approved text

**Description** (201 characters):
> A forbidden art that breaks the seal upon the user's soul, loosing its raw light to strike the enemy. The user is remade
> from within, wounds turning to new life as the strength drains from their blows.

*(+ the training warning, kept as approved)*

**Battle line:**
> %user throws their arms wide and the seal upon their soul cracks open, a golden core blazing to life at their chest.
> Ribbons of pale soul-light pour out of them and spin around their body before one lashes out to strike %target. Within
> that whirling light %user's body unmakes and remakes itself, turning every wound into new life, though the rebirth drinks
> the strength from their arms until their blows fall soft as ash. What rises from the light is harder to kill than what
> entered it.
