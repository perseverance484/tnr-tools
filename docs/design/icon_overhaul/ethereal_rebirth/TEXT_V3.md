## Draft

**Description** (215 characters):
> A forbidden art that breaks the seal upon the user's soul, driving a spiral of raw light into the enemy. Ribbons of that
> light then guard the user, turning wounds to new life as the strength drains from their blows.

*(+ the training warning, kept as approved)*

**Battle line** (552 characters, 4 sentences):
> %user throws their arms wide and the seal upon their soul cracks open, a golden core blazing to life at their chest.
> Ribbons of pale soul-light pour out and spin around their body, and two of them twist together into a spiralling spear
> that drives down onto %target and bursts around them. The ribbons left behind keep circling %user, drinking in each blow
> and turning every wound into new life, though the rebirth drains the strength from their arms until their blows fall
> soft as ash. What rises from the light is harder to kill than what entered it.
