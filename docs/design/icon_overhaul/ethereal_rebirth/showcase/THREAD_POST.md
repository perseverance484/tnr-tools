**Ethereal Rebirth: first premium rework done**

This is the first jutsu through the new process: a premium upgrade of the original icon (keeping the character players
know), text rewritten to match what the jutsu actually does, and a custom cast animation. All three tell the same moment:
the seal on the user's soul cracks open and ribbons of soul-light pour out.

- **Icon:** the same white-haired warrior from the original, now large in the frame on a calm background so it reads at
  phone size.
- **Text:** the old lines described green flame and elements, but the jutsu deals non-elemental damage. The new
  description and battle line match the icon and the animation.
- **Animation:** new. Gold and blue ribbons burst from the caster's chest, wrap around their avatar, one lashes out at the
  enemy, and they fade into flecks. Before, nothing played on the caster.

Sheet: old, reverted and new icons, text before and after, and frames from a live battle. GIF: the animation in battle.
Feedback welcome before it goes live.
