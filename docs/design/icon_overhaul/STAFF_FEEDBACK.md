## Director decisions stated in the thread

| Decision | Words |
|---|---|
| **No changes:** all Blood-Enchanted Eyes, all Crystal Essence, Blazing Fang Strike, Celestial Dew | "BEE, CE, Blazing fang strike and Celestial Dew are getting no changes. Unless someone says something I think those are good" |
| **No people in the icons** | Aven: "Are we removing the people from everything?" Director: "I *think* so. I do have a Jutsu Master for each offense for people renders" (Aven: "not my fave thing but if that's the goal I'm on board") |
| **Abyss of Shadows** moves off the dark red palette | "Looks to similar to BEE" (Aven agrees) |
| **Blue Blade Eyes** backgrounds | "definitely too noisy, I let the backgrounds take over" |
| **Surfing Strike** may change | "I might change the wave, at icon size it looks really similar to the dragon" |
| **Ethereal Rebirth** direction | "spinning energy for ethereal rebirth, more light blues"; multiple colours: "Sure" |
