# Tempest Cleave and Ethereal Rebirth: premium text, shown by the icon

- **Director, 2026-10-24:** "I want to look at upgrading both jutsu descriptions to be more premium, and I want our icons
  to show those descriptions in action. That should be the goal, they should clearly tie in to one another."
- **The rule this sets (RUL-2026-10-24-004):** the battle line describes one key moment, and the icon draws that moment.
  The description sums the jutsu up in the same images. Every visual element of the icon appears in the text, and every
  image the text promises is either in the icon or deliberately off-frame.
- **Status: DRAFTS for the director.** Final prose is the director's (CLAUDE.md section 10). These replace the texts
  approved on 2026-10-23 (`docs/design/jutsu_overhaul/EVENT_JUTSU_STORY_TEXT.md`) only once approved.
- **Writing rules:** `skills/building-tnr-content/references/jutsu_text.md` (on `claude/tnr-content-tickets-tcbi34`)
  for mechanics and placeholders; the event and forbidden voice rule (2026-10-23): the description is a short overview,
  the battle line a 3–4 sentence scene in action; plain colour words; no em dashes; area jutsu never use `%target`.
- **Length (director, 2026-10-24):** event jutsu descriptions may run to **260 characters** (the general guide says
  80–220).
- **Effects checked against the live records** (public read 2026-10-23). Neither text claims anything the effects do not
  do, and both cover every effect a player will notice.

## 1. Tempest Cleave (`Ul83iTziN__Z0i_UOzZTK`)

**Effects:** an area ring around the user that hits everyone in it, allies too (damage); then, for 2 rounds, hits they take
burn on (afterburn) and they take more damage (increased damage taken).

**Icon source (RUL-2026-10-24-003):** the original, a fox-masked fighter in dark armour and a white scarf, cyan lightning
crackling from their hands, a storm vortex wheeling in the sky behind them. The director's older version (a blue-white
whirlwind with ink streaks and cloud curls) supplies the vortex. Together: **the masked fighter at the eye of the
whirlwind, mid-turn, as the razor wind bursts outward around them.**

### Draft text

**Description:**
> The user turns once at the eye of a storm, and the wind becomes a blade that tears outward in a howling ring, sparing
> neither friend nor foe. Its cuts never quite close: every guard it touches hangs open, and each blow that follows sets
> the wounds burning anew.

*(+ the training warning, kept as approved)*

**Battle line:**
> %user plants their feet and turns once, and the storm answers: the sky wheels into a great vortex above them as the
> wind around them hardens into a spinning wall of razor air, lightning crackling through its arms. The tempest bursts
> outward in a howling ring and carves through everything within its reach, for it knows no allegiance. Every cut it
> leaves keeps singing with the wind and flares anew with each blow that lands, until even the hardiest guard lies in
> ribbons.

### How the icon shows it

| Text | Icon |
|---|---|
| "turns once", "plants their feet" | the masked fighter at the centre, braced low, body twisted mid-turn, scarf and coat whipped around by the spin |
| "the sky wheels into a great vortex" | the whirlwind (the older version's spiral) wheeling around and behind the fighter, its eye at the fighter's head |
| "a spinning wall of razor air" | the spiral arms drawn as thick ribbons of wind with bright, sharp white edges |
| "lightning crackling through its arms" | forked cyan lightning running along the spiral arms, sparking from the fighter's hands |
| "bursts outward in a howling ring" | the outer arms flaring past the fighter towards the frame edges; motion streaks pointing outward |
| "cuts... flare anew" | off-frame (an effect over time), suggested by small torn sparks flung out from the ring |

### What changed from the approved text and why

- Adds the vortex and the lightning, so the text names what the icon shows.
- The description now names the afterburn ("each blow that follows sets the wounds burning anew") and the exposed guard
  ("every guard it touches hangs open"), which the old one only implied ("its cuts keep singing").

## 2. Ethereal Rebirth (`K0t6Z-Q4lau1jFt8Tkpot`)

**Effects:** damages the target; for 3 rounds the user turns damage taken into health (absorb); for 2 rounds the user deals
less damage (the cost).

**Icon source (RUL-2026-10-24-003):** the original, a white-haired warrior in blue armour with a crimson scarf, mid-air,
a gold-white core blazing at the chest, ribbons of fire and water-light with crackling lightning spinning around the body.
The approved text speaks of "green flame", which the original never showed; it goes. **The moment: arms flung wide as the
seal at the chest breaks open and the elements spin out.**

### Draft text

**Description:**
> A forbidden art that breaks the seal upon the user's soul and looses fire, water and lightning through their body. The
> storm strikes the enemy and remakes the user from within, turning their wounds into new life while it drains the
> strength from their blows.

*(+ the training warning, kept as approved)*

**Battle line:**
> %user throws their arms wide and the seal upon their soul cracks open, a golden core blazing to life at their chest.
> Fire, water and lightning pour out of them in spinning ribbons that lash across the battlefield to strike %target.
> Within that whirling storm %user's body unmakes and remakes itself, turning every wound into new life, though the
> rebirth drinks the strength from their arms until their blows fall soft as ash. What rises from the storm is harder to
> kill than what entered it.

### How the icon shows it

| Text | Icon |
|---|---|
| "throws their arms wide" | the warrior mid-air, arms flung wide, white hair and crimson scarf streaming |
| "the seal upon their soul cracks open, a golden core blazing to life at their chest" | the gold-white core at the chest, the brightest point and only glow, with thin cracks of light spreading from it across the armour |
| "fire, water and lightning... in spinning ribbons" | two or three thick ribbons, orange fire and cyan water-light with forked lightning along them, in one tilted loop around the body |
| "lash across the battlefield to strike %target" | one ribbon whipping out past the frame edge (the target is off-frame) |
| "unmakes and remakes itself" | flecks of light peeling from the warrior's edges and flowing back into the ribbons |
| "blows fall soft as ash" | off-frame (the cost) |

### What changed from the approved text and why

- "Green flame" replaced by fire, water and lightning, the elements the original shows (and that the director's
  direction keeps: "spinning energy", multiple colours).
- The chest core, the arms flung wide and the spinning ribbons are now in the text, so the icon and the line describe the
  same instant.
