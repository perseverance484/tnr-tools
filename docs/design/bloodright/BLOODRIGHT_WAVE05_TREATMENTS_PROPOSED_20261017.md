**Contrast:** Teno Yuki = **quiet pale, translucent cathedral ice** versus Hyouga's saturated kinetic blue fractures and Blue Blade Eyes' steel-blue ice/weapon focus.

**Excluded:** Literal palace structures, separate ice rings, additional snowflake geometry or external frost background.
