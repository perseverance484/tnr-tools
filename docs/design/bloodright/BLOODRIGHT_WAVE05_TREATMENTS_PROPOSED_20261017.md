**Contrast:** Heavenly Sonata is blended indigo/cyan/rose/gold harmonious opal; Vaporia is blue water surrounding orange molten heat. Tenohira is **two opposed, equally strong chakra currents**, not a hydrodynamic blend.

**Excluded:** New yin-yang dots, circular taijitu insert, moon halo, flames outside masks, extra center ornaments or actor silhouettes.
