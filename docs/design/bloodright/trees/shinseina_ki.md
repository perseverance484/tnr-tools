# Shinseina Ki — Sacred Heartwood

**Bloodline:** Shinseina Ki (BR-066, rank A, `vA8I_7iBNgvkuKOpnG1yD`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance (roster pass) / Wood classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Wood (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Self amplification — Forest Wisdom's two Increase Damage Given rows, both live after one 40 AP cast and compounding (+6% route) · secondary Exposure — Guardian's Grove's area Increase Damage Taken, which multiplies every attacker's hits on the caught enemies (one row, +10% route, ×1.074) · tertiary Burst — Fangs of Heartwood primes both Forest Wisdom rows (+1%) and The Forest Devours pays off with a controlled +2 Damage on the three Wood strikes (Serpent and Grove 40 → 42, Eternal Forest 50 → 52 EP).

Shinseina Ki is a rank-A Burst kit whose six supported rows are three Wood formula hits (Exploding Wood Serpent 40, Guardian's Grove 40, Eternal Forest 50 EP at jutsu level 25; 60 AP, cooldown 7), two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows on the 40 AP Forest Wisdom cast, and one 35% Increase Damage Taken row on Grove's circle. Its biggest spike comes from those three multipliers compounding (×1.35³ ≈ ×2.46 on a window hit). Hallowed Seed answers "How do I make Forest Wisdom's two rounds my strongest window?" with self amplification of the caster's own Wood, Earth, Water and element-less hits (both rows on the kit's attacks). Grasping Roots answers "How do I turn the Grove's catch into a kill: open it to every attacker, or set up a heavier Wood strike?" with the exposure route and the burst route: Fangs of Heartwood's +1% Forest Wisdom setup, then a controlled +2 Damage payoff on The Forest Devours (the Blood-Enchanted Eyes / Shakunetsu Sakura shape; see above_nuke_rationale). Raw Damage sits apart from the full self buff, so no build stacks it on the +6% window. Recoil, poison, wound, summon and visual rows are unsupported, and Wood Warrior Summon (PVP only) carries no supported row. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four, as before the 2026-10-04 batch. The kit has three supported tags on six rows (Damage x3, Increase Damage Given x2 on one cast, Increase Damage Taken x1), so it holds exactly three identities: self amplification (Hallowed Seed → Rooted in Wisdom → Heartwood Awakened), exposure (Grasping Roots → Rootbound Prey → Judgement of the Grove) and burst (Grasping Roots → Fangs of Heartwood → The Forest Devours). A fourth capstone could only repeat one of those tags on the same rows. Universal node accepted: Grasping Roots is in all 8 legal full builds, because Heartwood Awakened's only prerequisite-closed fourth purchase is the other Foundation. A second Hidden Art under Hallowed Seed would be filler: an Increase Damage Given leaf twins Rooted in Wisdom on the same two rows and stacks with Heartwood Awakened, an Increase Damage Taken leaf twins Rootbound Prey, and a Damage leaf puts flat Damage on a Hidden Art (Eternal Forest 51 without a capstone) and becomes Heartwood Awakened's best fourth, raw Damage on the full +6% window.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. ## Design notes

- Structure: edges 01→04→05, 06→07→08 and 06→02→03. Hallowed Seed +2% / Rooted in Wisdom +1% / Heartwood Awakened +3% Increase Damage Given (route +6%); Grasping Roots +2% / Rootbound Prey +3% / Judgement of the Grove +5% Increase Damage Taken (route +10%); Fangs of Heartwood +1% Increase Damage Given (setup) / The Forest Devours +2 Damage (payoff), on Grasping Roots' +2% exposure. Any two capstones cost 5 or 6 BP. 8 legal full builds (6 numerically non-dominated): five capstone builds and three without a capstone. Maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +6%, Increase Damage Taken +10%, Damage +2 (only with The Forest Devours).
- History: the first pass of this batch removed flat Damage and shrank the tree to six nodes because any flat Damage lifts Eternal Forest past 50; the roster pass restored the burst route at +2 (the pre-batch route was +5, 40 → 45 and 50 → 55) but split it +1 on Fangs of Heartwood, so Eternal Forest reached 51 in three builds without the payoff. Final cleanup: The Forest Devours carries the whole +2 and Fangs of Heartwood is a +1% Increase Damage Given setup on Forest Wisdom, so only builds holding the capstone exceed 50 (the Blood-Enchanted Eyes / Shakunetsu Sakura shape, RUL-2026-10-04-001/002). The setup is not a twin of Rooted in Wisdom: the two sit under different Foundations, neither is a legal fourth for the other's route, and they meet only in the no-capstone 01, 02, 04, 06 (+4%, numerically dominated). Increase Damage Taken was not used for the setup: it would twin Rootbound Prey, its sibling under Grasping Roots, and put Judgement of the Grove + Fangs of Heartwood at +11%. Placement is forced by twin avoidance, not by the Foundation sentence: Fangs of Heartwood acts only on Forest Wisdom, Hallowed Seed's axis, and raises own Wood, Earth, Water and element-less hits whether or not the target is caught, so Grasping Roots' sentence covers it only loosely; under Hallowed Seed it would sit beside Rooted in Wisdom as a stacking twin (Heartwood Awakened + Fangs of Heartwood +7%; The Forest Devours + Rooted in Wisdom +4% under the +2 Damage).
- Why the printed totals differ: every Increase Damage Given point lands on both Forest Wisdom rows, which compound, so the +6% route is ×(1.41/1.35)² ≈ ×1.091 on own hits in the window; the +10% exposure route is ×1.45/1.35 ≈ ×1.074 on every attacker's hits on the caught enemies; the +2 Damage payoff is ×1.05 (×1.04 on Eternal Forest) on every Wood cast, in or out of a window. Duel opener (Forest Wisdom and Grove in round 1, Serpent and Eternal Forest in rounds 2 and 3) against the unmodified kit: The Forest Devours + Hallowed Seed ×1.099, Heartwood Awakened + Grasping Roots ×1.091, Judgement of the Grove + Hallowed Seed ×1.090. Burst leads the opener and every 40 EP Wood strike, in the window (×2.74 against ×2.72 in the circle, ×2.00 against ×1.99 away from it) and outside it (×1.05 against ×1.00); self amplification leads only on non-Wood own hits in the window (element-less, Earth, Water: ×1.99 against ×1.90 away from the circle) and on Eternal Forest by under 0.4% (×2.72 against ×2.71 in the circle, ×1.99 against ×1.98 away from it); exposure leads on allies' hits (×1.45).
- No-capstone builds (01, 02, 04, 06 at +4% Increase Damage Given / +2% exposure, numerically dominated by Heartwood Awakened + Grasping Roots; 01, 02, 06, 07 and 01, 04, 06, 07, both +3% / +5%) carry no Damage and are weaker in every situation than the capstone build suited to it; they exist only because the structure allows them.
- Fourth purchases: Heartwood Awakened takes Grasping Roots only. Judgement of the Grove takes Hallowed Seed (duel) or Fangs of Heartwood (+1% on both Forest Wisdom rows, numerically dominated by Hallowed Seed's +2%). The Forest Devours takes Hallowed Seed (Forest Wisdom 38%; the solo and duel default, see concerns) or Rootbound Prey (exposure 40%, team; duel opener ×1.092, still above Heartwood Awakened's ×1.091).
- Interactions (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3/§3b): Forest Wisdom row 0 (no element, Highest filter) matches the kit's Wood hits, every element-less hit of any stat and elemental hits of the caster's highest stat; row 1 (Earth/None/Water/Wood) matches those elements. Matching rows each multiply the hit, and Grove's exposure multiplies again; the bloodline passive (25+0.15/level, Wood/Earth/Water/None) multiplies last. Formula damage is linear in EP (tags.ts powerEffect 1436–1461), so +2 EP is ×42/40 on a 40 row before every multiplier.
- Delivery and timing: cooldown 7 on every jutsu; buff and debuff rows act only in the two rounds after their cast. Forest Wisdom (40 AP, SELF) and Grove (60 AP, radius-1 circle on an OTHER_USER target, range 4) fit one round, so Serpent and Eternal Forest land in the shared window; Grove's exposure never amplifies its own hit, and in that opener only the burst route raises it.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Wood is the single qualifying element; sharing it with Nature's Blessing is expected (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Forest Wisdom row 0 and Grove's exposure carry no element, so they need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver; the three Damage rows carry Wood natively. Wood Warrior Summon carries no Wood row and no supported row. Off-kit Wood coverage (NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN) is unverified.
- Damage tier: every build with The Forest Devours lifts Eternal Forest 50 → 52 EP, past the Nuke tier (above_nuke_rationale; director review); no allocation without it carries Damage. Serpent and Grove stop at 42, Normal tier. The +2 reaches every Wood Damage row, so an off-kit Wood row at 45 or 50 would read 47 or 52; the validator enumerates kit rows only.
- Stacking: Heartwood Awakened's two Forest Wisdom rows compound with each other and with Grove's exposure (×1.41² × 1.37 ≈ ×2.72 on a window hit in its complete build); Judgement of the Grove's 45% applies to every enemy in the circle and to every attacker's hits, so its team value grows with the number of allies. Not simulated.
- Ally hazard: Grove's damage row (friendly fire absent = ALL) and poison row (ALL) hit allies standing in the radius-1 circle at cast time; The Forest Devours raises that damage to 42 EP. The exposure row is ENEMIES only.
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
