# Shinseina Ki — Sacred Heartwood

**Bloodline:** Shinseina Ki (BR-066, rank A, `vA8I_7iBNgvkuKOpnG1yD`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance / Wood classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Wood (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Self amplification — Forest Wisdom's two Increase Damage Given rows, both live after one 40 AP cast and compounding (+6% route) · secondary Exposure — Guardian's Grove's area Increase Damage Taken, which multiplies every attacker's hits on the caught enemies (one row, +10% route) · tertiary none: Wood Damage (40 / 40 / 50 EP) is deliberately unamplified, because any flat Damage lifts Eternal Forest past the 50 Nuke tier.

Shinseina Ki is a rank-A Burst kit whose six supported rows are three Wood formula hits (Exploding Wood Serpent 40, Guardian's Grove 40, Eternal Forest 50 EP at jutsu level 25; 60 AP, cooldown 7), two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows on the 40 AP Forest Wisdom cast, and one 35% Increase Damage Taken row on Grove's circle. Its burst already comes from those three multipliers compounding (×1.35³ ≈ ×2.46 on a window hit), so the tree amplifies the multipliers, not the hits: Hallowed Seed answers "How do I make my own strikes land harder?" (self amplification on every own hit, any target) and Grasping Roots answers "How do I make the grove mark them for everyone?" (exposure that also multiplies allies' hits). Flat Damage is left out because Eternal Forest is a 50 EP Nuke. Recoil, poison, wound, summon and visual rows are unsupported, and Wood Warrior Summon (PVP only) carries no supported row. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

**Director review recommended:** Structural: the Damage route (Fangs of Heartwood → The Forest Devours) is removed rather than re-cut, because any flat Damage lifts Eternal Forest past 50; the tree shrinks to two chains with both Foundations universal. Confirm, or choose the Blood-Enchanted Eyes pattern as a third route (exposure setup → +2 Damage payoff, Serpent and Grove 40 → 42, Eternal Forest 50 → 52).

- Concern: Heartwood Awakened is held at +6% because both Forest Wisdom rows compound; Rooted in Wisdom at +2% (route +7%, about ×1.12 in a duel) would make self amplification the default even in duels.
- Concern: Rooted in Wisdom's +1% is a small printed step; it keeps the no-capstone hybrid (about ×1.08 in a duel) clearly below both capstones (about ×1.11).
- Concern: Exposure's advantage is team play; in solo fights against several enemies self amplification is stronger because it does not depend on the circle.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Six nodes and two Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on six rows (Damage x3, Increase Damage Given x2 on one cast, Increase Damage Taken x1), and this revision targets two of them. Flat Damage is declined: Eternal Forest is a 50 EP Nuke, so any flat Damage lifts it past 50, and the previous +5 route (Fangs of Heartwood → The Forest Devours) took Serpent and Grove 40 → 45 and Eternal Forest 50 → 55; that branch is removed rather than re-cut. The two identities are two 3-node chains: Hallowed Seed → Rooted in Wisdom → Heartwood Awakened (self amplification) and Grasping Roots → Rootbound Prey → Judgement of the Grove (exposure). Universal nodes accepted: with two 3-node chains every 4 BP build holds both Foundations, so the choice is which chain to finish, or the no-capstone hybrid (both Hidden Arts). A second Hidden Art under either Foundation could only repeat Increase Damage Given or Increase Damage Taken on the same rows (filler, and it would stack with that root's capstone).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Structure: edges 01→04→05 and 06→07→08. Hallowed Seed +2% / Rooted in Wisdom +1% / Heartwood Awakened +3% Increase Damage Given (route +6%); Grasping Roots +2% / Rootbound Prey +3% / Judgement of the Grove +5% Increase Damage Taken (route +10%). Three legal 4 BP builds: each capstone plus the other Foundation, and the no-capstone hybrid. Maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +6%, Increase Damage Taken +10%, no Damage.
- Why the printed totals differ: every Increase Damage Given point lands on both Forest Wisdom rows, which compound, so the +6% route is ×(1.41/1.35)² ≈ ×1.09 on own hits while the +10% exposure route is ×1.45/1.35 ≈ ×1.07 on every attacker's hits. In a duel, with Forest Wisdom and Grove cast in the same round, both complete builds reach about ×1.11 over the unmodified ×1.35³ window; self amplification also covers targets outside the circle, exposure also lifts allies.
- Damage is not targeted. Before this revision the Burst route reached +5 (Serpent and Grove 40 → 45, a full tier; Eternal Forest 50 → 55), and Judgement of the Grove's +1 and Fangs of Heartwood's +2 alone lifted Eternal Forest to 51 / 52. Any flat Damage does the same, so the kit's burst is left to its three native multipliers.
- Interactions (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3/§3b): Forest Wisdom row 0 (no element, Highest filter) matches the kit's Wood hits, every element-less hit of any stat and elemental hits of the caster's highest stat; row 1 (Earth/None/Water/Wood) matches those elements. Matching rows each multiply the hit, and Grove's exposure multiplies again; the bloodline passive (25+0.15/level, Wood/Earth/Water/None) multiplies last.
- Delivery and timing: cooldown 7 on every jutsu; buff and debuff rows act only in the two rounds after their cast. Forest Wisdom (40 AP, SELF) and Grove (60 AP, radius-1 circle on an OTHER_USER target, range 4) fit one round, so Serpent and Eternal Forest land in the shared window; Grove's exposure never amplifies its own hit.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Wood is the single qualifying element; sharing it with Nature's Blessing is expected (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Forest Wisdom row 0 and Grove's exposure carry no element, so they need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver. Wood Warrior Summon carries no Wood row and no supported row. Off-kit Wood coverage (NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN) is unverified.
- Stacking: Heartwood Awakened's two Forest Wisdom rows compound with each other and with Grove's exposure (×1.41² × 1.37 ≈ ×2.72 on a window hit in its complete build); Judgement of the Grove's 45% applies to every enemy in the circle and to every attacker's hits, so its team value grows with the number of allies. Not simulated.
- Ally hazard: Grove's damage and poison rows (friendly fire absent = ALL / ALL) still reach allies in the circle, but no node raises them; the exposure row is ENEMIES only.
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
