# Shinseina Ki — Sacred Heartwood

**Bloodline:** Shinseina Ki (BR-066, rank A, `vA8I_7iBNgvkuKOpnG1yD`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance (roster pass) / Wood classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Wood (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Self amplification — Forest Wisdom's two Increase Damage Given rows, both live after one 40 AP cast and compounding (+6% route) · secondary Exposure — Guardian's Grove's area Increase Damage Taken, which multiplies every attacker's hits on the caught enemies (one row, +10% route) · tertiary Burst — a controlled +2 Damage on the three Wood strikes (Serpent and Grove 40 → 42, Eternal Forest 50 → 52 EP) on every cast, +1 on the Hidden Art and +1 on the capstone.

Shinseina Ki is a rank-A Burst kit whose six supported rows are three Wood formula hits (Exploding Wood Serpent 40, Guardian's Grove 40, Eternal Forest 50 EP at jutsu level 25; 60 AP, cooldown 7), two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows on the 40 AP Forest Wisdom cast, and one 35% Increase Damage Taken row on Grove's circle. Its biggest spike comes from those three multipliers compounding (×1.35³ ≈ ×2.46 on a window hit). Hallowed Seed answers "How do I make Forest Wisdom's two rounds my strongest window?" with self amplification of the caster's own Wood, Earth, Water and element-less hits (both rows on the kit's attacks). Grasping Roots answers "How do I add damage beyond my own Forest Wisdom window: expose the Grove's prey to every attacker, or put more weight on every Wood strike?" with the exposure route and the burst route, a controlled +2 Damage split +1 on the Hidden Art and +1 on the capstone (the Blood-Enchanted Eyes / Shakunetsu Sakura magnitude, not their shape; see above_nuke_rationale). Raw Damage sits apart from the self buff, so no build stacks it on the +6% window. Recoil, poison, wound, summon and visual rows are unsupported, and Wood Warrior Summon (PVP only) carries no supported row. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

(1) Above-Nuke Damage outside the payoff: the burst route's +2 is split +1 on Fangs of Heartwood (Hidden Art) and +1 on The Forest Devours, with only Grasping Roots' shared +2% exposure under it, so it does not follow the Blood-Enchanted Eyes / Shakunetsu Sakura shape (RUL-2026-10-04-001/002). Eternal Forest reaches 52 with the capstone and 51 without it in three builds: Judgement of the Grove + Fangs of Heartwood (an exposure capstone's 4-BP build) and the no-capstone 01, 02, 04, 06 and 01, 02, 06, 07. The precedent shape makes burst the solo default here (above_nuke_rationale). Confirm 51 outside the payoff, take the precedent shape anyway, or keep Damage unamplified and return the tree to two chains. (2) Judgement of the Grove's +10% single-row area exposure is above the anchors' +5% exposure and should move with the roster's other exposure routes.

- Concern: Heartwood Awakened is held at +6% (Rooted in Wisdom only +1%) because both Forest Wisdom rows compound; at +7% (Rooted in Wisdom +2%, about ×1.12 in a duel) self amplification would be the default even in duels. The roster's other two-row Increase Damage Given routes sit at +7–8% (R3 allows up to +8%).
- Concern: With kit attacks Judgement of the Grove ties Heartwood Awakened at best in solo play (×2.72 against ×2.72 on a window hit in the circle; ×1.88 against ×1.99 on own hits away from it). Its edge is allies' hits (×1.45 against ×1.37) and off-element own hits in the circle (×1.99 against ×1.93). Grove's damage row (friendly fire absent = ALL) and poison row (ALL) hit any ally inside the radius-1 circle at cast time, so the melee allies the exposure route serves must not stand next to the target when Grove is cast. Values left for the director.
- Concern: Fangs of Heartwood carries flat Damage on a Hidden Art (roster rules R1/R6), so Eternal Forest reaches 51 in three builds without The Forest Devours. It is kept because both percentage commits fail the fourth-BP audit: +1% Increase Damage Given (a cross-Foundation twin of Rooted in Wisdom, all +2 on the capstone) lets Hallowed Seed put +3% on both compounding rows under +2 Damage (duel opener ×1.099 against ×1.091), and +1% Increase Damage Taken twins Rootbound Prey and reaches +11% with Judgement of the Grove.
- Concern: The burst route is modest: in a duel opener it trails the other two capstones (×1.085 against ×1.09) and leads only on Wood strikes outside the windows (×1.05 against ×1.00). Its value is uptime on every cast, not size.
- Concern: Grasping Roots is a universal node (all 8 legal full builds); see narrow_kit_exception.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four, as before the 2026-10-04 batch. The kit has three supported tags on six rows (Damage x3, Increase Damage Given x2 on one cast, Increase Damage Taken x1), so it holds exactly three identities: self amplification (Hallowed Seed → Rooted in Wisdom → Heartwood Awakened), exposure (Grasping Roots → Rootbound Prey → Judgement of the Grove) and burst (Grasping Roots → Fangs of Heartwood → The Forest Devours). A fourth capstone could only repeat one of those tags on the same rows. Universal node accepted: Grasping Roots is in all 8 legal full builds, because Heartwood Awakened's only prerequisite-closed fourth purchase is the other Foundation. A second Hidden Art under Hallowed Seed would be filler: an Increase Damage Given leaf twins Rooted in Wisdom on the same two rows and stacks with Heartwood Awakened, an Increase Damage Taken leaf twins Rootbound Prey, and a Damage leaf twins Fangs of Heartwood and becomes Heartwood Awakened's best fourth, the raw Damage on the compounding window that this layout keeps apart.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Structure: edges 01→04→05, 06→07→08 and 06→02→03. Hallowed Seed +2% / Rooted in Wisdom +1% / Heartwood Awakened +3% Increase Damage Given (route +6%); Grasping Roots +2% / Rootbound Prey +3% / Judgement of the Grove +5% Increase Damage Taken (route +10%); Fangs of Heartwood +1 / The Forest Devours +1 Damage (route +2, on Grasping Roots' +2% exposure). Any two capstones cost 5 or 6 BP. 8 legal full builds: five capstone builds and three without a capstone. Maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +6%, Increase Damage Taken +10%, Damage +2.
- Roster pass: the first pass of this batch removed flat Damage and shrank the tree to six nodes because any flat Damage lifts Eternal Forest past 50. The burst route is restored at the Blood-Enchanted Eyes / Shakunetsu Sakura magnitude (RUL-2026-10-04-001/002), +2 rather than the pre-batch +5 (40 → 45, 50 → 55): Serpent and Grove 40 → 42, Eternal Forest 50 → 52. It now hangs under Grasping Roots instead of Hallowed Seed: under Hallowed Seed, Fangs of Heartwood was Heartwood Awakened's best fourth (+6% Increase Damage Given with +1 Damage, ×2.75 on a window hit and the strongest duel opener of any build), because raw Damage and the compounding self buff multiply each other. Fangs of Heartwood is a +1 Damage commit (Terra Nova shape), not the precedent's percentage setup, because both percentage setups fail here: +1% Increase Damage Given (all +2 on The Forest Devours) makes Hallowed Seed the burst route's dominant fourth, and +1% Increase Damage Taken twins Rootbound Prey and puts Judgement of the Grove + Fangs of Heartwood at +11%, over the 10% ceiling (above_nuke_rationale).
- Why the printed totals differ: every Increase Damage Given point lands on both Forest Wisdom rows, which compound, so the +6% route is ×(1.41/1.35)² ≈ ×1.09 on own hits in the window; the +10% exposure route is ×1.45/1.35 ≈ ×1.07 on every attacker's hits on the caught enemies; the +2 Damage route is ×1.05 (×1.04 on Eternal Forest) on every Wood cast, in or out of a window. Duel opener (Forest Wisdom and Grove in round 1, Serpent and Eternal Forest in rounds 2 and 3) against the unmodified kit: Heartwood Awakened + Grasping Roots ×1.091, Judgement of the Grove + Hallowed Seed ×1.090, The Forest Devours + Hallowed Seed ×1.085. Burst leads on every Wood strike outside the windows (×1.05 against ×1.00), self amplification on own hits away from the circle, exposure on allies' hits.
- No-capstone builds (01, 02, 06, 07; 01, 02, 04, 06; 01, 04, 06, 07) are weaker in every situation (window hit in the circle, own hit away from it, hit outside the windows, allies' hits) than the capstone build suited to that situation; they exist only because the structure allows them.
- Fourth purchases: Heartwood Awakened takes Grasping Roots only. Judgement of the Grove takes Hallowed Seed (duel) or Fangs of Heartwood (41 / 41 / 51 EP; better only on Wood strikes outside the Forest Wisdom window). The Forest Devours takes Hallowed Seed (solo) or Rootbound Prey (exposure 40%, team).
- Interactions (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3/§3b): Forest Wisdom row 0 (no element, Highest filter) matches the kit's Wood hits, every element-less hit of any stat and elemental hits of the caster's highest stat; row 1 (Earth/None/Water/Wood) matches those elements. Matching rows each multiply the hit, and Grove's exposure multiplies again; the bloodline passive (25+0.15/level, Wood/Earth/Water/None) multiplies last. Formula damage is linear in EP (tags.ts powerEffect 1436–1461), so +2 EP is ×42/40 on a 40 row before every multiplier.
- Delivery and timing: cooldown 7 on every jutsu; buff and debuff rows act only in the two rounds after their cast. Forest Wisdom (40 AP, SELF) and Grove (60 AP, radius-1 circle on an OTHER_USER target, range 4) fit one round, so Serpent and Eternal Forest land in the shared window; Grove's exposure never amplifies its own hit, and in that opener only the burst route raises it.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Wood is the single qualifying element; sharing it with Nature's Blessing is expected (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Forest Wisdom row 0 and Grove's exposure carry no element, so they need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver; the three Damage rows carry Wood natively. Wood Warrior Summon carries no Wood row and no supported row. Off-kit Wood coverage (NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN) is unverified.
- Damage tier: every build with The Forest Devours lifts Eternal Forest 50 → 52 EP, past the Nuke tier, and Fangs of Heartwood alone gives 51 in three builds without it: Judgement of the Grove + Fangs of Heartwood and the no-capstone 01, 02, 04, 06 and 01, 02, 06, 07 (above_nuke_rationale; director review). Serpent and Grove stop at 42, Normal tier. The +2 reaches every Wood Damage row, so an off-kit Wood row at 45 or 50 would read 47 or 52; the validator enumerates kit rows only.
- Stacking: Heartwood Awakened's two Forest Wisdom rows compound with each other and with Grove's exposure (×1.41² × 1.37 ≈ ×2.72 on a window hit in its complete build); Judgement of the Grove's 45% applies to every enemy in the circle and to every attacker's hits, so its team value grows with the number of allies. Not simulated.
- Ally hazard: Grove's damage row (friendly fire absent = ALL) and poison row (ALL) hit allies standing in the radius-1 circle at cast time; Fangs of Heartwood and The Forest Devours raise that damage to 41 / 42 EP. The exposure row is ENEMIES only.
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
