# Shinseina Ki — Sacred Heartwood

**Bloodline:** Shinseina Ki (BR-066, rank A, `vA8I_7iBNgvkuKOpnG1yD`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Wood classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Wood (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Wood damage — Exploding Wood Serpent, Guardian's Grove, Eternal Forest (three formula rows, +6 ceiling) · secondary Increase Damage Given — Forest Wisdom's two self rows on one 40 AP cast (+7 ceiling) · tertiary Increase Damage Taken — Guardian's Grove's area exposure (one row, +10 ceiling).

Shinseina Ki is a rank-A Burst kit whose six supported rows split 3/2/1: three Wood formula damage rows (Exploding Wood Serpent 40, Guardian's Grove 40, Eternal Forest 50 at jutsu level 25; all 60 AP, cooldown 7), two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows that both ride the single 40 AP Forest Wisdom cast and are active together for two rounds, and one 35% Increase Damage Taken row on Guardian's Grove's circle. Raw Wood power is the primary route because it is the kit's own output and its Burst trait; the doubled Forest Wisdom buff is the secondary route because every point lands twice and also lifts normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks; Grove's exposure is the tertiary route because it is the kit's only enemy debuff and amplifies allies' hits as well. Recoil, poison, wound, summon and visual rows are unsupported, and Wood Warrior Summon (PVP only) carries no supported row.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on six rows (Damage x3, Increase Damage Given x2, Increase Damage Taken x1), so it supports exactly three routes: Hallowed Seed forks into a Wood damage route (Fangs of Heartwood → The Forest Devours, +6 at the Damage guardrail) and a Forest Wisdom route (Rooted in Wisdom → Heartwood Awakened, +7 on two self rows that are always up together); Grasping Roots → Rootbound Prey → Judgement of the Grove is a single chain on the kit's only enemy debuff. A fourth route would need a fourth tag the kit does not have. A second Hidden Art under Grasping Roots was rejected: a +2 Damage node is a twin of Fangs of Heartwood (identical bonus totals for 01,06,07,09 and 01,02,06,07), and a +2 IDG or +2 IDT node is dominated by Hallowed Seed or Rootbound Prey as a fourth purchase. The consequence is that Hallowed Seed is in every legal full build and the exposure route's fourth purchase is always Hallowed Seed, while Burst and Amplification each have two fourth-purchase choices. Three distinct complete builds exist, one per Advanced Art.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Wood-classified Shinseina Ki jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Fangs of Heartwood** — Heartwood does not splinter. It bites, and what it bites it holds. All three Wood damage rows: Serpent 40→43, Grove 40→43, Eternal Forest 50→53 with Hallowed Seed. Grove's circle also hits allies in it.
- **The Forest Devours** — Trunks close like jaws, and the field is forest again before the scream ends. Route total +6: Serpent 40→46, Grove 40→46, Eternal Forest 50→56 power; three Wood formula hits, 60 AP each, cooldown 7.
- **Rooted in Wisdom** — Age is not years but rings; the sage reads them and strikes where the grain runs. Forest Wisdom rows 0 and 1 35→39% with Hallowed Seed (self, 2 rounds, one 40 AP cast); both rows are active together. 2 rows.
- **Heartwood Awakened** — The sacred tree opens its eyes, and its will moves through every limb at once. Route +7: both Forest Wisdom rows 35→42% (self, 2 rounds); +14 on Wood and element-less hits, Earth/Water of a non-highest stat +7 only.
- **Grasping Roots** — Roots find the ankle first. After that, everything else finds the mark. Guardian's Grove exposure 35→37% (enemy debuff, 2 rounds, enemies in the circle, range 4, 60 AP, cooldown 7). 1 row.
- **Rootbound Prey** — Held fast in living timber, the prey can only watch the canopy lean in. Grove exposure 35→40% with Grasping Roots; all four stat types, no element, so every non-pierce hit on the held enemy counts. 1 row.
- **Judgement of the Grove** — The grove has deliberated long enough. Its sentence arrives from every side. Grove exposure reaches 45% with the route; +1 power on Serpent, Grove and Eternal Forest (42/42/52 with Hallowed Seed).

## Design notes

- Node split: 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts. Hallowed Seed (Damage +1 on three rows, IDG +2 on both Forest Wisdom rows; 5 of 6 rows) forks into Fangs of Heartwood → The Forest Devours (Damage +2/+3) and Rooted in Wisdom → Heartwood Awakened (IDG +2/+3). Grasping Roots → Rootbound Prey → Judgement of the Grove (IDT +2/+3/+5, plus Damage +1 on the capstone) is the separate enemy-facing chain. Every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP, so a build holds at most one.
- Flats by coverage: Damage reaches three rows, so the route totals +6 (1+2+3), at the guardrail and one above Taiyo Kami's three-row +5; kept as a user-owned choice since the three hits are each 60 AP on cooldown 7, so at most one lands per round. IDG reaches two self rows on one cast, up together, so its route stops at +7 (2+2+3; 35→42%). IDT is one row on the single-row scale (2+3+5; 35→45%). Judgement's secondary is Damage +1, not IDG: +2 IDG or more dominates the 01,04,06,07 hybrid.
- Interactions: Forest Wisdom row 0 is Highest-stat filtered with no element, so it matches the Wood hits and every element-less hit of any stat type (basic attacks, weapons, normal jutsu); row 1 lists Earth, None, Water and Wood, no stat filter. Both add power/100 × the staged base per hit; the bloodline's IDG passive (25 + 0.15/level) multiplies on top. Grove's exposure lists all four stat types, no element: every non-pierce hit on an exposed enemy, from caster or allies, is amplified.
- Delivery and uptime: every jutsu has cooldown 7. Serpent and Eternal Forest are 60 AP single-target hits at range 4. Guardian's Grove is a 60 AP radius-1 circle on an OTHER_USER target at range 4, applied once to each non-caster in it: the exposure row is friendly fire ENEMIES; the damage row's friendly fire is absent (= ALL) and the poison row is explicit ALL, so allies in the circle take both and the caster never does. Forest Wisdom is a 40 AP self cast; its IDG rows last 2 of its 7 rounds.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01,02,03) finishes with Rooted in Wisdom (buffs 39%; the advertised, solo-strongest build) or Grasping Roots (exposure 37%; team play); Amplification (01,04,05) with Fangs of Heartwood (hits 43/43/53; advertised) or Grasping Roots; Exposure (01,06,07,08) only with Hallowed Seed. No-capstone hybrids: 01,02,04,06 (43/43/53, 39%, 37%), 01,02,06,07 (43/43/53, 37%, 40%), 01,04,06,07 (41/41/51, 39%, 40%). 8 legal full allocations, all non-dominated, every node in at least one.
- Values held for user decision (OPEN_DECISIONS D8); strongest build 01,02,03,04 is 26 row-weighted on six rows (4.33/row; Taiyo Kami 3.44). Trim levers, not re-validated: Damage +6 → The Forest Devours +2 (route +5). IDG +7 on two always-together rows realizes +14 per cast over a multiplying passive (Taiyo two-row +5) → Heartwood Awakened +2 (route +6). IDT +10 on one area row also amplifies allies' hits (Taiyo single-target +7) → Judgement of the Grove +3 (route +8).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Wood is not exclusive in the census (Nature's Blessing is an INCLUDE kit with two Wood damage rows), and whether any NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN jutsu carries Wood is unverified. The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification; a bare element match would leak the Damage nodes to Nature's Blessing and vice versa.
- Resolver: 2 of the 6 supported rows (Forest Wisdom row 0 and Grove's exposure) have no element and fall back to None under the current resolver, so they are unreachable with affectedElements=['Wood'] without the proposed classification change; reaching them through None would also reach every element-less row on any jutsu the player casts.
- Ally hazard: Guardian's Grove row 1 (Wood damage) rides an AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN on an OTHER_USER target with friendly fire absent (treated as ALL), so allies in the circle take it; the caster is never a target. Hallowed Seed, Fangs of Heartwood, The Forest Devours and Judgement of the Grove raise it (up to 46). Positioning, not the node, decides; the poison row (ALL, unsupported) is unchanged.
- Ceilings pending user acceptance (design note 6): Heartwood Awakened puts both Forest Wisdom rows at 42%, +14 over base on the kit's Wood hits and every element-less hit for two rounds, over the multiplying IDG passive (BATTLE_TAG_STACKING on at the pin). Judgement of the Grove makes Grove's area exposure 45% for two rounds and amplifies allies' non-pierce hits as well as the caster's.
- Universal opener: every legal full build contains Hallowed Seed because the exposure chain has no other prerequisite-closed fourth purchase. A mirrored layout only moves the universal node to the other root; on six rows there is no second four-node subtree without filler, so the brief's preference for no mandatory opener is not met (Lycanthropy and Shinrai Ou precedent).
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the bloodline's IDG passive and the kit's own Forest Wisdom buffs, so +6 raw power is not linear +6 damage; realized value depends on stats. Serpent's recoil 40% and Eternal Forest's wound 25% are unsupported and unchanged. No combat simulation was performed.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there. Wood Warrior Summon is PVP-only and carries no supported row (summon and visual are not potency targets), so the kit's A-rank jutsu receives nothing from any node.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Shinseina Ki jutsu inherit Wood potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
