# Shadow Weaver — Threads of Night

**Bloodline:** Shadow Weaver (BR-063, rank A, `d0WYPbbsVx7Y_i0fddwxc`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance / Shadow classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Shadow (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Burst from Loom of Dusk: Barbed Thread sets up the three stacking self buffs (Increase Damage Given) and Night Unraveled pays off with a controlled +2 Damage on four 40 EP Shadow attacks · secondary Fortress on Shadow Shell's Decrease Damage Taken (+10%) and Suppression on Shadow Severance's Decrease Damage Given (+10%) · tertiary Increase Damage Given outside Burst: Barbed Thread as Suppression's offensive fourth (buffs 38%); the Fortress carries no offensive rider.

Shadow Weaver is a rank-A Bukijutsu Sustained DPS kit: four 40 EP Shadow attacks (Domain, Severance, Step, Dance; 60 AP, cooldown 7) and three 35% self Increase Damage Given buffs (Domain, Step, Shell) that compound when their windows overlap. The old +5 Damage route lifted all four attacks 40 → 45, a full tier each; Burst now sets up the buffs on the Hidden Art and pays off with +2 Damage (40 → 42) on the capstone. Because the three buffs compound, no legal allocation passes +5% Increase Damage Given, the level the roster holds for three compounding rows; the Burst route is where it peaks. Loom of Dusk answers "How do I win the exchange: strike harder or blunt their strikes?" (Burst or Suppression on Severance); Cloak of Woven Night answers "How do I turn my own weave into armour?" with one chain (Fortress on Shell), so Loom of Dusk is in every legal 4-BP build. No Afterburn, Lifesteal, Reflect, Heal or Increase Damage Taken row exists, so none is invented. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Shadow jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

**Director review recommended:** Roster questions: DQ-H (9 → 8 nodes: Umbral Whetstone, Barbed Thread's twin kept only so Loom of Dusk was not universal, is removed; Loom of Dusk is now in every legal 4-BP build).

- Concern: Burst against the old +5 Damage route: at the peak (all three buff windows live) about ×1.17 against ×1.18, and over one assumed rotation (Shell with Domain, then Step, Severance, Dance) about +12.5% against +15.6% (not simulated). The first pass's +6% version reached ×1.20 at the peak, above the old route, because compounding Increase Damage Given outgrew the 3 EP it replaced; holding the buffs at +5% makes the re-cut a small nerf at both the peak and the rotation while removing the four tier jumps. +3 Damage on Night Unraveled (40 → 43) is not proposed: four Normal rows keep the roster's +2 default.
- Concern: Loom of Dusk is universal (acknowledged in the narrow-kit exception): the Fortress build is fixed (01, 06, 07, 08), pure guard plus a 32% sever, and Seamless Shroud is a bare +4% capstone now that its +2% Increase Damage Given rider is gone.
- Concern: Loom of Dusk is now +2% Decrease Damage Given only, so a Burst build pays its first point for a 32% sever (Shakunetsu Sakura's Falling Ember Petals also opens its burst route with a +2% Decrease Damage Given Foundation). Moving the Foundation's +2% Increase Damage Given off is what lets Barbed Thread carry a +3% setup while no allocation passes +5% on the three compounding buffs.
- Concern: Suppression and Fortress each rest on one element-less row that the current resolver cannot reach (G1), and Shell's Increase Damage Given window (one of Burst's three) also needs G1 plus Shell's authored classification.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has four supported tags on nine rows (Damage x4, Increase Damage Given x3, Decrease Damage Given x1, Decrease Damage Taken x1). Burst takes Damage with Increase Damage Given as its setup, Suppression takes Decrease Damage Given and Fortress takes Decrease Damage Taken. A fourth capstone could only be a second Increase Damage Given route: it would read as Burst without the Damage, and on three compounding self buffs it could not exceed Burst's +5% without becoming the tree's strongest offensive stack. Cloak of Woven Night therefore runs one chain and Loom of Dusk is universal (the Voltara, Musashi Ken and Ha Yanagi pattern): it is in every legal 4-BP build, so the Fortress build is fixed (06, 07, 08 + 01) and every build carries Severance at 32% or more. Every leaf that could avoid this is filler: an Increase Damage Given leaf twins Barbed Thread (the pre-batch Umbral Whetstone, removed), a Decrease Damage Given leaf only re-sells Loom of Dusk as Fortress's fourth, and a Decrease Damage Taken leaf lifts Shell's single row past +10%.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Shadow jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Loom of Dusk** — Every thread the weaver draws tight is a thread pulled loose from the foe. Severance's Decrease Damage Given 30 → 32% on every non-pierce hit the Severed target deals (2 rounds per cast).
- **Barbed Thread** — Every strand the weaver wears grows a barb. Setup: Domain, Step and Shell self buffs 35 → 38%, live the 2 rounds after each cast. All three raise the kit's Shadow Bukijutsu attacks and element-less hits; Domain's and Step's also raise Fire, Lightning and other Shadow hits.
- **Night Unraveled** — Pull the last thread and the dark comes apart in their hands. Burst payoff: Domain, Severance, Step and Dance 40 → 42 EP (no tier change); self buffs 35 → 40% on the full route, so a strike under all three windows lands at ×1.40³ ≈ ×2.74 instead of ×2.46.
- **Frayed Resolve** — Cut enough strands and even the strongest arm swings hollow. Severance's Decrease Damage Given +3% (35% with Loom of Dusk); 2 rounds per cast; pierce excluded.
- **Strings Cut Short** — Cut the strings and the blow dies before it lands. Suppression: Severance 30 → 40% Decrease Damage Given on the full route (+10%), on every non-pierce hit the target deals for 2 rounds; Shadow Shell's Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 37%.
- **Cloak of Woven Night** — Darkness pulled close enough becomes armor. Shadow Shell's self Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 38% (2 rounds per 40 AP cast); every non-pierce hit taken.
- **Tightened Weave** — No gap between the threads; no gap for a blade. Shadow Shell's Decrease Damage Taken +3% (41% with Cloak of Woven Night).
- **Seamless Shroud** — Wrapped so tight that no blade finds a seam. Fortress: Shadow Shell 35 → 45% Decrease Damage Taken on the full route (+10%), so hits in its window land at ×0.55 instead of ×0.65.

## Design notes

- 2026-10-04 rebalance (BALANCE_REVIEW_METHOD.md). Kept: names, both Foundations, Frayed Resolve, Strings Cut Short and the Fortress line. First pass: Barbed Thread +2 Damage → Increase Damage Given; Night Unraveled +3 Damage → +2 Damage, +2% Increase Damage Given; Strings Cut Short's +3% Increase Damage Given rider → +2% Decrease Damage Taken. The old +5 Damage route lifted Domain, Severance, Step and Dance 40 → 45 (four Normal → High tier jumps), and Damage on the Hidden Art made +2 Damage the obvious fourth purchase for Suppression. Roster pass: Loom of Dusk drops its +2% Increase Damage Given (now +2% Decrease Damage Given only, the Falling Ember Petals pattern) and Barbed Thread +2 → +3%, so the three compounding buffs peak at +5% on the Burst route (first pass: +6% on Burst, +7% with no capstone). Final cleanup: Umbral Whetstone (+2% Increase Damage Given on Barbed Thread's three rows, kept only so Loom of Dusk was not universal) is removed, 9 → 8 nodes, and Seamless Shroud drops its +2% Increase Damage Given rider (no offensive rider on a fortress capstone), leaving it a bare +4% after Tightened Weave's +3%.
- Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +2 (40 → 42, no tier change), Increase Damage Given +5% (only on the Burst route), Decrease Damage Given +10%, Decrease Damage Taken +10%.
- Impact: the three self buffs are jutsu-sourced and multiply in sequence (§3b): ×1.35³ ≈ ×2.46 unenhanced, ×1.40³ ≈ ×2.74 on the Burst route (×1.115, the tree's top offensive package, below Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.234); a single window gains about +4%. Burst's +2 Damage multiplies under the same windows, about ×1.17 with all three live. Fortress cuts hits in Shell's window from ×0.65 to ×0.55 (−15%); Suppression cuts the Severed target's hits from ×0.70 to ×0.60 (−14%); both are single rows, milder than Blood-Enchanted Eyes' two-row ×0.747 / ×0.725. The bloodline passive (Increase Damage Given on Fire, Lightning, Shadow and element-less hits) multiplies last.
- Reach: Domain's and Step's buff rows list Fire, Lightning, Shadow and None, so they also raise Fire and Lightning jutsu, basic attacks and other element-less hits; Shell's buff is element-less (its Bukijutsu filter is not binding). Severance's Decrease Damage Given and Shell's Decrease Damage Taken list all four stat types and no element, so each covers every non-pierce hit the target deals or the caster takes in its window.
- Delivery: all five jutsu are cooldown 7; buffs and debuffs are live the two rounds after the cast and never in the cast round (§3b), so Domain's and Step's buffs never raise their own hit. Domain (GROUND spiral) and Step (EMPTY_GROUND circle) carry SELF buff rows realized on the caster at cast; their damage rows are friendly fire ENEMIES, so no supported row is an ally hazard.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Cloak of Woven Night (Shell 38%) or Frayed Resolve (Severance 35%), both defensive; Suppression takes Barbed Thread (buffs 38%, ×1.068 with all three windows) or Cloak of Woven Night (Shell 40%); Fortress's fourth is fixed, Loom of Dusk (Severance 32%). No fourth adds Damage or lifts Increase Damage Given past Burst's +5%. All eight full allocations are numerically non-dominated.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Shadow is the single qualifying element; sharing it with other bloodlines is expected (RUL-2026-10-03-005). 6 of 9 kit rows carry Shadow; Severance's Decrease Damage Given and both Shell rows carry no element, so Suppression and Fortress depend wholly on the proposed jutsu-classification resolver (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Shell's Increase Damage Given row is one of Burst's three compounding windows and is reached only through G1 plus Shell's authored classification, so the quoted ×1.40³ figures assume it: under today's resolver Burst's peak is ×1.40² × 1.35 (≈ ×1.13 rather than ≈ ×1.17). Off-kit Shadow coverage (NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN) is unverified.
- Off-kit Damage: +2 Damage reaches every Shadow Damage row the caster can use; an off-kit Shadow jutsu at 50 would become 52. No off-kit catalog exists to check.
- Increase Damage Given is the high-leverage tag: one +1% lifts three compounding windows, and Domain's and Step's rows also raise Fire, Lightning and element-less hits. Its +5% peak sits on the Burst route; combined final damage was not simulated.
- Uptime: every buff and debuff window is 2 rounds on a cooldown-7 cast, so Shell's 45% and Severance's 40% are live at most two rounds in seven.
- Shadow Step's move row is unsupported and flagged enemy-hazard (positive ground row, friendly fire none); no node changes it. No supported row is adverse, ally-hazard or enemy-hazard.
- Loom of Dusk is universal: it is in all 8 legal full allocations (acknowledged in the narrow-kit exception), so the Fortress build is fixed and no build is Loom-free.
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Shadow jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
