# Shadow Weaver — Threads of Night

**Bloodline:** Shadow Weaver (BR-063, rank A, `d0WYPbbsVx7Y_i0fddwxc`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Shadow classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Shadow (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (four Shadow Bukijutsu attacks, +5 ceiling) · secondary Increase Damage Given (three self-buff rows on Domain, Step and Shell, +5 ceiling) · tertiary Decrease Damage Given (Severance, +10 ceiling) and Decrease Damage Taken (Shell, +10 ceiling).

Shadow Weaver is a rank-A Bukijutsu Sustained DPS kit: four of its nine supported rows are Shadow damage at 40 (Domain, Severance, Step and Dance, all 60 AP / cooldown 7), so raw Shadow power is the primary emphasis and the deepest commitment. Three rows are 35% self Increase Damage Given buffs (Domain and Step on Fire, Lightning, Shadow and element-less hits; Shell element-less with a non-binding Bukijutsu filter), the kit's sustained-damage identity, held to +5 across Foundation, capstone secondaries and one Hidden Art because three rows stack. The single rows, Severance's 30% Decrease Damage Given and Shell's 35% Decrease Damage Taken, anchor the suppression and fortress routes at +10 each. No Afterburn, Lifesteal, Reflect, Heal or Increase Damage Taken row exists, so none is invented.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Nine nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has four supported tags on nine rows (Damage x4, Increase Damage Given x3, Decrease Damage Given x1, Decrease Damage Taken x1). The offensive root (Loom of Dusk) forks into a damage route and a suppression route; the defensive root (Cloak of Woven Night) carries the fortress chain and one Hidden Art leaf. Increase Damage Given is the only tag left for a fourth route and is deliberately not given one: it reaches three stacking rows, so it is held to the +5 that Taiyo Kami's two-row Increase Damage Given tolerates, and that +5 is already spent by the Foundation share (+2), the capstone secondaries (+3 / +2) and Umbral Whetstone (+3); a fourth capstone would either breach that ceiling or restate the Damage, Decrease Damage Given or Decrease Damage Taken capstones under a new name. Umbral Whetstone exists so that the defensive root completes a full build (06, 07, 08, 09) without Loom of Dusk; it is +3 while Seamless Shroud's secondary is +2 so that build is not dominated by 01, 06, 07, 08. Three distinct complete builds exist, one per Advanced Art.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Shadow-classified Shadow Weaver jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Loom of Dusk** — Every thread the weaver draws tight is a thread pulled loose from the foe. Domain, Step and Shell self buffs 35→37% (2 rounds); Severance's Decrease Damage Given 30→32% on every non-pierce hit the target deals.
- **Barbed Thread** — The strand that binds also bites. Domain, Severance, Step and Dance 40→42 (Shadow, Bukijutsu; 60 AP, cooldown 7). Domain spiral and Step circle strike enemies only.
- **Night Unraveled** — Pull the last thread and the dark comes apart in their hands. Same four Shadow damage rows; route total +5 (Domain, Severance, Step and Dance 45 at jutsu level 25).
- **Frayed Resolve** — Cut enough strands and even the strongest arm swings hollow. Severance's Decrease Damage Given only (+3 more: 35% with Loom of Dusk); 2 rounds per 60 AP cast; all four stat types, pierce excluded.
- **Strings Cut Short** — Their strikes end before they begin; yours never stop. Severance's debuff to 40% (route +10); Domain, Step and Shell self buffs +3 (40% with Loom of Dusk). Both are 2-round windows.
- **Cloak of Woven Night** — Darkness pulled close enough becomes armor. Shadow Shell's self Decrease Damage Taken 35→38% (40 AP self cast, 2 rounds); all four stat types, every non-pierce hit taken.
- **Tightened Weave** — No gap between the threads; no gap for a blade. Shadow Shell's Decrease Damage Taken only (+3 more: 41% with Cloak of Woven Night); non-pierce hits; 2 rounds per 40 AP cast.
- **Seamless Shroud** — Wrapped so tight that nothing enters, and nothing dulls the edge inside. Shadow Shell's reduction to 45% (route +10); Domain, Step and Shell self buffs +2 (37%, or 40% with Umbral Whetstone).
- **Umbral Whetstone** — Steel honed on shadow keeps its edge long after the light is gone. Domain, Step and Shell self buffs +3 (38%; 40% with Loom of Dusk or Seamless Shroud); Fire, Lightning, Shadow and element-less hits.

## Design notes

- Node split: the Damage route (+2 Hidden, +3 Advanced) reaches the four Shadow attacks and is held to +5 because it touches four rows (20 row-weight against Taiyo Kami's 15 on three). Strongest full build by row weight is Suppression plus Barbed Thread (01, 02, 04, 05) at 33 on 9 rows; Burst plus Cloak is 31 (Taiyo 31). Single-row tags carry the larger flats: Decrease Damage Given 2/3/5, Decrease Damage Taken 3/3/4, both +10. Cloak of Woven Night is a lone +3 so no Foundation adds Damage.
- Capstone secondaries never out-bid a sibling Hidden Art on the same tag: Strings Cut Short carries Increase Damage Given +3 (no Hidden Art under Loom of Dusk carries it) and Seamless Shroud carries +2, below its sibling Umbral Whetstone's +3. Night Unraveled is Damage-only like Solar Cataclysm. Increase Damage Given maxima: 01+05 = 5, 01+09 = 5, 08+09 = 5, 01+08 = 4; no legal build exceeds +5.
- Passives and downstream reach: the bloodline passive (Increase Damage Given 25% + 0.15/level on Fire, Lightning, Shadow, None) is not a potency target, but Domain's and Step's buff rows share that element list, so a 40% buff raises the Shadow attacks, Fire and Lightning jutsu, basic attacks and non-elemental jutsu. Shell's buff is element-less with a Bukijutsu filter, so it reaches every element-less hit of any stat type and every Bukijutsu hit. The three rows stack when their windows overlap.
- Severance's Decrease Damage Given lists all four stat types with no element, so it cuts every non-pierce hit the debuffed target deals for 2 rounds from any source; Shell's Decrease Damage Taken is filtered the same way and reduces every non-pierce hit the caster takes. Neither touches pierce. Both are buff/debuff-prevent gated.
- Delivery: all five jutsu are cooldown 7. Domain (GROUND spiral, range 4, 60 AP) and Step (EMPTY_GROUND circle, range 5, 60 AP) carry SELF buff rows realized on the caster at cast time, not through the tiles; their damage rows have friendly fire ENEMIES, so no supported row is an ally hazard. Severance and Dance are single-target range 4, 60 AP; Shell is a 40 AP self cast. Whether a cast's own damage row benefits from the buff that same cast applies was not verified.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Cloak of Woven Night (38% Shell) or Frayed Resolve (35% sever); Suppression takes Cloak or Barbed Thread (+2 power); Fortress takes Umbral Whetstone (40% buffs) or Loom of Dusk (39% buffs, 32% sever). Twelve legal full-budget allocations exist; 01, 04, 06, 09 is the one dominated allocation (Strings Cut Short beats Umbral Whetstone as its fourth purchase) and is reported by the validator.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Shadow is the single signature element but not exclusive in the census (13 collisions, including INCLUDE bloodlines Blood-Enchanted Eyes, Godstorm Eclipse, Night Parade and Oblivion Seal); the tree assumes a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification, not a bare Shadow match. Normal-jutsu Shadow use is unverified; the current resolver matches 6 of 9 rows directly, 3 fall to None.
- Damage reach: +5 on four 40-power rows is 20 row-weight against Taiyo Kami's 15; every attack costs 60 AP on cooldown 7, so the per-rotation gain depends on how many of the four are cast. Values are raw power before the Bukijutsu formula; final damage was not simulated.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: three 35% self buffs (40% at the ceiling) stack with each other, with the bloodline passive (bloodline-sourced modifiers multiply and honour allowBloodlineDamageIncrease) and with main-tree or gear modifiers. Shell's Bukijutsu filter is not binding for element-less hits, so weapons and basic attacks benefit too. Combined final damage was not simulated.
- Decrease Damage Given reach: Severance's debuff (all four stat types, no element) cuts every non-pierce hit the target deals for 2 rounds, including normal jutsu and weapons, so the Suppression build's low row-weight understates it; pierce hits are untouched, and debuffprevent on the target blocks it.
- Decrease Damage Taken: Shell's reduction skips pierce hits and lasts 2 rounds per 40 AP cast on cooldown 7, so the 45% ceiling is live for at most two rounds in seven; the percentage cap at 100 is far away. Shell's visual row is adverse but unsupported and untouched.
- Uptime: all buff and debuff windows are 2 rounds on cooldown-7 casts, so the three Increase Damage Given rows rarely overlap in one rotation and per-row figures overstate per-round value. Whether a buff applied by Domain or Step affects that same cast's damage row was not verified.
- Shadow Step's move row is unsupported and flagged enemy-hazard by the dossier (positive ground row, friendly fire none); no node changes it. No supported row is adverse, ally-hazard or enemy-hazard.
- Nine-node tree: Fortress has one Loom-free full build (06, 07, 08, 09); every other full allocation includes Loom of Dusk, so the offensive Foundation is near-universal. The row-weight gap between the roots (8 against 3) was accepted to keep Damage at +5.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Shadow Weaver jutsu inherit Shadow potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
