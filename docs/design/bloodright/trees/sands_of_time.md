# Sands of Time — Dominion of Hours

**Bloodline:** Sands of Time (BR-059, rank H, `yzbSbZ5rqRVIjiwN-farx`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Sand classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Sand (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Control — Increase Damage Taken (Timeshift, Momentum Shift; two enemy rows) and Decrease Damage Given (Timelapse circle) · secondary Tank — Decrease Damage Taken (Timelapse self row) and Heal (Cellular Regeneration) · tertiary Sand damage (Timeshift 40, Eternity Flux 50, Timelapse 40) and the Momentum Shift self Increase Damage Given.

Sands of Time is a rank H Tank / Control kit whose nine supported rows split 3 Damage, 2 Increase Damage Taken and one each of Increase Damage Given, Decrease Damage Given, Decrease Damage Taken and Heal. Control is primary because it owns the kit's only two-row tag (Timeshift's 35% IDT lists all four stat types and no element; Momentum Shift's 35% IDT lists Earth/None/Sand/Wind) and the only area debuff (Timelapse's 30% Decrease Damage Given on every enemy in the circle), both of which also amplify or blunt what allies deal and take. Tank is secondary: Timelapse's self Decrease Damage Taken (30%, four stat types, no element) and Cellular Regeneration's static Heal 25 (250 HP per tick for 2 ticks) are the kit's only self rows apart from Momentum Shift's IDG. Damage is tertiary by role even though it reaches three rows and takes the Taiyo-standard +5 route; stun, timecompression and barrier are unsupported and receive nothing.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Sand-classified Sands of Time jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Topology and five flats reuse Taiyo Kami: Borrowed Hours, Grinding Sands, Erosion of Eternity, Held in Stasis, Leaden Seconds = Dawnheart, Crown of Cinders, Solar Cataclysm, Golden Mantle, Ashen Verdict. Departures: no Afterburn rows, so Brittle with Age → The Inevitable Hour is two-row IDT (+2, then +3 IDT/+3 IDG); Heal replaces DDG on Suspended Moment and IDG on Timeless Bastion; Stilled Hourglass adds DDT +2, not IDT. It fits: three damage rows, exposure/self-amp, a self DDT, an area DDG.
- Calibration: Damage (3 rows, as Taiyo Kami) takes Taiyo's 2+3 = +5, not +6: tertiary. Two-row IDT takes +7 (2+2+3), Taiyo's single-row Stellar Inferno flat: 14 row-weighted vs Taiyo's 7 (its two-row IDG stops at +5), a user-owned lever (see Row weight). Single-row DDT is Taiyo's 2+3+5 = +10; single-row DDG is 3+5 = +8 as Timelapse hits every enemy in the circle. IDG and Heal are glue, +5 each. Capstone secondaries stay below the sibling Hidden Art (Stilled Hourglass DDT +2 < Held in Stasis +3).
- Row weight: strongest full builds score 25 (01,02,03,04; 01,02,04,05; 01,02,03,06) vs Taiyo Kami's 31 on nine rows; all 14 full allocations non-dominated, each node in one. Lowest-value: Held in Stasis and Leaden Seconds (+3, one row), Taiyo's Golden Mantle/Ashen Verdict shape. Levers (user-owned): if rank H reads conservative, Erosion of Eternity +4 Damage (route +6) or Stilled Hourglass +7 DDG (route +10); if two-row IDT (14 row-weighted vs 7) reads too strong, hold it at +5 (2+2+1 or 2+1+2).
- Matching (getEfficiencyRatio, tags.ts 3477–3510; G15/G16): Timeshift IDT and Timelapse DDG/DDT list four stat types, no element: every non-pierce hit of those stats or with no element. Momentum Shift IDT (Earth/None/Sand/Wind, no stat filter): same-element and element-less hits only. Momentum Shift IDG ('Highest', no element): element-less hits plus highest-stat elemental hits. Kit rows add power/100 × staged base, same-tag rows sum (§3b); only the bloodline IDG passive multiplies Sand damage.
- Delivery: cooldown 7 everywhere; buff/debuff rows act the two rounds after the cast round, never in it (§3b; 2-in-7 uptime), so Timeshift's IDT never amplifies its own hit. Timelapse (60 AP, range 4, radius-1 circle on any other user): Sand damage to every other user inside (allies too, never the caster), DDG enemies only, SELF DDT. Timeshift, Eternity Flux: 60 AP hits. Momentum Shift (40 AP): target debuff, caster buff. Cellular Regeneration (40 AP): SELF heal, 250 HP ticks the next two rounds.
- Fourth purchases: Burst → Suspended Moment or Brittle with Age (IDT 39%); Exposure → Suspended Moment or Grinding Sands; Fortress → Borrowed Hours or Leaden Seconds (DDG 33%); Suppression → Borrowed Hours or Held in Stasis (06,07,09,10: DDG 38%, DDT 37%, heal 270 per tick; the Timelapse-centred build). No-capstone hybrids such as 01,02,04,06 and 01,02,06,09 are legal and non-dominated. Four examples because each capstone answers a distinct role: burst, exposure, tank, area suppression.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Sand is the single signature element on all three damage rows and no other of the 95 census kits carries it; whether any non-bloodline jutsu carries Sand is unverified. Under the current resolver a Sand modifier reaches 4 of 9 rows directly; the other 5 fall back to None, which also reaches every non-elemental row the player casts. Assumes the proposed whole-kit classification.
- Ally hazard (validator warns): Timelapse row 0 Sand damage is an OTHER_USER circle, friendly fire none (= ALL): allies inside take the hit; the caster never does (actions.ts 1029-1060). The circle can be centred on any other user, ally included; only enemies get the DDG row and DDT is SELF-targeted, so the Suspended Moment routes carry no hazard. Grinding Sands/Erosion of Eternity raise it 40→45.
- Exposure: The Inevitable Hour puts both IDT rows at 42% for the two rounds after each cast. Timeshift's amplifies nearly every non-pierce hit the enemy takes (caster, weapons, normal jutsu, allies); Momentum Shift's only Earth/Sand/Wind or element-less hits. Stacking is on (process.ts 1109-1117): with both live a hit matching both gains 84% of its staged base, plus other IDT sources.
- Area suppression leakage: Stilled Hourglass puts Decrease Damage Given at 38% on every enemy inside the Timelapse circle for 2 rounds, cutting their damage to the caster's allies as well (four stat types or element-less, pierce excluded). The route is worth more in team battles than one row suggests, which is why it stops at +8 rather than Taiyo's +10; raising it is a user-owned decision.
- Broad self rows: Timeless Bastion puts Timelapse's self DDT at 40% for 2 rounds against nearly every non-pierce hit; Borrowed Hours plus The Inevitable Hour put Momentum Shift's IDG at 40%, raising basic attacks, weapons and element-less jutsu while active. Same-tag DDT/IDG sources add their percentages (process.ts 1109-1117); a future normal-tree potency budget is unaudited.
- Heal: static heal is ×10 HP per tick, so the +5 route adds 50 HP per tick (600 per cast vs 500 at level 25). The SELF-target row on the EMPTY_GROUND spiral is realized on the caster at cast time (actions.ts 980-1004), not through the tiles, so it is not positional; with rounds 2 it ticks on the two following rounds only (tags.ts 1721-1775; util.ts 2567-2577). Healprevent blocks it.
- Highest-stat resolution (G16): Sand damage rows and Momentum Shift's IDG use 'Highest', resolved via the caster's highestOffence (realizeTag copies it onto each effect); highestOffence is a required BattleUserState field (types.ts 156). If undefined, both sides push the literal 'Highest': the IDG row would still match element-less hits and the kit's own Sand rows, not other elemental jutsu.
- No combat simulation: 14/14 non-dominance and row weights are arithmetic, not equal combat strength. Stun, timecompression, the barrier, circle target counts, AP, uptime and delivery were not modelled. Both Suspended Moment routes ride on one 60 AP Timelapse cast; a sealed or prevented cast removes their value for that cycle. Ranked modes skip skill effects.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Sands of Time jutsu inherit Sand potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
