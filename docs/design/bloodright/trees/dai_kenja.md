# Dai Kenja — Doctrine of Overflow

**Bloodline:** Dai Kenja (BR-021, rank D, `Dqqw3zcIGDserD-qEE9QW`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Dai Kenja classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Dai Kenja (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Overloaded Impact offense (Damage and Increase Damage Given) · secondary Decrease Damage Given (Chakra Overload / Chakra Cannon suppression) · tertiary Increase Damage Taken (Chakra Overload exposure; adverse on Overloaded Impact).

The kit has six supported rows on three casts. Overloaded Impact carries the only Damage row (40 power at level 25) and the only Increase Damage Given row (30% self, Ninjutsu filter with no element, so it also admits every non-elemental hit; live only in the two rounds after the cast, so it never helps its own hit), making offense through that cast the primary in two separate forms: more power on the hit, or a stronger amplifier for the hits that follow; the bloodline's 15% passive multiplies under the same matching. Decrease Damage Given is the broadest tag (25% on Chakra Overload, 30% on the AOE Chakra Cannon, all stat types) and is secondary, so root B holds the heavier four-purchase builds by row weight (19–24 against 13–15 on root A) while root A holds the largest single-row flats (+6 Damage, +10 Increase Damage Given). Increase Damage Taken reaches Chakra Overload's 35% enemy exposure but also the adverse 25% self exposure on Overloaded Impact, so it is a small, explicitly accepted tertiary route; Chakra Cannon's pierce and Chakra Overload's pool-cost row are unsupported and untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Dai Kenja jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Brimming Cup** — A sage's chakra fills the cup past its rim and keeps pouring. Overloaded Impact self damage buff (30→33%); adds to Ninjutsu or non-elemental hits in the 2 rounds after each cast, never its own hit.
- **Chakra Surcharge** — Every impact carries more than the body was meant to hold. Overloaded Impact Damage only (40→43 power); single row, 60 AP, cooldown 6, single target at range 4. Chakra Cannon's pierce is unsupported.
- **Breaking Point** — Push the overload one breath further than wisdom allows. Overloaded Impact Damage (route 46 power) and its self buff (+2, 35% with Brimming Cup); the buff acts after the cast round, not on this hit
- **Flooded Meridians** — Open every channel; let the current run where it will. Overloaded Impact self damage buff only (33→36% with Brimming Cup); one row, Ninjutsu or non-elemental hits, 2 rounds after each cast.
- **Boundless Reservoir** — There is no bottom to the well the Great Sage draws from. Same self damage buff; route total +10 (30→40%, Ninjutsu or non-elemental hits, 2 rounds after each 60 AP cast, cooldown 6). No other row.
- **Sage's Rebuke** — A word from the sage, and the enemy's chakra falters. Chakra Overload (25→27%, enemies) and Chakra Cannon AOE (30→32%, any non-caster in circle) Decrease Damage Given, all stats, 2 rounds.
- **Smothered Current** — Their flow is pinched to a trickle under the sage's hand. Both Decrease Damage Given rows (+2 more: 29/34%); both debuffs stack on one target when both casts land; Cannon hits allies in its circle.
- **Edict of Silence** — The sage speaks once; the enemy's power answers in a whisper. Both suppression rows; route total +8 (Chakra Overload 33%, Chakra Cannon 38%; 71% stacked on one target). Cannon hits allies in its circle.
- **Cracked Vessel** — Overload leaves fissures in the vessel, yours and theirs. Chakra Overload enemy exposure (35→37%, Ninjutsu or non-elemental) AND adverse Overloaded Impact self exposure (25→27%, all damage).
- **Shattered Vessel** — What is overfilled must break; the sage chooses when. Enemy exposure route +5 (40%), self exposure 30% (adverse); both Decrease Damage Given rows +3 (route 30/35%); Cannon hits allies in circle.

## Design notes

- Node split mirrors the kit's two sides. Brimming Cup (self) opens Overloaded Impact, forking into raw power (Chakra Surcharge → Breaking Point) and self buff (Flooded Meridians → Boundless Reservoir); Sage's Rebuke (enemy) opens Chakra Overload / Chakra Cannon, forking into suppression (Smothered Current → Edict of Silence) and exposure (Cracked Vessel → Shattered Vessel). Every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep. Boundless Reservoir and Edict are single-tag: their roots hold no second non-adverse tag.
- Flats scale with coverage. Damage reaches one row (Overloaded Impact 40): +3/+3 (max 46 power), not the reference's +2/+3 over three rows. Increase Damage Given is one 30% self row: single-row ladder +3/+3/+4 (max 40%). Decrease Damage Given has two enemy rows that stack on one target (25%, 30%): +2/+2/+4 (max +8: 33/38%, 71% combined). Increase Damage Taken has one enemy and one adverse self row: one route at +2/+3 (max +5). Per-tag maxima are within guardrails and not jointly attainable.
- Timing (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b): Overloaded Impact's self buff is inert in its cast round (adjustDamageGiven skips new or cast-this-round effects, tags.ts 925; flags clear at round advance, util.ts 2567–2577), so it never touches its own hit, and cooldown 6 outlives it. In the two rounds after the cast the enhanced 33–40% adds that share of the staged base to every Ninjutsu or non-elemental hit (getEfficiencyRatio, tags.ts 3477–3510). Only the 15% bloodline passive multiplies.
- Delivery and uptime: Overloaded Impact is 60 AP, cooldown 6, one single-target hit at range 4; its buff and self exposure live two rounds in every six. Chakra Overload is 40 AP single target, cooldown 6; Chakra Cannon a 60 AP AOE circle, cooldown 7. Both suppression debuffs sit on one target only when the casts land in the same or consecutive rounds (100 AP in all), so 71% is a ceiling. Ranked PvP and ranked sparring suppress tree effects. Nothing was simulated.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Sage's Rebuke (27/32%) or Flooded Meridians (buff 38%); Amplifier, Sage's Rebuke or Chakra Surcharge (43 power); Suppression, Brimming Cup (buff 33%) or Cracked Vessel (exposure 37%, self 27%); Exposure, Smothered Current or Brimming Cup. Counting IDT as a gain, 13 of 14 full builds are non-dominated; IDT-neutral, 8 are and neither Vessel node appears, so Cracked Vessel earns its place as the Shattered Vessel gate. Adverse-aware dominance is tooling debt.
- Tuning for review (OPEN_DECISIONS D11; values user-owned, unchanged): Shattered Vessel is the largest node (raw 6, row-weighted 12, 9 without the adverse row) against Edict of Silence 4/8, Breaking Point 5/5 and Boundless Reservoir 4/4; its full build scores 24 (19 without the adverse row; 4.0 per row vs Taiyo Kami's 3.4) while root A's score 13–15. Options: cut Shattered Vessel's DDG secondary to +2 (Exposure build 31/36%) or raise Edict of Silence to +5 (max DDG +9).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Adverse row accepted (D11): IDT nodes also raise Overloaded Impact's self exposure (all damage; 27% Cracked Vessel, 30% Shattered Vessel) in the two rounds after each cast, atop the 5% IDT passive. Only the exposure route has the tag; the enemy row (Chakra Overload, Ninjutsu or non-elemental, 35→40%) needs its own 40 AP cast. Fallback: drop Shattered Vessel, keep Cracked Vessel as a leaf (max +2).
- Ally hazard (Chakra Cannon#1; warning kept): Cannon is an OTHER_USER AOE circle with friendlyFire ALL, so an ally in the circle (never the caster) gets its two-round suppression at the enhanced 32–38% (actions.ts 1029–1060; process.ts 154–183); every DDG node raises it; positioning decides. Chakra Overload's DDG is ENEMIES-only; its ALL exposure row lands on an ally only if aimed at one.
- Single-cast concentration: all five root-A nodes modify Overloaded Impact only (one 60 AP single-target cast, cooldown 6). The Burst route adds +6 power to one hit per cycle; the Amplifier route pays only on Ninjutsu or non-elemental hits landed in the two rounds after that cast, never on the cast's own hit.
- Suppression stacking: Chakra Overload and Chakra Cannon apply separate two-round Decrease Damage Given debuffs; with BATTLE_TAG_STACKING on, every same-tag effect applies and the percentages add (process.ts 1109–1117), so one target's hits lose 55% of their staged base at baseline and 71% at the route maximum, more with an allied Dai Kenja. Flats were cut to +2/+2/+4 for this; not simulated.
- Leakage: the self buff adds to every Ninjutsu or non-elemental hit the caster lands in the two rounds after its cast (normal jutsu, basic attacks, weapons); enemy-side suppression and exposure alter damage from allies and weapons too. Restricted casting scope does not restrict downstream benefit; stat filters on element-less rows are not binding (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3, getEfficiencyRatio).
- Classification: no row in this kit carries an element, so the proposed 'Dai Kenja' label is a bloodline-keyed extension with no existing element to fall back on. Under the current resolver all 6 supported rows fall back to None, which would also reach every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts; the normal-jutsu collision is unverified. The tree is inert until that extension exists.
- Unsupported rows: Chakra Cannon's pierce (58 at level 25) and Chakra Overload's increasepoolcost (100%) receive no bonus; because pierce is processed after the damage-modifier pass at the pin, the Amplifier and Exposure routes do not raise the pierce hit and the Suppression route does not reduce enemy pierce.
- Rank context: Dai Kenja is a D-rank bloodline with B/C/A-rank jutsu, weaker unmodified than the A-rank reference; the tree equalises marginal opportunity, not final strength, and cross-bloodline comparison is deferred to the roster review. Normal-tree potency policy is not approved, so a combined stacking audit is required before implementation. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Dai Kenja jutsu inherit Dai Kenja potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
