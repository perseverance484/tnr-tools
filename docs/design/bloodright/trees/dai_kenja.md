# Dai Kenja — Doctrine of Overflow

**Bloodline:** Dai Kenja (BR-021, rank D, `Dqqw3zcIGDserD-qEE9QW`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Dai Kenja classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Dai Kenja (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Overloaded Impact offense (Damage and Increase Damage Given) · secondary Decrease Damage Given (Chakra Overload / Chakra Cannon suppression) · tertiary Increase Damage Taken (Chakra Overload exposure; adverse on Overloaded Impact).

The kit has six supported rows across three casts. Overloaded Impact carries the only Damage row (40 power at jutsu level 25) and the only self Increase Damage Given row (30%, 2 rounds; its Ninjutsu stat filter has no element, so at the pin it also admits every non-elemental hit), and the bloodline passive is a 15% Increase Damage Given under the same matching, so offense through that one cast is the declared primary. Decrease Damage Given is the broadest tag (two rows, 25% on Chakra Overload and 30% on the AOE Chakra Cannon, all stat types) and is the secondary; root B therefore holds the heavier four-purchase builds by row weight (19–24 against 13–15 on root A), while root A holds the largest single-row flats (+6 Damage, +10 Increase Damage Given). Increase Damage Taken reaches Chakra Overload's 35% enemy exposure (Ninjutsu or non-elemental damage) but also the adverse 25% self exposure on Overloaded Impact (all damage), so it is held to a small, explicitly accepted tertiary route. Chakra Cannon's pierce and Chakra Overload's pool-cost row are unsupported and untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Dai Kenja jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Brimming Cup** — A sage's chakra fills the cup past its rim and keeps pouring. Overloaded Impact self damage buff (30→33%, 2 rounds per 60 AP cast); Ninjutsu and any non-elemental hit in the window benefit.
- **Chakra Surcharge** — Every impact carries more than the body was meant to hold. Overloaded Impact Damage only (40→43 power); single row, 60 AP, cooldown 6, single target at range 4. Chakra Cannon's pierce is unsupported.
- **Breaking Point** — Push the overload one breath further than wisdom allows. Overloaded Impact Damage (route total 46 power) and its self damage buff (+2, 35% with Brimming Cup). Both rows sit on one 60 AP cast.
- **Flooded Meridians** — Open every channel; let the current run where it will. Overloaded Impact self damage buff only (33→36% with Brimming Cup); one row, Ninjutsu or non-elemental hits, 2 rounds per cast.
- **Boundless Reservoir** — There is no bottom to the well the Great Sage draws from. Same self damage buff; route total +10 (30→40%, Ninjutsu or non-elemental hits, 2 rounds per 60 AP cast, cooldown 6). No other row touched.
- **Sage's Rebuke** — A word from the sage, and the enemy's chakra falters. Chakra Overload (25→27%, enemies) and Chakra Cannon AOE (30→32%, any non-caster in circle) Decrease Damage Given, all stats, 2 rounds.
- **Smothered Current** — Their flow is pinched to a trickle under the sage's hand. Both Decrease Damage Given rows (+2 more: 29/34%); both debuffs stack on one target when both casts land; Cannon hits allies in its circle.
- **Edict of Silence** — The sage speaks once; the enemy's power answers in a whisper. Both suppression rows; route total +8 (Chakra Overload 33%, Chakra Cannon 38%; 71% stacked on one target). Cannon hits allies in its circle.
- **Cracked Vessel** — Overload leaves fissures in the vessel, yours and theirs. Chakra Overload enemy exposure (35→37%, Ninjutsu or non-elemental) AND adverse Overloaded Impact self exposure (25→27%, all damage).
- **Shattered Vessel** — What is overfilled must break; the sage chooses when. Enemy exposure route total +5 (40%), self exposure 30% (adverse); both Decrease Damage Given rows +3 (30/35%); Cannon hits allies in circle.

## Design notes

- Node split: the two roots mirror the kit's two sides. Brimming Cup (self) opens Overloaded Impact and forks into a raw-power route (Chakra Surcharge → Breaking Point) and a self-buff route (Flooded Meridians → Boundless Reservoir). Sage's Rebuke (enemy) opens Chakra Overload / Chakra Cannon and forks into a suppression route (Smothered Current → Edict of Silence) and an exposure route (Cracked Vessel → Shattered Vessel). 2 Foundations, 4 Hidden Arts, 4 Advanced Arts; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two Advanced Arts cost 5 (same root) or 6 (different roots) BP against a 4 BP budget. Each route targets a different tag, so no node is a reskin of another.
- Flats are scaled by coverage. Damage reaches one row (Overloaded Impact 40), so the route carries +3/+3 (max +6, 46 power) instead of the reference's +2/+3 across three rows. Increase Damage Given is one self row at 30% (Ninjutsu or non-elemental hits), so it takes the single-row percentage ladder +3/+3/+4 (max +10, 40%); the Foundation is single-tag because Damage only touches one hit while the buff reaches every Ninjutsu or non-elemental hit for two rounds. Decrease Damage Given reaches two enemy rows that can stack on one target (25% + 30%), so it carries +2/+2/+4 (max +8: 33/38%, 71% stacked) rather than +2/+3/+5. Increase Damage Taken has one enemy row and one adverse self row, so it is confined to one route at +2/+3 (max +5).
- Main tree, passives and pierce: Overloaded Impact's self buff carries a Ninjutsu stat filter but no element, and at the pin getEfficiencyRatio (tags.ts 3477–3510) pushes 'None' into the tag list of any element-less effect on both sides of the comparison, so the enhanced 33–40% multiplies Overloaded Impact's own hit, every Ninjutsu jutsu and every non-elemental hit of any stat type (basic attacks, non-elemental weapons and jutsu) landed inside its two-round window; only elemental non-Ninjutsu damage is excluded. The bloodline's 15% Increase Damage Given passive is a separate bloodline-sourced modifier under the same matching that applies on top. Enemy-side Decrease Damage Given (all stat types) changes damage from every source; enemy-side Increase Damage Taken (Ninjutsu or non-elemental) amplifies the player's, allies' and weapons' matching hits. At the pin (process.ts 416–430, 563–577) pierce effects are applied after the damage-modifier pass, so Chakra Cannon's 58 pierce is raised by no node and enemy pierce hits are not reduced by the suppression route. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring.
- Delivery and uptime: Overloaded Impact costs 60 AP on cooldown 6 and its buff/self-debuff last 2 rounds, so every root-A percentage node has at most two rounds of value per six-round cycle; its Damage row is one single-target hit at range 4. Chakra Overload is a 40 AP single-target cast on cooldown 6; Chakra Cannon is a 60 AP AOE circle spawn on cooldown 7. Landing both suppression debuffs on one target in the same turn costs 100 AP, so the 71% stacked figure is a ceiling, not a rotation. No rotation or combat outcome was simulated.
- Fourth purchase choices: Burst takes Sage's Rebuke (suppression 27/32%) or Flooded Meridians (buff 38%); Amplifier takes Sage's Rebuke or Chakra Surcharge (43 power); Suppression takes Brimming Cup (buff 33%) or Cracked Vessel (exposure 37%/self 27%); Exposure takes Smothered Current (suppression 32/37%) or Brimming Cup. Of the 14 legal full-budget allocations, 13 are numerically non-dominated and every node appears in at least one; the dominated one is Brimming Cup + Sage's Rebuke + Smothered Current + Cracked Vessel (buff +3, suppression +4, exposure +2), which Brimming Cup + Sage's Rebuke + Cracked Vessel + Shattered Vessel beats on every tag. Caveat: that count scores every tag as a gain, including Increase Damage Taken, whose +2/+5 also raises the adverse self row. Under an IDT-neutral comparison only 8 of 14 full builds are non-dominated and neither Cracked Vessel nor Shattered Vessel appears in any of them (Sage's Rebuke + Smothered Current + Edict of Silence + Cracked Vessel falls to Brimming Cup + Sage's Rebuke + Smothered Current + Edict of Silence; the Exposure build falls to that build and to Sage's Rebuke + Smothered Current + Edict of Silence + Cracked Vessel). Cracked Vessel as a non-gate fourth purchase (+2 on one enemy row, +2 self exposure) is weaker than Smothered Current at the same depth (+2 on two rows, no penalty); it earns its place as the Shattered Vessel gate and for players who value enemy exposure, not on the dominance count. An adverse-aware dominance pass in audit_tree is recorded as tooling debt.
- Adverse handling: Increase Damage Taken is the only tag with an adverse row (Overloaded Impact row 2, self, all four stat types, no element, 25% at level 25, scaling 0.2/level versus 0.4/level on the enemy row). It appears only on Cracked Vessel and Shattered Vessel; neither Foundation nor the suppression capstone carries it, so three of the four routes and every Foundation-only hybrid leave the self exposure untouched. The route total is +5: enemy exposure 35→40% on Chakra Overload (Ninjutsu or non-elemental damage, from every attacker, two rounds per 40 AP cast), self exposure 25→30% (all damage) for the two rounds after each Overloaded Impact. If review judges that trade unacceptable, the reversible fallback is to delete Shattered Vessel and keep Cracked Vessel as a leaf Hidden Art (9 nodes, max exposure +2); no other node changes.
- Boundless Reservoir and Edict of Silence are single-tag capstones because their roots hold no second non-adverse tag; adding a cross-root secondary was rejected because it created dominated builds or pushed a tag past its guardrail. Maximum per-tag additions over all legal builds are Damage +6, Increase Damage Given +10, Decrease Damage Given +8, Increase Damage Taken +5, within the planning guardrails; they are not jointly attainable.
- Tuning surfaced for review, not settled here: Shattered Vessel is the tree's largest node (raw 6, row-weighted 12; 9 excluding the adverse row) against Edict of Silence 4/8, Breaking Point 5/5 and Boundless Reservoir 4/4, and it carries 75% of Edict's suppression (+3 vs +4) plus the only capstone exposure, so the Suppression route's unique contribution over the Exposure route is +1 suppression net. Its full build (Sage's Rebuke + Smothered Current + Cracked Vessel + Shattered Vessel) is the strongest by row weight (24; 19 excluding the adverse row; 4.0 per supported row against the Taiyo Kami reference's 3.4), while the declared primary root A yields the smallest full builds by row weight (13–15). Edict of Silence remains the single best defensive node. Options for the user: drop Shattered Vessel's Decrease Damage Given secondary to +2 (Exposure build 31/36% suppression, DDG +6) or raise Edict of Silence to +5 (route maximum DDG +9, within the ≤10 guardrail). Values are unchanged in this draft; balance values are user-owned. Registered as OPEN_DECISIONS.md D11 (secondary weighting left at +3/+4 pending review).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Adverse row accepted: every Increase Damage Taken node also raises Overloaded Impact's self exposure (25→27% with Cracked Vessel, 30% with Shattered Vessel) for two rounds after each cast, on top of the bloodline's permanent +5% Increase Damage Taken passive (Taijutsu/Bukijutsu filter, no element, so it also matches every non-elemental hit). The self row (all four stat types, no element) matches every damage effect; the enemy row on Chakra Overload (Ninjutsu filter, no element) matches Ninjutsu damage plus every non-elemental hit of any stat type from the player, allies and weapons (getEfficiencyRatio, tags.ts 3477–3510), excluding only elemental non-Ninjutsu damage. +2/+3 is kept after re-judging: the two rows are comparable per round (the self row matches all damage; the enemy row matches Ninjutsu or non-elemental damage from every attacker and needs its own 40 AP cast while the self row rides the primary 60 AP damage cast), both are two-round and small, and both exposure nodes are confined to one route.
- Ally hazard (dossier row Chakra Cannon#1; validator warning 'ally-hazard area rows amplified'): Chakra Cannon targets OTHER_USER with an AOE circle and both of its rows (pierce, Decrease Damage Given) carry friendlyFire ALL; at the pin getTargetUser (actions.ts 1226–1246) picks any non-caster on each affected tile and checkFriendlyFire (process.ts 154–182) returns true for ALL, so an ally standing in the circle receives the sticky two-round suppression at the enhanced 32–38% (and the unmodified pierce). Every Decrease Damage Given node (Sage's Rebuke, Smothered Current, Edict of Silence, Shattered Vessel) raises that ally-side figure by the same flat; positioning, not the node, decides. Chakra Overload's suppression row is ENEMIES-only; its exposure row is ALL but single-target, so it reaches an ally only if the caster targets the ally's tile. The dossier's 'enemy' recipient for OTHER_USER rows is a modelling assumption of bloodright_lib.recipient_for, not an engine guarantee.
- Single-cast concentration: all five root-A nodes modify Overloaded Impact only (one 60 AP single-target cast on cooldown 6). The Burst route's whole value is +6 power on one hit per cycle; the Amplifier route's value depends on casting Ninjutsu inside a two-round window that opens at most once per six rounds.
- Suppression stacking: Chakra Overload and Chakra Cannon each apply a separate two-round Decrease Damage Given debuff and BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is on at the pin, so one target can carry 55% base suppression and 71% at the route maximum. The flats were reduced to +2/+2/+4 for this reason; realised value was not simulated.
- Leakage: the Overloaded Impact self buff multiplies normal Ninjutsu and non-elemental hits in its window; enemy-side suppression and exposure alter damage from allies and weapons as well. Restricted casting scope does not restrict downstream benefit; stat filters on element-less rows are not binding (SOURCE_MECHANICS section 3, getEfficiencyRatio).
- Classification: no row in this kit carries an element, so the proposed 'Dai Kenja' label is a bloodline-keyed extension of the whole-kit classification change and has no existing element to fall back on. Under the current resolver all 6 supported rows fall back to None, which would also reach every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts; the normal-jutsu collision is unverified. The tree is inert until that extension exists.
- Unsupported rows: Chakra Cannon's pierce (58 at level 25) and Chakra Overload's increasepoolcost (100%) receive no bonus; because pierce is processed after the damage-modifier pass at the pin, the Amplifier and Exposure routes do not raise the pierce hit and the Suppression route does not reduce enemy pierce.
- Rank context: Dai Kenja is a D-rank bloodline whose jutsu are B/C/A rank; its unmodified kit is weaker than the A-rank reference, and this tree equalises marginal opportunity, not final strength. Cross-bloodline comparison is deferred to the roster review. Ranked PvP and ranked sparring suppress skill-tree effects; normal-tree potency policy is not approved, so a combined stacking audit is still required before any implementation. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Dai Kenja jutsu inherit Dai Kenja potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
