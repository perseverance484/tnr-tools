# Voltara Divine — Litany of Lightning

**Bloodline:** Voltara Divine (BR-091, rank C, `zOGMXO1nnBTEE0sAJRCuD`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance (roster pass) / Lightning classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Lightning (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Lightning amplification — Increase Damage Given (Luminous Illusion Bind, Ethereal Dragon's Whispers; two self rows) · secondary Lightning burst — Damage (Bind 40, Whispers 50 EP): a controlled +2 payoff on each strike's own cast, Whispers 50 → 52 above the Nuke tier · tertiary Blitz guard — Decrease Damage Taken (Voltara Blitz; one self row, the fortress route).

Five supported rows sit on three casts. Both Lightning strikes carry a 35% self Increase Damage Given row that is live the two rounds after the cast and never on its own hit: Bind's carries Lightning and amplifies only Lightning hits, Whispers' carries no element and lists all four stat types, so it amplifies every non-pierce hit the caster lands; one cast therefore drives many later hits, and the 15% Lightning passive multiplies last. That is the bloodline's highest-leverage lever and takes the primary route. The two strikes (Bind 40 EP, Whispers 50 EP; 60 AP each, so never in one turn; cooldowns 6 and 7, single target) take a controlled +2 Damage on the burst route, the only bonus that acts on a strike's own cast. Voltara Blitz (60 AP, cooldown 7) carries the caster in and leaves a 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row for 2 rounds, the kit's only defence and its only answer to the 10% Wind Increase Damage Taken passive; it roots the fortress route and opens the burst sequence (Blitz, Bind, Whispers). Blitz's move and pierce rows and Bind's stun are unsupported.

**Director review recommended:** Roster questions: DQ-A (Ethereal Dragon's Whispers 50 → 52, Luminous Illusion Bind 40 → 42, on the strike-led reading in above_nuke_rationale); DQ-H (9 → 8 nodes; Dragon's Whispered Wrath → Ethereal Dragon Unbound now hang from Halo of Static, so the burst setup is never Apotheosis of Thunder's fourth purchase).

- Concern: R1′ reading: Damage is kept because the kit is strike-led, but Ethereal Dragon's Whispers shares 60 AP with every kit cast and cooldown 7 with Voltara Blitz, so the finisher reading rests on its B rank and its place after Bind's stun, not on cost. If the director reads the kit as amplification-led, Damage goes unamplified and, with only Increase Damage Given and Decrease Damage Taken left, the tree drops to two routes (six nodes) rather than gain a duplicate capstone.
- Concern: Halo of Static is universal (in all 8 legal 4 BP builds) and the Amplification build is fixed (01, 04, 05 + 06); Unassailable Radiance's two fourths, Anointed by Lightning and Dragon's Whispered Wrath, give the same +2%.
- Concern: Burst and amplification stay close in total throughput. Burst with Anointed by Lightning leads on the strike pair, on off-kit Lightning jutsu hits outside the overlap round, and with one other non-Lightning hit in the windows per pair; the two tie at two non-Lightning hits (225.5 against 225.9) and Amplification leads from three (283.1 against 281.1). Amplification's case is weapon, basic-attack and other-element hits in the windows (×1.43 against ×1.39) and, by about 0.8%, an off-kit 40 EP Lightning jutsu hit in the overlap round.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on five rows (Increase Damage Given x2, Damage x2, Decrease Damage Taken x1; Blitz's move and pierce and Bind's stun are unsupported). A fourth Advanced Art would twin a capstone on the same rows or push Blitz's single Decrease Damage Taken row past +10%. Anointed by Lightning therefore runs one chain (Luminous Conduit → Apotheosis of Thunder) and Halo of Static is universal: it is in every legal 4 BP build, so the Amplification build is fixed (01, 04, 05 + 06) and every build carries +2% Decrease Damage Taken. A second leaf under Anointed by Lightning could only repeat Increase Damage Given on the two self rows (the twin this layout removes), and the pre-batch Heaven's Retort leaf (+2 Damage on a Hidden Art) is not restored.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Lightning jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Anointed by Lightning** — The storm chose this blood before birth; every pulse carries its blessing. Both self damage buffs 35 → 37% for 2 rounds: Bind's (Lightning hits only) and Whispers' (every non-pierce hit, any stat type).
- **Luminous Conduit** — Light runs through the body as through wire, losing nothing on the way. Commitment: both self buffs 37 → 39% with Anointed by Lightning; the amplification payoff sits on Apotheosis of Thunder.
- **Apotheosis of Thunder** — The faithful do not wield the lightning. They become it. Sustained amplification payoff: +4% of the route's +8%, both self buffs 39 → 43% (×1.43/1.35 ≈ ×1.06 per windowed hit on the full route, ≈ ×1.12 on a hit inside both windows). Whispers' row multiplies every non-pierce hit and Bind's every Lightning hit for the two rounds after each cast.
- **Halo of Static** — Where the blitz ends, a ring of charged air lingers and refuses to break. Voltara Blitz Decrease Damage Taken 30 → 32% (self at cast, all four stat types, 2 rounds, cooldown 7); the root of the fortress and burst routes.
- **Dragon's Whispered Wrath** — What the ethereal dragon whispers, the sky repeats as thunder. Setup: both self buffs 35 → 37% (39% with Anointed by Lightning), so Bind's window carries Whispers' raw Damage; Halo of Static's guard covers the strike turns.
- **Ethereal Dragon Unbound** — No seal, no sky, no prayer holds the dragon once it has been named. Burst: Luminous Illusion Bind 40 → 42 (still Normal) and Ethereal Dragon's Whispers 50 → 52 EP (above the 50 Nuke tier; director-flagged) on each strike's own cast; on the route both self buffs 37% and Blitz's guard 32%.
- **Aegis of Living Light** — A mantle of living light; blows arrive softened, as if through a dream. Voltara Blitz Decrease Damage Taken 32 → 35% with Halo of Static.
- **Unassailable Radiance** — Radiance so complete that harm itself hesitates at the threshold. Fortress: Voltara Blitz Decrease Damage Taken 30 → 40% on the full route (damage taken ×0.60 against ×0.70) against every non-pierce hit for the 2 rounds after each cast. It takes the full +10% because this is the kit's only defensive row and is live 2 rounds in 7.

## Design notes

- Structure: edges 01→04→05, 06→02→03 and 06→07→08. Anointed by Lightning +2% → Luminous Conduit +2% → Apotheosis of Thunder +4% Increase Damage Given (amplification, both self buffs 43%). Halo of Static (+2% Decrease Damage Taken on Blitz) forks into Dragon's Whispered Wrath +2% Increase Damage Given → Ethereal Dragon Unbound +2 Damage (burst) and Aegis of Living Light +3% → Unassailable Radiance +5% Decrease Damage Taken (Blitz guard 40%). Eight legal 4 BP builds, seven non-dominated. Maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +8% (Apotheosis of Thunder with Halo of Static), Decrease Damage Taken +10%, Damage +2 (Ethereal Dragon Unbound only).
- Damage (final cleanup, R1′): kept as a controlled +2 because the kit is strike-led. Its two single-target Lightning strikes carry all four offensive supported rows, and Ethereal Dragon's Whispers is the signature finisher: the kit's only B-rank jutsu (Bind and Blitz are D), its only 50 EP row, landing inside Luminous Illusion Bind's 2-round stun and Lightning window. Without it the third route has no distinct identity: only Increase Damage Given and Decrease Damage Taken remain, each already owned by a capstone.
- Burst against amplification is immediacy against window: flat Damage acts on the strike's own cast, the buffs never do, and Bind and Whispers (60 AP each) cannot share a turn. The burst sequence is Blitz, Bind, Whispers: Halo of Static's guard covers both strike turns and Bind's window carries Whispers' 52. The +2 reaches every Lightning Damage row, so Burst with Anointed by Lightning leads on the strike pair (42 + 52 × 1.39 = 114.3 against 40 + 50 × 1.43 = 111.5 EP-equivalent), on any Lightning strike outside a window (42/52 against 40/50) and on an off-kit 40 EP Lightning jutsu hit in one window (42 × 1.39 = 58.4 against 40 × 1.43 = 57.2). Apotheosis of Thunder leads on windowed hits the +2 does not reach (weapons, basic attacks, other-element jutsu: ×1.43 against ×1.39, about 2.9%) and, by about 0.8%, on an off-kit 40 EP Lightning jutsu hit in the overlap round, when both strikes are on cooldown (40 × 1.43² = 81.8 against 42 × 1.39² = 81.1). Per pair, Burst stays ahead with one other non-Lightning hit worth 40 EP in the windows (169.9 against 168.7) and the routes tie with two (225.5 against 225.9); with two off-kit 40 EP Lightning hits (one in the overlap round, one in Whispers' window only) Burst leads 253.8 against 250.5.
- Magnitudes: the amplification route is +8% on two rows that compound on a hit in their overlap round (≈ ×1.122 there, below Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.234 top package), split 2/2/4 so the capstone carries the payoff. Dragon's Whispered Wrath hangs from Halo of Static, so it is never Apotheosis of Thunder's fourth purchase and Increase Damage Given stays at +8% over every legal allocation (the earlier layout's capstone plus sibling setup reached +10%, ≈ ×1.154). Unassailable Radiance keeps +10% because Blitz's row is the kit's only defence and is live 2 rounds in 7 (damage taken ×0.60 against ×0.70, ≈ ×0.86).
- Timing (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b): each Increase Damage Given and Decrease Damage Taken row is live the two rounds after its cast, never on its own strike; Bind then Whispers (or the reverse) gives the second strike the first strike's row. Blitz's guard is realized on the caster at cast.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Lightning is shared with other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). Whispers' Increase Damage Given and Blitz's Decrease Damage Taken carry no element and need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver; Voltara Blitz (no Lightning row) qualifies in-kit only through an authored Lightning jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Lightning coverage is unverified, and Ethereal Dragon Unbound's +2 Damage reaches every Lightning Damage row in it.
- Broad self amplification: at up to 43% Whispers' row multiplies every non-pierce hit the caster lands for 2 rounds (weapons, basic attacks, normal jutsu) and Bind's every Lightning hit, off-kit Lightning jutsu included, with the 15% passive on top; the amplification route is the highest-leverage route. Not simulated.
- Ally-targeted casts: Bind and Whispers (OTHER_USER) accept any living non-caster user in range 4 / 3 (util.ts 2747–2749). On an ally the Damage and stun rows (ENEMIES) are withheld but the SELF Increase Damage Given row is still realized (actions.ts 1060–1067), so with an ally present the buff can be taken without landing a strike; that favours amplification over burst. Not simulated.
- Guard is one row at 2 rounds in 7, and Blitz is a 60 AP displacement cast, so the fortress and the burst sequence it opens also commit position. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in ranked PvP / sparring at the pin.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Lightning jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
