# Voltara Divine — Litany of Lightning

**Bloodline:** Voltara Divine (BR-091, rank C, `zOGMXO1nnBTEE0sAJRCuD`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance / Lightning classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Lightning (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Lightning amplification — Increase Damage Given (Luminous Illusion Bind, Ethereal Dragon's Whispers; two self rows) · secondary Blitz guard — Decrease Damage Taken (Voltara Blitz; one self row, the fortress route) · tertiary none: Lightning Damage (Bind 40, Whispers 50 EP) is deliberately unamplified, because any flat Damage lifts Whispers past the 50 Nuke tier.

Five supported rows sit on three casts. Both Lightning strikes carry a 35% self Increase Damage Given row that is live the two rounds after the cast and never on its own hit: Bind's carries Lightning and amplifies only Lightning hits, Whispers' carries no element and lists all four stat types, so it amplifies every non-pierce hit the caster lands; one cast therefore drives many later hits, and the 15% Lightning passive multiplies last. That is the bloodline's highest-leverage lever and takes the primary route. Voltara Blitz (60 AP, cooldown 7) carries the caster in and leaves a 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row for 2 rounds, the kit's only defence and its only answer to the 10% Wind Increase Damage Taken passive; it takes the fortress route. The two strikes (Bind 40 EP, Whispers 50 EP; 60 AP, cooldowns 6 and 7, single target) take no flat Damage: any +N lifts Whispers past the 50 Nuke tier, and the second strike of the pair already lands inside the first's buff window. Blitz's move and pierce rows and Bind's stun are unsupported.

**Director review recommended:** Structural: the flat-Damage route is removed rather than kept at Whispers 50 → 52, because with names hidden it read as the amplification route with +5% Increase Damage Given swapped for +2 Damage (about 2% on the strike pair). The tree shrinks to two 3-node chains (6 nodes, two Advanced Arts) with both Foundations universal, and no Damage row changes. Confirm the two-route tree.

- Concern: Apotheosis of Thunder is the tree's only offensive route and its highest-leverage one; it keeps +10% for the reasons in the design notes, and a +2/+3/+4 cut (+9%) is the trim if the director wants one.
- Concern: Both Foundations are in every legal full build (two 3-node chains); the choice is which chain to finish, or the no-capstone hybrid.
- Concern: Damage is left unamplified: the strike pair is primed only through the buff rows, and any flat Damage would lift Whispers 50 past the Nuke tier.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Six nodes and two Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on five rows (Increase Damage Given x2, Damage x2, Decrease Damage Taken x1; Blitz's move and pierce and Bind's stun are unsupported), and this revision targets two of them. Flat Damage is declined: Ethereal Dragon's Whispers is a 50 EP Nuke, so any flat Damage lifts it past 50, and the +2 Damage re-cut (behind a +3% Increase Damage Given setup) read as the amplification route with one tag swapped. The two identities are two 3-node chains: Anointed by Lightning → Luminous Conduit → Apotheosis of Thunder (sustained amplification) and Halo of Static → Aegis of Living Light → Unassailable Radiance (fortress). Universal nodes accepted as inherent to a 3-tag, 5-row kit: with two 3-node chains every 4 BP build holds both Foundations, so the choice is which chain to finish, or the no-capstone hybrid (both Hidden Arts). A second Hidden Art under either Foundation could only repeat Increase Damage Given or Decrease Damage Taken on the same rows (filler, and it would stack with that root's capstone).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Lightning jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Structure: edges 01→04→05 and 06→07→08. Anointed by Lightning +2% / Luminous Conduit +3% / Apotheosis of Thunder +5% Increase Damage Given (both self buffs 45%); Halo of Static +2% / Aegis of Living Light +3% / Unassailable Radiance +5% Decrease Damage Taken (Blitz guard 40%). Three legal 4 BP builds: each capstone plus the other Foundation (a forced fourth purchase) and the no-capstone hybrid (+5% / +5%). Maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +10%, Decrease Damage Taken +10%, no Damage.
- Damage is not targeted. Before this revision the Burst route reached +5 (Bind 40 → 45, a full tier; Whispers 50 → 55), and Heaven's Retort (+2 Damage on a Hidden Art) and Unassailable Radiance's +1 Damage spread flat Damage into the guard side. A +2 re-cut (Dragon's Whispered Wrath +3% Increase Damage Given → Ethereal Dragon Unbound +2 Damage) was also dropped: its 4 BP build (+2 Damage, +5% Increase Damage Given, +2% Decrease Damage Taken) was the amplification build with +5% Increase Damage Given swapped for +2 Damage, about 2% ahead on the strike pair (Bind then Whispers: 42 + 52 × 1.40 ≈ 114.8 against 40 + 50 × 1.45 = 112.5 EP-equivalent) and behind on every other windowed hit (×1.40 against ×1.45), bought with Whispers 50 → 52.
- Why both capstones keep +10%. Apotheosis of Thunder: its two rows sit on different 60 AP casts, which cannot share a 100 AP turn, so the windows overlap only in the round after the second cast, when both strikes are on cooldown (6, 7) and Blitz's hit is pierce; per windowed hit the route is ×1.45/1.35 ≈ ×1.07 over base, and its forced fourth (Halo of Static) adds no Increase Damage Given. Unassailable Radiance: Blitz's row is the kit's only defence and is live 2 rounds in 7 (damage taken ×0.60 against ×0.70, ≈ ×0.86). Apotheosis of Thunder's Advanced-Art step 5 → 4 (route +9%) is the trim if the director wants one.
- Timing (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b): each Increase Damage Given and Decrease Damage Taken row is live the two rounds after its cast, never on its own strike; Bind then Whispers (or the reverse) gives the second strike the first strike's row. Blitz's guard is realized on the caster at cast.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Lightning is shared with other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). Whispers' Increase Damage Given and Blitz's Decrease Damage Taken carry no element and need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver; Voltara Blitz (no Lightning row) qualifies in-kit only through an authored Lightning jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Lightning coverage is unverified.
- Broad self amplification: at 45% Whispers' row multiplies every non-pierce hit the caster lands for 2 rounds (weapons, basic attacks, normal jutsu) and Bind's every Lightning hit, off-kit Lightning jutsu included, with the 15% passive on top; Apotheosis of Thunder is the highest-leverage route. Not simulated.
- Ally-targeted casts: Bind and Whispers (OTHER_USER) accept any living non-caster user in range 4 / 3 (util.ts 2747–2749). On an ally the Damage and stun rows (ENEMIES) are withheld but the SELF Increase Damage Given row is still realized (actions.ts 1060–1067), so with an ally present the buff can be taken without landing a strike. Not simulated.
- Guard is one row at 2 rounds in 7, and Blitz is a 60 AP displacement cast, so the fortress also commits position. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in ranked PvP / sparring at the pin.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Lightning jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
