# Voltara Divine — Litany of Lightning

**Bloodline:** Voltara Divine (BR-091, rank C, `zOGMXO1nnBTEE0sAJRCuD`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Lightning classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Lightning (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Lightning amplification — Increase Damage Given (Luminous Illusion Bind, Ethereal Dragon's Whispers; two self rows) · secondary Lightning damage — Bind and Whispers (two formula rows at 40 and 50 EP) · tertiary Guard — Decrease Damage Taken (Voltara Blitz; one row).

Five supported rows sit on three casts. Both Lightning strikes carry a 35% self Increase Damage Given row, live the two rounds after the cast and never on its own hit: Bind's is Lightning-elemented and amplifies only Lightning hits, Whispers' lists all four stat types with no element and amplifies every non-pierce hit; both apply when live together and the 15% Lightning Increase Damage Given passive multiplies on top, so self amplification is the bloodline's identity and takes the primary route. The two Highest-stat formula hits (Bind 40 EP at rank D, cooldown 6; Whispers 50 EP at rank B, cooldown 7; 60 AP each, single target) are the kit's only direct damage and take the Damage route. Voltara Blitz's single 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row (all four stat types, realized on the caster at cast, 2 rounds, 60 AP, cooldown 7) is the only defensive lever and the only answer to the bloodline's 10% Wind Increase Damage Taken passive, so it carries the tertiary chain; Blitz's move and pierce rows and Bind's stun are unsupported. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Lightning jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Nine nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on five rows (Increase Damage Given x2, Damage x2, Decrease Damage Taken x1); Blitz's move and pierce and Bind's stun are unsupported. A fourth Advanced Art would need a second capstone on the same rows: a twin of the Damage or amplification route, which opens a second path to that tag's maximum, or a guard capstone past +10%. Instead Halo of Static carries one leaf, Heaven's Retort (Damage +2), so the guard root has its own 4 BP build and no node is universal. 12 legal full allocations, 9 non-dominated, every node in at least one.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Lightning jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Anointed by Lightning** — The storm chose this blood before birth; every pulse carries its blessing. Both self damage buffs 35 → 37% for 2 rounds: Bind's (Lightning hits only) and Whispers' (every non-pierce hit, any stat type).
- **Dragon's Whispered Wrath** — What the ethereal dragon whispers, the sky repeats as thunder. Luminous Illusion Bind 40 → 42 and Ethereal Dragon's Whispers 50 → 52 EP; two Lightning formula hits, 60 AP, cooldowns 6 and 7.
- **Ethereal Dragon Unbound** — No seal, no sky, no prayer holds the dragon once it has been named. Route total +5 Damage: Bind 40 → 45, Whispers 50 → 55 EP. 2 rows, no gates or hidden rows.
- **Luminous Conduit** — Light runs through the body as through wire, losing nothing on the way. Both self buffs 40% with Anointed by Lightning; Bind's row amplifies Lightning hits, Whispers' row every non-pierce hit.
- **Apotheosis of Thunder** — The faithful do not wield the lightning. They become it. Tempo route total +10%: both self buffs 35 → 45% (2 rounds each); plus Voltara Blitz Decrease Damage Taken 30 → 32% (34% with Halo of Static).
- **Halo of Static** — Where the blitz ends, a ring of charged air lingers and refuses to break. Voltara Blitz Decrease Damage Taken 30 → 32% (self at cast, all four stat types, 2 rounds, cooldown 7). No Damage on the root, so the Damage route stays at +5.
- **Aegis of Living Light** — A mantle of living light; blows arrive softened, as if through a dream. Voltara Blitz Decrease Damage Taken only: 35% with Halo of Static; one element-less row against every non-pierce hit, 2 rounds per cast.
- **Unassailable Radiance** — Radiance so complete that harm itself hesitates at the threshold. Guard route total +10%: Blitz guard 30 → 40% for 2 rounds; plus Bind and Whispers +1 (41/51 EP, 43/53 with Heaven's Retort).
- **Heaven's Retort** — Strike at the haloed and the sky strikes back, louder than the blow it answers. Bind 40 → 42, Whispers 50 → 52 EP (43/53 with Unassailable Radiance); the guard root's strike purchase.

## Design notes

- Node split: 2 Foundations, 4 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts. Anointed by Lightning (IDG +2%) forks into Dragon's Whispered Wrath → Ethereal Dragon Unbound (Damage +2/+3) and Luminous Conduit → Apotheosis of Thunder (IDG +3%, then IDG +5% with DDT +2%). Halo of Static (DDT +2%) forks into Aegis of Living Light → Unassailable Radiance (DDT +3%, then DDT +5% with Damage +1) and the leaf Heaven's Retort (Damage +2). Advanced Arts are 3 BP deep; two cost 5 BP under one root, 6 across.
- Routes: Burst +5 Damage (Dragon's Whispered Wrath +2, Ethereal Dragon Unbound +3; 45/55 EP); Tempo +10% Increase Damage Given (Anointed by Lightning +2%, Luminous Conduit +3%, Apotheosis of Thunder +5%; 45% per row); Guard +10% Decrease Damage Taken (Halo of Static +2%, Aegis of Living Light +3%, Unassailable Radiance +5%; 30 → 40%), all in Taiyo Kami's shapes. Recalibration: Halo of Static's former +1 Damage is removed because Halo can be the Burst build's fourth purchase (it put that build at +6), and the Tempo route rises from +7% to +10%. Outside the Burst route Damage reaches +4 at most (Heaven's Retort with Dragon's Whispered Wrath).
- Interactions: Bind's IDG row carries Lightning and no stat types, so it matches only Lightning damage (the kit's strikes, any Lightning jutsu or weapon), never basic attacks or element-less jutsu. Whispers' IDG and Blitz's DDT have no element and list all four stat types, so they match every non-pierce hit. Same-tag rows from different casts all apply (process.ts 1109–1117); the 15% Lightning passive (bloodline-sourced) is applied separately. Pierce, Blitz's included, is untouched.
- Timing (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b): each IDG and DDT row is live the two rounds after its cast, never on its own strike or another action that round. Each cast costs 60 AP, one per round. Bind then Whispers (or the reverse) gives the second strike the first strike's row only (35%, 45% on Tempo); in the one overlap round both strikes are on cooldown (6, 7) and Blitz's hit is pierce, so only a non-kit Lightning hit takes both rows. Blitz's DDT is realized on the caster at cast.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01,02,03) takes Halo of Static (guard 32%) or Luminous Conduit (buffs 40%); Tempo (01,04,05) takes Halo of Static (guard 34%) or Dragon's Whispered Wrath (42/52 EP, guard 32%); Guard (06,07,08) takes Heaven's Retort (43/53 EP) or Anointed by Lightning (41/51 EP, buffs 37%). Three no-capstone hybrids are dominated (01,02,04,06 and 01,04,06,09 by 01,02,04,05; 01,02,06,09 by Burst), and 01,06,07,09 equals 01,02,06,07; every node still appears in a non-dominated build.
- Aegis of Living Light is the lowest-value Hidden Art (+3% on one row), the price of the only defensive lever; Halo of Static (+2% on the same row) is the lightest Foundation after the recalibration.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Lightning is shared with other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 3 of 5 supported kit rows carry Lightning; Whispers' IDG and Blitz's DDT carry no element and need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Voltara Blitz (no Lightning row) qualifies in-kit only through an authored Lightning jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Lightning coverage is unverified.
- Broad self amplification: Apotheosis of Thunder puts Whispers' IDG at 45% on every non-pierce hit the caster lands for 2 rounds (weapons, basic attacks, normal jutsu) and Bind's at 45% on every Lightning hit, with the 15% Lightning passive multiplying on top; a non-kit Lightning hit in the overlap round takes both rows. Not simulated.
- Ally-targeted casts: Bind and Whispers (OTHER_USER) accept any living non-caster user in range 4 / 3 (util.ts 2747–2749). On an ally the Damage and stun rows (ENEMIES) are withheld but the SELF IDG row is still realized (actions.ts 1060–1067), so with an ally present the buff can be taken without landing a strike; uptime is bounded by cooldowns 6/7 and 60 AP, not by enemy range. Not simulated.
- Layout (user-visible option): Heaven's Retort is a ninth node added so the guard root has its own 4 BP build and no node sits in every full build. Without it the tree has eight nodes and Anointed by Lightning is in every full build; the leaf adds three dominated hybrids and two pairs of equal-total hybrids.
- Guard is one row at 2 rounds in 7: Unassailable Radiance puts Blitz's DDT at 40% against every non-pierce hit of any stat type, Wind included, but Blitz is a 60 AP displacement cast (the move row carries the caster in), so the guard also commits position. It stacks with other DDT sources; the bloodline's own 10% Wind IDT passive is unchanged.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt Highest-stat scaling), multiplied by the 15% Lightning passive and raised by active jutsu buffs, so +5 EP is not a linear +5 damage; bases are 40 EP (Bind, rank D) and 50 EP (Whispers, rank B) on a rank-C bloodline. Each row fires at most once per 6 or 7 rounds; Ethereal Dragon Unbound is the lowest-weight capstone (+3 on two rows).
- Unsupported rows and modes: Blitz's move and 56 EP pierce and Bind's 100% stun receive nothing, and the pierce is outside every IDG and DDT modifier too; the passives (15% Lightning IDG, 10% Wind IDT) are not jutsu rows. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in ranked PvP / sparring at the pin. No hidden, item-gated, mode-restricted, adverse or hazard supported rows exist in this kit.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Lightning jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
