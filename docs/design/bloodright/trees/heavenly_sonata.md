# Heavenly Sonata — Music of the Spheres

**Bloodline:** Heavenly Sonata (BR-032, rank A, `clh4d6q6s000atb0h0qdqdh1z`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Yin-Yang classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Yin-Yang (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Decrease Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Given (Celestial Harmony Shield fortress, Harmonic Chamber suppression) · secondary Damage (three Yin-Yang Genjutsu attacks) · tertiary Increase Damage Taken and Increase Damage Given (Foreboding Interlude exposure, Lullaby empowerment).

Heavenly Sonata is a Defensive/Tank Genjutsu kit whose two 40 AP casts carry its identity: Celestial Harmony Shield's 2-round self buff (Decrease Damage Taken 35%, realized on the caster at cast, beside an unsupported 100 shield) and Harmonic Chamber's lock (Decrease Damage Given 35% beside unsupported drain 210 and cleanseprevent); both rows list all four stat types with no element, so at the pin they touch every non-pierce hit, and each is a +10% route. Three of the seven supported rows are Yin-Yang Genjutsu Damage on 60 AP casts (Foreboding Interlude 40, Frozen Melody 45, Lullaby 40 EP at jutsu level 25), so offense is a full secondary +5 Damage route. Foreboding Interlude's 35% exposure (Genjutsu-filtered, element-less) and Lullaby's 35% self buff (all four stat types, element-less) are the tertiary amplifiers: exposure is a +10% route, empowerment is Foundation-plus-capstone glue capped at +5% because that buff reaches every non-pierce hit the caster lands. No Afterburn, Lifesteal, Reflect, Heal or Increase Heal row exists, so none is invented. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Yin-Yang jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Yin-Yang jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Opening Measure** — Every sonata begins with a single measure that decides how the rest will fall. Lullaby self buff 35 → 37% (4 stat types, element-less: any non-pierce hit); Interlude exposure 35 → 37% (Genjutsu or element-less hits).
- **Rising Crescendo** — The melody swells until the hall itself begins to tremble. Foreboding Interlude 40 → 42, Frozen Melody 45 → 47, Lullaby 40 → 42 EP (Yin-Yang Genjutsu, range 4, 60 AP, cooldown 7); Melody: enemy-only circle.
- **Fortissimo Finale** — The last movement, played at full voice, brings the heavens down. Same three Yin-Yang Damage rows; route total +5 Damage (Foreboding Interlude 45, Frozen Melody 50, Lullaby 45 EP at jutsu level 25).
- **Dissonant Chord** — One wrong note, held too long, and the whole piece turns against its listener. Foreboding Interlude exposure only (40% with Opening Measure); live 2 rounds after each 60 AP cast; Genjutsu or element-less non-pierce hits.
- **Prelude to Ruin** — The interlude was only a warning; this is the ruin it foretold. Foreboding Interlude exposure route total +10% (35 → 45%) and Lullaby self buff 40% (+5% with Opening Measure); both 2-round windows on 60 AP casts.
- **Harmonic Balance** — Yin answers yang: every note has its silence, every blow its shelter. Celestial Harmony Shield self reduction 35 → 37%; Harmonic Chamber suppression 35 → 37% (every non-pierce hit the target deals); 2 rounds.
- **Wall of Sound** — Layer upon layer of chorus, until no blade can find the singer. Shield reduction only (40% with Harmonic Balance): SELF row realized on the caster at cast, live 2 rounds after, any position; non-pierce.
- **Celestial Sanctum** — Within the shield's hymn the caster stands untouched, as in a chapel of light. Shield reduction route total +10% (35 → 45%); Harmonic Chamber suppression +2% (39%; 42% with Muted Strings). The 100 shield row is unchanged.
- **Muted Strings** — The chamber swallows the enemy's song until only a murmur escapes. Harmonic Chamber suppression only (40% with Harmonic Balance); 40 AP single target, range 4; every non-pierce hit the target deals.
- **Final Rest** — The last rest in the score: no note, no breath, no strength left to strike. Harmonic Chamber suppression route total +10% (35 → 45%); Shield reduction +2% (39%; 42% with Wall of Sound). Drain, cleanseprevent unchanged.

## Design notes

- Node split: ten nodes on five supported tags and seven rows, four roles (burst, exposure, fortress, suppression), one example per Advanced Art. Routes (RUL-2026-10-03-005): Burst +5 Damage (Rising Crescendo +2, Fortissimo Finale +3); Exposure +10% Increase Damage Taken (Opening Measure +2%, Dissonant Chord +3%, Prelude to Ruin +5%); Fortress +10% Decrease Damage Taken and Suppression +10% Decrease Damage Given (each 2/3/5). Increase Damage Given is glue (+2%, +3%; +5% maximum) because Lullaby's buff reaches every non-pierce hit the caster lands. The Damage route is the highest row-weighted build (23 on 7 rows).
- Recalibration from Draft 3: Dissonant Chord +2% → +3% and Prelude to Ruin's exposure +3% → +5% move the Exposure route from +7% to the +10% band (Interlude 35 → 45%). All other values are unchanged; maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +5, Increase Damage Given +5%, Increase Damage Taken +10%, Decrease Damage Taken +10%, Decrease Damage Given +10%.
- Capstone secondaries: the tank capstones each take +2% of the other's tag (Celestial Sanctum +2% Decrease Damage Given, Final Rest +2% Decrease Damage Taken), below the sibling Hidden Arts' +3%, so the fortress/suppression hybrid (01, 06, 07, 09) is never dominated and neither tank capstone is an offensive buy. Prelude to Ruin takes +3% Increase Damage Given; Fortissimo Finale is Damage-only.
- Downstream reach: the bloodline passive (Increase Damage Given 25% + 0.15/level on Fire, Lightning, Yin-Yang, None) is not a potency target; as a bloodline source it applies last to the enhanced Yin-Yang damage, after the kit rows multiply the hit in turn (computeDamagePacket, §3b). Lullaby's buff and Harmonic Chamber's suppression list all four stat types, no element: every non-pierce hit the caster lands or the target deals. Interlude's exposure lists only Genjutsu: Genjutsu hits of any element and element-less hits, not elemental Nin/Tai/Buki.
- Delivery and timing: all five jutsu are cooldown 7; Interlude, Melody and Lullaby cost 60 AP, Shield and Chamber 40 AP. Every buff/debuff row is live the two rounds after its cast round, never in it (SOURCE_MECHANICS 3b), so Interlude's exposure never raises its own hit nor Lullaby's buff its own; Interlude, then Lullaby, then Frozen Melody puts the exposure on both later hits and the buff on Melody. The Shield's SELF row is realized on the caster at cast (4b), not via the circle tiles.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Harmonic Balance (37% / 37% defensive glue) or Dissonant Chord (40% exposure); Exposure takes Harmonic Balance or Rising Crescendo (+2 Damage); Fortress takes Muted Strings (42% suppression, pure tank) or Opening Measure (37% buff and exposure); Suppression takes Opening Measure or Wall of Sound (42% reduction, Shield only). All 14 full-budget allocations are numerically non-dominated and every node appears in one.
- Reference reuse: topology and six nodes copy Taiyo Kami's values (Foundations +2% IDG/+2% IDT, +2% DDT/+2% DDG; Damage +2/+3; Hidden +3% DDT, +3% DDG). Departures: no Afterburn row exists, so Taiyo's Afterburn route becomes the Exposure route (Dissonant Chord +3%; Prelude to Ruin +5% IDT, +3% IDG); the tank capstones take +2% of the sibling tank tag, not Taiyo's +3% IDG/+5% IDT, so neither is an offensive buy.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: element-wide Yin-Yang scope (RUL-2026-10-03-005): matching supported tags on all Yin-Yang jutsu, whatever their source; sharing Yin-Yang with other bloodlines is expected. 3 of the 7 supported rows carry Yin-Yang; the rest need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Celestial Harmony Shield and Harmonic Chamber (no Yin-Yang row) qualify in-kit only through an authored jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Yin-Yang coverage is unverified.
- Shield timing: the Decrease Damage Taken row has target SELF, so it is realized on the caster at cast, not via the circle tiles (actions.ts 980-1004, 1060+; SOURCE_MECHANICS 4b): no standing-still cost, no enemy hazard. Like every modifier it skips its cast round (adjustDamageTaken, tags.ts 1010) and covers the two rounds after, so it must be cast a round ahead of the hits it should absorb.
- Tank-row breadth: Chamber's suppression and the Shield's reduction list four stat types, no element: any non-pierce hit qualifies. 45% suppression (40 AP) lowers the target's damage against allies too for the two rounds after the cast, beside drain 210 and cleanseprevent. Same-tag effects all apply and compound (process.ts 1109-1117, 1692-1825): two casters' Chambers on one target give ×0.55 × 0.55 ≈ ×0.30. Not simulated.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: the +5% glue lifts Lullaby's self buff to 40%, which compounds with every other jutsu, main-tree or gear percentage (with 45% exposure on the target, ×1.40 × 1.45 = ×2.03); the bloodline passive applies last (fromType bloodline, allowBloodlineDamageIncrease). Four stat types, no element: every non-pierce hit the caster lands in the two rounds after the cast.
- Exposure reach: Foreboding Interlude's row lists only Genjutsu with no element, so it amplifies Genjutsu hits of any element (the kit's Yin-Yang attacks) and every element-less hit from any source (basic attacks, weapons, non-elemental jutsu), allies' included, not elemental non-Genjutsu hits. Damage modifiers skip pierce. The Exposure build's row weight (19) understates its reach on a Genjutsu caster.
- Realized value: three 60 AP attacks on cooldown 7 mean each 2-round window reaches at most the other two attacks per rotation, never its own jutsu's hit (SOURCE_MECHANICS 3b), so per-row numbers overstate per-round value. Lullaby's self buff needs any other living user in range 4; aimed at an ally, its damage (friendly fire ALL, raised by the Damage route) and buffprevent land on that ally (3b).
- Frozen Melody is an AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire ENEMIES, so it is not an ally hazard; its stun is unsupported and unchanged. It is the kit's only multi-target reach, so +5 Damage on its 45 EP is the largest raw gain in the tree. Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Yin-Yang jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
