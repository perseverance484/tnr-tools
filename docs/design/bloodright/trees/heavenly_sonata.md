# Heavenly Sonata — Music of the Spheres

**Bloodline:** Heavenly Sonata (BR-032, rank A, `clh4d6q6s000atb0h0qdqdh1z`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Yin-Yang classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Yin-Yang (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Decrease Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Given (Celestial Harmony Shield fortress, Harmonic Chamber suppression) · secondary Damage (three Yin-Yang Genjutsu attacks) · tertiary Increase Damage Taken and Increase Damage Given (Foreboding Interlude exposure, Lullaby empowerment).

Heavenly Sonata is a Defensive/Tank Genjutsu kit whose two 40 AP casts carry its identity: Celestial Harmony Shield's self circle (Decrease Damage Taken 35% beside an unsupported 100 shield) and Harmonic Chamber's lock (Decrease Damage Given 35% beside unsupported drain 210 and cleanseprevent); both rows list all four stat types with no element, so at the pin they touch every non-pierce hit, and as single rows they carry the largest flats (+10 ceilings). Three of the seven supported rows are Yin-Yang Genjutsu Damage on 60 AP casts (Foreboding Interlude 40, Frozen Melody 45, Lullaby 40 at jutsu level 25), so offense is a full secondary route at the Taiyo Kami +5 scale. Foreboding Interlude's 35% exposure (Genjutsu-filtered, element-less) and Lullaby's 35% self buff (all four stat types, element-less) are the tertiary amplifiers: exposure gets a route held to +7, empowerment is Foundation-plus-capstone glue capped at +5 because that buff reaches every hit the caster lands. No Afterburn, Lifesteal, Reflect, Heal or Increase Heal row exists, so none is invented.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Yin-Yang-classified Heavenly Sonata jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Opening Measure** — Every sonata begins with a single measure that decides how the rest will fall. Lullaby self buff 35→37% (all four stat types, element-less: every hit); Foreboding Interlude exposure 35→37% (Genjutsu/element-less hits).
- **Rising Crescendo** — The melody swells until the hall itself begins to tremble. Foreboding Interlude 40→42, Frozen Melody 45→47, Lullaby 40→42 (Yin-Yang Genjutsu, range 4, 60 AP, cooldown 7); Melody: enemy-only circle.
- **Fortissimo Finale** — The last movement, played at full voice, brings the heavens down. Same three Yin-Yang damage rows; route total +5 (Foreboding Interlude 45, Frozen Melody 50, Lullaby 45 at jutsu level 25).
- **Dissonant Chord** — One wrong note, held too long, and the whole piece turns against its listener. Foreboding Interlude exposure only (+2 more: 39% with Opening Measure); 2 rounds per 60 AP cast; Genjutsu or element-less hits qualify.
- **Prelude to Ruin** — The interlude was only a warning; this is the ruin it foretold. Foreboding Interlude exposure to 42% (route +7) and Lullaby self buff to 40% (+5 with Opening Measure); both 2-round windows on 60 AP casts.
- **Harmonic Balance** — Yin answers yang: every note has its silence, every blow its shelter. Celestial Harmony Shield self circle 35→37% reduction; Harmonic Chamber suppression 35→37% (every non-pierce hit the target deals); 2 rds.
- **Wall of Sound** — Layer upon layer of chorus, until no blade can find the singer. Celestial Harmony Shield reduction only (+3 more: 40% with Harmonic Balance); 40 AP self circle, re-applied while standing in it; non-pierce
- **Celestial Sanctum** — Within the shield's hymn the caster stands untouched, as in a chapel of light. Shield circle reduction to 45% (route +10); Harmonic Chamber suppression +2 (39%; 42% with Muted Strings). The 100 shield row is unchanged.
- **Muted Strings** — The chamber swallows the enemy's song until only a murmur escapes. Harmonic Chamber suppression only (+3 more: 40% with Harmonic Balance); 40 AP single target, range 4; all four stat types: every non-pierce
- **Final Rest** — The last rest in the score: no note, no breath, no strength left to strike. Harmonic Chamber suppression to 45% (route +10); Shield circle reduction +2 (39%; 42% with Wall of Sound). Drain, cleanseprevent unchanged.

## Design notes

- Node split: the Damage route (+2 Hidden, +3 Advanced) reaches the three Yin-Yang attacks as Taiyo Kami's does and is the highest row-weighted build (23 on 7 rows; Taiyo 31 on 9). Single-row percentage tags carry the larger flats: Increase Damage Taken is held to +7 (2/2/3); Decrease Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Given reach +10 (2/3/5). Increase Damage Given is glue only (+2 Foundation, +3 on Prelude to Ruin; +5 ceiling) because Lullaby's buff reaches every hit the caster lands.
- Capstone secondaries: the tank capstones each take +2 of the other's tag (Celestial Sanctum +2 Decrease Damage Given, Final Rest +2 Decrease Damage Taken), below the sibling Hidden Arts' +3, so the fortress/suppression hybrid (01, 06, 07, 09) is never dominated and neither tank capstone is an offensive buy. Prelude to Ruin takes +3 Increase Damage Given; Fortissimo Finale is Damage-only. An Increase Damage Taken secondary on Final Rest would dominate 01, 04, 06, 09; rejected.
- Downstream reach: the bloodline passive (Increase Damage Given 25% + 0.15/level on Fire, Lightning, Yin-Yang, None) is not a potency target but multiplies the enhanced Yin-Yang damage. Lullaby's buff and Harmonic Chamber's suppression list all four stat types, no element: the buff raises every hit the caster lands and the suppression lowers every non-pierce hit the target deals. Interlude's exposure lists only Genjutsu: Yin-Yang Genjutsu and element-less hits, not elemental Nin/Tai/Buki.
- Delivery: all five jutsu are cooldown 7. Foreboding Interlude, Frozen Melody and Lullaby are 60 AP, range 4 (Melody is an enemy-only AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with an unsupported 2-round stun; Lullaby's self buff still needs an enemy target in range). Celestial Harmony Shield and Harmonic Chamber are 40 AP; the Shield is a SELF AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN, range 0, no move row, so its reduction is re-applied each round to a caster standing in the circle; cast-round application and window overlaps were not verified.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Harmonic Balance (37%/37% defensive glue) or Dissonant Chord (39% exposure); Exposure takes Harmonic Balance or Rising Crescendo (+2 power); Fortress takes Muted Strings (42% suppression, pure tank) or Opening Measure (37% buff and exposure); Suppression takes Opening Measure or Wall of Sound (42% reduction, circle only). All 14 full-budget allocations are reported by the validator; see the audit for dominance.
- Ten nodes are justified by five supported tags on seven rows across four distinct roles (burst, exposure, fortress, suppression); no node is a reskin of another, and four examples are given because each Advanced Art is a different tag and role. Stun, drain, shield, buffprevent and cleanseprevent are unsupported and untouched; no Afterburn, Lifesteal, Reflect, Heal or Increase Heal row exists, so none is invented.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Yin-Yang is the single signature element but not exclusive in the census (8 collisions: INCLUDE Ethereal Monarch, Tenohira Musei; DEFER Celestial Mage, DvEM, Manhattan Project, Tenryūseigan, Tenshin Shoden Yami-Ryu; EXCLUDE Ethereal Regent); the tree assumes bloodline-scoped classification. Normal-jutsu Yin-Yang use is unverified; the resolver matches 3 of 7 rows directly.
- Shield circle uptime: the Decrease Damage Taken row is a SELF row on an AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN (range 0, no move row). Per the ground-circle reading it is re-applied each round only while the caster stands in the tiles; whether a SELF-target spawn applies on the cast round was not read. Harmonic Balance, Wall of Sound, Celestial Sanctum and Final Rest's +2 depend on standing still; not simulated.
- Breadth of the tank rows: Harmonic Chamber's suppression and the Shield's reduction list all four stat types with no element, so at the pin every non-pierce hit qualifies. 45% suppression on a 40 AP cast lowers the target's damage against allies too for 2 rounds, with cleanseprevent (1 round) guarding it from removal and drain 210 beside it; the PvP value of that lock was not simulated.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: the +5 glue adds statically to Lullaby's 35% self buff, which stacks with the bloodline passive (bloodline sources multiply and honour allowBloodlineDamageIncrease) and any main-tree or gear modifiers; BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is true at the pin. The row lists all four stat types with no element, so the buff reaches every hit the caster lands for 2 rounds. Not simulated.
- Exposure reach: Foreboding Interlude's row lists only Genjutsu with no element, so it amplifies the kit's Yin-Yang Genjutsu attacks and every element-less hit from any source (basic attacks, weapons, non-elemental jutsu) but not elemental non-Genjutsu hits from allies or normal jutsu. Damage modifiers skip pierce. The Exposure build's row weight (16) understates its reach on a Genjutsu caster.
- Realized value: three of five casts are 60 AP on cooldown 7, so Interlude's 2-round exposure and Lullaby's 2-round buff rarely cover all three attacks in one rotation; per-row numbers overstate per-round value. Whether the self buff applied by Lullaby affects that same cast's damage row was not verified.
- Frozen Melody is an AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire ENEMIES, so it is not an ally hazard; its stun is unsupported and unchanged. It is the kit's only multi-target reach, so +5 on its 45 damage is the largest raw gain in the tree. Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Heavenly Sonata jutsu inherit Yin-Yang potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
