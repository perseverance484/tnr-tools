# Sea-King's Blessing — Dominion of the Deep

**Bloodline:** Sea-King's Blessing (BR-061, rank C, `juZy8qwituqM2Km6cOiL4`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Sea-King's Blessing classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Sea-King's Blessing (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Increase Damage Taken — Crushing Abyss's broad circle exposure and Water Dome's Water-filtered exposure (two rows, +7 ceiling) · secondary Water damage — Sea King's Armor and Crushing Abyss (two formula rows at 38, +6 ceiling) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken — Sea King's Armor's self reduction (one row, +10 ceiling).

Sea-King's Blessing is a rank-C kit whose five supported rows split 2/2/1: two 38-power Water hits (Sea King's Armor, Crushing Abyss; both 60 AP, cooldown 7), two enemy Increase Damage Taken rows (Abyss 30% on enemies in its circle, all four stat types and no element; Water Dome 35% Water-filtered on a 40 AP, cooldown-6 cast) and one 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row on Armor. Exposure is the primary emphasis because it is the kit's most distinctive and most frequent effect, amplifies the caster's own Water hits and every ally's non-pierce hit, and the kit's own hits are modest. Raw Water power is secondary because it is the kit's direct output and the bloodline's Water IDG passive multiplies it. Armor's reduction is tertiary: one row, two rounds in seven. Absorb and shield rows are unsupported.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on five rows (Damage x2, Increase Damage Taken x2, Decrease Damage Taken x1), so it supports exactly three routes: Scales of the Deep forks into a Water damage route (Grip of the Riptide → Weight of the Trench, +6 at the Damage guardrail) and an Armor route (Deepwater Carapace → Hull of the Leviathan, +10 on the single reduction row); Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King is a single chain on the kit's two enemy debuffs (+7, the two-row ceiling). A fourth route needs a fourth tag the kit does not have (absorb and shield are not potency targets). The consequence is that Scales of the Deep is in every legal full build and the exposure route's fourth purchase is always Scales of the Deep, while Burst and Bulwark each have two fourth-purchase choices. Three named capstone builds exist, one per Advanced Art; eight legal full allocations in total (five with a capstone, three hybrids).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Sea-King's Blessing jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Node split: 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts. Scales of the Deep (Damage +1 on both hits, Decrease Damage Taken +2 on Armor; 3 of 5 rows) is the self-side root and forks into Grip of the Riptide → Weight of the Trench (Damage +2/+3) and Deepwater Carapace → Hull of the Leviathan (DDT +3/+5 with Damage +1). Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King (IDT +2/+2/+3 with Damage +1) is the enemy-side chain. Every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Flats by coverage: Damage reaches two rows, so the route totals +6 (1+2+3; 38→44 on both hits), at the guardrail, one above Taiyo Kami's three-row +5. Increase Damage Taken reaches two rows (one broad, one Water-filtered), so its route stops at the two-row ceiling +7 (2+2+3; 30→37%, 35→42%). Decrease Damage Taken is one row and takes the single-row shape (2+3+5 = +10; 30→40%), Taiyo Kami's DDT line. Both capstone secondaries are Damage +1, below Grip of the Riptide's +2.
- Interactions: Armor's DDT row and Abyss's IDT row list all four stat types with no element, so they match every non-pierce hit of any element (Highest resolves to one of the four). Dome's IDT row carries Water and no stat filter: Water damage only (the caster's two hits, allies' Water jutsu), not basic attacks, weapons or plain jutsu. IDT rows add power/100 × the staged base per hit and stack additively; the bloodline's Water IDG passive (15 + 0.15/level) multiplies the two Water hits on top.
- Delivery and uptime: all three jutsu target OTHER_USER at range 4. Sea King's Armor (60 AP, cooldown 7, SINGLE) hits the target for Water damage and gives the caster DDT 30% plus an unsupported 100 shield for two rounds. Crushing Abyss (60 AP, cooldown 7) spawns a circle on the target: Water hit plus 30% exposure to enemies in it; friendly fire ENEMIES on both rows, so no ally hazard. Water Dome (40 AP, cooldown 6, SINGLE) exposes the target 35% and gives the caster unsupported absorb and shield. Buffs and debuffs last 2 rounds.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01,02,03) finishes with Salt in the Wound (exposure 32/37%) or Deepwater Carapace (Armor 35%); Bulwark (01,04,05) with Salt in the Wound or Grip of the Riptide (hits 42/42); Exposure (01,06,07,08) only with Scales of the Deep. No-capstone hybrids: 01,02,04,06 (41/41, 35%, 32/37%), 01,02,06,07 (41/41, 32%, 34/39%) and 01,04,06,07 (39/39, 35%, 34/39%); 01,02,03,04 and 01,02,04,05 stay inside the first root. 8 legal full allocations, all non-dominated, every node used.
- Row-weighted value: 01,06,07,08 is the strongest full allocation at 20 (IDT +7 on two rows, Damage +2 on two, DDT +2 on one), against Taiyo Kami's 31 on nine rows and Shinseina Ki's 26 on six; per supported row (4.0) it sits between them. Lowest-value purchases by row weight are Deepwater Carapace (+3 on one row) and Salt in the Wound (+2 on two rows); Carapace's value is defensive uptime on a 60 AP cast, Salt's is downstream on hits the exposed enemy takes (any under Abyss, Water under Dome).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Water is a basic element carried by 23 census bloodlines (Shinrai Ou, Tetsugan, Shiroi Youso, Teno Yuki and other INCLUDE kits among them) and whether NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN jutsu carry it is unverified. The tree assumes the bloodline-keyed classification extension; a bare Water element match would leak every node to other Water rows and vice versa.
- Resolver: 2 of 5 supported rows (Armor's DDT, Abyss's IDT) have no element and fall back to None under the current resolver, so affectedElements=['Water'] cannot reach them; reaching them through None would also reach every element-less row on any jutsu the player casts. The three Water rows match directly.
- Exposure breadth (user-owned value): with Wrath of the Sea-King both debuffs can sit on one target: Abyss 37% + Dome 42% = 79 points of IDT for two rounds (65 at base) on Water hits only (the caster's two hits, allies' Water jutsu); other allies' hits get Abyss's 37% alone. BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is on at the pin. Trim the capstone to +2 (route +6) if double exposure proves too strong.
- Universal opener: every legal full build contains Scales of the Deep because the exposure chain has no other legal fourth purchase. A second Hidden Art under Salt in the Wound was rejected: Damage +2 twins Grip of the Riptide, IDT +2 would push the two-row IDT route to +9, above its +7 ceiling, IDT +3 duplicates Dragged Under's totals at 4 BP. Shinseina Ki, Lycanthropy and Shinrai Ou precedent.
- DDT breadth: Armor's reduction lists all four stat types with no element, so +10 makes it 40% against effectively every non-pierce hit for two of every seven rounds; pierce bypasses it, and the bloodline's Earth Increase Damage Taken 10% passive still applies outside the buff, so the Earth weakness stays. Armor's own 100 shield and Dome's absorb are unsupported and unchanged.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the bloodline's Water IDG passive and any exposure on the target, so +6 raw power is not linear +6 damage; realized value depends on stats and enemy state. Rank C kit with two 60 AP hits on cooldown 7; no combat simulation was performed.
- Dome's Water-filtered exposure: the row lists Water with no stat filter, so under getEfficiencyRatio (SOURCE_MECHANICS.md §3) its tag list is [Water]: it matches Water damage only, since an element-list row reaches element-less hits only when its list includes None. Basic attacks, weapons and non-Water jutsu are unaffected by Dome's 42%; only Abyss's row (four stat types, no element) is broad.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Sea-King's Blessing jutsu inherit Sea-King's Blessing potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
