# Sea-King's Blessing — Dominion of the Deep

**Bloodline:** Sea-King's Blessing (BR-061, rank C, `juZy8qwituqM2Km6cOiL4`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Water classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Water (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Increase Damage Taken — Crushing Abyss's broad circle exposure and Water Dome's Water-only exposure (two rows, +10% route) · secondary Water Damage — Sea King's Armor and Crushing Abyss (two formula rows at 38 EP, +5 route) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken — Sea King's Armor's self reduction (one row, +10% route).

Sea-King's Blessing is a rank-C kit whose five supported rows split 2/2/1: two 38 EP Water hits (Sea King's Armor, Crushing Abyss; both 60 AP, cooldown 7), two enemy Increase Damage Taken rows (Abyss 30% on enemies in its circle, all four stat types and no element; Water Dome 35% Water-only on a 40 AP, cooldown-6 cast) and one 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row on Armor. Exposure is primary because it is the kit's most distinctive and most frequent effect and, for the two rounds after each cast, amplifies every non-pierce hit the target takes (Abyss) or every Water hit (Dome), allies' included; the kit's own hits are modest. Water Damage is secondary: it is the kit's direct output and the bloodline's Water IDG passive multiplies it. Armor's reduction is tertiary: one row, two rounds in seven. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Water jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Absorb and shield rows are unsupported.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on five rows (Damage x2, Increase Damage Taken x2, Decrease Damage Taken x1), so it supports exactly three routes: Scales of the Deep forks into a Water Damage route (Grip of the Riptide → Weight of the Trench, +5 Damage) and an Armor route (Deepwater Carapace → Hull of the Leviathan, +10% on the single reduction row); Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King is a single chain on the kit's two enemy debuffs (+10%). A fourth route needs a fourth tag the kit does not have (absorb and shield are not potency targets). Universal node accepted: Scales of the Deep is in all 8 legal full builds, because no 1 BP leaf Hidden Art under Salt in the Wound has a distinct role. Any Increase Damage Taken on the leaf lifts 06,07,08,leaf above +10%, and Damage +4 opens a second path above +5 Damage; every other leaf up to Damage +3 and Decrease Damage Taken +5% leaves at least one full build dominated or twinned (all enumerated). A mirrored layout would only move the universal node to the other root. So the exposure route's fourth purchase is always Scales of the Deep, while Burst and Bulwark each have two.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Node split: 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts. Scales of the Deep (Decrease Damage Taken +2% on Armor) is the self-side root and forks into Grip of the Riptide → Weight of the Trench (Damage +2/+3) and Deepwater Carapace → Hull of the Leviathan (DDT +3%/+5% with Damage +1). Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King (IDT +2%/+3%/+5% with Damage +1) is the enemy-side chain. Every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Reference reuse and recalibration (RUL-2026-10-03-005): Grip of the Riptide → Weight of the Trench copies Taiyo Kami's Crown of Cinders → Solar Cataclysm (+2/+3 Damage), the Armor route its DDT line (2/3/5 = +10%; 30 → 40%), and the exposure chain takes the same 2/3/5 shape on two rows (Abyss 30 → 40%, Dome 35 → 45%). Scales of the Deep drops its +1 Damage so Burst is the default +5 (38 → 43 EP; was +6); Dragged Under +2% → +3% and Wrath of the Sea-King +3% → +5% move Exposure from +7% to +10%. No IDG rows, so both capstone secondaries are Damage +1, below Grip's +2. Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +5, Increase Damage Taken +10%, Decrease Damage Taken +10%.
- Interactions: Armor's DDT row and Abyss's IDT row list all four stat types with no element, so they match every non-pierce hit of any element (Highest resolves to one of the four). Dome's IDT row carries Water and no stat filter: Water damage only (the caster's two hits, allies' Water jutsu), not basic attacks, weapons or plain jutsu. Each IDT row multiplies a matching hit by 1 + p/100 and two live rows compound (§3b); the bloodline's Water IDG passive (15 + 0.15/level) multiplies the two Water hits last.
- Delivery: all three jutsu are OTHER_USER at range 4; 2-round rows are live the two rounds after the cast round. Armor (60 AP, cooldown 7, SINGLE) hits for Water and gives the caster DDT 30% and an unsupported 100 shield (SELF rows). Abyss (60 AP, cooldown 7, radius-1 AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN) hits and exposes enemies in the circle 30% (friendly fire ENEMIES; never the caster). Dome (40 AP, cooldown 6, SINGLE) exposes the target 35%. Aimed at an ally, ENEMIES rows are withheld; SELF rows still land.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01,02,03) finishes with Salt in the Wound (exposure 32/37%) or Deepwater Carapace (Armor 35%); Bulwark (01,04,05) with Salt in the Wound or Grip of the Riptide (hits 41/41 EP); Exposure (01,06,07,08) only with Scales of the Deep. No-capstone hybrids: 01,02,04,06 (40/40 EP, 35%, 32/37%), 01,02,06,07 (40/40 EP, 32%, 35/40%) and 01,04,06,07 (38/38 EP, 35%, 35/40%).
- Row-weighted value: 01,06,07,08 is the strongest full allocation at 24 (IDT +10% on two rows, Damage +1 on two, DDT +2% on one); Burst and Bulwark score 16. Exposure is the primary route; its value is downstream on hits the exposed enemy takes (any under Abyss, Water under Dome).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Water is shared with many other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 3 of 5 kit rows carry Water; Armor's DDT and Abyss's IDT rows have no element and sit on Water jutsu, so they need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver. Off-kit Water coverage is unverified.
- Exposure breadth: with Wrath of the Sea-King, Abyss 40% and Dome 45% compound to ×1.40 × 1.45 ≈ ×2.03 (×1.30 × 1.35 ≈ ×1.76 at base) while both are up, on Water hits only: the caster's Armor hit and allies' Water jutsu (Abyss's hit gets Dome's 45% at most). Other hits get Abyss's 40% alone. Same-tag effects all apply, each as its own multiplier (§3b).
- Universal opener (accepted; narrow_kit_exception has the leaf enumeration): an Exposure player always buys Scales of the Deep's +2% Decrease Damage Taken, wanted or not. Any leaf that relieves it breaks a ceiling or adds a twin or dominated build.
- DDT breadth: Armor's reduction lists all four stat types with no element, so +10% makes it 40% against effectively every non-pierce hit for the two rounds after each cast (two in seven); pierce bypasses it. The bloodline's Earth IDT 10% passive multiplies Earth hits with or without the buff, so the Earth weakness stays. Armor's 100 shield and Dome's absorb are unsupported and unchanged.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the bloodline's Water IDG passive, while any exposure on the target multiplies it again, so +5 raw EP is not linear +5 damage; realized value depends on stats and enemy state. Rank C kit with two 60 AP hits on cooldown 7; no combat simulation was performed.
- Dome's Water-filtered exposure: the row lists Water with no stat filter, so under getEfficiencyRatio (SOURCE_MECHANICS.md §3) its tag list is [Water]: it matches Water damage only, since an element-list row reaches element-less hits only when its list includes None. Basic attacks, weapons and non-Water jutsu are unaffected by Dome's 45%; only Abyss's row (four stat types, no element) is broad.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
