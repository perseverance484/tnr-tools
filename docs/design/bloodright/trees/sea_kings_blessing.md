# Sea-King's Blessing — Dominion of the Deep

**Bloodline:** Sea-King's Blessing (BR-061, rank C, `juZy8qwituqM2Km6cOiL4`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance / Water classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Water (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Increase Damage Taken — Crushing Abyss's broad circle exposure and Water Dome's Water-only exposure (two rows, +8% route, ×1.124) · secondary Burst — Grip of the Riptide's exposure setup (Abyss 32%, Dome 37%) paid off by Weight of the Trench's +2 Damage on Sea King's Armor and Crushing Abyss (two 38 EP formula rows, 38 → 40) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken — Sea King's Armor's self reduction (one row, +10% route).

Sea-King's Blessing is a rank-C kit whose five supported rows split 2/2/1: two 38 EP Water hits (Sea King's Armor, Crushing Abyss; 60 AP, cooldown 7), two enemy Increase Damage Taken rows (Abyss 30% on enemies in its circle, all four stat types and no element; Water Dome 35% Water-only on a 40 AP, cooldown-6 cast) and one 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row on Armor. Exposure is primary: one cast amplifies every matching hit the target takes for two rounds, allies' included, and the two rows compound on Water hits, so the route stops at +8% (×1.124 with both windows live, against Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.115 exposure maximum). Scales of the Deep answers "How do I win the exchange myself: hit harder, or take less?" (Burst, Fortress); Salt in the Wound answers "How do I make them take more from everyone?" (Exposure). Each Advanced Art carries one tag; the only flat Damage is the burst capstone's +2 (Weight of the Trench, after Grip of the Riptide's +2% exposure setup), which takes both hits from Light to Normal (38 → 40). Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Water jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Absorb and shield rows are unsupported.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four, as in the pre-batch tree. The kit has three supported tags on five rows (Damage x2, Increase Damage Taken x2, Decrease Damage Taken x1), one route each: Scales of the Deep forks into Burst (Grip of the Riptide → Weight of the Trench) and Fortress (Deepwater Carapace → Hull of the Leviathan); Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King is the single exposure chain. A fourth route needs a fourth tag (absorb and shield are not potency targets). Universal node accepted: Scales of the Deep is in all 8 legal full builds, so Exposure's fourth purchase is always its +2% Decrease Damage Taken. A 1 BP leaf under Salt in the Wound would end that, but each option fails: Increase Damage Taken twins Dragged Under and hands Exposure's fourth its own tag again (back toward the +10% the route was cut from), Damage puts flat Damage on the exposure capstone's build, and Decrease Damage Taken is an off-axis twin of Scales of the Deep (or dominates or is dominated by it).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Weight of the Trench** — Miles of black water press down at once, and nothing built by hands keeps its shape. Burst payoff: Armor and Abyss 38 → 40 EP (+2 Damage, Light → Normal), landing into Grip of the Riptide's exposure (Abyss's hit into Dome's 37%, Armor's into both: Abyss 32%, Dome 37%); the bloodline's Water IDG passive multiplies on top.

- **Dragged Under** — Once the current has your ankle, every blow lands on someone who cannot stand. Abyss exposure 32 → 34%, Dome 37 → 39% with Salt in the Wound (enemy debuffs, 2 rounds).

## Design notes

- Node split: 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts. Scales of the Deep (+2% Decrease Damage Taken) is the win-the-exchange root: Grip of the Riptide (+2% Increase Damage Taken) → Weight of the Trench (+2 Damage) and Deepwater Carapace (+3%) → Hull of the Leviathan (+5% Decrease Damage Taken). Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King (+2%/+2%/+4% Increase Damage Taken) is the team-exposure chain. Any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP.
- 2026-10-04 rebalance: the flat-Damage route goes from +5 to +2 Damage (38 → 40, Light → Normal, instead of 43), the roster default for kits whose only hits are 38 EP Light rows; +3 (38 → 41) was the first-pass value. R9′ makes it a burst: Grip of the Riptide's +1 Damage commit becomes a +2% Increase Damage Taken setup and Weight of the Trench pays +2 Damage. The setup does not twin Dragged Under: it sits under Scales of the Deep, so neither can be bought into the other's route; the capstone-free maximum on the tag is +6% (01,02,06,07) against Wrath of the Sea-King's +8%, while +3% would reach +7%, a near-tie. Decrease Damage Taken does nothing for the route's hits. Exposure goes +10% → +8% (Dragged Under +3% → +2%, Wrath of the Sea-King +5% → +4%): two team-wide rows that compound on Water hits sit at the roster's two-row cap (×1.124 with both live, against Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.115 and Shakunetsu Sakura's ×1.075), and the capstone still pays more than its Hidden Art. The +1 Damage riders on Hull of the Leviathan and Wrath of the Sea-King are removed as filler. Maxima over every legal allocation: +2 Damage, +8% Increase Damage Taken, +10% Decrease Damage Taken.
- Wiring kept after review: an offense/defense split (Salt in the Wound parenting Exposure and the burst, Scales of the Deep → Fortress alone) was rejected because Exposure's obvious fourth becomes the burst's Hidden Art, and with Grip as an exposure setup the pair would stack the route's own tag. Under Scales of the Deep the setup cannot reach Exposure's build, whose only fourth is a +2% Decrease Damage Taken.
- Interactions: Armor's DDT row and Abyss's IDT row list all four stat types with no element, so they match effectively every hit; Dome's IDT row lists Water only. Each IDT row multiplies a matching hit by 1 + p/100 and two live rows compound (§3b); the bloodline's Water IDG passive (15 + 0.15/level) multiplies the two Water hits last. Rows are live the two rounds after the cast round, never on their own jutsu's hit.
- Delivery: all three jutsu are OTHER_USER at range 4. Armor (60 AP, cooldown 7, SINGLE) hits and gives the caster DDT 30% plus an unsupported 100 shield. Abyss (60 AP, cooldown 7, radius-1 AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN) hits and exposes enemies in the circle (friendly fire ENEMIES; never the caster). Dome (40 AP, cooldown 6, SINGLE) exposes the target. Aimed at an ally, ENEMIES rows are withheld; SELF rows still land.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01,02,03) takes Salt in the Wound (Abyss 34%, Dome 39%) or Deepwater Carapace (Armor 35%); Fortress (01,04,05) takes Salt in the Wound or Grip of the Riptide, the same +2% on both exposure rows (32%/37%); Exposure (01,06,07,08) only Scales of the Deep. Raw EP × exposure for the caster's two kit hits, Dome then Abyss then Armor: base ≈ 118, Burst ≈ 127 (40 × 1.37 + 40 × 1.32 × 1.37), Burst + Salt ≈ 130 (40 × 1.39 + 40 × 1.34 × 1.39), Exposure ≈ 129 (38 × 1.43 + 38 × 1.38 × 1.43); with Abyss and Dome in one round Abyss's hit is unexposed and base ≈ 105, Burst ≈ 112, Burst + Salt ≈ 115, Exposure ≈ 113. Burst + Salt stacks the setup's tag at +4% (×1.061, ≈ ×1.117 with 40/38), under Exposure's ×1.124 and the ×1.234 top package. Exposure leads with allies on the marked target (+8% against +4%) and on the caster's non-Water hits in Abyss's window (×1.38 against ×1.34); the burst's +2 lands on every Water hit, off-kit and outside the windows included. The no-capstone hybrid 01,02,06,07 (38/38 EP, 36%/41%) totals ≈ 126 (Dome first) or ≈ 111 (same round), under both capstone routes; Exposure dominates it (+8% against +6% Increase Damage Taken, the same +2% Decrease Damage Taken).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Water is shared with many other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 3 of 5 kit rows carry Water; Armor's DDT and Abyss's IDT rows have no element and sit on Water jutsu, so they need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver. Off-kit Water coverage is unverified.
- Exposure is the highest-leverage route: Abyss's 38% reaches effectively every hit any ally lands on each enemy in the circle, and with Dome's 43% Water hits take ×1.97 (×1.755 at base): ×1.124 from the tree, above Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.115 exposure maximum and Shakunetsu Sakura's ×1.075. It is level with the burst route plus Salt in the Wound on the caster's own rotation (≈ 129 against ≈ 130 raw EP) and stronger in group fights; no simulation was run.
- Off-kit flat Damage: +2 reaches every Water damage row the caster uses. The kit's two rows end at 40, but an off-kit 45 or 50 EP Water row would become 47 or 52 (the Arashima and Blood-Enchanted Eyes precedent levels); that catalog is unverified.
- Universal opener (accepted; narrow_kit_exception has the leaf enumeration): an Exposure player always buys Scales of the Deep's +2% Decrease Damage Taken.
- DDT breadth: Armor's reduction applies to effectively every non-pierce hit, so +10% makes it 40% for the two rounds after each cast (two in seven); pierce bypasses it. The bloodline's Earth IDT 10% passive stays. Armor's shield and Dome's absorb are unsupported and unchanged.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the Water IDG passive and any exposure on the target, so +2 raw EP is not linear +2 damage; the rotation figures in the design notes are raw EP × exposure only.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
