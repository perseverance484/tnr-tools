# Sea-King's Blessing — Dominion of the Deep

**Bloodline:** Sea-King's Blessing (BR-061, rank C, `juZy8qwituqM2Km6cOiL4`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance / Water classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Water (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Increase Damage Taken — Crushing Abyss's broad circle exposure and Water Dome's Water-only exposure (two rows, +10% route) · secondary Water Damage — Sea King's Armor and Crushing Abyss (two 38 EP formula rows, +3 route) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken — Sea King's Armor's self reduction (one row, +10% route).

Sea-King's Blessing is a rank-C kit whose five supported rows split 2/2/1: two 38 EP Water hits (Sea King's Armor, Crushing Abyss; 60 AP, cooldown 7), two enemy Increase Damage Taken rows (Abyss 30% on enemies in its circle, all four stat types and no element; Water Dome 35% Water-only on a 40 AP, cooldown-6 cast) and one 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row on Armor. Exposure is primary: one cast amplifies every matching hit the target takes for two rounds, allies' included, and the two rows compound on Water hits. Scales of the Deep answers "How do I win the exchange myself: hit harder, or take less?" (Burst, Fortress); Salt in the Wound answers "How do I make them take more from everyone?" (Exposure). Each route carries one tag; the only flat Damage is the Burst route's +3. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Water jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Absorb and shield rows are unsupported.

**Review status:** Fable proposal (2026-10-04 batch rebalance); not director-approved

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on five rows (Damage x2, Increase Damage Taken x2, Decrease Damage Taken x1), one route each: Scales of the Deep forks into Burst (Grip of the Riptide → Weight of the Trench) and Fortress (Deepwater Carapace → Hull of the Leviathan); Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King is the single exposure chain. A fourth route needs a fourth tag (absorb and shield are not potency targets). Universal node accepted: Scales of the Deep is in all 8 legal full builds, so Exposure's fourth purchase is always its +2% Decrease Damage Taken. A 1 BP leaf under Salt in the Wound would end that, but each option fails: Increase Damage Taken lifts the exposure build past +10%, Damage puts flat Damage on the exposure capstone's build, and Decrease Damage Taken is an off-axis twin of Scales of the Deep (or dominates or is dominated by it).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Scales of the Deep** — The sea-king's gift settles on the skin first: salt-hard scale over everything it guards. Armor's self Decrease Damage Taken 30 → 32% (2 rounds, all four stat types, no element). Opens Burst and Fortress.
- **Grip of the Riptide** — What the riptide takes, it takes whole. There is no half-drowning. Both Water hits, Sea King's Armor and Crushing Abyss, 38 → 39 EP; still in the Light tier.
- **Weight of the Trench** — Miles of black water press down at once, and nothing built by hands keeps its shape. Burst: Armor and Abyss 38 → 41 EP on the full route (+3 Damage, Light → Normal tier); the bloodline's Water IDG passive multiplies on top.
- **Deepwater Carapace** — Pressure that would crush a hull only sets the sea-king's armor tighter. Armor's Decrease Damage Taken 32 → 35% with Scales of the Deep (self, 2 rounds per 60 AP cast, cooldown 7).
- **Hull of the Leviathan** — No storm has ever opened the leviathan. The sea-king wears its hide and dares the storm. Fortress: Armor's reduction 30 → 40% on the full route (+10%) for the 2 rounds after each cast; pierce bypasses it.
- **Salt in the Wound** — Brine finds every cut. After the sea-king marks you, so does everything else. Abyss exposure 30 → 32% (enemies in the circle, all stat types, no element); Dome exposure 35 → 37% (Water hits only).
- **Dragged Under** — Once the current has your ankle, every blow lands on someone who cannot stand. Abyss exposure 32 → 35%, Dome 37 → 40% with Salt in the Wound (enemy debuffs, 2 rounds).
- **Wrath of the Sea-King** — The deep has a king, and the king has run out of mercy. Exposure: Abyss 30 → 40% and Dome 35 → 45% on the full route (+10%); on Water hits while both are up they compound, ×1.40 × 1.45 ≈ ×2.03.

## Design notes

- Node split: 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts. Scales of the Deep (+2% Decrease Damage Taken) is the self-side root: Grip of the Riptide (+1 Damage) → Weight of the Trench (+2 Damage) and Deepwater Carapace (+3%) → Hull of the Leviathan (+5% Decrease Damage Taken). Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King (+2/+3/+5% Increase Damage Taken) is the enemy-side chain. Any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP.
- 2026-10-04 rebalance: Burst drops from +5 to +3 Damage (38 → 41 instead of 43). Grip of the Riptide goes +2 → +1 so the Hidden Art leaves both hits in the Light tier and the Light → Normal step belongs to Weight of the Trench (+3 → +2). The +1 Damage riders on Hull of the Leviathan and Wrath of the Sea-King are removed: they were filler, so Fortress and Exposure now carry one effect each. Maximum Damage over every legal allocation: +5 → +3.
- Wiring kept after review: an offense/defense split (Salt in the Wound parenting Exposure and Burst, Scales of the Deep → Fortress alone) was rejected because Exposure's obvious fourth becomes Burst's Hidden Art. With Grip as flat Damage, Exposure gains raw Damage, matches Burst on the caster's own hits and still out-amplifies the team; with Grip as exposure setup the pair reaches +13% Increase Damage Taken. Under the self/enemy split, the exposure capstone's only fourth is a +2% Decrease Damage Taken.
- Interactions: Armor's DDT row and Abyss's IDT row list all four stat types with no element, so they match effectively every hit; Dome's IDT row lists Water only. Each IDT row multiplies a matching hit by 1 + p/100 and two live rows compound (§3b); the bloodline's Water IDG passive (15 + 0.15/level) multiplies the two Water hits last. Rows are live the two rounds after the cast round, never on their own jutsu's hit.
- Delivery: all three jutsu are OTHER_USER at range 4. Armor (60 AP, cooldown 7, SINGLE) hits and gives the caster DDT 30% plus an unsupported 100 shield. Abyss (60 AP, cooldown 7, radius-1 AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN) hits and exposes enemies in the circle (friendly fire ENEMIES; never the caster). Dome (40 AP, cooldown 6, SINGLE) exposes the target. Aimed at an ally, ENEMIES rows are withheld; SELF rows still land.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01,02,03) takes Salt in the Wound (32/37% exposure) or Deepwater Carapace (Armor 35%); Fortress (01,04,05) takes Salt in the Wound or Grip of the Riptide (39/39 EP); Exposure (01,06,07,08) only Scales of the Deep. Raw EP × exposure, for one Abyss hit then one Armor hit under both exposures: base ≈ 105, Burst + Salt ≈ 115 (41 + 41 × 1.32 × 1.37), Exposure ≈ 115 (38 + 38 × 1.40 × 1.45). Exposure wins with allies on the marked target; Burst wins on unexposed and off-kit Water hits.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Water is shared with many other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 3 of 5 kit rows carry Water; Armor's DDT and Abyss's IDT rows have no element and sit on Water jutsu, so they need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver. Off-kit Water coverage is unverified.
- Exposure is the highest-leverage route: Abyss's 40% reaches effectively every hit any ally lands on each enemy in the circle, and with Dome's 45% Water hits take ×2.03 (×1.755 at base). It ties Burst on the caster's own rotation and is stronger in group fights; no simulation was run.
- Off-kit flat Damage: +3 reaches every Water damage row the caster uses. The kit's two rows end at 41, but an off-kit 45 or 50 EP Water row would become 48 or 53; that catalog is unverified.
- Universal opener (accepted; narrow_kit_exception has the leaf enumeration): an Exposure player always buys Scales of the Deep's +2% Decrease Damage Taken.
- DDT breadth: Armor's reduction applies to effectively every non-pierce hit, so +10% makes it 40% for the two rounds after each cast (two in seven); pierce bypasses it. The bloodline's Earth IDT 10% passive stays. Armor's shield and Dome's absorb are unsupported and unchanged.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the Water IDG passive and any exposure on the target, so +3 raw EP is not linear +3 damage; the rotation figures in the design notes are raw EP × exposure only.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
