# Sea-King's Blessing — Dominion of the Deep

**Bloodline:** Sea-King's Blessing (BR-061, rank C, `juZy8qwituqM2Km6cOiL4`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance (roster pass) / Water classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Water (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Increase Damage Taken — Crushing Abyss's broad circle exposure and Water Dome's Water-only exposure (two rows, +8% route) · secondary Water Damage — Sea King's Armor and Crushing Abyss (two 38 EP formula rows, +2 route) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken — Sea King's Armor's self reduction (one row, +10% route).

Sea-King's Blessing is a rank-C kit whose five supported rows split 2/2/1: two 38 EP Water hits (Sea King's Armor, Crushing Abyss; 60 AP, cooldown 7), two enemy Increase Damage Taken rows (Abyss 30% on enemies in its circle, all four stat types and no element; Water Dome 35% Water-only on a 40 AP, cooldown-6 cast) and one 30% self Decrease Damage Taken row on Armor. Exposure is primary: one cast amplifies every matching hit the target takes for two rounds, allies' included, and the two rows compound on Water hits, so the route stops at +8%. Scales of the Deep answers "How do I win the exchange myself: hit harder, or take less?" (Burst, Fortress); Salt in the Wound answers "How do I make them take more from everyone?" (Exposure). Each Advanced Art carries one tag; the only flat Damage is the Burst route's +2 (Grip of the Riptide +1, Weight of the Trench +1), which takes both hits from Light to Normal (38 → 40). Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Water jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Absorb and shield rows are unsupported.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four, as in the pre-batch tree. Universal node accepted: Scales of the Deep is in all 8 legal full builds, so Exposure's fourth purchase is always its +2% Decrease Damage Taken. A 1 BP leaf under Salt in the Wound would end that, but each option fails: Increase Damage Taken twins Dragged Under and hands Exposure's fourth its own tag again (back toward the +10% the route was cut from), Damage puts flat Damage on the exposure capstone's build, and Decrease Damage Taken is an off-axis twin of Scales of the Deep (or dominates or is dominated by it).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. - **Grip of the Riptide** — What the riptide takes, it takes whole. There is no half-drowning. Both Water hits, Sea King's Armor and Crushing Abyss, 38 → 39 EP; still in the Light tier.
- **Weight of the Trench** — Miles of black water press down at once, and nothing built by hands keeps its shape. Burst: Armor and Abyss 38 → 40 EP on the full route (+2 Damage), Light → Normal; the bloodline's Water IDG passive multiplies on top.
- **Deepwater Carapace** — Pressure that would crush a hull only sets the sea-king's armor tighter. Armor's Decrease Damage Taken 32 → 35% with Scales of the Deep (self, 2 rounds per 60 AP cast, cooldown 7).
- **Hull of the Leviathan** — No storm has ever opened the leviathan. The sea-king wears its hide and dares the storm. Fortress: Armor's reduction 30 → 40% on the full route (+10%) for the 2 rounds after each cast; pierce bypasses it.
- **Salt in the Wound** — Brine finds every cut. After the sea-king marks you, so does everything else. Abyss exposure 30 → 32% (enemies in the circle, all stat types, no element); Dome exposure 35 → 37% (Water hits only).
- **Dragged Under** — Once the current has your ankle, every blow lands on someone who cannot stand. Abyss exposure 32 → 34%, Dome 37 → 39% with Salt in the Wound (enemy debuffs, 2 rounds).
- **Wrath of the Sea-King** — The deep has a king, and the king has run out of mercy. Exposure: Abyss 30 → 38% and Dome 35 → 43% on the full route (+8%); a Water hit under both takes ×1.38 × 1.43 ≈ ×1.97 (×1.755 at base).

## Design notes

- Node split: 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts. Scales of the Deep (+2% Decrease Damage Taken) is the self-side root: Grip of the Riptide (+1 Damage) → Weight of the Trench (+1 Damage) and Deepwater Carapace (+3%) → Hull of the Leviathan (+5% Decrease Damage Taken). Salt in the Wound → Dragged Under → Wrath of the Sea-King (+2%/+2%/+4% Increase Damage Taken) is the enemy-side chain. Any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP.
- 2026-10-04 rebalance: Burst goes from +5 to +2 Damage (38 → 40, Light → Normal, instead of 43), the roster default for kits whose only hits are 38 EP Light rows; +3 (38 → 41) was the first-pass value. Grip of the Riptide keeps a +1 commit because the kit's only other tags each own a Hidden Art on the same rows: a percentage setup there would twin Dragged Under or Deepwater Carapace. The +1 leaves both hits Light (39) and Weight of the Trench's +1 makes the Normal step. Exposure goes +10% → +8% (Dragged Under +3% → +2%, Wrath of the Sea-King +5% → +4%): two team-wide rows that compound on Water hits sit at the roster's two-row cap, and the capstone still pays more than its Hidden Art. The +1 Damage riders on Hull of the Leviathan and Wrath of the Sea-King are removed as filler. Maxima over every legal allocation: +2 Damage, +8% Increase Damage Taken, +10% Decrease Damage Taken.
- Wiring kept after review: an offense/defense split (Salt in the Wound parenting Exposure and Burst, Scales of the Deep → Fortress alone) was rejected because Exposure's obvious fourth becomes Burst's Hidden Art. With Grip as flat Damage, Exposure gains raw Damage and still out-amplifies the team; with Grip as an exposure setup the pair stacks the route's own tag. Under the self/enemy split, the exposure capstone's only fourth is a +2% Decrease Damage Taken.
- Interactions: Armor's DDT row and Abyss's IDT row list all four stat types with no element, so they match effectively every hit; Dome's IDT row lists Water only. Each IDT row multiplies a matching hit by 1 + p/100 and two live rows compound (§3b); the bloodline's Water IDG passive (15 + 0.15/level) multiplies the two Water hits last. Rows are live the two rounds after the cast round, never on their own jutsu's hit.
- Delivery: all three jutsu are OTHER_USER at range 4. Armor (60 AP, cooldown 7, SINGLE) hits and gives the caster DDT 30% plus an unsupported 100 shield. Abyss (60 AP, cooldown 7, radius-1 AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN) hits and exposes enemies in the circle (friendly fire ENEMIES; never the caster). Dome (40 AP, cooldown 6, SINGLE) exposes the target. Aimed at an ally, ENEMIES rows are withheld; SELF rows still land.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01,02,03) takes Salt in the Wound (32%/37% exposure) or Deepwater Carapace (Armor 35%); Fortress (01,04,05) takes Salt in the Wound or Grip of the Riptide (39/39 EP); Exposure (01,06,07,08) only Scales of the Deep. Raw EP × exposure for the caster's two kit hits, Dome then Abyss then Armor: base ≈ 118, Burst + Salt ≈ 127 (40 × 1.37 + 40 × 1.32 × 1.37), Exposure ≈ 129 (38 × 1.43 + 38 × 1.38 × 1.43); with Abyss and Dome in one round Abyss's hit is unexposed and base ≈ 105, Burst + Salt ≈ 112, Exposure ≈ 113. Exposure leads with allies on the marked target and on the caster's non-Water hits in Abyss's window (×1.38 against ×1.32); Burst's +2 lands on every Water hit, off-kit and outside the windows included. The no-capstone hybrid 01,02,06,07 (39/39 EP, 34%/39%) totals ≈ 127 (Dome first) or ≈ 112 (same round): it trades Burst's second Damage point for +2% more exposure, a margin choice rather than a dominant one.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Water is shared with many other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 3 of 5 kit rows carry Water; Armor's DDT and Abyss's IDT rows have no element and sit on Water jutsu, so they need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver. Off-kit Water coverage is unverified.
- Exposure is the highest-leverage route: Abyss's 38% reaches effectively every hit any ally lands on each enemy in the circle, and with Dome's 43% Water hits take ×1.97 (×1.755 at base, about ×1.12 from the tree). It is level with or slightly ahead of Burst on the caster's own rotation and stronger in group fights; no simulation was run.
- Off-kit flat Damage: +2 reaches every Water damage row the caster uses. The kit's two rows end at 40, but an off-kit 45 or 50 EP Water row would become 47 or 52 (the Arashima and Blood-Enchanted Eyes precedent levels); that catalog is unverified.
- Universal opener (accepted; narrow_kit_exception has the leaf enumeration): an Exposure player always buys Scales of the Deep's +2% Decrease Damage Taken.
- DDT breadth: Armor's reduction applies to effectively every non-pierce hit, so +10% makes it 40% for the two rounds after each cast (two in seven); pierce bypasses it. The bloodline's Earth IDT 10% passive stays. Armor's shield and Dome's absorb are unsupported and unchanged.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the Water IDG passive and any exposure on the target, so +2 raw EP is not linear +2 damage; the rotation figures in the design notes are raw EP × exposure only.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Water jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
