# Ha Yanagi — Lament of the Willow

**Bloodline:** Ha Yanagi (BR-030, rank D, `uNZ2UMfA3BuX-J_1g0fHU`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Ha Yanagi classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Ha Yanagi (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Enemy debuff control: Decrease Damage Given (Wailing Bark, Petal Nightmare) and Increase Damage Taken (Blighted Tree) · secondary Raw power on the two damage casts (Blighted Tree formula 40, Petal Nightmare static 40) · tertiary Self amplification: the single Increase Damage Given row on Wailing Bark.

Three of the kit's six supported rows are enemy debuffs: Decrease Damage Given on Wailing Bark (35%) and Petal Nightmare (30%), both two rounds and stackable on one target, and Increase Damage Taken on Blighted Tree (30%), so suppression and exposure are the declared primary and hold the heaviest four-purchase builds by row weight (19-20). Damage reaches two rows (40 power each at jutsu level 25) and is the secondary. Increase Damage Given is one self row at 35% whose four-stat, element-less filter matches every hit the caster lands for two rounds; it is tertiary by coverage breadth, not by flat size, and carries the deepest single-row ladder (+10). Every percentage row in the kit lists all four stat types with no element, so each node's downstream reach is every damage effect, not Genjutsu only.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Ha Yanagi jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Mourning Boughs** — The willow bows under its grief, and every arm raised against it grows heavy. Wailing Bark (35→37%) and Petal Nightmare (30→32%) Decrease Damage Given; enemy, 2 rounds, all four stat types; stacks on one target.
- **Veil of Falling Petals** — Petals drift between the foe and its purpose until the blow forgets its aim. Both suppression rows again (39/34% with Mourning Boughs); a 40 AP and a 60 AP single-target cast, cooldown 6 each.
- **Grave Willow's Hush** — Beneath the oldest willow nothing strikes true; the grave keeps its quiet. Both suppression rows; route total +8 (Wailing Bark 43%, Petal Nightmare 38%; 81% when both debuffs overlap on one target).
- **Blight Takes Root** — Rot finds the soft heart of a tree, and of a foe, and settles in to stay. Blighted Tree enemy exposure only (30→33%, 2 rounds per 60 AP cast, cooldown 7); every non-pierce hit the target takes, any attacker.
- **Hollow at the Heart** — What the blight has hollowed, any passing wind can break. Blighted Tree exposure route total +8 (38%), plus +2 on both suppression rows (39/34% with Mourning Boughs). Three enemy rows.
- **Whispering Canopy** — The leaves speak in a thousand voices, and all of them say strike. Wailing Bark self damage buff (35→38%, 2 rounds per 40 AP cast, cooldown 6); all four stat types, so every non-pierce hit you land.
- **Hardened Heartwood** — Seasons of grief turn the willow's soft wood into something that bruises. Blighted Tree (formula) and Petal Nightmare (static) Damage, 40→42 power each; two 60 AP single-target casts at range 4.
- **Nightmare in Bloom** — The dream opens all at once; petals, bark and terror fall together. Both Damage rows (route total 45 power each) and the Wailing Bark self buff (+2: 40% with Whispering Canopy).
- **Rising Sap** — Spring climbs the trunk, and the willow remembers how to be fierce. Wailing Bark self damage buff only (38→41% with Whispering Canopy); one row, 2 rounds, every hit you land inside the window.
- **Thousand-Leaf Dream** — Every leaf a dream, every dream a wound; the canopy closes overhead. Same self buff; route total +10 (35→45%, 2 rounds per 40 AP cast, cooldown 6). No other row touched.

## Design notes

- Node split: the two roots mirror engine sides. Mourning Boughs (enemy) opens both suppression rows and forks into Veil of Falling Petals → Grave Willow's Hush (suppression) and Blight Takes Root → Hollow at the Heart (exposure). Whispering Canopy (self) opens the Wailing Bark buff and forks into Hardened Heartwood → Nightmare in Bloom (raw power) and Rising Sap → Thousand-Leaf Dream (amplifier). 2/4/4 nodes; each Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5-6 BP; each route owns one tag.
- Flats scale with coverage. Decrease Damage Given reaches two enemy rows that stack on one target (35% + 30%), so it takes +2/+2/+4 (max +8: 43/38%, 81% stacked) instead of a single-row +2/+3/+5. Increase Damage Taken is one enemy row at 30%: +3/+5 (max +8, 38%) with a +2 suppression secondary that equals, not out-bids, the sibling Hidden Art. Damage reaches two 40-power rows: +2/+3 (max +5, 45 each). Increase Damage Given is one self row at 35%: the single-row ladder +3/+3/+4 (max +10, 45%).
- Main tree, passives, pierce: every percentage row lists all four stat types and no element; getEfficiencyRatio returns 1 on any stat overlap, so each matches every damage effect of any element. The enhanced Wailing Bark buff multiplies normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks in its window; suppression cuts every non-pierce hit the target makes; exposure amplifies allies' hits. The +15% bloodline IDG passive multiplies on top. Pierce runs after the modifier pass; ranked modes suppress skills.
- Delivery and uptime: Wailing Bark is a 40 AP single-target cast on cooldown 6 at range 5 carrying a suppression row and the self buff, so both roots touch it; Blighted Tree is 60 AP on cooldown 7 at range 4 (formula damage plus exposure); Petal Nightmare is 60 AP on cooldown 6 at range 4 (static damage plus suppression). Percentage rows last two rounds. Landing both suppression debuffs on one target in one turn costs 100 AP, so 81% stacked is a ceiling, not a rotation. Nothing was simulated.
- Fourth purchases and dominance: Suppression adds Whispering Canopy (38% buff) or Blight Takes Root (33%); Exposure adds Veil of Falling Petals (41/36%) or Whispering Canopy; Burst adds Rising Sap (43% buff) or Mourning Boughs (37/32%); Amplifier adds Mourning Boughs or Hardened Heartwood. 13 of 14 full builds are non-dominated and every node appears in one; the dominated 01+02+04+06 (suppression +4, exposure +3, buff +3) loses to 01+04+05+06 on exposure at equal suppression and buff.
- Tuning surfaced, not settled: by row weight (flat × rows) full builds run from 14 (Amplifier) to 20 (Exposure), Suppression/Burst 18-19; the Taiyo Kami reference is 31 on 9 rows (3.4 per row) against 3.3 here. Row weight understates the Amplifier route (its one row multiplies every hit) and overstates suppression (both rows counted even when one debuff is up). Options: lift Thousand-Leaf Dream to +4 buff / +1 Damage, or trim Hollow at the Heart's +2 suppression secondary. Balance is user-owned.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: no row carries an element, so the proposed 'Ha Yanagi' label is a bloodline-keyed extension of the whole-kit classification change. Under the current resolver all 6 supported rows fall back to None, which also reaches every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts; the normal-jutsu collision is unverified. The tree is inert until that extension exists.
- Suppression stacking: Wailing Bark and Petal Nightmare each apply a separate two-round Decrease Damage Given debuff and BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is on at the pin, so one target already carries 65% base suppression when both land and 81% at the route maximum (getPower caps each row at 100, not the sum). The flats were held to +2/+2/+4 for this reason. Realised value was not simulated.
- Leakage: every percentage row has all four stat types and no element, so the enhanced Wailing Bark self buff multiplies normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks for two rounds; enemy-side suppression alters damage from every source the target controls; enemy-side exposure amplifies hits from allies and weapons. Restricted casting scope does not restrict downstream benefit.
- Pierce: the kit has no pierce rows and no node touches pierce. At the pin pierce is applied after the damage-modifier pass (process.ts 416-430, 563-577), so the Suppression and Exposure routes do not reduce or amplify enemy pierce hits and the self buff does not raise any pierce the player lands.
- Single-window concentration: the Amplifier route's whole value is one 35→45% self row that lasts two rounds per 40 AP Wailing Bark cast on cooldown 6, and the Exposure route's primary value is one 30→38% enemy row that lasts two rounds per 60 AP Blighted Tree cast on cooldown 7. Both routes depend on casting into those windows; the Burst and Suppression routes spread across two rows.
- Recipient modelling: the dossier's 'enemy' recipient for OTHER_USER rows is a modelling assumption of bloodright_lib.recipient_for, not an engine guarantee. Both Decrease Damage Given rows carry friendly fire ALL, so targeting an ally's tile would suppress that ally at the enhanced 37-43%; every Ha Yanagi cast is SINGLE method, so this is player-controlled; the dossier marks no ally-hazard rows.
- Standing self exposure: the bloodline's permanent +5% Increase Damage Taken passive (Taijutsu/Bukijutsu filter, no element, so it also matches every element-less hit) is a passive, not a jutsu row; no node raises or removes it. The Suppression route offsets it on debuffed targets only. The dossier marks no adverse supported rows, so no node makes any row worse for the caster.
- Rank and scope: Ha Yanagi is a D-rank bloodline; this tree equalises marginal opportunity, not final strength; cross-bloodline comparison is deferred to the roster review. Blighted Tree's Damage row is formula-calculated, so +5 raw power lands differently from +5 on Petal Nightmare's static row. Normal-tree potency policy is unapproved; no combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Ha Yanagi jutsu inherit Ha Yanagi potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
