# Ancient Tailed Demon — Rites of the Sealed

**Bloodline:** Ancient Tailed Demon (BR-004, rank B, `Au5rBnnukEqUv_lRrk1yO`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Ancient Tailed Demon classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Ancient Tailed Demon (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Enemy pressure — Afterburn and Increase Damage Taken on Demonic Embrace · secondary Sustained damage — Increase Damage Given on Demonic Vitae; burst — Damage on Ancient Demon Roar · tertiary Defense — Decrease Damage Taken on Demonic Vitae.

The kit is three casts with one supported row per tag: Demonic Embrace carries the two enemy debuffs (Afterburn 35%, Increase Damage Taken 35%, 2 rounds, 40 AP), Demonic Vitae the two self buffs (Increase Damage Given 35% for 3 rounds, Decrease Damage Taken 25% for 2 rounds, 40 AP) and Ancient Demon Roar the only damage (45 power area hit, 60 AP). The Sustained Damage trait and the bloodline's own damage-given passive make pressure and empowerment the natural emphasis; Roar burst is a real but single-cast alternative; mitigation is tertiary because its row is the weakest (25%) and shortest (2 rounds) in the kit.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Ancient Tailed Demon jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Demon's Grasp** — A claw closes around the prey, and what it touches begins to smoulder. Demonic Embrace only: Afterburn 35→37% and exposure 35→37% on one target for 2 rounds. 40 AP, range 4, CD 7. No gates.
- **Rending Bellow** — The roar that cracked the old mountains has not grown quieter in its cage. Ancient Demon Roar damage only: 45→48 power at jutsu level 25 (one row, area spawn, 60 AP, range 4, CD 7). Wound row unchanged.
- **Howl of Annihilation** — One breath, and the field remembers why the ancients sealed it away. Roar 48→51 power (the +6 single-row guardrail); Embrace exposure +2 (39% with Demon's Grasp), so a Roar cast a round later lands harder.
- **Festering Brand** — Flesh the demon marks does not stop burning when the claw lets go. Demonic Embrace Afterburn only: 37→40% with Demon's Grasp; adds to every non-pierce hit the target takes for 2 rounds.
- **Consuming Malice** — Hatred older than the villages, poured into a single victim. Demonic Embrace: Afterburn 40→45% and exposure 37→40% on the route (both rows, one target, 2 rounds). Below the 60% per-hit cap.
- **Demon's Marrow** — The blood runs black and hot, and the host grows harder to kill. Demonic Vitae only (self, 40 AP, CD 7): damage given 35→37% for 3 rounds, damage taken 25→27% for 2; neither touches pierce hits.
- **Scarred Hide** — A thousand years of wounds, and every one of them closed over. Demonic Vitae Decrease Damage Taken only: 27→30% for 2 rounds (non-pierce hits of any stat type). One self cast per 7 rounds.
- **Unyielding Husk** — What the host cannot dodge, the demon simply refuses to feel. Demonic Vitae: damage taken 30→35% (the +10 route) and damage given +2 (39% with Demon's Marrow). Pierce still passes through.
- **Boiling Ichor** — Let a little more of it loose, and every strike carries the heat. Demonic Vitae Increase Damage Given only: 37→40% for 3 rounds, on all your non-pierce damage (jutsu, weapons, basics) while it lasts.
- **Primordial Rampage** — The seal holds, barely. Everything within reach learns what that costs. Demonic Vitae: damage given 40→45% (the +10 route, 3 rounds) and damage taken +2 (29% with Demon's Marrow). One cast per 7 rounds.

## Design notes

- Node split: the two roots follow the two support casts. Demon's Grasp raises both Demonic Embrace rows and forks into Rending Bellow → Howl of Annihilation (Roar damage) and Festering Brand → Consuming Malice (Afterburn). Demon's Marrow raises both Demonic Vitae rows and forks into Scarred Hide → Unyielding Husk (DDT) and Boiling Ichor → Primordial Rampage (IDG). 2/4/4; every capstone is 3 BP deep; two capstones cost 5 (shared root) or 6 BP. No node is in every legal 4-BP build.
- Reference reuse: topology and most flats follow Taiyo Kami nearly verbatim (Foundations +2/+2, Festering Brand and Scarred Hide = Emberwake and Golden Mantle +3, Husk close to Sovereign Sun). Departures: each Foundation carries one support cast's row pair; Rending Bellow is +3, not +2 (Damage reaches one row, not three); Malice is +5 AB/+3 IDT, not +7/+3/+3 (Grasp already adds AB +2; Embrace has no IDG); the DDG route becomes IDG (no DDG row). The shape fits two support casts plus one strike.
- Flats: every tag reaches one ungated row and the four routes sit on four tags, so route maxima sit at the single-row guardrails: Damage +6 (45→51 on one 60 AP area hit), Afterburn, DDT and IDG +10. Vitae capstones (08, 10) carry +5 primary and a +2 secondary below the sibling Hidden Art's +3, so hybrids like 01,06,07,09 stay non-dominated. IDT shares Embrace with Afterburn and adds to the same hits, so it has no route: Foundation +2 plus Howl +2 or Malice +3, max +5. Row weight 19 (Taiyo 31).
- Interaction with the kit: every row is element-less and lists all four stat types (Vitae IDG also Highest), so each already matches every non-pierce hit (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3: stat filters on element-less rows are not binding); the nodes raise percentages, not reach. Kit rows add power/100 × staged base (§3b); only the bloodline IDG passive (20% + 0.15/lvl) multiplies, on top. Its 5% IDT passive is a standing self-weakness the Vitae routes partly offset (DDT +2 to +10).
- Delivery and timing: all three jutsu have cooldown 7. Vitae (SELF, 40 AP) gives 3 rounds of IDG but only 2 of DDT; Embrace (one target, range 4, 40 AP) 2 rounds. Rows act only in the rounds after their cast round (§3b), so Vitae and Embrace (80 AP together) go a round before Roar to touch it. Roar (AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN, range 4, 60 AP) is the only damage: damage row friendly fire ENEMIES, unsupported Wound row friendly fire none (ally hazard, no node touches it).
- Fourth purchase: normally the other Foundation (the four examples) or a sibling Hidden Art (01,02,03,04 Afterburn 40%; 01,02,04,05 Roar 48; 06,07,08,09 and 06,07,09,10 all-Vitae). All 14 legal full builds are non-dominated (arithmetic only). Value options (user-owned, unchanged): IDG +10 and Damage +6 sit at the guardrails, fallbacks +8 (Ichor 3/Rampage 3) and +5 (Bellow 2/Howl 3); Howl IDT +3 (build IDT +5, within the ceiling) would lift the Roar burst build.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: no element isolates this kit (every row is element-less). The tree requires the bloodline-keyed classification extension; under the current resolver a modifier would need affectedElements including None, which reaches every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts. Whether NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT jutsu could collide is unverified (no non-bloodline catalog in the repository).
- Afterburn at 45% on the pressure route: in the 2 rounds after the cast it adds floor(hit × 45%) to every non-pierce hit the target takes from any source; Afterburn sources add, capped at 60% of each hit, so it saturates when another source (main tree, items, allies) is also on the target. Value scales with hits landed in the window, not with the one application row; not simulated.
- Leakage: Demonic Embrace's IDT debuff adds to every non-pierce hit the target takes from the caster and allies, normal jutsu, weapons and basics included; Demonic Vitae's IDG self buff raises every non-pierce hit the caster alone lands. Both rows are element-less: potency widens the number, not the scope. Same-tag effects from allies, the main tree or recasts apply and add (process.ts 1109–1117).
- Pierce: IDG, IDT and DDT do not modify pierce hits and Afterburn skips them, so the whole tree is blind to pierce damage. Only Rending Bellow / Howl of Annihilation (raw Roar power) is unaffected by that.
- Weakest route (user-owned value call): the Roar burst example (01,02,03,06) is the likely weakest of the four: route row weight 12 against 14–15, full build 16 against 18–19, and its primary lands on one 60 AP cast per 7 rounds while the other routes ride 2–3-round windows over every non-pierce hit. The reversible lift (Howl IDT +3) is in design notes; values are unchanged.
- Ally hazard: Ancient Demon Roar's Wound row (35%, friendly fire none) lands on allies inside the circle; the caster is never a target of an OTHER_USER area jutsu (§4b). No node touches it, but the Roar route invites more Roar casts. No supported row is an area hazard; Embrace aimed at an ally (OTHER_USER permits it, §3b) would land both raised debuffs on that ally.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin (combat.ts 3053–3078), so no node does anything there.
- No combat simulation. Non-dominance is arithmetic on per-tag flats; main-tree effects, equipment, AP economy, actual hit counts in the Embrace window and the bloodline's 5% damage-taken passive were not modelled. Percentage caps are not approached (highest value 45%).

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Ancient Tailed Demon jutsu inherit Ancient Tailed Demon potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
