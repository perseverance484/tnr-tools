# Ancient Tailed Demon — Rites of the Sealed

**Bloodline:** Ancient Tailed Demon (BR-004, rank B, `Au5rBnnukEqUv_lRrk1yO`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Ancient Tailed Demon classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Ancient Tailed Demon (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Enemy pressure — Afterburn and Increase Damage Taken on Demonic Embrace · secondary Sustained damage — Increase Damage Given on Demonic Vitae; burst — Damage on Ancient Demon Roar · tertiary Defense — Decrease Damage Taken on Demonic Vitae.

The kit is three casts with one supported row per tag: Demonic Embrace carries the two enemy debuffs (Afterburn 35%, Increase Damage Taken 35%, 2 rounds, 40 AP), Demonic Vitae the two self buffs (Increase Damage Given 35% for 3 rounds, Decrease Damage Taken 25% for 2 rounds, 40 AP) and Ancient Demon Roar the only damage (45 power area hit, 60 AP). The Sustained Damage trait and the bloodline's own damage-given passive make pressure and empowerment the natural emphasis; Roar burst is a real but single-cast alternative; mitigation is tertiary because its row is the weakest (25%) and shortest (2 rounds) in the kit.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Ancient Tailed Demon jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Node split: the two roots follow the two support casts. Demon's Grasp (enemy side) raises both Demonic Embrace rows and forks into Rending Bellow → Howl of Annihilation (Roar damage) and Festering Brand → Consuming Malice (Afterburn pressure). Demon's Marrow (self side) raises both Demonic Vitae rows and forks into Scarred Hide → Unyielding Husk (mitigation) and Boiling Ichor → Primordial Rampage (damage given). 2/4/4; every capstone is 3 BP deep; any two capstones cost 5 (shared root) or 6 BP.
- Ten nodes on five rows is sustainable because every supported tag is a single, ungated, always-castable row and the four routes sit on four different tags (Damage, Afterburn, DDT, IDG); no route duplicates another and none breaches a guardrail. IDT is the exception: it shares Demonic Embrace with Afterburn and amplifies the same hits, so a separate IDT route would be a second pressure route. It is held to the Foundation (+2) plus capstone secondaries (+2 Howl, +3 Malice), maximum +5 in a build.
- Flats scale by coverage: every tag reaches one row, so route maxima sit at the single-row guardrails: Damage +6 (45→51 on one 60 AP area hit), Afterburn, DDT and IDG +10. Hidden Arts are +3. Vitae capstones (08, 10) carry +5 primary and a +2 secondary below the sibling Hidden Art's +3, so hybrids like 01,06,07,09 stay non-dominated. Embrace capstones: Howl +3 Damage/+2 IDT, Malice +5 Afterburn/+3 IDT; no Hidden Art sits on IDT, held to +5 per build. Row weight: 19 (Taiyo 31).
- Interaction with the kit: the Vitae IDG row lists all four stat types plus Highest, and the DDT, Afterburn and IDT rows all four stat types, with no element, so each already matches every non-pierce hit of any stat type (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3); the nodes raise percentages, not reach. The bloodline's own IDG passive (20% + 0.15/lvl, bloodline-sourced, multiplicative) then scales the enhanced Roar; its 5% IDT passive is a standing self-weakness that the Vitae routes partly offset (DDT +2 to +10).
- Delivery and uptime: all three jutsu have cooldown 7. Demonic Vitae (SELF, 40 AP) gives 3 rounds of IDG but only 2 of DDT, so the mitigation routes buy a shorter window than the damage routes. Demonic Embrace (single target, range 4, 40 AP) lasts 2 rounds on one enemy. Ancient Demon Roar (AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN, range 4, 60 AP) is the kit's only damage; its damage row has friendly fire ENEMIES, its unsupported Wound row has friendly fire none (ally hazard, unchanged by any node).
- Fourth-purchase choices: every capstone route is 3 BP, so the fourth purchase is normally the other Foundation (the four examples) or a sibling Hidden Art (01,02,03,04 adds Afterburn 40%; 01,04,05,02 adds Roar 48; 06,07,08,09 and 06,07,09,10 are all-Vitae with no Embrace gain). Fourteen legal full builds exist; the tooling checks their non-dominance arithmetically only.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: no element isolates this kit (every row is element-less). The tree requires the bloodline-keyed classification extension; under the current resolver a modifier would need affectedElements including None, which reaches every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts. Whether NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT jutsu could collide is unverified (no non-bloodline catalog in the repository).
- Afterburn at 45% on the pressure route: it adds floor(hit × 45%) to every non-pierce hit the target takes for 2 rounds from any source, capped cumulatively at 60% of each hit, so it saturates when another Afterburn source (main tree, items, allies) is also on the target. Downstream value was not simulated; it scales with how many hits land in the window, not with the one application row.
- Broad exposure and leakage: Demonic Embrace's IDT debuff amplifies every non-pierce hit the target takes from the caster and allies, normal jutsu, weapons and basics included; Demonic Vitae's IDG self buff raises every non-pierce hit the caster alone lands, normal hits included. Both rows are element-less: potency widens the number, not the scope. BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is on, so they stack.
- Pierce: IDG, IDT and DDT do not modify pierce hits and Afterburn skips them, so the whole tree is blind to pierce damage. Only Rending Bellow / Howl of Annihilation (raw Roar power) is unaffected by that.
- Value decisions (user-owned): IDG +10 on the 3-round self buff (Ichor +3, Rampage +5) and Damage +6 on one area hit sit at the guardrails; +8 (3/3) and +5 (2/3) are the fallbacks. The Roar burst example (01,02,03) is the likely weakest of the four: row weight 12 vs 14-15, one 60 AP cast per 7 rounds against multi-hit windows. Howl IDT +3 (build IDT +5, within the ceiling) is the reversible lift.
- Ally hazard: Ancient Demon Roar's Wound row (35%, friendly fire none) lands on allies inside the circle; the caster is never a target of an OTHER_USER area jutsu. It is unsupported and no node touches it, but the Roar route encourages casting Roar more, so the hazard is used more often. No supported row in the kit is an ally hazard.
- Ranked modes: skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin (combat.ts 3053–3078), so no node does anything there.
- No combat simulation. Non-dominance is arithmetic on per-tag flats; main-tree effects, equipment, AP economy, actual hit counts in the Embrace window and the bloodline's 5% damage-taken passive were not modelled. Percentage caps are not approached (highest value 45%).

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Ancient Tailed Demon jutsu inherit Ancient Tailed Demon potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
