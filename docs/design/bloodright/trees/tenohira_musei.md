# Tenohira Musei — The Voiceless Hand

**Bloodline:** Tenohira Musei (BR-078, rank A, `18Byy1tMXkQETmB88JLl5`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Yin-Yang classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Yin-Yang (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Yin-Yang offense (Damage on three casts; self Increase Damage Given on Silent Rift and Yin-Yang Cascade) · secondary Enemy exposure (Increase Damage Taken on Moonlit Inferno and Yin-Yang Cascade) · tertiary Suppression (Decrease Damage Given on Moonlit Inferno).

Eight supported rows sit on four casts, and five are offensive: three 40 EP Yin-Yang Damage rows (Silent Rift's ground circle, Yin-Yang Cascade, Silent Palm Strike) and two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows (Rift: Highest-filtered, no element; Cascade: Fire/Lightning/Yin-Yang/None) that match the kit's own hits and stack on the caster. The Defensive and Control traits live in unsupported rows (seal on Silent Palm Strike, buffprevent/debuffprevent on Eternal Stillness, move on Silent Rift), so potency reaches control only through Moonlit Inferno's two 35% enemy debuffs (Decrease Damage Given and Increase Damage Taken, all four stat types, 40 AP) and Cascade's 35% exposure. Root Yang therefore carries the offense routes and root Yin the enemy-debuff routes. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Yin-Yang jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Yin-Yang jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Waxing Crescent** — The hand opens as the moon fills; what it gathers, it does not yet spend. Both self damage buffs 35 → 38% for 2 rounds: Silent Rift (Highest or element-less hits) and Yin-Yang Cascade (Fire/Lightning/Yin-Yang or element-less hits).
- **Unspoken Blow** — No cry, no warning. The palm has already landed. Silent Rift (ground circle), Yin-Yang Cascade and Silent Palm Strike Yin-Yang Damage 40 → 42 EP; 60 AP each, cooldown 7.
- **Deafening Silence** — The quietest strike leaves the loudest ruin. Same three Damage rows: 40 → 45 EP on the full route (+5 Damage). Seal on Silent Palm Strike and move on Silent Rift are unsupported and unchanged.
- **Held Breath** — Between one breath and the next, the rift widens. Both self damage buffs 40% with Waxing Crescent; 2 rounds per 60 AP cast, and both can be live on the caster at once.
- **Tipping the Balance** — Yin drains from the enemy; yang pools in the open hand. Amplifier route total +10%: both self damage buffs 35 → 45%; plus Moonlit Inferno suppression 35 → 37% (39% with Waning Crescent).
- **Waning Crescent** — Under the thinning moon the enemy's fire gutters and their guard slips. Moonlit Inferno exposure and suppression 35 → 37% (all four stat types, 40 AP); Yin-Yang Cascade exposure 35 → 37% (Highest or element-less hits).
- **Hushed Inferno** — A blaze can be silenced like any other voice. Moonlit Inferno Decrease Damage Given only: 40% with Waning Crescent; one row, 2 rounds per 40 AP cast, cooldown 7.
- **Eclipse in Hand** — Close the fingers and the enemy's light goes out; keep what spills. Suppression route total +10%: Moonlit Inferno 35 → 45%; plus both self damage buffs +2% (40% with Waxing Crescent).
- **Bared to the Moon** — Stillness strips the guard away and leaves them bare under the moon. Both enemy exposure rows 40% with Waning Crescent: Moonlit Inferno (all four stat types) and Yin-Yang Cascade (Highest or element-less); both can sit on one target.
- **Stillness Breaks** — The silence ends all at once, and the stillness falls on them. Exposure route total +10%: Moonlit Inferno and Yin-Yang Cascade 35 → 45%; plus Damage 40 → 42 EP on Silent Rift, Cascade and Silent Palm Strike.

## Design notes

- Node split: root Yang (Waxing Crescent, +3% self Increase Damage Given on Rift and Cascade) forks into a Damage route (Unspoken Blow → Deafening Silence) and a self-buff route (Held Breath → Tipping the Balance). Root Yin (Waning Crescent, +2% exposure, +2% suppression) forks into a suppression route (Hushed Inferno → Eclipse in Hand) and an exposure route (Bared to the Moon → Stillness Breaks). 2/4/4 nodes; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP against the 4 BP cap.
- Routes: Burst +5 Damage (Unspoken Blow +2, Deafening Silence +3); Amplifier +10% Increase Damage Given (Waxing Crescent +3%, Held Breath +2%, Tipping the Balance +5%); Suppression +10% Decrease Damage Given (Waning Crescent +2%, Hushed Inferno +3%, Eclipse in Hand +5%); Exposure +10% Increase Damage Taken (Waning Crescent +2%, Bared to the Moon +3%, Stillness Breaks +5%). Waxing Crescent stays a single-tag +3% Foundation (root Yang's only broad tag), so the Amplifier steps are 3/2/5. Capstone secondaries cross roots (+2% suppression, +2% self buff, +2 Damage); maxima over all legal allocations: Damage +5, every percentage tag +10%.
- Filters (§3): both self IDG rows are element-less or list None, so they match the kit's Yin-Yang hits, basic attacks and element-less normal jutsu; Moonlit Inferno's debuffs list all four stat types and no element, so they match every non-pierce source. Same-tag rows from different casts all apply (process.ts 1109–1117); the 25% + 0.15/level Increase Damage Given passive (fromType bloodline) is applied separately. IDG/IDT/DDG never touch pierce; no node touches seal, prevention or move.
- Delivery and uptime: all casts cooldown 7; Rift, Cascade, Palm Strike 60 AP, Moonlit Inferno 40 AP. Each percentage row is live the two rounds after its cast round, never in it, so no cast's own buff or debuff touches its own hit (§3b). Rift's IDG row is SELF: realized on the caster at cast time wherever they stand (actions.ts 980–1004, §4b); Cascade's SELF IDG still lands if it is aimed at an ally (Damage/IDT withheld). Overlapping windows are ceilings, not rotations.
- Fourth purchases: Burst Waning Crescent or Held Breath; Amplifier Waning Crescent or Unspoken Blow; Suppression Waxing Crescent or Bared to the Moon; Exposure Hushed Inferno or Waxing Crescent. 14 full builds, 12 non-dominated, every node in one. Hybrids 01+02+06+09 and 01+04+06+07 are dominated (by 01+06+09+10 and 01+06+07+08) because Stillness Breaks' +2 Damage and Eclipse in Hand's +2% self buff equal Unspoken Blow's and Held Breath's flats; accepted (+1 secondaries would clear both).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Self-buff reach: neither IDG row has a binding stat filter at the pin (Rift: Highest, no element; Cascade: Fire/Lightning/Yin-Yang/None), so the Amplifier route's 45% raises every matching normal jutsu, weapon and basic hit in its window, and the Rift and Cascade windows can both be live. Not simulated.
- Exposure stacking: Moonlit Inferno's Increase Damage Taken (all four stat types, no element) and Cascade's (Highest, no element) are separate two-round debuffs that both apply to one target (process.ts 1109–1117); at the Exposure route maximum both are 45%, on player, ally and weapon hits. Pierce is unaffected.
- Suppression concentration: Decrease Damage Given has one row (Moonlit Inferno, 40 AP, range 4, 2 rounds) and the route takes it to 45%; its whole value rides one single-target cast per seven rounds, and it does not reduce enemy pierce. Moonlit Inferno is the kit's only cast that this tree makes defensive.
- Silent Rift delivery: its IDG row is target SELF on an EMPTY_GROUND AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN jutsu, so it is realized on the caster at cast time and live the two rounds after the cast round wherever the caster stands (actions.ts 980–1004; SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b, §4b), never boosting Rift's own hit. The Damage row is friendly-fire ENEMIES; the only hazard-flagged row (move) is unsupported and untouched.
- Classification: Yin-Yang is shared with other bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 4 of 8 supported kit rows carry Yin-Yang; the rest need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Moonlit Inferno and Eternal Stillness (no Yin-Yang row) qualify in-kit only through an authored Yin-Yang jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Yin-Yang coverage is unverified.
- Unsupported control: seal (Silent Palm Strike), buffprevent/debuffprevent (Eternal Stillness) and move (Silent Rift) receive nothing, so the bloodline's Defensive and Control traits are not strengthened by any route; the tree makes an A-rank control kit hit harder and debuff harder, which may read as off-trait to the user. Eternal Stillness is untouched by every build.
- Dominance: 12 of 14 full builds are numerically non-dominated; the two dominated no-capstone hybrids are named in design_notes and accepted. The dominance count treats every tag as a gain and ignores delivery, so it is structural evidence only.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring; normal-tree potency policy is not approved, so a combined stacking audit is required before implementation; Yin-Yang Cascade (3 supported rows) is touched by every route, making it the kit's load-bearing cast. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Yin-Yang jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
