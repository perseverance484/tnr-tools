# Tenohira Musei — The Voiceless Hand

**Bloodline:** Tenohira Musei (BR-078, rank A, `18Byy1tMXkQETmB88JLl5`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Yin-Yang classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Yin-Yang (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Yin-Yang offense (Damage on three casts; self Increase Damage Given on Silent Rift and Yin-Yang Cascade) · secondary Enemy exposure (Increase Damage Taken on Moonlit Inferno and Yin-Yang Cascade) · tertiary Suppression (Decrease Damage Given on Moonlit Inferno).

Eight supported rows sit on four casts, and five of them are offensive: three 40-power Yin-Yang Damage rows (Silent Rift's ground circle, Yin-Yang Cascade, Silent Palm Strike) and two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows (Rift, Highest-filtered with no element; Cascade, Fire/Lightning/Yin-Yang/none) that both match the kit's own damage, stack on the caster and sit over a 25% + 0.15/level Increase Damage Given passive. The bloodline's Defensive and Control traits live in unsupported rows (seal on Silent Palm Strike, buffprevent/debuffprevent on Eternal Stillness, move on Silent Rift), so potency reaches control only through Moonlit Inferno's two 35% enemy debuffs (Decrease Damage Given and Increase Damage Taken, all four stat types, 40 AP) and Cascade's 35% exposure. Root Yang therefore carries the offense routes and root Yin the enemy-debuff routes; row-weighted full builds run 19–28 against the Taiyo Kami reference's 16–31.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Yin-Yang-classified Tenohira Musei jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Waxing Crescent** — The hand opens as the moon fills; what it gathers, it does not yet spend. Both self damage buffs 35→38%, 2 rounds: Silent Rift (Highest or element-less hits), Yin-Yang Cascade (Fire/Lightning/Yin-Yang/none).
- **Unspoken Blow** — No cry, no warning. The palm has already landed. Silent Rift (ground circle), Yin-Yang Cascade and Silent Palm Strike Damage 40→42 power; three Yin-Yang hits, 60 AP each, cooldown 7.
- **Deafening Silence** — The quietest strike leaves the loudest ruin. Same three Damage rows; route total 45 power (+5). Seal on Silent Palm Strike and move on Silent Rift are unsupported and unchanged.
- **Held Breath** — Between one breath and the next, the rift widens. Both self damage buffs again: 38→40% with Waxing Crescent; two rounds per 60 AP cast, and both can stack on the caster at once.
- **Tipping the Balance** — Yin drains from the enemy; yang pools in the open hand. Self buffs to route total 42% (+7; 84% when both are active); plus Moonlit Inferno suppression 35→37% (39% with Waning Crescent).
- **Waning Crescent** — Under the thinning moon the enemy's fire gutters and their guard slips. Moonlit Inferno exposure and suppression 35→37% (all stat types, 40 AP); Yin-Yang Cascade exposure 35→37% (Highest or element-less hits).
- **Hushed Inferno** — A blaze can be silenced like any other voice. Moonlit Inferno Decrease Damage Given only: 37→40% with Waning Crescent; one universal row, 2 rounds per 40 AP cast, cooldown 7.
- **Eclipse in Hand** — Close the fingers and the enemy's light goes out; keep what spills. Moonlit Inferno suppression to route total 45% (+10); plus both self damage buffs 35→37% (40% with Waxing Crescent).
- **Bared to the Moon** — Stillness strips the guard away and leaves them bare under the moon. Both enemy exposure rows 37→39% with Waning Crescent; Moonlit Inferno (universal) and Yin-Yang Cascade (Highest or none) stack on a target.
- **Stillness Breaks** — The silence ends all at once, and the stillness falls on them. Exposure route total 42% on both rows (+7; 84% stacked on one target); plus Damage 40→42 on Silent Rift, Cascade and Silent Palm Strike.

## Design notes

- Node split: root Yang (Waxing Crescent, +3 self Increase Damage Given on Rift and Cascade) forks into a Damage route (Unspoken Blow → Deafening Silence) and a self-buff route (Held Breath → Tipping the Balance). Root Yin (Waning Crescent, +2 exposure, +2 suppression) forks into a suppression route (Hushed Inferno → Eclipse in Hand) and an exposure route (Bared to the Moon → Stillness Breaks). 2/4/4 nodes; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP against the 4 BP cap.
- Flats by coverage. Reuse: the two-root topology, the Damage ladder +2/+3 (max +5, 45 power on three rows) and the one-row Decrease Damage Given ladder +2/+3/+5 (max +10, 45%) copy Taiyo Kami's Crown of Cinders/Solar Cataclysm and Sunward Oath/Ashen Verdict/Dying Light, since row counts match. Departures: a single-tag +3 IDG Foundation (root Yang's only broad tag), and IDG +3/+2/+2 and IDT +2/+2/+3 routes held to +7 (42%) because each reaches two stacking rows (OPEN_DECISIONS D14 for IDT).
- Capstone secondaries cross roots (+2 suppression, +2 self buff, +2 Damage). Row weights: Foundations 6/6, Hidden Arts 6/4/3/4, Advanced Arts 9/6/9/12; full builds 19–28 (Taiyo Kami 16–31 on nine rows). Tipping the Balance's 6 is accepted: its worth is the 42% self buff (84% when windows overlap) on Yin-Yang and element-less hits, unpriced by row weight. User-owned options: Tipping +3 IDG/+2 DDG with Held Breath +1 (still +7), or a +5 IDG route (Waxing Crescent +2, Held Breath +1, Tipping +2).
- Arithmetic: both self IDG rows are element-less or list None, so (getEfficiencyRatio, SOURCE_MECHANICS §3) they match the kit's Yin-Yang hits, basic attacks and non-elemental normal jutsu, each adding power/100 × staged base (two windows add 70–84%; §3b); only the 25% + 0.15/level IDG passive (fromType bloodline) multiplies. Moonlit Inferno's debuffs list all four stat types, no element: every non-pierce source. IDG/IDT/DDG never touch pierce; no node touches seal, prevention or move.
- Delivery and uptime: all casts cooldown 7; Rift, Cascade, Palm Strike 60 AP, Moonlit Inferno 40 AP. Each percentage row is live the two rounds after its cast round, never in it, so no cast's own buff or debuff touches its own hit (§3b). Rift's IDG row is SELF: realized on the caster at cast time wherever they stand (actions.ts 980–1004, §4b); Cascade's SELF IDG still lands if it is aimed at an ally (Damage/IDT withheld). 84% figures need two overlapping casts: ceilings, not rotations.
- Fourth purchases: Burst Waning Crescent or Held Breath; Amplifier Waning Crescent or Unspoken Blow; Suppression Waxing Crescent or Bared to the Moon; Exposure Hushed Inferno or Waxing Crescent. 14 full builds, 12 non-dominated, every node in one. Hybrids 01+02+06+09 and 01+04+06+07 are dominated (by 01+06+09+10 and 01+06+07+08) because Stillness Breaks' +2 Damage and Eclipse in Hand's +2 self buff equal Unspoken Blow's and Held Breath's flats; accepted (+1 secondaries clear both; user-owned).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Self-buff leakage: neither IDG row has a binding stat filter at the pin (Rift: Highest, no element; Cascade: Fire/Lightning/Yin-Yang/None), so the +7 route raises every matching normal jutsu, weapon and basic hit in its window; two windows add to 84% under the 25% + 0.15/level passive. Taiyo Kami's two-row IDG stops at +5; +7 awaits a roster two-row IDG ceiling (the +5 option is in design_notes).
- Exposure stacking: Moonlit Inferno's Increase Damage Taken (all four stat types, no element) and Cascade's (Highest, no element) are separate two-round debuffs that both apply to one target (process.ts 1109–1117), adding 70% of the staged base and 84% at the +7 route maximum to player, ally and weapon hits. Pierce is unaffected. Flats held to the two-row +7 ceiling (OPEN_DECISIONS D14).
- Suppression concentration: Decrease Damage Given has one row (Moonlit Inferno, 40 AP, range 4, 2 rounds) and the route takes it to 45%; its whole value rides one single-target cast per seven rounds, and it does not reduce enemy pierce. Moonlit Inferno is the kit's only cast that this tree makes defensive.
- Silent Rift delivery: its IDG row is target SELF on an EMPTY_GROUND AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN jutsu, so it is realized on the caster at cast time and live the two rounds after the cast round wherever the caster stands (actions.ts 980–1004; SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b, §4b), never boosting Rift's own hit. The Damage row is friendly-fire ENEMIES; the only hazard-flagged row (move) is unsupported and untouched.
- Classification: Yin-Yang is the signature element on all three Damage rows but eight census bloodlines share it (Ethereal Monarch and Heavenly Sonata are INCLUDE; six DEFER/EXCLUDE), so the label works only as a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification, not a bare element match. Under the current resolver 4 of 8 supported rows fall back to None. Normal-jutsu collision is unverified.
- Unsupported control: seal (Silent Palm Strike), buffprevent/debuffprevent (Eternal Stillness) and move (Silent Rift) receive nothing, so the bloodline's Defensive and Control traits are not strengthened by any route; the tree makes an A-rank control kit hit harder and debuff harder, which may read as off-trait to the user. Eternal Stillness is untouched by every build.
- Dominance: 12 of 14 full builds are numerically non-dominated; the two dominated no-capstone hybrids are named in design_notes and accepted. The dominance count treats every tag as a gain and ignores delivery, so it is structural evidence only.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring; normal-tree potency policy is not approved, so a combined stacking audit is required before implementation; Yin-Yang Cascade (3 supported rows) is touched by every route, making it the kit's load-bearing cast. No combat simulation was performed; balance values remain user-owned.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Tenohira Musei jutsu inherit Yin-Yang potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
