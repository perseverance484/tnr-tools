# Crystal Essence — Prism and Lattice

**Bloodline:** Crystal Essence (BR-020, rank A, `ssevlOGQ4JjPn2sHZtq0c`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Crystal classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Crystal (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Crystal burst on all three attacks, +5) · secondary Increase Damage Taken (Crystal Sphere and Crystal Cave exposure, two rows, +10%) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken (Crystal Sphere, +10%) and Reflect (Crystal Cave, +10%), with Increase Damage Given as route glue (up to +5% on Kōsai Shippū).

Crystal Essence is a rank-A Defensive / Sustained Damage kit with eight supported rows on five tags. Three of its five jutsu carry a Crystal Damage row (Prism 50, Kōsai Shippū 40, Crystal Cave 40 EP, all 60 AP / cooldown 7), so raw Crystal Damage is the primary emphasis and the deepest offensive commitment. Its two Increase Damage Taken rows (Sphere single target, Cave area enemies, both 35% for two rounds) anchor an exposure route, while the 40 AP Crystal Sphere's 35% Decrease Damage Taken and the Cave's 40% Reflect each anchor a defensive route that honours the Defensive trait. Increase Damage Given has a single row (Shippū 35%, self) that the bloodline's own Increase Damage Given passive already multiplies, so it stays Foundation and Advanced Art secondary glue capped at +5% rather than earning a route.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Crystal jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Prismatic Focus** — Light bent through one facet burns where it lands and steadies the hand that aims it. Kōsai Shippū Increase Damage Given 35 → 37% (self, 2 rounds) and exposure on Crystal Sphere (single) and Crystal Cave (area enemies) 35 → 37% for 2 rounds.
- **Razor Lattice** — Grown edge upon edge, the lattice cuts along every plane at once. Crystal Damage on Prism 50 → 52 EP and on Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave 40 → 42 EP (all Ninjutsu, 60 AP, cooldown 7). Prism's wound row is untouched.
- **Splintered Mountain Heart** — Break the mountain's heart and every splinter remembers the weight it carried. Damage route total +5: Prism 50 → 55 EP, Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave 40 → 45 EP.
- **Flaw in the Stone** — Every stone has a flaw. Find it, and the whole face waits to split. Both exposure rows 40% with Prismatic Focus: Crystal Sphere (single target, Ninjutsu filter, no element) and Crystal Cave (area enemies, Crystal/Earth/Fire/None), 2 rounds.
- **Break Along the Flaw** — The first crack is the invitation; everything after travels the line it opened. Increase Damage Taken route total +10%: Sphere and Cave exposure 35 → 45%; +3% Increase Damage Given lifts Shippū's self buff to 40% with Prismatic Focus.
- **Jade Lattice** — Jade does not flinch. What strikes it rings, and the ringing carries back. Crystal Sphere Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 37% and Crystal Cave Reflect 40 → 42% (self, 2 rounds each); both element-less, all four stat types.
- **Flawless Sphere** — A sphere without seam gives a blow no edge to catch and no face to break. Crystal Sphere Decrease Damage Taken 40% with Jade Lattice (self, 2 rounds, nearly every non-pierce hit); the same cast's exposure row is unchanged by it.
- **Adamant Core** — What cannot be cut does not need to hurry; it sharpens while the enemy tires. Decrease Damage Taken route total +10% (Crystal Sphere 35 → 45%); +3% Increase Damage Given lifts Shippū's self buff to 38%, or 40% with Prismatic Focus.
- **Light Turned Back** — The cavern keeps no light for itself; it returns every spark to the one who brought it. Crystal Cave Reflect 45% with Jade Lattice (pierce included, 60% cap), live 2 rounds after a 60 AP area cast, cooldown 7.
- **Entombed in Crystal** — Walls that bite, and a heart that cuts sharper for every blow they turn away. Reflect route total +10% (Crystal Cave 40 → 50%); the +3% Increase Damage Given rides Shippū's self buff (38%, or 40% with Prismatic Focus), not the Cave.

## Design notes

- Reference reuse: flats follow Taiyo Kami where rows match (Prismatic Focus = Dawnheart; Razor Lattice and Splintered Mountain Heart = Crown of Cinders and Solar Cataclysm; Flawless Sphere and Adamant Core = Golden Mantle and Sovereign Sun), as the kit shares Taiyo's three-row burst and Increase Damage Given / Increase Damage Taken glue. Departures: no Decrease Damage Given or Afterburn rows, so Jade Lattice pairs Decrease Damage Taken with Reflect, and the exposure and Reflect routes use the same +2% / +3% / +5% ladder.
- Routes (RUL-2026-10-03-005): Burst +5 Damage on three rows; Exposure +10% Increase Damage Taken on two rows (Prismatic Focus +2%, Flaw in the Stone +3%, Break Along the Flaw +5%); Bastion +10% Decrease Damage Taken and Reprisal +10% Reflect (Jade Lattice +2%, Hidden Art +3%, Advanced Art +5%). Recalibration: Flaw in the Stone +2% → +3% and Break Along the Flaw +3% → +5% Increase Damage Taken (route +7% → +10%; the earlier two-row hold is superseded).
- Advanced Art secondaries: Break Along the Flaw, Adamant Core and Entombed in Crystal all carry +3% Increase Damage Given. It is the only kit tag no Hidden Art carries, so it cannot out-bid a sibling purchase; the cost is that three routes share Shippū's 40% and differ by primary. All 14 legal full builds are non-dominated.
- Main tree and passives: for the two rounds after its cast, Shippū's Increase Damage Given row (Crystal/Earth/Fire/None, Ninjutsu filter) amplifies any Ninjutsu hit and any Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less hit of any stat type: later Crystal strikes, basic attacks, and any matching normal jutsu or weapon (normal-jutsu catalog unverified). Main-tree or item Increase Damage Given applies as its own multiplier and the bloodline passive (25% + 0.15/lvl) multiplies last (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b). Both exposure rows amplify allies' matching hits too. Pierce ignores Increase Damage Given, Increase Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Taken.
- Delivery and uptime: every jutsu has cooldown 7. Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave are 60 AP circle spawns on an OTHER_USER target at range 4; Prism is a 60 AP single-target shot; Crystal Sphere a 40 AP single-target cast. Buff and exposure rows are live only in the two rounds after the cast round, so staggered casts give enhanced windows on four to six of every seven rounds. SELF rows (Shippū Increase Damage Given, Sphere Decrease Damage Taken, Cave Reflect) land on the caster at cast time once the cast has a legal target.
- Fourth-purchase choices: Burst (01, 02, 03) adds Flaw in the Stone (exposure 40%) or Jade Lattice (Decrease Damage Taken 37%, Reflect 42%); Exposure (01, 04, 05) Razor Lattice (+2 Damage) or Jade Lattice; Bastion (06, 07, 08) Prismatic Focus (Increase Damage Given 40%, exposure 37%) or Light Turned Back (Reflect 45%); Reprisal (06, 09, 10) Prismatic Focus (Increase Damage Given 40%) or Flawless Sphere (Decrease Damage Taken 40%). Hybrids with no Advanced Art (e.g. 01, 02, 04, 06) spread +2 to +5 over five tags.
- Defensive routes and untouched rows: Sphere's Decrease Damage Taken and the Cave's Reflect rows list all four stat types and no element, so each matches nearly every hit the caster takes (Decrease Damage Taken excludes pierce; Reflect includes it and bypasses shields). 45% and 50% stay under the caps (100; Reflect 60% of the hit); the 15% Earth Decrease Damage Taken passive applies last. Prism's wound and the Jade summon are unsupported and get nothing. Skill-tree effects and passives are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Crystal is shared with Sea-Maiden's Kiss (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 5 of 8 kit rows carry Crystal; Crystal Sphere's two rows and Crystal Cave's Reflect row need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Crystal Sphere (and Summoning: Jade, which has no supported row) carry no Crystal row, so they qualify only through an authored Crystal jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Crystal coverage is unverified.
- Exposure breadth: +10% makes both Increase Damage Taken rows 45% for two rounds (Sphere single, Cave area); both amplify allies' hits as well as the caster's (pierce excluded), and an enemy under both marks takes both: ×1.45 × 1.45 ≈ ×2.10 on a hit matching both filters (e.g. Prism), against ×1.35 × 1.35 ≈ ×1.82 unmodified (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b). Team value was not simulated.
- Reflect cap and uptime: 50% leaves ten points of headroom under the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage cap, but main-tree or item Reflect sources add to it and could saturate the cap, wasting part of the +10%. Reflect pays only when the caster is hit during the two rounds after a 60 AP Cave cast; realized value depends on enemy behaviour and was not simulated.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: Shippū's row compounds with any main-tree or item Increase Damage Given (each applies as its own multiplier) and the bloodline passive (about 28.75% at level 25) multiplies last; the +5% maximum lifts Shippū's row to 40%. Each row stays far below the 100 cap, but the combined per-hit amplification in a Shippū window was not simulated against peer kits.
- Decrease Damage Taken scope: it is a damage modifier and does not touch pierce hits; the Sphere's 35 → 45% window also only exists when the 40 AP cast has a legal OTHER_USER target (any living non-caster user, allies included) in range 4. The exposure half of the same cast is unchanged by the Decrease Damage Taken nodes and lands on whoever is targeted.
- Damage calibration: Burst reaches +5 on three rows (Prism 55, Shippū 45, Cave 45 EP), Taiyo Kami parity at the hard ceiling. By row weight the Exposure build (01, 02, 04, 05) is now the strongest full build, because its +10% lands on two rows.
- Delivery and friendly fire: Shippū's and the Cave's damage and the Cave's exposure reach only enemies on the spawn tiles (friendly fire ENEMIES; no ally-hazard rows); their SELF rows land on the caster at cast time. Prism's damage (ALL) and Sphere's exposure (none = ALL) land on an ally if one is targeted: the Damage nodes raise that hit, the exposure nodes that ally's exposure (45% with 01, 04, 05).
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Crystal jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
