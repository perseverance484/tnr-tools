# Crystal Essence — Prism and Lattice

**Bloodline:** Crystal Essence (BR-020, rank A, `ssevlOGQ4JjPn2sHZtq0c`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Crystal classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Crystal (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Crystal burst on all three attacks, +5 ceiling) · secondary Increase Damage Taken (Crystal Sphere and Crystal Cave exposure, two rows, +7 ceiling) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken (Crystal Sphere, +10 ceiling) and Reflect (Crystal Cave, +10 ceiling), with Increase Damage Given as route glue (+5 ceiling on Kōsai Shippū).

Crystal Essence is a rank-A Defensive / Sustained Damage kit with eight supported rows on five tags. Three of its five jutsu carry a Crystal damage row (Prism 50, Kōsai Shippū 40, Crystal Cave 40, all 60 AP / cooldown 7), so raw Crystal power is the primary emphasis and the deepest offensive commitment. Its two Increase Damage Taken rows (Sphere single target, Cave area enemies, both 35% for two rounds) anchor an exposure route, while the 40 AP Crystal Sphere's 35% Decrease Damage Taken and the Cave's 40% Reflect each anchor a defensive route that honours the Defensive trait. Increase Damage Given has a single row (Shippū 35%) that the bloodline passive already multiplies, so it stays Foundation and capstone-secondary glue capped at +5 rather than earning a route.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Crystal-classified Crystal Essence jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Prismatic Focus** — Light bent through one facet burns where it lands and steadies the hand that aims it. Shippū IDG 35% (self, 2 rounds) and exposure on Crystal Sphere (single) and Crystal Cave (area enemies), 35% for 2 rounds. No gates.
- **Razor Lattice** — Grown edge upon edge, the lattice cuts along every plane at once. Damage on Prism 50, Kōsai Shippū 40 and Crystal Cave 40; all Crystal, Ninjutsu, 60 AP, cooldown 7. Prism's wound row is untouched.
- **Splintered Mountain Heart** — Break the mountain's heart and every splinter remembers the weight it carried. Same three Crystal damage rows; the route total of +5 lifts Prism to 55 and Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave to 45.
- **Flaw in the Stone** — Every stone has a flaw. Find it, and the whole face waits to split. Both exposure rows: Crystal Sphere (single target, None/Ninjutsu filter) and Crystal Cave (area enemies, Crystal/Earth/Fire/None), 2 rounds.
- **Break Along the Flaw** — The first crack is the invitation; everything after travels the line it opened. Exposure reaches 42% on Sphere and Cave with the full route; +3 IDG lifts Shippū's self buff to 40% with Prismatic Focus.
- **Jade Lattice** — Jade does not flinch. What strikes it rings, and the ringing carries back. Crystal Sphere DDT 35% (self, 2 rounds, 40 AP) and Crystal Cave Reflect 40% (self, 2 rounds); both element-less, all four stat types.
- **Flawless Sphere** — A sphere without seam gives a blow no edge to catch and no face to break. Crystal Sphere DDT row only (35%, self, 2 rounds, every non-pierce hit); the same 40 AP cast carries the exposure half unchanged.
- **Adamant Core** — What cannot be cut does not need to hurry; it sharpens while the enemy tires. Sphere DDT reaches 45% with the route; +3 IDG lifts Shippū's self buff to 38%, or 40% with Prismatic Focus.
- **Light Turned Back** — The cavern keeps no light for itself; it returns every spark to the one who brought it. Crystal Cave Reflect row only (40% of each hit returned, pierce included, 60% cap); 2 rounds after a 60 AP area cast, cooldown 7.
- **Entombed in Crystal** — Walls that bite, and a heart that cuts sharper for every blow they turn away. Cave Reflect reaches 50% with the route; the +3 IDG rides Shippū's self buff (38%, or 40% with Prismatic Focus), not the Cave.

- **Break Along the Flaw:** The exposure route takes both Increase Damage Taken rows from 35% to 42% for their two rounds: Crystal Sphere's single-target mark and the Cave's area mark, amplifying every non-pierce Ninjutsu, Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less hit the exposed enemies take from the caster or allies. - **Adamant Core:** The bastion route takes Crystal Sphere's Decrease Damage Taken from 35% to 45% for its two-round window on a 40 AP cast, and the capstone's +3 IDG with Prismatic Focus lifts Shippū's self buff to 40%, so the kit both weathers more and hits harder in the Shippū window. Prismatic Focus also leaves both exposure rows at 37% and Jade Lattice the Cave Reflect at 42%. Light Turned Back (Reflect 45%) is the alternative fourth purchase for a pure defender who skips the offensive root.
- **Entombed in Crystal:** The reprisal route takes Crystal Cave's Reflect from 40% to 50%, ten points under the 60% per-hit cap, for the two rounds after the Cave lands, and its capstone's +3 IDG with Prismatic Focus lifts Shippū's self buff to 40%, so the caster who weathers the counter-attack hits back harder in the next window. Prismatic Focus also leaves both exposure rows at 37% and Jade Lattice the Sphere DDT at 37%. Flawless Sphere (DDT 40%, Reflect 50%, IDG 38%) is the alternative fourth purchase for a caster who never buys into the offensive root.

## Design notes

- Node split and calibration: Foundations +2/+2 (Prismatic Focus: IDG and exposure, 3 rows; Jade Lattice: DDT and Reflect, 2 rows). Hidden Arts take one tag each: +2 Damage on three rows, +2 IDT on two rows, +3 DDT and +3 Reflect on single rows. Capstones: +3 Damage (route +5, Taiyo parity on three rows), +3 IDT with +3 IDG (two-row IDT held to +7), +5 DDT or +5 Reflect with +3 IDG. IDG sits only on the Foundation and capstone secondaries, so it tops out at +5 (Shippū 35 -> 40).
- Main tree and passives: Shippū's IDG row lists Crystal/Earth/Fire/None with a Ninjutsu filter, so it amplifies the kit's Crystal hits and any Ninjutsu, Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less damage (basic attacks, most normal jutsu, weapons) for two rounds; the bloodline IDG passive (25 + 0.15/lvl) multiplies on top. Sphere's IDT (None/Ninjutsu) and the Cave's IDT (Crystal/Earth/Fire/None, no stat filter) amplify allies' matching hits too. Pierce hits ignore IDG/IDT/DDT; Reflect includes them.
- Delivery and uptime: every jutsu has cooldown 7. Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave are 60 AP circle spawns on an OTHER_USER target at range 4 (damage rows friendly fire ENEMIES, no ally hazard); Prism is a 60 AP single-target shot; Crystal Sphere a 40 AP single-target cast. Buffs and exposure last two rounds, so staggered Shippū, Sphere and Cave casts give enhanced windows on four to six of every seven rounds. Shippū's self IDG and Sphere's self DDT arrive only when the cast has a legal target.
- Fourth-purchase choices: Burst (01, 02, 03) finishes with Flaw in the Stone (exposure 39%) or Jade Lattice (DDT 37%, Reflect 42%); Exposure (01, 04, 05) with Razor Lattice (+2 Damage) or Jade Lattice; Bastion (06, 07, 08) with Prismatic Focus (IDG 40%, exposure 37%) or Light Turned Back (Reflect 45%); Reprisal (06, 09, 10) with Prismatic Focus (IDG 40%) or Flawless Sphere (DDT 40%). Capstone-less hybrids (e.g. 01, 02, 04, 06) spread +2/+5 across several tags; 14 legal full builds.
- Defensive routes: Sphere's DDT row and the Cave's Reflect row list all four stat types with no element, so each matches nearly every hit the caster takes (DDT excludes pierce; Reflect includes it and bypasses shield absorption). DDT 35 -> 45 and Reflect 40 -> 50 stay under their caps (100; 60% of the hit for Reflect). The bloodline's 15% Earth DDT passive multiplies rather than adds. Reflect pays only when the caster is struck in the two rounds after a 60 AP area cast: a duelist's build.
- Untouched rows: Prism's 25% wound and Summoning: Jade's summon (A rank, 70 AP, cooldown 10, PVP only) are unsupported tags and receive nothing; the Jade summon is the kit's only mode-restricted jutsu and carries no supported row. Bloodline passives are not potency targets. Skill-tree effects are suppressed in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Crystal is not exclusive in the census (Sea-Maiden's Kiss, a deferred record, carries Crystal on 5 rows, 3 of them damage), and whether any NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN jutsu carries Crystal is unverified (no non-bloodline catalog with effect rows exists in the repository). The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification; a bare element match would leak to that kit.
- Resolver: 3 of the 8 supported rows (Crystal Sphere DDT and IDT, Crystal Cave Reflect) have no element and fall back to None under the current resolver, so they are unreachable without the proposed classification change; reaching them with affectedElements including None would also reach every element-less row on any jutsu the player casts.
- Exposure breadth: +7 makes both Increase Damage Taken rows 42% for two rounds, one single-target (Sphere) and one on area enemies (Cave), and they amplify allies' matching hits as well as the caster's (pierce excluded). The route was held to +7 rather than +10 because the tag reaches two rows; team value exceeds solo value and was not simulated. Trim Break Along the Flaw's +3 IDT first.
- Reflect cap and uptime: 50% leaves ten points of headroom under the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage cap, but main-tree or item Reflect sources could saturate it and waste part of the +10. Reflect requires being hit during the two rounds after a 60 AP Cave cast and both combatants alive; realized value depends on enemy behaviour and was not simulated.
- IDG stacking: Shippū's 35% row already stacks additively with any main-tree or item IDG and the bloodline IDG passive (about 28.75% at level 25) multiplies the result; the +5 ceiling adds five additive points to one row. Each row stays far below the 100 cap, but the combined per-hit amplification in a Shippū window was not simulated against peer kits.
- Decrease Damage Taken scope: DDT is a damage modifier and does not touch pierce hits; the Sphere's 35 -> 45 window also only exists when the 40 AP cast has a legal OTHER_USER target in range 4, so the defensive routes depend on an enemy being reachable, not on the caster's choice alone. The exposure half of the same cast is unchanged by the DDT nodes.
- Damage calibration: the Burst build reaches +5 on three rows (Prism 55, Shippū 45, Cave 45), Taiyo Kami parity; the strongest row-weighted full build is below Taiyo Kami's 31 because this kit has one fewer supported row and no Afterburn. If the tree proves weak, Jade Lattice is the lever (a +1 Damage secondary would raise the ceiling to the +6 guardrail, as Shinrai Ou's Raiment of Storms does).
- Delivery and friendly fire: Shippū and Cave are circle spawns whose realized hits and exposure depend on positioning; the dossier flags no ally-hazard rows (friendly fire ENEMIES on both area damage rows and the Cave's area exposure). Prism's damage row has friendly fire ALL on a single-target cast, so if an ally is targeted the Damage nodes raise that hit too.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Crystal Essence jutsu inherit Crystal potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
