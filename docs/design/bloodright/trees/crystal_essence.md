# Crystal Essence — Prism and Lattice

**Bloodline:** Crystal Essence (BR-020, rank A, `ssevlOGQ4JjPn2sHZtq0c`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance (roster pass) / Crystal classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Crystal (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Offense from Prismatic Focus: the Kōsai Shippū self buff (Increase Damage Given, one row, up to +10%; sustained amplification) or the Crystal Sphere and Crystal Cave exposure marks (Increase Damage Taken, two rows, up to +8%) · secondary Defense from Jade Lattice: Crystal Sphere Decrease Damage Taken (fortress, +10%) or Crystal Cave Reflect (retaliation, +10%) · tertiary No flat Damage: the traits are Defensive and Sustained Damage, not Burst, and Prism's 50 EP row would pass the Nuke tier.

Crystal Essence is a rank-A Defensive / Sustained Damage kit with eight supported rows on five tags. Its three Crystal attacks are Prism 50 and Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave 40 EP, all 60 AP on cooldown 7, so the tree amplifies the rows that multiply those attacks and leaves Damage alone. Prismatic Focus answers "How do I make my Crystal strikes count: sharpen my own blows or crack the enemy open for everyone?" (Shippū self buff 35 → 45%, or both exposure marks 35 → 43%). Jade Lattice answers "How do I hold my ground: endure the blows or turn them back?" (Sphere Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 45%, or Cave Reflect 40 → 50%). Each Advanced Art carries one tag; no tag is shared glue.

**Director review recommended:** Roster questions: DQ-B (Break Along the Flaw +8% Increase Damage Taken on two rows, ×1.122).

- Concern: Damage is unamplified: the traits are Defensive and Sustained Damage, and Prism shares cooldown 7 and 60 AP with Shippū and the Cave, so the kit names no finisher to lift past the 50 Nuke tier. If the director reads Prism as one, Splintered Mountain Heart is where a Blood-Enchanted Eyes-style +2 Damage payoff after Razor Lattice's setup would go.
- Concern: Break Along the Flaw keeps exposure at +8% on two compounding team-wide rows (≈ ×1.122, against Blood-Enchanted Eyes' three-row ×1.115); it should move with the roster's other two-row exposure routes, not alone.
- Concern: Splintered Mountain Heart is a bare +10% on one self row (≈ ×1.074). The anchors that reach +10% on one row carry a rider, but this kit has no sustain row and every candidate rider is another route's tag; the fallback is Razor Lattice +2 and the capstone +4 (route +8%, ≈ ×1.059).
- Concern: Adamant Core and Entombed in Crystal are single-tag, lighter than the anchors' defensive riders; the kit has one row per defensive tag and the only riders are each other's tag or the offensive rows (design notes).
- Concern: Sustained amplification + Flaw in the Stone (≈ ×1.139) and Exposure + Razor Lattice (≈ ×1.164) are close in a fully set-up Shippū window on a doubly marked enemy; Exposure leads there and on allies' hits, Sustained amplification on every other target in the window. Uptime and party play were not simulated.
- Concern: Fortress, Retaliation and half of Exposure depend on Crystal Sphere's authored classification or the element-less Cave Reflect row reaching the proposed resolver (G1).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Crystal jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Final cleanup (2026-10-04): the roster pass's +2 Damage payoff on Splintered Mountain Heart is removed, so Prism stays 50 EP and Shippū and the Cave 40 EP. The dossier traits are Defensive and Sustained Damage, not Burst, and Prism is not a signature finisher: it shares cooldown 7 and 60 AP with Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave. Splintered Mountain Heart now pays off Razor Lattice with +5% Increase Damage Given (sustained amplification), and Break Along the Flaw's split moves from 2/3/3 to 2/2/4 so the capstone exceeds its Hidden step. Maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +10%, Increase Damage Taken +8%, Decrease Damage Taken +10%, Reflect +10%, no Damage.
- Magnitudes follow leverage, not printed totals. Increase Damage Given has one row, but it multiplies every matching hit the caster lands on any target in the two rounds after Shippū, so it takes the one-row +10% (×1.35 → ×1.45, ≈ ×1.074; the bloodline passive multiplies last). Increase Damage Taken has two rows from two casts that amplify every attacker's matching hits and compound on a doubly marked enemy (×1.82 → ×2.04, ≈ ×1.122, against Blood-Enchanted Eyes' three-row ×1.115 and Shakunetsu Sakura's ×1.075), so it stops at +8%. Decrease Damage Taken +10% (a hit taken ×0.65 → ×0.55, ≈ ×0.846) and Reflect +10% (40 → 50% returned) each sit on one row.
- Capstones stay single-tag. The kit has no sustain row, and its only defensive rows are Sphere's Decrease Damage Taken and the Cave's Reflect, one each; a mirrored rider in the Blood-Enchanted Eyes shape would make Fortress + Light Turned Back (+10% / +8%) and Retaliation + Flawless Sphere (+8% / +10%) the same two-tag build, and Decrease Damage Taken trims what Reflect returns. Any rider on Splintered Mountain Heart would be another route's tag.
- Fourth purchases: Sustained amplification (01, 02, 03) adds Flaw in the Stone (marks 39%) or Jade Lattice; Exposure (01, 04, 05) Razor Lattice (Shippū 40%) or Jade Lattice; Fortress (06, 07, 08) Light Turned Back (Reflect 45%) or Prismatic Focus; Retaliation (06, 09, 10) Flawless Sphere (Decrease Damage Taken 40%) or Prismatic Focus. On a doubly marked enemy in the Shippū window, Sustained amplification + Flaw lands ×1.45 × 1.39 × 1.39 ≈ ×2.80 against Exposure + Razor's ×1.40 × 1.43 × 1.43 ≈ ×2.86 (≈ ×1.139 and ≈ ×1.164 of the unmodified kit; the latter is the tree's top offensive package, under Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.234). While Shippū's window is live, Sustained amplification leads on every enemy that is not doubly marked and needs one cast; Exposure leads on a doubly marked enemy and on allies' hits, so neither dominates.
- Filters and delivery: Shippū's self buff (Crystal/Earth/Fire/None, Ninjutsu filter) amplifies any Ninjutsu hit and any Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less hit of any stat type; Sphere's mark (Ninjutsu, no element) any Ninjutsu or element-less hit; the Cave's mark Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less hits. Shippū, Prism, the Sphere and the Cave all have cooldown 7; Shippū and the Cave are 60 AP circle spawns, Prism a 60 AP single shot, the Sphere a 40 AP single cast. Rows are live in the two rounds after their cast round, and SELF rows land on the caster at cast time once the cast has a legal target. Pierce ignores every modifier here except Reflect.
- Untouched rows: the three Damage rows are unamplified; Prism's wound and the Jade summon are unsupported. Skill-tree effects and passives are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Crystal is shared with Sea-Maiden's Kiss (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 5 of 8 kit rows carry Crystal; Crystal Sphere's two rows and Crystal Cave's Reflect row need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Crystal Sphere qualifies only through an authored Crystal jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Crystal coverage is unverified.
- Exposure breadth: at +8% both marks are 43% for two rounds and amplify allies' matching hits as well as the caster's (pierce excluded); a doubly marked enemy takes ×1.43 × 1.43 ≈ ×2.04 against ×1.82 unmodified (≈ ×1.122). Team value was not simulated.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: Shippū's 45% compounds with any main-tree or item Increase Damage Given, and the bloodline passive (about 28.75% at level 25) multiplies last (×1.45 × 1.29 ≈ ×1.87 in the window). The route's value depends on landing Prism or the Cave inside the two-round window.
- Reflect cap and uptime: 50% leaves ten points under the 60%-of-hit cap, but main-tree or item Reflect could saturate it. Reflect pays only when the caster is hit in the two rounds after a 60 AP Cave cast, and Decrease Damage Taken active at the same time lowers what is reflected.
- Friendly fire: Crystal Sphere is an OTHER_USER single cast whose mark (friendly fire none = ALL) lands on an ally if the Sphere targets one, up to 43% on the Exposure route; its Decrease Damage Taken window also needs a legal target in range 4. Prism's damage (friendly fire ALL) hits an ally it targets. Shippū's and the Cave's damage and the Cave's mark reach only enemies.
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Crystal jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
