# Crystal Essence — Prism and Lattice

**Bloodline:** Crystal Essence (BR-020, rank A, `ssevlOGQ4JjPn2sHZtq0c`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance (roster pass) / Crystal classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Crystal (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Offense from Prismatic Focus: a Kōsai Shippū self-buff setup paid off with a controlled +2 Damage on all three Crystal attacks (Burst), or the Crystal Sphere and Crystal Cave exposure marks (Increase Damage Taken, two rows, up to +8%) · secondary Defense from Jade Lattice: Crystal Sphere Decrease Damage Taken (fortress, +10%) or Crystal Cave Reflect (retaliation, +10%) · tertiary Increase Damage Given (Kōsai Shippū, one row) only as the Burst setup, up to +5%.

Crystal Essence is a rank-A Defensive / Sustained Damage kit with eight supported rows on five tags. Its three Crystal attacks are Prism 50 and Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave 40 EP. Prismatic Focus answers "How do I make my Crystal strikes count: sharpen my own blows or crack the enemy open for everyone?": Burst sharpens Shippū's self buff (35 → 40%) and pays off with +2 Damage (Prism 50 → 52, above the Nuke tier on the Blood-Enchanted Eyes / Shakunetsu Sakura precedent; Shippū and Cave 40 → 42), while Exposure takes both marks 35 → 43% for every attacker. Jade Lattice answers "How do I hold my ground: endure the blows or turn them back?" (Sphere Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 45%, or Cave Reflect 40 → 50%). Each Advanced Art carries one tag; no tag is shared glue.

- Concern: Prism 50 → 52 under Splintered Mountain Heart is above the Nuke tier; it rests on the director precedent, not on a ruling for this kit.
- Concern: Break Along the Flaw keeps exposure at +8% on two compounding team-wide rows, the batch's two-row convention and above the anchors' +5%.

Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. - **Entombed in Crystal** — Walls that bite: every blow thrown inside the cavern rings back to the hand that threw it. Retaliation route total +10%: Crystal Cave Reflect 40 → 50%, ten points under the 60%-of-hit cap, for the two rounds after the Cave lands.

## Design notes

- Roster pass (2026-10-04): the first batch pass replaced the pre-batch +5 Damage route (Prism 50 → 55, Shippū and Cave 40 → 45) with a bare +10% Increase Damage Given capstone on Shippū's one row that was still labelled burst. This pass restores the director pattern (RUL-2026-10-04-001/002): Razor Lattice's +3% Increase Damage Given is the setup and Splintered Mountain Heart pays it off with +2 Damage (Prism 50 → 52, Shippū and Cave 40 → 42). The setup is a self buff rather than a second +3% Increase Damage Taken Hidden Art, because Flaw in the Stone already owns both exposure rows. Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +2, Increase Damage Given +5%, Increase Damage Taken +8%, Decrease Damage Taken +10%, Reflect +10%.
- Magnitudes follow leverage, not printed totals. Increase Damage Given has one row; at +5% it is a setup (the caster's matching hits ×1.35 → ×1.40 for two rounds after Shippū), not a route of its own. Increase Damage Taken has two rows from two casts that amplify every attacker's matching hits and compound on a doubly marked enemy (×1.82 → ×2.04), so it stops at +8%. Decrease Damage Taken +10% (a hit taken ×0.65 → ×0.55) and Reflect +10% (40 → 50% returned) each sit on one row.
- Defensive capstones stay single-tag. The kit's only defensive rows are Sphere's Decrease Damage Taken and the Cave's Reflect, one each; a mirrored rider in the Blood-Enchanted Eyes shape would make Fortress + Light Turned Back (+10% / +8%) and Retaliation + Flawless Sphere (+8% / +10%) the same two-tag build, and Decrease Damage Taken trims what Reflect returns. The remaining candidates (Shippū's buff, the marks) would blur the offense/defense split.
- Fourth purchases: Burst (01, 02, 03) adds Flaw in the Stone (marks 40%) or Jade Lattice; Exposure (01, 04, 05) Razor Lattice (Shippū 40%) or Jade Lattice; Fortress (06, 07, 08) Light Turned Back (Reflect 45%) or Prismatic Focus; Retaliation (06, 09, 10) Flawless Sphere (Decrease Damage Taken 40%) or Prismatic Focus. On a doubly marked enemy in the Shippū window, Burst + Flaw lands Prism at 52 EP × 1.40 × 1.40 × 1.40 (≈ ×2.85 of a 50 EP hit) against Exposure + Razor's ×1.40 × 1.43 × 1.43 ≈ ×2.86. Burst leads on Shippū and the Cave (42 EP, ≈ ×2.88) and outside the windows (+2 Damage on every own strike), Exposure on allies' hits, so neither dominates.
- Filters and delivery: Shippū's self buff (Crystal/Earth/Fire/None, Ninjutsu filter) amplifies any Ninjutsu hit and any Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less hit of any stat type; Sphere's mark (Ninjutsu, no element) any Ninjutsu or element-less hit; the Cave's mark Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less hits. Every jutsu has cooldown 7; Shippū and the Cave are 60 AP circle spawns, Prism a 60 AP single shot, the Sphere a 40 AP single cast. Rows are live in the two rounds after their cast round, and SELF rows land on the caster at cast time once the cast has a legal target. Pierce ignores every modifier here except Reflect.
- Untouched rows: Prism's wound and the Jade summon are unsupported. Skill-tree effects and passives are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Crystal is shared with Sea-Maiden's Kiss (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 5 of 8 kit rows carry Crystal; Crystal Sphere's two rows and Crystal Cave's Reflect row need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Crystal Sphere qualifies only through an authored Crystal jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Crystal coverage is unverified.
- Damage tier: Prism 50 → 52 under Splintered Mountain Heart is past the 50 Nuke tier. It rests on the Blood-Enchanted Eyes / Shakunetsu Sakura precedent, not on a ruling for this kit; no legal allocation adds more than +2, and Shippū and the Cave stay Normal at 42.
- Exposure breadth: at +8% both marks are 43% for two rounds and amplify allies' matching hits as well as the caster's (pierce excluded); a doubly marked enemy takes ×1.43 × 1.43 ≈ ×2.04 against ×1.82 unmodified. Team value was not simulated.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: Shippū's 40% compounds with any main-tree or item Increase Damage Given, and the bloodline passive (about 28.75% at level 25) multiplies last (×1.40 × 1.29 ≈ ×1.80 in the window). The window's value depends on landing Prism or the Cave inside it; the +2 Damage applies to every own strike regardless.
- Reflect cap and uptime: 50% leaves ten points under the 60%-of-hit cap, but main-tree or item Reflect could saturate it. Reflect pays only when the caster is hit in the two rounds after a 60 AP Cave cast, and Decrease Damage Taken active at the same time lowers what is reflected.
- Friendly fire: Crystal Sphere is an OTHER_USER single cast whose mark (friendly fire none = ALL) lands on an ally if the Sphere targets one, up to 43% on the Exposure route; its Decrease Damage Taken window also needs a legal target in range 4. Prism's damage (friendly fire ALL) hits an ally it targets, at 52 EP with Splintered Mountain Heart. Shippū's and the Cave's damage and the Cave's mark reach only enemies.
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Crystal jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
