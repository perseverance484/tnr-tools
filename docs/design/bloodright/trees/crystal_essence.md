# Crystal Essence — Prism and Lattice

**Bloodline:** Crystal Essence (BR-020, rank A, `ssevlOGQ4JjPn2sHZtq0c`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Crystal classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Crystal (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Crystal burst on all three attacks, +5 ceiling) · secondary Increase Damage Taken (Crystal Sphere and Crystal Cave exposure, two rows, +7 ceiling) · tertiary Decrease Damage Taken (Crystal Sphere, +10 ceiling) and Reflect (Crystal Cave, +10 ceiling), with Increase Damage Given as route glue (+5 ceiling on Kōsai Shippū).

Crystal Essence is a rank-A Defensive / Sustained Damage kit with eight supported rows on five tags. Three of its five jutsu carry a Crystal damage row (Prism 50, Kōsai Shippū 40, Crystal Cave 40, all 60 AP / cooldown 7), so raw Crystal power is the primary emphasis and the deepest offensive commitment. Its two Increase Damage Taken rows (Sphere single target, Cave area enemies, both 35% for two rounds) anchor an exposure route, while the 40 AP Crystal Sphere's 35% Decrease Damage Taken and the Cave's 40% Reflect each anchor a defensive route that honours the Defensive trait. Increase Damage Given has a single row (Shippū 35%, self) whose matching hits the bloodline IDG passive already multiplies, so it stays Foundation and capstone-secondary glue capped at +5 rather than earning a route.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Crystal-classified Crystal Essence jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Prismatic Focus** — Light bent through one facet burns where it lands and steadies the hand that aims it. Shippū IDG 35% (self, 2 rounds) and exposure on Crystal Sphere (single) and Crystal Cave (area enemies), 35% for 2 rounds. No gates.
- **Razor Lattice** — Grown edge upon edge, the lattice cuts along every plane at once. Damage on Prism 50, Kōsai Shippū 40 and Crystal Cave 40; all Crystal, Ninjutsu, 60 AP, cooldown 7. Prism's wound row is untouched.
- **Splintered Mountain Heart** — Break the mountain's heart and every splinter remembers the weight it carried. Same three Crystal damage rows; the route total of +5 lifts Prism to 55 and Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave to 45.
- **Flaw in the Stone** — Every stone has a flaw. Find it, and the whole face waits to split. Both exposure rows: Crystal Sphere (single target, None/Ninjutsu filter) and Crystal Cave (area enemies, Crystal/Earth/Fire/None), 2 rounds.
- **Break Along the Flaw** — The first crack is the invitation; everything after travels the line it opened. Exposure reaches 42% on Sphere and Cave with the full route; +3 IDG lifts Shippū's self buff to 40% with Prismatic Focus.
- **Jade Lattice** — Jade does not flinch. What strikes it rings, and the ringing carries back. Crystal Sphere DDT 35% (self, 2 rounds, 40 AP) and Crystal Cave Reflect 40% (self, 2 rounds); both element-less, all four stat types.
- **Flawless Sphere** — A sphere without seam gives a blow no edge to catch and no face to break. Crystal Sphere DDT row only (35%, self, 2 rounds, nearly every non-pierce hit); the same 40 AP cast carries the exposure half unchanged.
- **Adamant Core** — What cannot be cut does not need to hurry; it sharpens while the enemy tires. Sphere DDT reaches 45% with the route; +3 IDG lifts Shippū's self buff to 38%, or 40% with Prismatic Focus.
- **Light Turned Back** — The cavern keeps no light for itself; it returns every spark to the one who brought it. Crystal Cave Reflect row only (40% of each hit returned, pierce included, 60% cap); 2 rounds after a 60 AP area cast, cooldown 7.
- **Entombed in Crystal** — Walls that bite, and a heart that cuts sharper for every blow they turn away. Cave Reflect reaches 50% with the route; the +3 IDG rides Shippū's self buff (38%, or 40% with Prismatic Focus), not the Cave.

- **Splintered Mountain Heart:** The full damage route puts +5 raw Crystal power on all three attacks: Prism 50 -> 55, Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave 40 -> 45. Flaw in the Stone as the fourth purchase takes both exposure rows to 39% with Prismatic Focus, so an enemy marked by Sphere or the Cave takes more from a Prism cast in the following two rounds; Shippū's self buff sits at 37%. Jade Lattice is the alternative fourth purchase (Sphere DDT 37%, Cave Reflect 42%) for a caster who opens with the Cave and expects to be hit.
- **Break Along the Flaw:** The exposure route takes both Increase Damage Taken rows from 35% to 42% for the two rounds after each cast, pierce excluded. Sphere's single-target mark amplifies element-less hits of any stat type and Ninjutsu hits of any element; the Cave's area mark amplifies Crystal, Earth, Fire and element-less hits of any stat type; both count allies' hits, and an enemy under both marks takes both. The capstone's +3 IDG with Prismatic Focus lifts Shippū's self buff to 40%, and Razor Lattice adds +2 power to all three Crystal attacks; Jade Lattice is the defensive alternative fourth purchase.
- **Adamant Core:** The bastion route takes Crystal Sphere's Decrease Damage Taken from 35% to 45% for the two rounds after each 40 AP cast, and the capstone's +3 IDG with Prismatic Focus lifts Shippū's self buff to 40%, so the kit both weathers more and hits harder in the Shippū window. Prismatic Focus also leaves both exposure rows at 37% and Jade Lattice the Cave Reflect at 42%. Light Turned Back (Reflect 45%) is the alternative fourth purchase for a pure defender who skips the offensive root.
- **Entombed in Crystal:** The reprisal route takes Crystal Cave's Reflect from 40% to 50%, ten points under the 60% per-hit cap, for the two rounds after the Cave lands, and its capstone's +3 IDG with Prismatic Focus lifts Shippū's self buff to 40%, so the caster who weathers the counter-attack hits back harder in the next window. Prismatic Focus also leaves both exposure rows at 37% and Jade Lattice the Sphere DDT at 37%. Flawless Sphere (DDT 40%, Reflect 50%, IDG 38%) is the alternative fourth purchase for a caster who never buys into the offensive root.

## Design notes

- Reference reuse: flats copy Taiyo Kami nearly verbatim where rows match (Prismatic Focus = Dawnheart; Razor Lattice and Splintered Mountain Heart = Crown of Cinders and Solar Cataclysm; Flawless Sphere and Adamant Core = Golden Mantle and Sovereign Sun), as the kit shares Taiyo's three-row burst and IDG/IDT glue. Departures: no DDG or Afterburn rows, so Jade Lattice pairs DDT with Reflect and Reflect gets a +3/+5 route; IDT gets a +2 Hidden Art and +3 capstone, held to +7 on two rows.
- Capstone secondaries: Break Along the Flaw, Adamant Core and Entombed in Crystal all carry +3 IDG on purpose. IDG is the only kit tag no Hidden Art carries, so it is the only secondary that cannot out-bid a sibling purchase (+2 IDT on Entombed in Crystal would make 01-04-06-09 dominated by 01-06-09-10). The cost is that three routes share IDG 40% and differ by primary. User-owned alternative that stays non-dominated: Entombed in Crystal +2 DDT instead of +3 IDG (Reprisal DDT 39%, IDG 37%).
- Main tree and passives: for the two rounds after its cast, Shippū's IDG row (Crystal/Earth/Fire/None, Ninjutsu filter) amplifies any Ninjutsu hit and any Crystal, Earth, Fire or element-less hit of any stat type: later Crystal strikes, basic attacks, and any matching normal jutsu or weapon (normal-jutsu catalog unverified). Main-tree or item IDG adds to it; the bloodline IDG passive (25 + 0.15/lvl) multiplies on top. Both IDT rows amplify allies' matching hits too. Pierce ignores IDG/IDT/DDT.
- Delivery and uptime: every jutsu has cooldown 7. Kōsai Shippū and Crystal Cave are 60 AP circle spawns on an OTHER_USER target at range 4; Prism is a 60 AP single-target shot; Crystal Sphere a 40 AP single-target cast. Buff and exposure rows are live only in the two rounds after the cast round, so staggered casts give enhanced windows on four to six of every seven rounds. SELF rows (Shippū IDG, Sphere DDT, Cave Reflect) land on the caster at cast time once the cast has a legal target.
- Fourth-purchase choices: Burst (01, 02, 03) adds Flaw in the Stone (exposure 39%) or Jade Lattice (DDT 37%, Reflect 42%); Exposure (01, 04, 05) Razor Lattice (+2 Damage) or Jade Lattice; Bastion (06, 07, 08) Prismatic Focus (IDG 40%, exposure 37%) or Light Turned Back (Reflect 45%); Reprisal (06, 09, 10) Prismatic Focus (IDG 40%) or Flawless Sphere (DDT 40%). Capstone-less hybrids (e.g. 01, 02, 04, 06) spread +2 to +4 over five tags; all 14 legal full builds are non-dominated.
- Defensive routes and untouched rows: Sphere's DDT and the Cave's Reflect rows list all four stat types and no element, so each matches nearly every hit the caster takes (DDT excludes pierce; Reflect includes it and bypasses shields). 45% and 50% stay under the caps (100; Reflect 60% of the hit); the 15% Earth DDT passive multiplies. Prism's wound and the Jade summon are unsupported and get nothing. Skill-tree effects and passives are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Crystal is not exclusive in the census (Sea-Maiden's Kiss, a deferred record, carries Crystal on 5 rows, 3 of them damage), and whether any NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN jutsu carries Crystal is unverified (no non-bloodline catalog with effect rows exists in the repository). The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification; a bare element match would leak to that kit.
- Resolver: 3 of the 8 supported rows (Crystal Sphere DDT and IDT, Crystal Cave Reflect) have no element and fall back to None under the current resolver, so they are unreachable without the proposed classification change; reaching them with affectedElements including None would also reach every element-less row on any jutsu the player casts.
- Exposure breadth: +7 makes both IDT rows 42% for two rounds (Sphere single, Cave area); both amplify allies' hits as well as the caster's (pierce excluded), and an enemy under both marks takes both: 84% of the staged base added to a hit matching both filters (e.g. Prism). Held to +7, not +10, as the tag reaches two rows; team value was not simulated. Trim Break Along the Flaw's +3 IDT first.
- Reflect cap and uptime: 50% leaves ten points of headroom under the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage cap, but main-tree or item Reflect sources add to it and could saturate the cap, wasting part of the +10. Reflect pays only when the caster is hit during the two rounds after a 60 AP Cave cast; realized value depends on enemy behaviour and was not simulated.
- IDG stacking: Shippū's 35% row already stacks additively with any main-tree or item IDG and the bloodline IDG passive (about 28.75% at level 25) multiplies the result; the +5 ceiling adds five additive points to one row. Each row stays far below the 100 cap, but the combined per-hit amplification in a Shippū window was not simulated against peer kits.
- Decrease Damage Taken scope: DDT is a damage modifier and does not touch pierce hits; the Sphere's 35 -> 45 window also only exists when the 40 AP cast has a legal OTHER_USER target (any living non-caster user, allies included) in range 4. The exposure half of the same cast is unchanged by the DDT nodes and lands on whoever is targeted.
- Damage calibration: Burst reaches +5 on three rows (Prism 55, Shippū 45, Cave 45), Taiyo Kami parity; the strongest row-weighted full build is 25 on 8 rows (Burst or Exposure) against Taiyo Kami's 31 on 9, as this kit has one fewer supported row and no Afterburn. If the tree proves weak, Jade Lattice is the lever (a +1 Damage secondary would raise the ceiling to the +6 guardrail).
- Delivery and friendly fire: Shippū's and the Cave's damage and the Cave's exposure reach only enemies on the spawn tiles (friendly fire ENEMIES; no ally-hazard rows); their SELF rows land on the caster at cast time. Prism's damage (ALL) and Sphere's exposure (none = ALL) land on an ally if one is targeted: the Damage nodes raise that hit, the IDT nodes that ally's exposure (42% with 01, 04, 05).

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Crystal Essence jutsu inherit Crystal potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
