# Blood-Enchanted Eyes — Tithe of the Red Eye

**Bloodline:** Blood-Enchanted Eyes (BR-011, rank S, `ovZIWu28ANn-Cij5TjT8S`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Shadow classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Shadow (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Shadow; two castable rows per keystone) · secondary Enemy exposure (Increase Damage Taken + Afterburn) · tertiary Self sustain (Decrease Damage Taken, Lifesteal) with Increase/Decrease Damage Given support.

Damage is the kit's broadest supported tag: four Shadow rows (Ring of Spilled Blood 40, Crimson Tithe 40, Reaper's Embrace 50, Crimson Impact 40), three of them AoE, split two-and-two across the Wraith Pendant and Reaper's Ring keystones, so with a keystone equipped exactly two of them are castable (none without one). Increase Damage Taken reaches three enemy-debuff rows at 35% (Sanguine Plague on the Pendant side; Crimson Impact and Thousand Strike on the Ring side) and Sanguine Plague also carries the kit's only Afterburn row, so exposure is the secondary emphasis and the Pendant-side pressure route. The base kit's two 40 AP self casts, Unholy Enhancement (IDG 35, DDG 35) and Hemocure (DDT 35, IDG 35), are ungated and anchor the Foundations; Hemocure's DDT and Crimson Impact's Lifesteal 20% (Ring side) give the two sustain routes. Absorb, vamp, shield, stun, pierce and the other unsupported rows are untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Shadow-classified Blood-Enchanted Eyes jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Scarlet Gaze** — The red eye opens; it sees where the blood runs thin, and it does not blink. IDG: Unholy Enhancement, Hemocure 35% (ungated, 40 AP). IDT: Sanguine Plague (Pendant), Crimson Impact, Thousand Strike (Ring) 35%; gated.
- **Opened Veins** — Every cut the eye marks is a cut that bleeds a little longer. Damage 40/40 on Ring of Spilled Blood, Crimson Tithe (Wraith Pendant); 50/40 on Reaper's Embrace, Crimson Impact (Reaper's Ring).
- **Rite of Exsanguination** — What begins as a wound ends as an emptying. Nothing is left in the vessel. Same four Shadow Damage rows: 40 -> 46 and 50 -> 56 with Opened Veins; only the equipped keystone's pair is castable in a battle.
- **Carrion Fever** — The sickness in the blood wakes with every blow and feeds on the heat of it. Sanguine Plague row 1 Afterburn 35% (enemy, 2 rounds, 40 AP, Wraith Pendant); extra damage on each non-pierce hit the target takes.
- **Red Pestilence** — Once the fever takes, the body fights for the enemy. Every strike lands twice. Sanguine Plague Afterburn 35 -> 45% with Carrion Fever (60% cap per hit); IDT 35 -> 40% on Plague, Crimson Impact, Thousand Strike.
- **Iron in the Blood** — The blood of the eye runs thick and heavy; blades slide off it and arms grow tired. DDT: Hemocure 35% (ungated), Reaper's Embrace 15% (Ring circle). DDG: Unholy Enhancement 35% (ungated), Ring of Spilled Blood 30% (Pendant).
- **Closed Wounds** — Flesh knits before the blade has finished its pass. Hemocure row 1 DDT 35% (self, 2 rounds, 40 AP, no gate) and Reaper's Embrace row 3 DDT 15% (ally-area circle, Reaper's Ring).
- **Deathless Vitality** — The heart has forgotten how to stop; the arm that strikes it forgets its strength. Hemocure DDT 35 -> 43% and Embrace DDT 15 -> 23% on the full route; DDG 35 -> 40% on Unholy Enhancement, 30 -> 35% on Ring of Spilled Blood.
- **Crimson Thirst** — The eye drinks before the mouth does, and it is never satisfied. Crimson Impact row 1 Lifesteal 20% (self, 2 rounds, 60 AP AoE, Reaper's Ring); shares the 60% leech budget with Embrace's vamp.
- **Feast of the Fallen** — What is taken from the enemy is not returned. Every mouthful feeds the next blow. Crimson Impact Lifesteal 20 -> 28% on the full route; IDG 35 -> 40% on Unholy Enhancement and Hemocure (both ungated) with Scarlet Gaze.

- **Rite of Exsanguination:** Damage +6 lifts whichever keystone pair is equipped: Ring of Spilled Blood and Crimson Tithe to 46 on the Wraith Pendant, or Reaper's Embrace to 56 and Crimson Impact to 46 on the Reaper's Ring, with the bloodline's Shadow/None IDG passive multiplying that raw power downstream. Scarlet Gaze is the required ancestor (+2% IDG on both ungated self buffs, +2% IDT on the exposure rows) and Iron in the Blood is the fourth purchase for a little mitigation and suppression. It is the only example build that adds Damage power (five other legal allocations carry Opened Veins' +2 or the full +6) and it gives up Afterburn, deep DDT and Lifesteal entirely.
- **Red Pestilence:** The Wraith Pendant pressure build: Sanguine Plague's Afterburn goes 35% -> 45% and its exposure 35% -> 40% for the same two-round 40 AP cast, so every non-pierce hit the plagued enemy takes (Ring of Spilled Blood, Crimson Tithe, normal jutsu, weapons, allies) is multiplied by the exposure and then burns again at 45% (60% cap per hit). The IDT also reaches Crimson Impact and Thousand Strike if the Reaper's Ring is worn instead, so the capstone is never dead on the other keystone, but the Afterburn half is Pendant-only. Iron in the Blood rounds it with +2% DDT/DDG; no Damage power at all, and the capstone deliberately carries only two modifiers (design note 3).
- **Feast of the Fallen:** The Reaper's Ring sustain build: Crimson Impact's Lifesteal goes 20% -> 28% for its two-round window, so every hit in that window (Reaper's Embrace at its base 50, since this build adds no Damage power; normal jutsu; Thousand Strike's pierce if lifesteal applies to it) heals more, and the capstone's +3% IDG with Scarlet Gaze takes both ungated self buffs, Unholy Enhancement and Hemocure, from 35% to 40% so the leech feeds on bigger hits. Scarlet Gaze also adds +2% IDT on Impact and Thousand Strike and Iron in the Blood +2% DDT/DDG. With Embrace's own 25% vamp the combined leech is 53%, under the 60% shared cap. No Damage power, no Afterburn, no deep mitigation; on the Wraith Pendant the Lifesteal half is dead and the build is the weakest example (castable row-weighted 18).
- **Deathless Vitality:** The keystone-independent build: Hemocure, a 40 AP self cast with no item gate, becomes 43% DDT for its two rounds (Reaper's Embrace's ally-area circle 23% on the Ring side once the caster stands in it), and the capstone's +3% DDG with Iron in the Blood takes Unholy Enhancement's suppression to 40% and Ring of Spilled Blood's spiral to 35% on the Pendant side. Beyond Scarlet Gaze's +2% IDG/IDT it carries no offensive modifier: Damage power, Afterburn and Lifesteal are unchanged, so it trades every offensive route for mitigation plus suppression. The alternative fourth purchase (Crimson Thirst instead of Scarlet Gaze) is the pure turtle at 43% DDT, 40% DDG, 23% lifesteal. Castable row-weighted it is 24 on the Pendant and 29 on the Ring (design note 3).

## Design notes

- Node split: four routes map onto the kit's four distinct non-glue tags (Damage, Afterburn, Decrease Damage Taken, Lifesteal), each a Foundation -> Hidden Art -> Advanced Art chain of 3 BP. Increase Damage Given, Increase Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Given are glue tags: each appears on exactly one Foundation at +2 and as one +3 capstone secondary (IDG: Scarlet Gaze and Feast of the Fallen; IDT: Scarlet Gaze and Red Pestilence; DDG: Iron in the Blood and Deathless Vitality), never on a Hidden Art, so no capstone secondary equals a sibling Hidden Art's value and every one of the 14 full-budget allocations stays numerically non-dominated. The defensive capstone's secondary is Decrease Damage Given rather than Increase Damage Given so that the mitigation route carries no offence multiplier (Draft 2 change). Two Advanced Arts under one Foundation cost 5 BP, under different Foundations 6 BP; the cap is 4.
- Keystone structure: the repository item reference (skills/building-tnr-content/references/item.md, KEYSTONE is a native equip slot; swapping the equipped keystone swaps the active side) means the Wraith Pendant triplet (Ring of Spilled Blood, Crimson Tithe, Sanguine Plague) and the Reaper's Ring triplet (Reaper's Embrace, Crimson Impact, Thousand Strike) are mutually exclusive in a battle while Unholy Enhancement and Hemocure are always castable. Castable supported rows per side: Pendant = Damage 2, IDG 2, DDG 2, IDT 1, DDT 1, Afterburn 1, Lifesteal 0; Ring = Damage 2, IDG 2, DDG 1, IDT 2, DDT 2, Afterburn 0, Lifesteal 1. Routes were chosen so that Damage (two rows per side) and the Hemocure fortress work on either keystone, the Afterburn route is the Pendant build and the Lifesteal route is the Ring build. Each Foundation carries at least one ungated row (IDG on Scarlet Gaze; DDT and DDG on Iron in the Blood), so neither Foundation is fully dead without a keystone, but Scarlet Gaze's IDT half, the Hidden Arts Opened Veins, Carrion Fever and Crimson Thirst, the capstones Rite of Exsanguination and Red Pestilence, and Feast of the Fallen's Lifesteal half are entirely keystone-gated and deliver nothing without the matching keystone.
- Calibration (Draft 2, re-derived from this kit's castable rows per keystone side, with Taiyo Kami as the scale, not the template): Foundations +2/+2 as the reference. Damage: Opened Veins +2, Rite of Exsanguination +4 (route +6, the guardrail ceiling) because only two Damage rows are castable at once (row-weighted 12) against the reference's +5 on three (15). Decrease Damage Taken: Closed Wounds +2 (two rows, the Hidden Art multi-row value) and Deathless Vitality +4 (route +8: Hemocure 43%, Embrace circle 23%) because the kit has two DDT rows where the reference had one (+10 on one row = 10; here 8 on the Pendant, 16 on the Ring). Afterburn: Carrion Fever +3, Red Pestilence +7 (route +10, single application row, as the reference). Lifesteal: Crimson Thirst +3, Feast of the Fallen +5 (route +8, 20% -> 28%, single Ring-side row, no Foundation carries it). Capstone secondaries +3 (DDG on Deathless Vitality, IDG on Feast of the Fallen, IDT on Red Pestilence). Red Pestilence carries two modifiers (Eternal Noon has three) as a deliberate discount of two fully keystone-gated tags (OPEN_DECISIONS D5(b)): with the Pendant equipped its IDT reaches one castable row, exactly as Eternal Noon's does, and the Plague build already matches the Burst build on that side (castable row-weighted 25 vs 24); restoring the reference's +3% IDG would lift it to 29 and make it the Pendant side's dominant build. Castable row-weighted totals of the four examples (flat x castable rows, the same arithmetic as the audit's row-weighted total restricted to one side): Burst 24 Pendant / 26 Ring; Plague 25 / 20; Sustain 18 / 28; Fortress 24 / 29. The audit's all-row total now ranks the Burst build first (42) and the Fortress fifth (36); in Draft 1 the Fortress was first (40) with IDG +5 on both ungated self buffs. Residual for review (OPEN_DECISIONS D8): on the Ring side the Fortress still edges the Sustain build (29 vs 28) by this count because the Reaper's Embrace DDT row is counted at full weight although it is a 15% base, FRIENDLY-area ground circle that reaches the caster only from the round after the cast; Taiyo Kami's strongest full build is 31 by the same metric and its Fortress 24.
- Item-gate weighting (OPEN_DECISIONS D5): this draft deviates from the literal D5(b) wording ('keep flats on gated-only tags lower'). Six of the eight jutsu need one of the two bloodline keystones and the two ungated jutsu are both self buffs, so the kit has no attack at all without a keystone; a keystone is the kit's normal state rather than optional equipment. The gate discount is therefore applied as 'count only the rows castable on one keystone side' (never all four Damage rows or all three IDT rows at once) and as the two-modifier Red Pestilence, not as a further haircut on the flats: Damage +6 (4/4 rows gated) sits at the guardrail ceiling, Afterburn +10 (1/1 gated) and Lifesteal +8 (1/1 gated) are at full weight, and IDT +5 (3/3 gated) is below the reference's +7. The cross-bloodline comparison should read these flats as full-weight per castable row; if the user prefers the literal D5(b) haircut, the levers are Rite of Exsanguination +3 (Damage +5), Red Pestilence +5 (Afterburn +8) and Feast of the Fallen +4 (Lifesteal +7).
- Interaction with the main tree and passives: the bloodline's own Increase Damage Given passive (25% + 0.15/level on Water/Fire/Lightning/None/Shadow, Highest stat) multiplies every enhanced Damage row downstream. The efficiency ratio was read at the pin (tags.ts 3477-3510, 75-115, 3407-3414): ten of the kit's eleven buff/debuff rows (both IDG, both DDG, both DDT, the Afterburn, the Lifesteal and the Crimson Impact / Thousand Strike IDT rows) carry statTypes Bukijutsu/Genjutsu/Ninjutsu/Taijutsu, no generalTypes and no element, and the four Damage rows carry statTypes ['Highest'], generalTypes ['Highest'], elements ['Shadow']. getEfficiencyRatio replaces 'Highest' with the caster's highest offence stat (realizeTag copies highestOffence from the casting user onto damage effects and modifiers alike), pushes nothing for an empty generalTypes list and pushes 'None' for an empty element list, then returns 1 on any overlap; so every one of those ten rows applies at ratio 1 to the kit's own Shadow Damage rows, to basic attacks and weapons (statTypes ['Highest'], no element) and to any normal jutsu whose stat resolves to one of the four or that has no element. Sanguine Plague's IDT row is element-only (Fire/Lightning/None/Shadow/Water): it amplifies the kit's Shadow damage and element-less damage but not off-element elemental damage such as Wind. Unholy Enhancement's and Hemocure's IDG and DDT rows are two-round self buffs, so raising them also raises damage and mitigation on normal jutsu, weapons and the unsupported Thousand Strike pierce inside the window; Unholy Enhancement is cast at an OTHER_USER in range 5, so its self buff needs a legal target (see the friendly-fire risk). Increase Damage Taken, Decrease Damage Given and Afterburn are enemy debuffs, so they also amplify or soften allies' and normal jutsu damage against that enemy; Afterburn's extra damage is computed from every non-pierce hit the target takes, so Thousand Strike's pierce does not trigger it. None of these downstream effects were simulated.
- Delivery and uptime: every jutsu in the kit has a 7-round cooldown; the four Damage casts and Thousand Strike cost 60 AP, the buff/debuff casts 40 AP. Ring of Spilled Blood is a GROUND-targeted spiral shoot around the caster and Reaper's Embrace an EMPTY_GROUND radius-1 circle spawn whose move row relocates the caster to the clicked centre tile after the other ground effects of that round resolve (move sorts last, util.ts 1218), so its Damage lands on enemies standing in the circle on the cast round and its DDT reaches the caster and any ally in the circle from the following round for the ground effect's remaining duration (process.ts 323-340 re-applies ground effects each round as one-round user effects). Crimson Impact's Lifesteal, IDT and Damage ride one 60 AP AoE. Skill-tree effects (and therefore Bloodright potency) are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, and bloodline keystone jutsu need the keystone equipped.
- Fourth-purchase choices: after any 3 BP route the natural fourth is the other Foundation (+2/+2 on two more tags). Hybrids with no Advanced Art are legal and non-dominated, e.g. 01+02+04+06 (+2 Damage, +3 Afterburn, +2 on IDG/IDT/DDT/DDG) or 01+02+06+09 (+2 Damage, +3 Lifesteal, +2 on four tags). The strongest unadvertised builds are 01+02+03+04 (+6 Damage, +3 Afterburn, +2 IDG/IDT: the all-in Pendant burst with no mitigation, all-row 37), 01+02+04+05 (+2 Damage, +10 Afterburn, +5 IDT, +2 IDG: the all-in Pendant burn, 37) and 06+07+08+09 (+8 DDT, +5 DDG, +3 Lifesteal: the all-defence Ring turtle with no offensive glue, 29).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Shadow is not exclusive in the census. Thirteen other bloodlines carry Shadow damage rows, including the approved Godstorm Eclipse, Night Parade of A Thousand Demons, Oblivion Seal and Shadow Weaver, and the excluded sibling records Blood-Enshrined Eyes and Blood-Enthralled Eyes; whether any NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN jutsu carries Shadow is unverified. The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification; a bare element match would leak to those kits.
- Resolver: 10 of the 15 supported rows (every IDG, DDT, DDG, Afterburn and Lifesteal row, and the IDT rows on Crimson Impact and Thousand Strike) have no element and fall back to None under the current resolver, so they are unreachable without the proposed classification change; reaching them with affectedElements including None would also reach every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts. Only the four Shadow Damage rows and Sanguine Plague's exposure row (Fire/Lightning/None/Shadow/Water) match Shadow directly.
- Item gates: six of the eight jutsu require a bloodline keystone (Wraith Pendant 1wJsQWvK2L0fhziIE4gnA or Reaper's Ring heZTbHCjEACrJ-_Vrulq4). Every Damage, IDT, Afterburn and Lifesteal row is gated, so the Burst, Plague and Sustain routes deliver nothing without the matching keystone, and Plague and Sustain are mutually exclusive if only one keystone can be equipped at a time. That exclusivity is inferred from the repository item reference, not read at the game-source pin; if two keystones can be worn, Damage +6 reaches all four rows at once (row-weighted 24, above the reference's 15) and the Damage route is correspondingly stronger than calibrated.
- Lifesteal cap: lifesteal and vamp share one 60%-of-pre-shield-damage leech budget per hit. The route tops out at 28%; Reaper's Embrace's own 25% vamp (same keystone side, cast-round only) would bring a simultaneous window to 53%, leaving seven points of headroom before any normal-tree or item leech saturates the cap. Lifesteal requires both combatants alive and is blocked by healprevent on the caster; whether it leeches from Thousand Strike's pierce damage was not verified.
- Afterburn: a single application row (Sanguine Plague, 2 rounds, 40 AP, Pendant side). 45% is under the 60% per-hit cap, but any second Afterburn source on the same target saturates quickly. Its realised value depends on how many non-pierce hits land during two rounds and was not simulated; Thousand Strike's pierce is excluded by the handler.
- Efficiency ratio: the captured rows do carry stat filters. Every IDG, DDG, DDT, Afterburn and Lifesteal row and the Crimson Impact / Thousand Strike IDT rows have statTypes ['Bukijutsu', 'Genjutsu', 'Ninjutsu', 'Taijutsu'], no generalTypes and no element; Sanguine Plague's IDT row has no stat filter and elements Fire/Lightning/None/Shadow/Water; the four Damage rows have statTypes ['Highest'], generalTypes ['Highest'], elements ['Shadow']. No combat simulation: non-dominance of the 14 allocations is an arithmetic result over per-row additions, not evidence of equal combat strength; AoE target counts, placement, uptime, AP economy and the IDG passive's multiplication were not modelled.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Blood-Enchanted Eyes jutsu inherit Shadow potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
