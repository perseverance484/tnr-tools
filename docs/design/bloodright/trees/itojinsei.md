# Itojinsei — The Iron Loom

**Bloodline:** Itojinsei (BR-036, rank A, `j4_9ypNqYq8Ifbqf-9D2r`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Magnet classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Magnet (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Magnet burst (Damage on all four casts: Iron Web Entrapment, Wire Blast Plus, Spiraling Wires, Lightning Threads) · secondary Enemy suppression (Decrease Damage Given on Wire Blast Plus and Eternal Embrace) · tertiary Wielder amplification and exposure (self Increase Damage Given on Spiraling Wires and Eternal Embrace; Increase Damage Taken on Lightning Threads).

Nine supported rows sit on five casts, all cooldown 7: four Magnet Bukijutsu Damage rows (Iron Web Entrapment 45; Wire Blast Plus, Spiraling Wires and Lightning Threads 40) carry the burst, and two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows (Spiraling Wires, Bukijutsu-filtered with no element; Eternal Embrace, Lightning/Magnet/None/Wind) apply on top of a 25% + 0.15/level Increase Damage Given passive. The Control trait reaches potency through two universal Decrease Damage Given rows (Wire Blast Plus 30%, an ally-hazard circle; Eternal Embrace 35%) and one 35% Increase Damage Taken row on Lightning Threads. The kit has no Decrease Damage Taken, Reflect, Lifesteal or Heal row, so there is no defensive route: the wielder root Taut Filament carries the Damage and Increase Damage Given routes, the control root Tangling Snare the Decrease Damage Given and Increase Damage Taken routes. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Magnet jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Magnet jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Taut Filament** — Draw the wire tight and every tremor on the line is yours to read. Self damage buffs 35 → 37% (Spiraling Wires; Eternal Embrace), 2 rounds each.
- **Razor Strands** — Fine enough to vanish, keen enough to part iron. Iron Web Entrapment 45 → 47; Wire Blast Plus, Spiraling Wires and Lightning Threads 40 → 42 EP; four Magnet hits, 60 AP each, cooldown 7.
- **Severing Lattice** — Where the web closes, nothing leaves in one piece. Same four Damage rows; route total +5 Damage (Iron Web Entrapment 50, the other three 45 EP). Stun, redirection and decrease-heal riders are unsupported.
- **Threadbound Vigor** — Each strand feeds the wielder's arm; the pull becomes the strike. Both self buffs 37 → 40% with Taut Filament: Spiraling Wires (Bukijutsu or element-less hits), Eternal Embrace (Lightning/Magnet/Wind/none).
- **Weaver Ascendant** — The loom no longer needs hands. It needs only a will. Route total +10%: both self buffs 35 → 45% (×1.45 × 1.45 ≈ ×2.10 when both windows overlap); Lightning Threads exposure +2% (39% with Tangling Snare).
- **Tangling Snare** — A caught thing fights the wire, and the wire does not tire. Wire Blast Plus 30 → 32% and Eternal Embrace 35 → 37% Decrease Damage Given (the Wire Blast circle is an ally hazard); Lightning Threads exposure 35 → 37%.
- **Constricting Coils** — Every struggle draws the knot a little tighter. Both suppression rows 32 → 35% / 37 → 40% with Tangling Snare; two-round enemy debuffs on a 60 AP circle and a 40 AP circle.
- **Iron Cocoon** — Wrapped, stilled, and kept: a silence spun from steel. Route total +10%: Wire Blast Plus 30 → 40%, Eternal Embrace 35 → 45%; Lightning Threads exposure +2% (39% with Tangling Snare).
- **Conductive Seam** — Lightning runs the thread and finds the fault beneath the skin. Lightning Threads exposure only: 37 → 40% with Tangling Snare; one row, 2 rounds per 60 AP single-target cast, cooldown 7.
- **Galvanic Marionette** — Strung and sparking, the enemy dances to a current not its own. Route total +10%: Lightning Threads exposure 35 → 45%; both suppression rows +2% (34% / 39% with Tangling Snare).

## Design notes

- Recalibration (RUL-2026-10-03-005): the amplifier, suppression and exposure routes move onto the +10% band in the Foundation +2% / Hidden +3% / Advanced +5% shape. To keep Increase Damage Taken within +10% in every legal allocation, its +2% Foundation glue moves from Taut Filament to Tangling Snare, the exposure route's own root. Changes: Taut Filament loses +2% IDT; Threadbound Vigor +2% → +3%; Weaver Ascendant IDG +3% → +5%; Tangling Snare DDG +3% → +2% and gains +2% IDT; Constricting Coils +2% → +3%; Iron Cocoon DDG +4% → +5%. Names, tiers and edges are unchanged.
- Routes: Burst +5 Damage (Razor Strands +2, Severing Lattice +3); Amplifier +10% Increase Damage Given (Taut Filament, Threadbound Vigor, Weaver Ascendant); Suppression +10% Decrease Damage Given (Tangling Snare, Constricting Coils, Iron Cocoon); Exposure +10% Increase Damage Taken (Tangling Snare, Conductive Seam, Galvanic Marionette). Capstone secondaries are +2%: Weaver Ascendant and Iron Cocoon add exposure, Galvanic Marionette adds suppression. Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +5, IDG +10%, DDG +10%, IDT +10%.
- Arithmetic and filters (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3/§3b): each jutsu IDG/IDT row multiplies a matching hit in turn (×(1 + p/100)) and each DDG row reduces it in turn (×(1 − p/100)), so same-tag rows compound rather than sum; the bloodline-sourced 25% + 0.15/level IDG passive applies last. Spiraling Wires' IDG row (Bukijutsu/Speed/Strength, no element) reaches any Bukijutsu-typed or element-less hit, weapons and basic attacks included; Eternal Embrace's any Lightning, Magnet, Wind or element-less hit. Both DDG rows (four stat types, no element) reach every non-pierce hit; nothing here touches pierce.
- Delivery and uptime: every cast is cooldown 7; every percentage row is live the two rounds after its cast round, never on its own hit. Iron Web Entrapment (line), Wire Blast Plus (circle spawn), Spiraling Wires and Lightning Threads (single) cost 60 AP, so one Damage cast fits a 100 AP turn, with room for Eternal Embrace (40 AP circle spawn). All five are OTHER_USER: any living non-caster user, an ally included, is a legal target; SELF rows land on the caster either way.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Tangling Snare (suppression 32%/37%, exposure 37%) or Threadbound Vigor (buffs 40%); Amplifier takes Tangling Snare or Razor Strands (47/42/42/42 EP); Suppression takes Taut Filament (buffs 37%) or Conductive Seam (exposure 42%); Exposure takes Taut Filament or Constricting Coils (suppression 37%/42%). All 14 full allocations are non-dominated and every node appears in one; four examples, one per capstone.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Self-buff reach: both Increase Damage Given rows reach beyond the kit (Spiraling Wires: any Bukijutsu-typed or element-less hit; Eternal Embrace: any Lightning, Magnet, Wind or element-less hit), so the +10% maximum raises normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks in each window; where the windows overlap both apply (×1.45 × 1.45 ≈ ×2.10, against ×1.82 at base), before the bloodline-sourced 25% + 0.15/level passive applies last. Uptime not simulated.
- Ally hazard: Wire Blast Plus row 1 (Decrease Damage Given) is an OTHER_USER AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire none (=ALL), so allies in the radius-1 circle also receive the debuff; the caster is never a target of an OTHER_USER area jutsu (SOURCE_MECHANICS §4b). The suppression route takes it from 30% to 40%; Eternal Embrace's row is friendly fire ENEMIES. Positioning decides.
- Exposure concentration: Increase Damage Taken has one row (Lightning Threads, 60 AP, single target, 2 rounds); the exposure route reaches 45%, amplifying player, weapon and ally Lightning, Magnet, Wind or element-less non-pierce hits once per 7-round cast.
- Damage concentration: +5 EP lands on four Magnet rows, the widest Damage reach in this tree; the hits are formula-calculated and the IDG passive multiplies them, so +5 EP is not +5 damage. Not simulated.
- Eternal Embrace delivery: the self IDG row is realized on the caster at cast time (actions.ts 980–1004/1060–1075; SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b) whenever the OTHER_USER circle spawn has a legal target, an ally included; not positional, no ground effect. The DDG row (friendly fire ENEMIES) hits each living enemy in the radius-1 circle once and is withheld from allies. Buffprevent is unsupported.
- Classification: Magnet is shared with Houkyuken (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 6 of 9 kit rows carry Magnet; the three element-less rows (Wire Blast Plus and Eternal Embrace suppression, Spiraling Wires' self buff) need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver. Off-kit Magnet coverage is unverified.
- No defensive route: the kit has no Decrease Damage Taken, Reflect, Lifesteal or Heal row, and the 15% Lightning Decrease Damage Taken passive is not a jutsu row, so every build is offense or control; stun (Iron Web Entrapment), redirection (Spiraling Wires), decrease-heal (Lightning Threads) and buffprevent (Eternal Embrace) receive nothing.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring; normal-tree potency policy is unapproved (combined stacking audit required). The dominance count ignores delivery and is structural evidence only. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Magnet jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
