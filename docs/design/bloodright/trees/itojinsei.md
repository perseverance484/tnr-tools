# Itojinsei — The Iron Loom

**Bloodline:** Itojinsei (BR-036, rank A, `j4_9ypNqYq8Ifbqf-9D2r`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Magnet classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Magnet (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Magnet burst (Damage on all four casts: Iron Web Entrapment, Wire Blast Plus, Spiraling Wires, Lightning Threads) · secondary Enemy suppression (Decrease Damage Given on Wire Blast Plus and Eternal Embrace) · tertiary Wielder amplification and exposure (self Increase Damage Given on Spiraling Wires and Eternal Embrace; Increase Damage Taken on Lightning Threads).

Nine supported rows sit on five casts, all cooldown 7: four Magnet Bukijutsu Damage rows (Iron Web Entrapment 45; Wire Blast Plus, Spiraling Wires and Lightning Threads 40) carry the burst, and two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows (Spiraling Wires, Bukijutsu-filtered with no element; Eternal Embrace, Lightning/Magnet/None/Wind) stack over a 25% + 0.15/level Increase Damage Given passive. The Control trait reaches potency through two universal Decrease Damage Given rows (Wire Blast Plus 30%, an ally-hazard circle; Eternal Embrace 35%) and one 35% Increase Damage Taken row on Lightning Threads (Lightning/Magnet/None/Wind). The kit has no Decrease Damage Taken, Reflect, Lifesteal or Heal row, so the tree offers no defensive route: root Taut Filament carries the burst and amplifier routes, root Tangling Snare the suppression and exposure routes; row-weighted full builds over all 14 legal allocations run 19–32 against the Taiyo Kami reference's 16–31 on nine rows (Advanced-bearing builds 22–32 against 20–31).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Magnet-classified Itojinsei jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Conductive Seam** — Lightning runs the thread and finds the fault beneath the skin. Lightning Threads exposure only: 35→38% (40% with Taut Filament); one row, 2 rounds per 60 AP single-target cast, cooldown 7.
- **Galvanic Marionette** — Strung and sparking, the enemy dances to a current not its own. Exposure to route total 43% (45% with Taut Filament, +10); suppression 33→35% / 38→40% (37% / 42% with Constricting Coils).

## Design notes

- Reference reuse: the two-root, four-route topology is Taiyo Kami's and the burst side copies it verbatim (Taut Filament = Dawnheart +2 IDG/+2 IDT; Razor Strands/Severing Lattice = Crown of Cinders/Solar Cataclysm, +2/+3 Damage), since the kit has the same offensive mix of Damage, self IDG and one IDT row. Departures: no Afterburn or DDT row exists, so an IDG amplifier route replaces Emberwake/Eternal Noon and Tangling Snare is one +3 DDG root forking into suppression and exposure routes.
- Flats by coverage. Damage (four Magnet rows, 45/40/40/40) keeps the reference +2/+3 ladder (max +5: Iron Web 50, the rest 45), row-weighted 20 against Taiyo Kami's 15. Increase Damage Given (two stacking self rows reaching beyond the kit): +2/+2/+3, max +7 (42%), the two-row ceiling. Decrease Damage Given (two rows, one an ally hazard): +3/+2/+4, max +9 (39%/44%), under the single-row +10. Increase Damage Taken (one row): +2 root, +3/+5 route, max +10 (45%) only with both roots.
- Capstone secondaries: Weaver Ascendant and Iron Cocoon add +2 exposure under Conductive Seam's +3; Galvanic Marionette's +2 suppression equals Constricting Coils' +2, so the capstone-less hybrid (both roots, Coils, Seam: DDG +5, IDT +5) is dominated by Galvanic Marionette in Coils' place (IDT +10). Row-weighted nodes: Foundations 6/6, Hidden Arts 8/4/4/3, Advanced Arts 12/8/10/9; full builds over all 14 allocations 19–32 against Taiyo Kami's 16–31 (Advanced-bearing 22–32 against 20–31).
- Arithmetic and filters (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3/§3b): jutsu IDG/DDG/IDT rows add power/100 × staged base and same-tag rows sum; only the 25% + 0.15/level IDG passive multiplies. Spiraling Wires' IDG row (Bukijutsu/Speed/Strength, no element) reaches any Bukijutsu-typed or element-less hit, weapons and basic attacks included; Eternal Embrace's any Lightning, Magnet, Wind or element-less hit. Both DDG rows (four stat types, no element) reach every non-pierce hit; nothing here touches pierce.
- Delivery and uptime: every cast is cooldown 7; every percentage row is live the two rounds after its cast round, never on its own hit. Iron Web Entrapment (line), Wire Blast Plus (circle spawn), Spiraling Wires and Lightning Threads (single) cost 60 AP, so one Damage cast fits a 100 AP turn, with room for Eternal Embrace (40 AP circle spawn). All five are OTHER_USER: any living non-caster user, an ally included, is a legal target; SELF rows land on the caster either way.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Tangling Snare (suppression 33%/38%) or Threadbound Vigor (buffs 39%); Amplifier takes Tangling Snare or Razor Strands (47/42/42/42); Suppression takes Taut Filament (buffs 37%, exposure 39%) or Conductive Seam (exposure 40%); Exposure takes Taut Filament (exposure 45%) or Constricting Coils (suppression 37%/42%, exposure 43%). 13 of 14 full allocations are non-dominated (the Coils + Seam hybrid above) and every node appears in one. Four examples, one per capstone.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Self-buff leakage: both Increase Damage Given rows reach beyond the kit (Spiraling Wires: any Bukijutsu-typed or element-less hit; Eternal Embrace: any Lightning, Magnet, Wind or element-less hit), so the +7 maximum raises normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks in each window; overlapping windows add 84% of the staged base alongside the multiplying 25% + 0.15/level passive. Uptime not simulated.
- Ally hazard: Wire Blast Plus row 1 (Decrease Damage Given) is an OTHER_USER AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire none (=ALL), so allies in the radius-1 circle also receive the debuff; the caster is never a target of an OTHER_USER area jutsu (SOURCE_MECHANICS §4b). The suppression route takes it from 30% to 39%; Eternal Embrace's row is friendly fire ENEMIES. Positioning decides.
- Exposure concentration: Increase Damage Taken has one row (Lightning Threads, 60 AP, single target, 2 rounds); the full route reaches 45% (+10, the percentage guardrail; reference IDT maximum +7), amplifying player, weapon and ally Lightning, Magnet, Wind or element-less non-pierce hits per 7-round cast. User-owned fallback: Conductive Seam +3 / Galvanic Marionette +4 (+9 with Taut Filament).
- Damage concentration: +5 power lands on four Magnet rows, so the burst route is row-weighted 20 against the reference's 15 on three rows (+33%) and the strongest full build reads 32 against Taiyo Kami's 31; floors sit 1–3 above the reference (19 vs 16; Advanced-bearing 22 vs 20). User-owned fallback: Razor Strands +2 / Severing Lattice +2 (+4; Iron Web 49, others 44). Not simulated.
- Eternal Embrace delivery: the self IDG row is realized on the caster at cast time (actions.ts 980–1004/1060–1075; SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b) whenever the OTHER_USER circle spawn has a legal target, an ally included; not positional, no ground effect. The DDG row (friendly fire ENEMIES) hits each living enemy in the radius-1 circle once and is withheld from allies. Buffprevent is unsupported.
- Classification: Magnet is the single signature element on all four Damage rows, but Houkyuken [INCLUDE] carries Magnet on 6 rows (4 damage), so the label works only as a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification, not a bare element match. Under the current resolver 6 of 9 supported rows match Magnet directly; the three element-less rows fall back to None. Normal-jutsu collision unverified.
- No defensive route: the kit has no Decrease Damage Taken, Reflect, Lifesteal or Heal row, and the 15% Lightning Decrease Damage Taken passive is not a jutsu row, so every build is offense or control; stun (Iron Web Entrapment), redirection (Spiraling Wires), decrease-heal (Lightning Threads) and buffprevent (Eternal Embrace) receive nothing.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring; normal-tree potency policy is unapproved (combined stacking audit required). The dominance count ignores delivery, so 13/14 non-dominated is structural evidence only; user-owned re-tunes restoring 14/14: Galvanic Marionette suppression +1, or Constricting Coils +3 / Iron Cocoon +3 (route still +9).

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Itojinsei jutsu inherit Magnet potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
