# Itojinsei — The Iron Loom

**Bloodline:** Itojinsei (BR-036, rank A, `j4_9ypNqYq8Ifbqf-9D2r`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Magnet classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Magnet (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Magnet burst (Damage on all four casts: Iron Web Entrapment, Wire Blast Plus, Spiraling Wires, Lightning Threads) · secondary Enemy suppression (Decrease Damage Given on Wire Blast Plus and Eternal Embrace) · tertiary Wielder amplification and exposure (self Increase Damage Given on Spiraling Wires and Eternal Embrace; Increase Damage Taken on Lightning Threads).

Nine supported rows sit on five casts, all cooldown 7: four Magnet Bukijutsu Damage rows (Iron Web Entrapment 45; Wire Blast Plus, Spiraling Wires and Lightning Threads 40) carry the burst, and two 35% self Increase Damage Given rows (Spiraling Wires, Bukijutsu-filtered with no element; Eternal Embrace's circle, Lightning/Magnet/None/Wind) stack over a 25% + 0.15/level Increase Damage Given passive. The Control trait reaches potency through two universal Decrease Damage Given rows (Wire Blast Plus 30%, an ally-hazard circle; Eternal Embrace 35%) and one 35% Increase Damage Taken row on Lightning Threads (Lightning/Magnet/None/Wind). The kit has no Decrease Damage Taken, Reflect, Lifesteal or Heal row, so the tree offers no defensive route: root Taut Filament carries the burst and amplifier routes, root Tangling Snare the suppression and exposure routes; row-weighted full builds run 19–32 against the Taiyo Kami reference's 20–31 on nine rows.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Magnet-classified Itojinsei jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Node split: root Taut Filament (+2 self Increase Damage Given on two rows, +2 exposure on one) forks into a Damage route (Razor Strands → Severing Lattice) and an amplifier route (Threadbound Vigor → Weaver Ascendant). Root Tangling Snare (+3 Decrease Damage Given on two rows, the one broadly useful control tag outside the offense root) forks into a suppression route (Constricting Coils → Iron Cocoon) and an exposure route (Conductive Seam → Galvanic Marionette). 2/4/4 nodes; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Flats scaled by coverage. Damage reaches four Magnet rows (45/40/40/40) and keeps the reference ladder +2/+3 (max +5: Iron Web 50, the rest 45), row-weighted 20 against Taiyo Kami's 15. Increase Damage Given reaches two stacking self rows that leak beyond the kit: +2/+2/+3 (max +7, 42%), the two-row ceiling. Decrease Damage Given reaches two universal rows, one an ally hazard: +3/+2/+4 (max +9; 39%/44%), under the single-row +10. Increase Damage Taken is one row: +2 root, +3/+5 route, max +10 (45%) only with both roots.
- Capstone secondaries never out-bid a sibling Hidden Art on its own tag: Weaver Ascendant and Iron Cocoon add +2 exposure under Conductive Seam's +3; Galvanic Marionette adds +2 suppression, equal to Constricting Coils' +2 and below the root's +3; Severing Lattice is single-tag. Row-weighted node values: Foundations 6/6, Hidden Arts 8/4/4/3, Advanced Arts 12/8/10/9; full builds 19–32 (Taiyo Kami 20–31). Maxima over all legal builds: Damage +5, IDG +7, IDT +10, DDG +9; not jointly attainable.
- Main tree, passives, pierce: Spiraling Wires' self Increase Damage Given row is Bukijutsu/Speed/Strength-filtered with no element, so at the pin (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3) it multiplies every Bukijutsu, Speed/Strength or element-less hit, weapons and basic attacks included; Eternal Embrace's row (Lightning/Magnet/None/Wind) multiplies same-element and element-less hits. Both stack on the 25% + 0.15/level IDG passive, which also multiplies the enhanced Magnet Damage rows. The DDG rows list all four stat types with no element and reach every non-pierce hit; no modifier here touches pierce.
- Delivery and uptime: every cast is cooldown 7 and every percentage row lasts 2 rounds. Iron Web Entrapment (A, line, range 4), Wire Blast Plus (D, circle spawn, range 4), Spiraling Wires (C, single, range 5) and Lightning Threads (A, single, range 4) all cost 60 AP, so one Damage cast lands per round. Eternal Embrace is the only cheap cast: a 40 AP B-rank circle spawn on an enemy (range 5) with two supported rows; its self buff row needs a legal enemy target. Lightning Threads is the single exposure carrier: one row, 2 rounds per 7.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Tangling Snare (suppression 33%/38%) or Threadbound Vigor (buffs 39%); Amplifier takes Tangling Snare or Razor Strands (47/42/42/42 power); Suppression takes Taut Filament (buffs 37%, exposure 39%) or Conductive Seam (exposure 40%); Exposure takes Taut Filament (exposure 45%) or Constricting Coils (suppression 37%/42%, exposure 43%). All 14 legal full allocations are non-dominated on tag totals and every node appears in one. Four examples: each capstone closes a different role.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Self-buff leakage: neither Increase Damage Given row has a binding filter at the pin (Spiraling Wires: Bukijutsu/Speed/Strength, no element; Eternal Embrace: Lightning/Magnet/None/Wind), so the +7 maximum raises matching normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks in each two-round window, and both rows stack on the caster (84%) over the 25% + 0.15/level passive. Uptime not simulated.
- Ally hazard: Wire Blast Plus row 1 (Decrease Damage Given) is an AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire none (=ALL), so allies and the caster standing in the circle also lose damage given; the suppression route takes it from 30% to 39%. Eternal Embrace's row is friendly fire ENEMIES. Positioning, not the node, decides who is suppressed.
- Exposure concentration: Increase Damage Taken has one row (Lightning Threads, A rank, 60 AP, range 4, single target, 2 rounds) and the full route reaches 45% (+10), amplifying the player's, weapons' and allies' same-element or element-less non-pierce hits. The whole value rides one cast per seven rounds; pierce hits are unaffected (modifiers run before pierce).
- Damage concentration: +5 power lands on four Magnet rows at once, so the burst route is row-weighted 20 against the reference's 15 and the strongest full build (Severing Lattice + Tangling Snare) reads 32 against Taiyo Kami's 31 on nine rows. All four hits cost 60 AP on cooldown 7, so one lands per round; the final value is user-owned and no combat simulation was performed.
- Eternal Embrace delivery: its self Increase Damage Given row sits on an OTHER_USER AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN (B rank, 40 AP, range 5) and needs a legal enemy target; the dossier marks it self, and the batch-1 lesson says a spawn's effect is re-applied to whoever stands in it. SELF-row handling on an enemy-targeted spawn was not separately source-verified. Its buffprevent row is unsupported.
- Classification: Magnet is the single signature element on all four Damage rows, but Houkyuken [INCLUDE] carries Magnet on 6 rows (4 damage), so the label works only as a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification, not a bare element match. Under the current resolver 6 of 9 supported rows match Magnet directly; the three element-less rows fall back to None. Normal-jutsu collision unverified.
- No defensive route: the kit has no Decrease Damage Taken, Reflect, Lifesteal or Heal row, and the 15% Lightning Decrease Damage Taken passive is not a jutsu row, so every build is offense or control; stun (Iron Web Entrapment), redirection (Spiraling Wires), decrease-heal (Lightning Threads) and buffprevent (Eternal Embrace) receive nothing. Galvanic Marionette's +2 suppression equals, not undercuts, Constricting Coils' +2.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring; normal-tree potency policy is not approved, so a combined stacking audit is required before implementation. The dominance count treats every tag as a gain and ignores delivery, so 14/14 non-dominated is structural evidence only. No combat simulation was performed; balance values remain user-owned.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Itojinsei jutsu inherit Magnet potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
