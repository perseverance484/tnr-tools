# Reptilian — Cold Blood and Scale

**Bloodline:** Reptilian (BR-057, rank B, `z78aHAPRQfctiw7_qNXMx`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Reptilian classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Reptilian (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Increase Damage Given — two stacking 35% rows (Reptile Chimera self, Cool-Blooded Empowerment circle) · secondary Decrease Damage Taken — Reptile Chimera hide (single row, +10 ceiling) · tertiary Lifesteal — Cool-Blooded Empowerment feeding (single ally/self row, +10 ceiling).

Four supported rows sit on two 40 AP, cooldown-7 buffs: Reptile Chimera (A-rank, SELF) carries 35% Increase Damage Given (Ninjutsu-filtered, element-less) and 35% Decrease Damage Taken (all four stat types); Cool-Blooded Empowerment (D-rank, ALLY, radius-1 circle) carries 35% Increase Damage Given and 35% Lifesteal (Highest-filtered, element-less), each for 2 rounds. The caster is a legal ALLY target at the pin, so both IDG rows can sit on the caster at once; IDG is primary by coverage; its +7 two-row route (above Taiyo Kami's two-row +5) is an open user decision isolated in design note 2 and held as a reversible Draft 2 placeholder. Mitigation and lifesteal are single rows and take the Taiyo Kami +10 single-row shape. Summoning: Reptile Zoo (summon, injectjutsus, visual; PVP only) and its injected Reptile King are unsupported.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on four rows (Increase Damage Given x2 on Reptile Chimera and Cool-Blooded Empowerment; Decrease Damage Taken x1 on Reptile Chimera; Lifesteal x1 on Cool-Blooded Empowerment); Summoning: Reptile Zoo and its injected Reptile King carry only summon, injectjutsus and visual rows. The self-facing root (Chimeric Blood) forks into an Increase Damage Given route and a Decrease Damage Taken route; the circle-facing root (Blood of the Brood) is a single Foundation → Hidden → Advanced lifesteal chain. A fourth route would have to repeat one of the three tags: a second IDG Hidden Art under Blood of the Brood would twin Apex Instinct and open a second path past the IDG route ceiling (design note 2), and a second DDT or Lifesteal chain would breach the +10 single-row guardrail in a full build or duplicate an existing route on the same single row, so it is declined. Because the lifesteal side has three nodes, every legal 4 BP build contains Chimeric Blood; a mirrored layout would only move the universal node to the other root. Three distinct complete builds (Predation, Fortress, Pack sustain) exist; Predation and Fortress each have two fourth-purchase choices, Pack sustain's fourth purchase is always Chimeric Blood. 8 legal full-budget allocations, all 8 numerically non-dominated (three capstone-less hybrids only by one lifesteal point; design note 5).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Reptilian jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Chimeric Blood** — The blood remembers every beast it has been and lends their strength to its bearer. Reptile Chimera IDG 35→37% and DDT 35→37%; Cool-Blooded Empowerment IDG 35→37% (circle: allies and caster); 2 rounds per 40 AP cast.
- **Apex Instinct** — Nothing in the swamp hunts the hunter. It learns to strike first. Both IDG rows (+2: 39%); element-less non-pierce hits, plus elemental hits of Ninjutsu (Chimera) or the caster's highest stat (Empowerment).
- **Jaws of the Chimera** — Three sets of jaws close at once, and whatever they tear is swallowed. Both IDG rows to 42% (route +7); Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal 35→37% (+2, below Serpent's Thirst). Each window 2 rounds.
- **Hardened Scales** — Each shed skin leaves the next one thicker. Reptile Chimera Decrease Damage Taken only (+3 more: 40% with Chimeric Blood); every stat-typed non-pierce hit; 2 rounds per cast.
- **Basilisk Carapace** — Blades skate off the carapace; what little bleeds is drunk back. Reptile Chimera DDT to 45% (route +10); Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal +2 (37%, circle recipients). Pierce bypasses DDT.
- **Blood of the Brood** — The brood shares one cold blood, and what one drinks warms them all. Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal 35→38% for same-village allies in the radius-1 circle (caster included); 2 rounds; 60% leech cap.
- **Serpent's Thirst** — A serpent does not stop at the first mouthful. Same Lifesteal row (+3 more: 41% with Blood of the Brood); includes pierce hits the recipient lands; needs both combatants alive.
- **Feast of Scales** — The brood feeds until its hide is too thick to bite through. Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal to 45% (route +10, under the 60% cap); Reptile Chimera DDT +2 (37%, or 39% with Chimeric Blood).

- **Jaws of the Chimera:** The offense build: both Increase Damage Given rows reach 42%, so the caster's own Reptile Chimera window and the circle's buff on allies (and on the caster standing in it) land harder; Hardened Scales makes Chimera's hide 40%, so the same 40 AP cast is also the mitigation window; the +2 Lifesteal secondary adds a trickle of sustain. Blood of the Brood is the alternative fourth purchase (circle lifesteal 40%, hide 37%) for a player who fights beside allies.
- **Basilisk Carapace:** The survival build: Reptile Chimera's Decrease Damage Taken reaches 45% for its 2-round window every 7 rounds, its Increase Damage Given 37%, and Cool-Blooded Empowerment's Lifesteal 40% (+2 from the capstone, +3 from Blood of the Brood) for same-village allies in the circle (caster included). Apex Instinct is the alternative fourth purchase (IDG 39% on both rows, no extra lifesteal) for a solo player who wants the hide and a sharper bite.
- **Feast of Scales:** The lifesteal build: Cool-Blooded Empowerment's Lifesteal reaches 45% for every same-village ally in the circle, caster included, leaving 15 points of headroom under the 60% leech budget for the bloodline's own 10% Ninjutsu passive; the capstone's +2 Decrease Damage Taken and Chimeric Blood's +2 take Chimera's hide to 39% and its buff to 37%. Chimeric Blood is the only legal fourth purchase; the route trades the Chimera routes' depth for the strongest team sustain.

## Design notes

- Node split: Chimeric Blood (self-facing; both Reptile Chimera rows plus the circle's IDG row) forks into Apex Instinct → Jaws of the Chimera (Increase Damage Given 2/2/3 = +7 on two rows) and Hardened Scales → Basilisk Carapace (Decrease Damage Taken 2/3/5 = +10 on one row). Blood of the Brood (circle-facing) runs one chain, Serpent's Thirst → Feast of Scales (Lifesteal 3/3/4 = +10 on one row). 2/3/3 Foundations/Hidden/Advanced; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP.
- USER DECISION (open): IDG ceiling. 01/02/03 = +7 on two rows that stack on the caster (+14), above Taiyo Kami's two-row +5. (a) Hold +5 (01 +2/02 +1/03 +2): Predation 17 row-weighted (4.25/row, 124%); peak becomes Fortress 19 (138%). (b) Keep +7, drop the +2 capstone secondaries: Predation 19 (138%); (a)+(b): peak 17. (c) Keep Draft 1 (21, 5.25/row, 153%) and rule Reptilian, Lycanthropy and Shinseina Ki (+7 two-row IDG) as one roster decision. Draft 2 holds (c) as a reversible placeholder.
- Passives: the Ninjutsu IDG passive (20 + 0.15/lvl, multiplicative) and the 10% Ninjutsu Lifesteal passive stack with the rows; both buffs in one round (80% AP) give 70% → 84% IDG for 2 rounds (Predation). Both IDG rows are element-less and jutsu-sourced (additive): each adds its percentage to every element-less non-pierce hit of any stat type plus elemental hits of its stat type (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3). DDT lists all four stat types, so it blunts Genjutsu hits the 10% Genjutsu IDT passive worsens.
- Delivery: both carriers: 40 AP, cooldown 7, 350 chakra/stamina; windows are 2 following rounds, so at most 2 of 7 rounds carry enhanced value. Reptile Chimera is SELF, range 0. Cool-Blooded Empowerment is ALLY, range 4, AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN: the clicked tile must hold a living same-side user (the caster's own tile qualifies: util.ts 2750–2752); its rows apply once at cast as user effects to every same-village user within radius 1 (actions.ts 1239–1240; friendlyFire FRIENDLY). Not a ground effect.
- Fourth purchases: Predation (01,02,03) adds Hardened Scales (hide 40%) or Blood of the Brood (lifesteal 40%); Fortress (01,04,05) adds Blood of the Brood (lifesteal 40%) or Apex Instinct (IDG 39%); Pack sustain (06,07,08) only Chimeric Blood. Capstone-less hybrids (01,02,04,06; 01,02,06,07; 01,04,06,07) are legal and numerically non-dominated by one lifesteal point each (+1 LS against +5 DDT or +3 two-row IDG); not recommended builds. Row-weighted peak 21, raw 14 (design note 2).
- Shape: Decrease Damage Taken and Lifesteal are single rows and take the Taiyo Kami single-row +10 shape (2/3/5 and 3/3/4); capstone secondaries (+2) sit below the +3 Hidden Arts on their tags, so no capstone out-bids a sibling Hidden Art. For comparison, Taiyo Kami's two stacking IDG rows stop at +5 and Loup-Garou's single IDG row reaches +8; the IDG stack here is the only ceiling left open (design note 2).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: every supported row is element-less, so no existing element isolates this kit; under the current resolver the rows are reachable only with affectedElements including None, which also reaches every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts. The tree assumes the proposed bloodline-keyed classification extension (label Reptilian). Normal-jutsu collision is unverified.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: Reptile Chimera and Cool-Blooded Empowerment can both buff the caster in the same window (the caster is a legal ALLY target at the pin), so the Predation build adds +14 percentage points across the two rows (70% → 84%) on top of the Ninjutsu passive; BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is true at the pin. Combined final damage was not simulated.
- Lifesteal budget: lifesteal and vamp share one 60%-of-damage leech budget per hit. The route reaches 45% on Cool-Blooded Empowerment; a caster inside the circle adds the 10% Ninjutsu passive (55%), so any vamp or lifesteal from the main tree or items saturates the capstone. The buffed user heals from their own hits (pierce included); both must be alive; healprevent on the recipient blocks it.
- Highest resolution: Cool-Blooded Empowerment's rows carry statTypes [Highest], which realizeTag resolves from the casting user's highestOffence (G16), so on an ally the efficiency ratio tests the caster's highest stat, not the ally's. Element-less rows still match every element-less hit of any stat type; only elemental hits of another stat type are excluded. Undefined highestOffence unverified.
- Team dependence and village check: the circle buffs land on same-village users (getTargetUser compares villageId), so an ally from another village is not reached even though the tile filter accepts any same-side user; in a 1v1 the circle reaches only the caster. Blood of the Brood's chain therefore carries less value solo (single recipient) than in team fights.
- Summoning: Reptile Zoo (PVP only) and its injected Reptile King (SPECIAL, empty bloodlineId, summon row only) have no supported rows; no node changes them, and the injected child is not counted under open decision D4 option (a). The 10% Genjutsu Increase Damage Taken passive remains a standing weakness outside the Chimera window.
- Per-row intensity: the strongest full build is row-weighted 21 on 4 rows (5.25 per row against Taiyo Kami's 3.44), above the balance matrix's 125% outlier line, because every purchase lands on one of four rows; raw additions (14) are the lowest among drafted trees. Isolated as a user decision with options (a)/(b)/(c) in design note 2; Draft 2 does not retune it.
- No adverse supported rows, hidden rows, item gates or ally-hazard rows exist in this kit; the unsupported visual rows (adverse) are untouched by every node. Uptime, whether a same-round buff applies to that round's hits, and AP sequencing were not verified against combat timing.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Reptilian jutsu inherit Reptilian potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
