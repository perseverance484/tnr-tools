# Reptilian — Cold Blood and Scale

**Bloodline:** Reptilian (BR-057, rank B, `z78aHAPRQfctiw7_qNXMx`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Reptilian classification extension / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Reptilian (classification extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Increase Damage Given — two stacking 35% rows (Reptile Chimera self, Cool-Blooded Empowerment circle) · secondary Decrease Damage Taken — Reptile Chimera hide (single row, +10% route) · tertiary Lifesteal — Cool-Blooded Empowerment feeding (single ally/self row, +5% hard ceiling).

Four supported rows sit on two 40 AP, cooldown-7 buffs: Reptile Chimera (A-rank, SELF) carries 35% Increase Damage Given (Ninjutsu-filtered, element-less) and 35% Decrease Damage Taken (all four stat types); Cool-Blooded Empowerment (D-rank, ALLY, radius-1 circle) carries 35% Increase Damage Given and 35% Lifesteal (Highest-filtered, element-less), each live for the 2 rounds after its cast round. The caster is a legal ALLY target at the pin, so both IDG rows can sit on the caster at once; IDG is primary by coverage, and because the two rows compound on the caster its route is held to +5% (×1.40 × 1.40 = ×1.96 when both buffs overlap, against ×1.8225 unenhanced; the same per-row weight as the single-row +10% Decrease Damage Taken route). Lifesteal is a single row capped at +5%. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all jutsu of the proposed Reptilian classification (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Summoning: Reptile Zoo (summon, injectjutsus, visual; PVP only) and its injected Reptile King are unsupported.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has three supported tags on four rows (Increase Damage Given x2 on Reptile Chimera and Cool-Blooded Empowerment; Decrease Damage Taken x1 on Reptile Chimera; Lifesteal x1 on Cool-Blooded Empowerment); Summoning: Reptile Zoo and its injected Reptile King carry only summon, injectjutsus and visual rows. The self-facing root (Chimeric Blood) forks into an Increase Damage Given route and a Decrease Damage Taken route; the circle-facing root (Blood of the Brood) is a single lifesteal chain already at the +5% hard ceiling. Chimeric Blood is therefore a universal node (in all 8 legal 4 BP builds), kept because no 1 BP leaf under Blood of the Brood has a distinct role: Lifesteal cannot rise past +5%; a leaf that beats Chimeric Blood in the 06,07,08 closure needs Increase Damage Given +3% or more, which opens a second path above the +5% IDG route (06,09,01,02 reaches +6%), or Decrease Damage Taken +3%, which twins Hardened Scales on the same row; smaller flats leave that closure dominated by, or tied with, Pack sustain. A mirrored layout would only move the universal node to the other root. Three distinct complete builds (Predation, Fortress, Pack sustain) exist; Predation and Fortress each have two fourth-purchase choices, Pack sustain's is always Chimeric Blood.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Reptilian-classified jutsu (requires classification extension). Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Chimeric Blood** — The blood remembers every beast it has been and lends their strength to its bearer. Reptile Chimera IDG and DDT 35 → 37%; Cool-Blooded Empowerment IDG 35 → 37% (circle: same-village allies, caster); 2 rounds per 40 AP cast.
- **Apex Instinct** — Nothing in the swamp hunts the hunter. It learns to strike first. Both IDG rows (+1% more: 38% with Chimeric Blood); element-less non-pierce hits, plus elemental hits of Ninjutsu (Chimera) or the caster's highest stat (Empowerment).
- **Jaws of the Chimera** — Three sets of jaws close at once, and whatever they tear is swallowed. Both IDG rows to 40% (route +5%); Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal 35 → 37% (+2%, equal to Serpent's Thirst). Each window 2 rounds.
- **Hardened Scales** — Each shed skin leaves the next one thicker. Reptile Chimera Decrease Damage Taken only (+3% more: 40% with Chimeric Blood); every stat-typed non-pierce hit; 2 rounds per cast.
- **Basilisk Carapace** — Blades skate off the carapace; what little bleeds is drunk back. Reptile Chimera DDT to 45% (route +10%); Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal +2% (37%, circle recipients). Pierce bypasses DDT.
- **Blood of the Brood** — The brood shares one cold blood, and what one drinks warms them all. Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal 35 → 36% for same-village allies in the radius-1 circle (caster included); 2 rounds; 60% leech cap.
- **Serpent's Thirst** — A serpent does not stop at the first mouthful. Same Lifesteal row (+2% more: 38% with Blood of the Brood); includes pierce hits the recipient lands; needs both combatants alive.
- **Feast of Scales** — The brood feeds until its hide is too thick to bite through. Cool-Blooded Empowerment Lifesteal to 40% (route +5%, the hard ceiling); Reptile Chimera DDT +2% (37%, or 39% with Chimeric Blood).

- **Jaws of the Chimera:** The offense build: both Increase Damage Given rows reach 40% (+5%), so the caster's own Reptile Chimera window and the circle's buff on allies (and on the caster standing in it) land harder; Hardened Scales makes Chimera's hide 40%, so the same 40 AP cast is also the mitigation window; the +2% Lifesteal secondary (37%) adds a trickle of sustain. Blood of the Brood is the alternative fourth purchase (circle lifesteal 38%, hide 37%) for a player who fights beside allies.
- **Basilisk Carapace:** The survival build: Reptile Chimera's Decrease Damage Taken reaches 45% (+10%) for its 2-round window every 7 rounds, its Increase Damage Given 37%, and Cool-Blooded Empowerment's Lifesteal 38% (+2% from the capstone, +1% from Blood of the Brood) for same-village allies in the circle (caster included). Apex Instinct is the alternative fourth purchase (IDG 38% on both rows, no extra lifesteal) for a solo player who wants the hide and a sharper bite.
- **Feast of Scales:** The lifesteal build: Cool-Blooded Empowerment's Lifesteal reaches 40% (+5%, the hard ceiling) for every same-village ally in the circle, caster included, leaving 20 points of the 60% leech budget, 10 of them taken by the bloodline's own Ninjutsu passive on the caster; the capstone's +2% Decrease Damage Taken and Chimeric Blood's +2% take Chimera's hide to 39% and its buff to 37%. Chimeric Blood is the only legal fourth purchase; the route trades the Chimera routes' depth for team sustain.

## Design notes

- Node split: Chimeric Blood (self-facing; both Reptile Chimera rows plus the circle's IDG row) forks into Apex Instinct → Jaws of the Chimera (Increase Damage Given 2/1/2 = +5% on two rows) and Hardened Scales → Basilisk Carapace (Decrease Damage Taken 2/3/5 = +10% on one row). Blood of the Brood (circle-facing) runs one chain, Serpent's Thirst → Feast of Scales (Lifesteal 1/2/2 = +5% on one row). 2/3/3 Foundations/Hidden/Advanced; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Recalibration (RUL-2026-10-03-005): Predation moves from +7% to the +5% band (Apex Instinct +2% → +1%, Jaws of the Chimera +3% → +2%), so each row reaches 40% and the two compound to ×1.96 on the caster (×1.8225 at base; ×2.10 at +10%). The +10% band (2/3/5) was not taken because every purchase lands on one of only four rows: it would put Predation at 27 row-weighted (6.75 per row, about twice Taiyo Kami's 3.44) against 17 for Fortress. Lifesteal is cut to the +5% hard ceiling: Blood of the Brood +3% → +1%, Serpent's Thirst +3% → +2%, Feast of Scales +4% → +2% (Empowerment 35 → 40%). Fortress is unchanged at +10% Decrease Damage Taken. Maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +5%, Decrease Damage Taken +10%, Lifesteal +5% (the capstones' +2% secondaries reach 3% with Blood of the Brood).
- Passives: the Ninjutsu IDG passive (20 + 0.15/lvl, multiplicative) and the 10% Ninjutsu Lifesteal passive stack with the rows. Both IDG rows are jutsu-sourced and element-less, so each multiplies every element-less non-pierce hit of any stat type plus elemental hits of its stat (§3, §3b); both cast in one round (80% AP) compound to ×1.96 over the next 2 rounds on Predation (×1.8225 unenhanced), and the Ninjutsu passive multiplies last. DDT lists all four stat types, so it blunts Genjutsu hits the 10% Genjutsu IDT passive worsens.
- Delivery: both carriers: 40 AP, cooldown 7, 350 chakra/stamina; windows are 2 following rounds, so at most 2 of 7 rounds carry enhanced value. Reptile Chimera is SELF, range 0. Cool-Blooded Empowerment is ALLY, range 4, AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN: the clicked tile must hold a living same-side user (the caster's own tile qualifies: util.ts 2750–2752); its rows apply once at cast as user effects to every same-village user within radius 1 (actions.ts 1239–1240; friendlyFire FRIENDLY). Not a ground effect.
- Fourth purchases: Predation (01,02,03) adds Hardened Scales (hide 40%) or Blood of the Brood (lifesteal 38%); Fortress (01,04,05) adds Blood of the Brood (lifesteal 38%) or Apex Instinct (IDG 38%); Pack sustain (06,07,08) only Chimeric Blood. Capstone-less hybrids are enumerated by the validator; see the audit.
- Reference reuse: the DDT route copies Taiyo Kami's Sunward Oath → Golden Mantle → Sovereign Sun (2/3/5) nearly verbatim; Chimeric Blood keeps Dawnheart's two-tag +2% shape. Departures: Basilisk Carapace's secondary is +2% Lifesteal, not +3% IDG; Blood of the Brood carries one tag, so the lifesteal chain is 1/2/2 under the +5% ceiling; IDG runs 2/1/2 on two stacking rows.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification (director decision): no row carries a non-None element, so no existing element identifies this kit. The proposal needs a classification extension: a new jutsu classification (placeholder 'Reptilian') assigned to jutsu records. It is not a bloodline-id selector; which jutsu carry it is a director/engine decision, and targeting None would reach every non-elemental row in the game. All three kit jutsu qualify only through that authored classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit coverage is unverified.
- IDG stacking: Reptile Chimera and Cool-Blooded Empowerment can both buff the caster in one window (the caster is a legal ALLY target); same-tag effects from different jutsu all apply, each as its own multiplier (§3b; process.ts 1692–1825), so Predation's rows give ×1.40 × 1.40 = ×1.96 (×1.8225 unenhanced) before the Ninjutsu passive multiplies last. Combined final damage was not simulated.
- Lifesteal budget: lifesteal and vamp share one 60%-of-damage leech budget per hit. The route reaches 40% on Cool-Blooded Empowerment; a caster inside the circle adds the 10% Ninjutsu passive (50%), so vamp or lifesteal from the main tree or items quickly fills the last 10 points. The buffed user heals from their own hits (pierce included); both must be alive; healprevent on the recipient blocks it.
- Highest resolution: Cool-Blooded Empowerment's rows carry statTypes [Highest], which realizeTag resolves from the caster's highestOffence (G16), so on an ally the ratio tests the caster's highest stat, not the ally's. Element-less rows still match every element-less hit of any stat type and exclude only elemental hits of another stat. highestOffence is a required field (types.ts 156; §4b).
- Team dependence and village check: the circle buffs land on same-village users (getTargetUser compares villageId), so an ally from another village is not reached even though the tile filter accepts any same-side user; in a 1v1 the circle reaches only the caster. Blood of the Brood's chain therefore carries less value solo (single recipient) than in team fights.
- Summoning: Reptile Zoo (PVP only) and its injected Reptile King (SPECIAL, empty bloodlineId, summon row only) have no supported rows; no node changes them, and injection provenance is not a selector. The 10% Genjutsu Increase Damage Taken passive remains a standing weakness outside the Chimera window.
- No adverse supported rows, hidden rows, item gates or ally-hazard rows exist in this kit; the unsupported visual rows (adverse) are untouched by every node. Neither buff acts in its cast round (§3b): a cast in round N is live in rounds N+1 and N+2, so hits in the 80% AP double-cast round get no bonus. Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. Uptime against real opponents was not simulated.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Reptilian-classified jutsu (requires classification extension). Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
