# Shakunetsu Sakura — Dragon of Falling Petals

**Bloodline:** Shakunetsu Sakura (BR-064, rank A, `aa1lZukkHpz6ihmcxLaei`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Scorch classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Scorch (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Scorch area damage on the Hiru-Sakura circles (Sakuragari, Sakura-ame) · secondary Increase Damage Taken exposure (Sakuragari circle, Sakura Dragon Tree) and the Sakura Dragon Tree self Increase Damage Given · tertiary Decrease Damage Given suppression (Sakura-ame) and Heal (Sakura Dragon Pearl).

Seven supported rows on four of the five casts. The two A-rank Hiru-Sakura circles carry the only Damage rows (Sakuragari 40, Sakura-ame 50 Scorch power at jutsu level 25, one hit on every user inside the radius-1 area) and are the declared primary; the kit's Damage Over Time trait lives on Dancing Embers' unsupported wound. Increase Damage Taken is the broadest tag by reach (two 35% rows on the Sakuragari circle and the Sakura Dragon Tree, all four stat types and no element, so every non-pierce hit on the exposed target is amplified), and the D-rank 40 AP Sakura Dragon Tree also carries the kit's single 35% self Increase Damage Given (Fire, None, Scorch, Wind) on top of the bloodline's same-element passive, so those two share the secondary. Decrease Damage Given (Sakura-ame 30%, enemies only) and the Sakura Dragon Pearl's 25-power Heal (250 HP per tick) are single rows and take the lesser routes.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Scorch-classified Shakunetsu Sakura jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Ember Dragon's Roots** — Night pearl and morning bough: the dragon stirs before the blossoms burn. Sakura Dragon Tree self damage buff 35→37% (2 rounds per 40 AP cast); Sakura Dragon Pearl Heal 25→27 power (270 HP per tick). 2 rows.
- **Kindled Boughs** — Every branch of the dragon tree catches, and the heat carries into each strike. Sakura Dragon Tree self buff only (one row, 2 rounds): 37→40% with Ember Dragon's Roots; Scorch, Fire, Wind and element-less hits.
- **Dragon in Full Blossom** — At the height of its bloom the dragon burns brightest and does not tire. Sakura Dragon Tree self buff 40→45% (the +10 route maximum); Sakura Dragon Pearl Heal 27→30 power (300 HP per tick, the +5 guardrail).
- **Branded by Petals** — Where a scorched petal lands, the mark stays and every blow finds it. Exposure on the Sakuragari circle (ally hazard) and Sakura Dragon Tree, 35→37% for 2 rounds; amplifies every non-pierce hit on the target.
- **Scorching Hanami** — The blossom-viewing turns to a hunt, and the dragon watches it burn. Both exposure rows 37→41% (route +6; Sakuragari circle is an ally hazard); Sakura Dragon Tree self buff 37→39% with Ember Dragon's Roots.
- **Falling Ember Petals** — Petals drift down already alight, and whatever they settle on burns. Sakuragari and Sakura-ame Scorch area damage 40→41 / 50→51 (ally hazard); Sakura-ame Decrease Damage Given 30→32% (enemies only). 3 rows.
- **Burning Petal Carpet** — The ground beneath the blossoms is a carpet of embers. Sakuragari and Sakura-ame Scorch area damage only (60 AP, range 4, cooldown 7): 41→43 / 51→53 with Falling Ember Petals. Ally hazard.
- **Conflagration in Bloom** — The whole orchard goes up at once. The same two Scorch area damage rows; the route totals +6 power (Sakuragari 40→46, Sakura-ame 50→56), then the Scorch IDG passive applies.
- **Smothering Petal Rain** — Blossom rain thick enough to choke the breath from a strike. Sakura-ame Decrease Damage Given only (one row, 2 rounds, enemies inside the circle): 32→35% with Falling Ember Petals.
- **Deluge of Burning Petals** — Under the burning rain nothing they throw lands whole, and the petals keep scalding. Sakura-ame suppression 35→40% (route +10); Sakuragari and Sakura-ame Scorch area damage +1 (44/54 with Burning Petal Carpet). Ally hazard.

## Design notes

- Node split: two roots mirror the kit's two faces. Ember Dragon's Roots (Pearl, Dragon Tree) forks into Kindled Boughs → Dragon in Full Blossom (Increase Damage Given) and Branded by Petals → Scorching Hanami (Increase Damage Taken). Falling Ember Petals (the two Hiru-Sakura circles) forks into Burning Petal Carpet → Conflagration in Bloom (Damage) and Smothering Petal Rain → Deluge of Burning Petals (Decrease Damage Given). 2F/4H/4A; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Flats scale by coverage. Damage reaches two rows: 1+2+3 = +6 (the guardrail). Increase Damage Given is one self row: 2/3/5 = +10 (35→45%). Increase Damage Taken reaches two rows that stack on one target, so it stops at +6 (2+4, 35→41%), under the +7 ceiling. Decrease Damage Given is one enemy row: 2/3/5 = +10 (30→40%). Heal is one raw-power row: 2+3 = +5 (300 HP a tick). Capstone secondaries under-bid the Hidden Art on their tag (IDG +2 < Kindled Boughs +3; Damage +1 < Burning Petal Carpet +2).
- Interactions: the Dragon Tree buff row (Fire, None, Scorch, Wind) multiplies the Scorch circles, element-less hits (basic attacks, weapons) and Fire/Wind jutsu in its 2-round window; the bloodline's same-element IDG passive (25 + 0.15/level) stacks on top. Both IDT rows list all four stat types and no element, so every hit on the exposed target, from the player, allies or weapons, is amplified. Pierce runs after the modifier pass and is neither raised nor amplified.
- Delivery: every cast has cooldown 7. Sakuragari and Sakura-ame are 60 AP OTHER_USER AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN casts at range 4; per the batch-1 Aerathiel read, their INHERIT rows land once on every other living user in the radius-1 circle, with no ground effect: one Scorch hit and 2-round debuffs per user. The Dragon Tree is a 40 AP single-target cast at range 5 (legal target needed; self buff and enemy exposure together). The Pearl is a 40 AP self cast; its Heal ticks on following rounds.
- Fourth purchases: Burst → Ember Dragon's Roots (buff 37%, Pearl 270) or Smothering Petal Rain (suppression 35%); Exposure → Falling Ember Petals (circles 41/51, suppression 32%) or Kindled Boughs (buff 42%); Amplifier → Branded by Petals (exposure 37%) or Falling Ember Petals; Suppression → Burning Petal Carpet (circles 44/54) or Ember Dragon's Roots (circles 42/52). No-capstone hybrids: 01,02,04,06 (buff 40%, exposure 37%) and 01,06,07,09 (circles 43/53, suppression 35%).
- Row weight (Σ flat × rows reached): the strongest full builds are 01,04,05,06 (22) and 01,02,04,05 (21) on 7 rows, 3.1 per row against Taiyo Kami's 31 on 9 rows (3.4). Burst builds score 17–18 because Damage reaches two rows, but each Damage point lands on every user in a circle and the Scorch passive multiplies it, so the metric undervalues area damage. All 14 legal full allocations are non-dominated and every node appears in at least one (audit). Not simulated.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Scorch is not exclusive in the census (Taiyo Kami carries 4 Scorch rows, 3 damage). Under the current resolver a Scorch modifier reaches the 3 Scorch rows directly and the 4 element-less rows only via None, which also reaches other non-elemental jutsu; the tree assumes the proposed bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification. NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT jutsu carrying Scorch is unverified.
- Ally hazard: Sakuragari rows 0 (Damage) and 1 (exposure) and Sakura-ame row 0 (Damage) are AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN rows with no friendlyFire value (=ALL), so allies inside the radius-1 circle take the enhanced Scorch hit and exposure too; the caster is never a target (batch-1 read). Nodes 06/07/08/10 (Damage) and 04/05 (IDT) raise that; positioning decides. Sakura-ame's suppression row is ENEMIES only.
- Exposure stacking: the Sakuragari circle and the Dragon Tree each apply a separate 2-round Increase Damage Taken debuff and BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is on at the pin, so a target caught by both carries 70% base and 82% at the route maximum, amplifying every non-pierce hit from the player, allies and weapons. The route is held to +6 and both IDT nodes sit on one branch for this reason; not simulated.
- Increase Damage Given leakage: the Sakura Dragon Tree self buff (Fire, None, Scorch, Wind) multiplies normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks in its window, not only kit casts, and stacks with the bloodline's same-element passive (about 29% at level 25) as separate effects; 45% is far below the 100 cap. Restricted casting scope does not restrict downstream benefit.
- Area delivery: the dossier footnote calls AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN rows re-applied ground effects reaching the caster; the batch-1 Aerathiel read found OTHER_USER circles apply INHERIT rows once, directly to users in the radius-1 area, with no ground effect and the caster excluded. This tree follows that read, unverified here; if the circles persist, the Damage route is worth more than stated.
- Unsupported rows: Dancing Embers (pierce 60, wound 30%), the Pearl's debuffprevent, Sakuragari's self shield and the visual rows receive no bonus. Because pierce bypasses the damage-modifier pass, the Amplifier and Exposure routes do not raise the pierce hit and the Suppression route does not reduce enemy pierce; the kit's B-rank attack is outside every node.
- Heal: the Pearl's Heal row has rounds 2 and applies on following rounds (static, ×10 HP per power), so +5 is 300 HP per tick against 250; tick count per cast and healprevent were not simulated. Heal appears only on the dragon root (Foundation +2, Dragon in Full Blossom +3), so the Amplifier capstone carries the whole sustain gain; the row is small enough that this is accepted rather than split.
- Rank and modes: an A-rank bloodline whose Damage rows are formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the Scorch IDG passive, so +6 raw power is not a linear +6. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring; normal-tree potency policy is not approved, so a combined stacking audit precedes any implementation. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Shakunetsu Sakura jutsu inherit Scorch potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
