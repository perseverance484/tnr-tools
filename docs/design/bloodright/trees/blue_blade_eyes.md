- **Sapphire Edge** — Blue chakra runs down the edge; what it touches keeps no edge of its own. Increase Damage Given 35 → 37% on Glacial Volley, Blue Crimson, Arctic Frost and Quintessential Flake (self); Decrease Damage Given 30 → 32% on Icebound Might's circle (enemy).
- **Honed Crescent** — Between strikes the crescent is honed, until the air itself parts before it. Burst set-up: all four self damage buffs 40% with Sapphire Edge (Glacial Volley, Blue Crimson, Arctic Frost, Quintessential Flake); they compound with one another on every matching hit in their windows.
- **Stroke That Splits Stone** — One stroke splits the boulder. Nothing you face is harder than granite. Burst payoff: +2 Damage on all five Ice attacks, Icebound Might, Glacial Volley and Blue Crimson 40 → 42 EP, Ice Shackles and Blade Resonance 45 → 47 EP; no row changes tier.
- **Frostbitten Grip** — Hands numbed by frost strike without conviction. Icebound Might Decrease Damage Given 35% with Sapphire Edge (every enemy in the circle, 2 rounds, 60 AP); the kit's only suppression row.
- **Numb to the Marrow** — Their blows lose all force in the cold, and what little still lands slides off the frost. Suppression: Icebound Might Decrease Damage Given 30 → 40% on the full route; guard rider Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 37% on Quintessential Flake and Sapphire Command.
- **Unblinking Sapphire** — Sapphire eyes that never close see the opening and the blow alike. Increase Damage Taken 35 → 37% on Sapphire Command, Blade Resonance and Arctic Frost (enemy); Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 37% on Quintessential Flake and Sapphire Command (self).
- **Hoarfrost Bulwark** — Hoarfrost thickens on the skin until steel slides off it. Decrease Damage Taken 40% with Unblinking Sapphire on Quintessential Flake and Sapphire Command (self rows, live 2 rounds after the cast).
- **Glacier Does Not Yield** — Stand in the cold long enough and nothing moves you; every blow breaks on the ice. Fortress: Decrease Damage Taken 35 → 45% on the full route on Quintessential Flake and Sapphire Command, the two casts that need no weapon.
- **Thirst of the Frost** — Frost does not merely bite. It drinks. Arctic Frost Lifesteal 40 → 42% (self, 2 rounds, 40 AP); leeches from every hit landed in the window, pierce included.
- **Winter Takes Its Due** — Winter asks nothing twice; what it takes from the enemy it keeps. Sustain offense: Arctic Frost Lifesteal 40 → 45% on the full route (the hard ceiling); exposure 40% with Unblinking Sapphire on Sapphire Command, Blade Resonance and Arctic Frost.

## Design notes

- Structure kept from Draft 4 (01→02→03, 01→04→05, 06→07→08, 06→09→10). Each Foundation pairs a self buff with an enemy debuff and its children lean to opposite sides: Sapphire Edge (Increase Damage Given + Decrease Damage Given) forks into Burst (Honed Crescent → Stroke That Splits Stone) and Suppression (Frostbitten Grip → Numb to the Marrow); Unblinking Sapphire (Increase Damage Taken + Decrease Damage Taken) forks into Fortress (Hoarfrost Bulwark → Glacier Does Not Yield) and Drain (Thirst of the Frost → Winter Takes Its Due). Lifesteal stands in for Afterburn (no Afterburn rows).
- Damage: Draft 4's +5 route (Honed Crescent +2, Stroke That Splits Stone +3) lifted every Damage row in the kit a tier: Icebound Might, Glacial Volley and Blue Crimson 40 → 45, Ice Shackles and Blade Resonance 45 → 50 (High to Nuke). Burst now sets up on Honed Crescent (+3% Increase Damage Given) and pays a single +2 Damage on Stroke That Splits Stone: 40 → 42 and 45 → 47 EP, no tier change, and +2 is the maximum over every legal allocation.
- Leverage: Increase Damage Given (four self rows) and Increase Damage Taken (three enemy rows; every attacker on the target benefits) compound per hit with one another and with the 28.75% passive, so both stop at +5%: three rows up at once give ×1.40³ ≈ ×2.74 against ×2.46 at base (+11.5%). Draft 4's +3% Increase Damage Given rider on Numb to the Marrow (Suppression out-amplified Burst) and +3% exposure rider on Glacier Does Not Yield (both Unblinking Sapphire capstones leaned on exposure) are removed, and Winter Takes Its Due's exposure rider falls from +5% to +3% so Lifesteal stays the Drain identity (Increase Damage Taken maximum +7% → +5%).
- Narrow coverage: Decrease Damage Given (Icebound Might) and Lifesteal (Arctic Frost) each reach one row, so those capstones carry a light identity-compatible rider (guard +2% Decrease Damage Taken, below Hoarfrost Bulwark's +3%; exposure +3% Increase Damage Taken) instead of a larger primary. The Damage (five rows) and Decrease Damage Taken (two rows) capstones carry their primary alone.
- Roster pass (R1–R7): graph and values unchanged. The kit has no 50 EP Damage row, and Burst already follows the director pattern: a percentage setup on the Hidden Art (Honed Crescent, self amplification, a tag no other Hidden Art carries) and a controlled +2 Damage payoff on the Advanced Art (R1, R4–R6). Increase Damage Given and Increase Damage Taken stay at R3's three-row +5% cap. Glacier Does Not Yield's +10% Decrease Damage Taken sits on two rows that compound, above R3's +8% two-row default, and is recorded for the director. The kit reason is Blood-Enchanted Eyes' director-reviewed Deathless Vitality, which holds the same +10% on two compounding self rows with a +5% Decrease Damage Given rider on top; both rows here guard only the caster. Weaponless castability argues the other way (every owner can run the route), so +8% (Glacier Does Not Yield +3, 43%) is the stated alternative. At +10% a guarded hit falls to (0.55 / 0.65)² ≈ ×0.72 of its base-guard value (×0.70 with Sapphire Edge's suppression inside Icebound Might's circle), so the Fortress takes no rider.
- Castability: six of eight jutsu need one of two HAND-slot bloodline weapons (The Threefold Gaze: Icebound Might, Glacial Volley, Ice Shackles; The Blue Blade: Blade Resonance, Blue Crimson, Arctic Frost); no node lists an item and no bonus is limited by one. Castable rows: Threefold Gaze = Damage 3, IDG 2, DDG 1, IDT 1, DDT 2; Blue Blade = Damage 2, IDG 3, IDT 3, DDT 2, Lifesteal 1; no weapon = IDG 1, IDT 1, DDT 2. Dual wielding was not read at the pin.
- Resolver and delivery: nine of eleven buff/debuff rows have a stat filter but no element, so getEfficiencyRatio (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3) matches them to Ice hits, normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks alike; Blade Resonance's exposure and Arctic Frost's damage buff carry Earth/Ice/None/Water/Wind. Cooldown 7 on every jutsu; damage casts cost 60 AP, the rest 40 AP; percentage rows live the 2 rounds after the cast (§3b), so Glacial Volley's and Blue Crimson's own buff never boosts their own hit. Quintessential Flake's guard and damage buff are SELF rows realized on the caster at cast, not through the tile (actions.ts 980-1004).
- Fourth purchases: Burst adds Unblinking Sapphire or Frostbitten Grip; Suppression adds Unblinking Sapphire or Honed Crescent; Fortress adds Sapphire Edge or Thirst of the Frost; Drain adds Hoarfrost Bulwark or Sapphire Edge. No capstone shares a tag with its sibling Hidden Art, so no fourth purchase stacks a capstone's own tag; the strongest cross-route fourth, Honed Crescent under Suppression, trades Stroke That Splits Stone's +2 Damage for Numb to the Marrow's suppression and guard.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Ice is shared with Hyouga Yui, Teno Yuki and other census bloodlines (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 7 of 16 kit rows carry Ice; the other nine need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Quintessential Flake and Sapphire Command (no Ice row) additionally need an authored Ice jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Ice coverage is unverified.
- Castability: 12 of 16 supported rows sit on weapon-gated jutsu. Burst's Damage rows are split across both weapons (three on The Threefold Gaze, two on The Blue Blade); Suppression's row is cast with The Threefold Gaze and Drain's with The Blue Blade; only the Fortress's primary rows need no weapon. Gates decide castability, not eligibility.
- Ally hazard: Icebound Might row 0 (Damage, AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN, friendly fire none = ALL) hits allies in the circle, never the caster; Stroke That Splits Stone raises that to 42 EP for allies inside. ## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Ice jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
