# Blue Blade Eyes — Edge of Sapphire

**Bloodline:** Blue Blade Eyes (BR-015, rank S, `clh4d6qo4000itb0hrx8t06wq`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Ice classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Ice (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Burst offense (Damage on five Ice rows; Increase Damage Given on four self rows) · secondary Control (Increase Damage Taken on three enemy rows; Decrease Damage Given on Icebound Might) · tertiary Self preservation and sustain (Decrease Damage Taken on the two ungated casts; Lifesteal on Arctic Frost).

Damage is the kit's broadest tag: five Ice rows at 40/45 power (Icebound Might, Glacial Volley, Ice Shackles on The Threefold Gaze; Blade Resonance, Blue Crimson on The Blue Blade), and Increase Damage Given reaches four self rows at 35% (Glacial Volley, Blue Crimson, Arctic Frost, Quintessential Flake) under a 25% + 0.15/level bloodline passive of the same tag, so burst is the declared primary and the Burst trait's home. Increase Damage Taken (35% on Sapphire Command, Blade Resonance, Arctic Frost) and the single Decrease Damage Given row (30% on Icebound Might's AOE circle) are the Control trait's rows and the secondary. Decrease Damage Taken sits only on the two ungated 40 AP casts (Quintessential Flake, Sapphire Command, 35% each) and Lifesteal only on Arctic Frost (40%), so guard and drain are the tertiary routes. Shield, stun, buffprevent, wound, move and redirection are unsupported and untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Ice-classified Blue Blade Eyes jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Sapphire Edge** — Blue chakra runs down the edge; what it touches keeps no edge of its own. IDG 35→37% on Glacial Volley, Blue Crimson, Arctic Frost, Quintessential Flake (ungated); DDG 30→32% on Icebound Might's circle (Gaze).
- **Honed Crescent** — The crescent is honed until the air itself parts before it. Damage 40→42 on Icebound Might, Glacial Volley, Blue Crimson; 45→47 on Ice Shackles, Blade Resonance. All five need a bloodline weapon.
- **Stroke That Splits Stone** — Musa split the boulder in one motion. Nothing you face is harder than granite. Route total +5: 40→45 and 45→50 on the same five Ice Damage rows; three castable with the Threefold Gaze, two with the Blue Blade.
- **Frostbitten Grip** — Hands numbed by frost strike without conviction. Icebound Might DDG 32→35% with Sapphire Edge (every enemy in the circle, 2 rounds, 60 AP, Threefold Gaze); the kit's only suppression row.
- **Numb to the Marrow** — Their blows lose all force in the cold; yours lose none. Icebound Might DDG 39% on the full route (one gated AOE row); IDG 35→40% with Sapphire Edge on all four self damage buffs.
- **Unblinking Sapphire** — Sapphire eyes that never close see the opening and the blow alike. IDT 35→37% on Sapphire Command (ungated), Blade Resonance, Arctic Frost; DDT 35→37% on Quintessential Flake and Sapphire Command (ungated).
- **Hoarfrost Bulwark** — Hoarfrost thickens on the skin until steel slides off it. DDT 37→40% with Unblinking Sapphire on Sapphire Command and Quintessential Flake's tile (from the next round); both ungated 40 AP casts.
- **Glacier Does Not Yield** — Stand in the cold long enough and nothing moves you; everything else cracks. DDT route total +8 (35→43%) on both ungated self rows; IDT 40% with Unblinking Sapphire on Sapphire Command, Blade Resonance, Arctic Frost.
- **Thirst of the Frost** — Frost does not merely bite. It drinks. Arctic Frost Lifesteal 40→43% (self, 2 rounds, 40 AP, Blue Blade); leeches from every hit landed in the window, pierce included.
- **Winter Takes Its Due** — Winter asks nothing twice; what it takes from the enemy it keeps. Arctic Frost Lifesteal 48% on the full route (60% leech cap); IDT 40% with Unblinking Sapphire on all three exposure rows.

## Design notes

- Node split: Sapphire Edge (IDG + DDG) is the Threefold Gaze root, forking into raw power (Honed Crescent → Stroke That Splits Stone) and suppression (Frostbitten Grip → Numb to the Marrow). Unblinking Sapphire (IDT + DDT, both on the ungated Sapphire Command) is the ungated / Blue Blade root, forking into guard (Hoarfrost Bulwark → Glacier Does Not Yield) and drain (Thirst of the Frost → Winter Takes Its Due). 2F/4H/4A; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Calibration by coverage: Damage +2/+3 (max +5) because it reaches five rows (25 row-weighted; Taiyo 15 on three). IDG glue only (+2 Foundation, +3 capstone secondary; max +5 on four rows). IDT glue only (+2 Foundation, +3 on either root-B capstone; max +5 on three rows). DDT +2/+3/+3 = +8 on two ungated rows (43%). DDG +2/+3/+4 = +9 on one gated AOE row (39%), under Taiyo's +10 per D5(b). Lifesteal +3/+5 = +8 on one gated row (48%). Foundations 10 row-weighted each; capstones 14–16.
- Item gates (D5): six of eight jutsu need The Threefold Gaze (Icebound Might, Glacial Volley, Ice Shackles) or The Blue Blade (Blade Resonance, Blue Crimson, Arctic Frost); both are HAND-slot weapons (41_INDEX_item.json), not keystones; dual wielding was not read at the pin. Castable rows: Gaze = Damage 3, IDG 2, DDG 1, IDT 1, DDT 2; Blue Blade = Damage 2, IDG 3, IDT 3, DDT 2, Lifesteal 1; no weapon = IDG 1, IDT 1, DDT 2. The ungated jutsu are buffs: no attack without a weapon.
- Resolver: the 25% + 0.15/level IDG passive multiplies enhanced Damage rows downstream. Nine of eleven buff/debuff rows have a stat filter but no element, so getEfficiencyRatio (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3) matches them to every element-less hit and to elemental hits of a listed stat type; they act on Ice hits, normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks alike. Blade Resonance IDT and Arctic Frost IDG carry Earth/Ice/None/Water/Wind. IDG/IDT/DDG/DDT skip pierce; lifesteal includes it.
- Delivery: every jutsu has cooldown 7; damage casts cost 60 AP, the rest 40 AP; percentage rows last 2 rounds. Quintessential Flake targets EMPTY_GROUND with a move row that sorts last, so its guard and damage buff reach the caster from the following round and only while standing on the tile. Icebound Might's AOE Damage row (friendly fire none = ALL) is the one ally-hazard row; its suppression row is ENEMIES-only. Sapphire Command's exposure lands on whichever single unit it is cast on.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Unblinking Sapphire (37% exposure/guard) or Frostbitten Grip (35% suppression); Suppression takes Unblinking Sapphire or Honed Crescent (+2 power); Bulwark takes Sapphire Edge (37% buffs) or Thirst of the Frost (43% leech); Drain takes Hoarfrost Bulwark (40% guard) or Sapphire Edge. All 14 full-budget allocations are non-dominated and every node is used. Strongest unadvertised: Stroke That Splits Stone + Frostbitten Grip (+5 power, 35% suppression, no guard).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Ice is not exclusive: Hyouga Yui and Teno Yuki (INCLUDE), Blue Edge Eyes (EXCLUDE) and five DEFER records carry Ice rows; the normal-jutsu collision is unverified. Today 7 of 16 supported rows match Ice directly and 9 fall back to None, which also reaches non-elemental rows on any jutsu. The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped classification and is inert or leaky without it.
- Item gates: 12 of 16 supported rows need The Threefold Gaze or The Blue Blade (HAND-slot weapons). All Damage, DDG and Lifesteal rows are gated: Burst gets +5 on three rows (Gaze) or two (Blue Blade), Suppression needs the Gaze, Drain the Blue Blade; only Bulwark is fully ungated. If both can be wielded at once (not read at the pin), all five Damage rows are live; trim the capstone to +2.
- Ally hazard: Icebound Might row 0 (Damage, AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN, friendly fire none = ALL) hits allies and the caster in the circle; Honed Crescent and Stroke That Splits Stone raise that to 42/45 for all inside. Blue Crimson's Damage row is ENEMIES-only (its wound hazard is unsupported). Sapphire Command's exposure row (friendly fire none, single target) exposes an ally at 37–40% if cast on one.
- IDG stacking: four separate self rows each reach 40% at the route maximum, BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is true at the pin, and the 25% + 0.15/level passive multiplies on top; a Blue Blade caster can stack three of them in one window for 160 AP. Flats were held to +5 total; the ceiling was not simulated. Normal-tree potency policy is unapproved, so a combined stacking audit is still required.
- Lifesteal: Arctic Frost's leech reaches 48% of every hit landed in its two-round window (all four stat types, no element: Ice hits, normal jutsu, weapons and basic attacks qualify; pierce included). It shares the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage budget with vamp; the kit has no vamp row, so other leech saturates after 12 more points. Needs both combatants alive; healprevent on the caster blocks it.
- Suppression and guard: DDG is one AOE row on a 60 AP, cooldown-7, Gaze-gated cast (39% at the maximum; value depends on enemies in the circle). DDT is two 35% rows on 40 AP casts; both at the route maximum give 86% reduction on matching hits in the overlapping rounds, which is why the route stops at +8. Quintessential Flake's guard needs the caster to stay on the spawned tile.
- No adverse rows, hidden rows, mode restrictions or injected children exist; the unsupported shield, buffprevent, stun, wound, move and redirection rows are unchanged and pierce is absent. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked modes. No combat simulation: non-dominance of the 14 allocations is an arithmetic result over per-row additions, not evidence of equal combat strength.
- Rank context: Blue Blade Eyes is an S-rank bloodline with a 28.75% Increase Damage Given passive at jutsu level 25 and a 100 regen bonus; this tree equalises marginal opportunity against the reference, not final strength. Cross-bloodline comparison is deferred to the roster review.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Blue Blade Eyes jutsu inherit Ice potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
