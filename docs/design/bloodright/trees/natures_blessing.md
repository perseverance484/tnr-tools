# Nature's Blessing — Root and Thorn

**Bloodline:** Nature's Blessing (BR-049, rank B, `clh4d6qbf000ctb0h7o5amr0o`) · **Revision:** Draft 5 / Wood classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Wood (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Co-primary — Sustain (Verdant Bastion Heal and Increase Heal) and Wood damage (Nature's Fury, Nature's Curse) · secondary Exposure — Increase Damage Taken (Nature's Fury) · tertiary None — absorb and drain on Nature's Curse are unsupported.

Verdant Bastion (A-rank, 40 AP, cooldown 7) carries the two sustain rows (Heal 40 = one 400 HP tick on the following round; Increase Heal 30% for the 2 rounds after the cast) and the two B-rank 60 AP attacks carry the two Wood damage rows (40 power each at level 25), so sustain and damage are co-primary; the Burst Healing / Sustained Healing traits lean the theme toward sustain, held within the +5 Heal and +10% guardrails (maxima 5 and 9). Increase Damage Taken is one 35% row on Nature's Fury, secondary by row count; because it adds to every non-pierce hit the target takes in its window (kit, normal jutsu, weapons, allies), its route is held to Taiyo Kami's +7 IDT maximum. Absorb and drain on Nature's Curse are unsupported and receive nothing directly.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Nine nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has four supported tags on five rows (Damage x2, Increase Damage Taken x1, Increase Heal x1, Heal x1). A fourth Advanced Art under Sap of the Grove would need a second sustain capstone (any Heal flat breaches +5, which Sap of the Grove and Everbloom Sanctuary already reach) or a damage/exposure capstone duplicating the Thorn and Rot routes and opening a second path to +6 Damage or +7 IDT. Instead the grove root carries one leaf, Bramble Warden, so it has its own 4 BP build and no node is universal: a pure Damage leaf would twin Ironwood Lash (Draft 1's Thorned Bloom tied it on every bonus vector), a pure Increase Heal leaf ties or loses to Deepening Roots, and a pure IDT leaf loses to Thorn and Rot's builds.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Wood-classified Nature's Blessing jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Thorn and Rot** — The forest takes its tithe in blood and bark alike. Nature's Fury exposure 35→37% (single target, 2 rounds); Nature's Fury and Nature's Curse Wood damage 40→41 power. 3 rows, no gates.
- **Ironwood Lash** — Boughs hardened over a hundred winters swing like iron. Nature's Fury and Nature's Curse Wood damage rows (41→43 power with Thorn and Rot). 2 rows, both 60 AP, range 4, cooldown 7.
- **Old Growth's Wrath** — What the old trees remember, they repay. The same two Wood damage rows; the route totals +6 power (40→46 on both attacks), then scaled by the bloodline's Wood damage passive.
- **Stripped Bark** — Peel away the bark and the heartwood cannot hide. Nature's Fury Increase Damage Taken only (single enemy row, 2 rounds, 60 AP, cooldown 7): 37→39% with Thorn and Rot.
- **Blight and Harvest** — Every blighted thing returns to the soil, and the soil feeds the grove. Fury exposure 39→42% on the route (one target, 2 rounds); Bastion Increase Heal 30→33% (35% with Sap), you or allies in the circle.
- **Sap of the Grove** — Living sap runs where blood once spilled. Verdant Bastion only: Heal 40→42 (400→420 HP next round); Increase Heal 30→32% for 2 rounds, for you or allies in the circle. 40 AP, CD 7.
- **Deepening Roots** — Roots that reach deeper drink more than rain. Verdant Bastion Increase Heal only (one row, 2 rounds, you or allies in the circle): 32→35% with Sap of the Grove. Lifts absorb/lifesteal.
- **Everbloom Sanctuary** — Within the bastion, nothing withers. Verdant Bastion Heal 42→45 (one 450 HP tick on the following round) and Increase Heal 35→38% for you or allies in the circle. 40 AP, CD 7.
- **Bramble Warden** — The grove's warden fights from among the briars and mends between blows. Fury and Curse Wood damage 40→41; Bastion Increase Heal 32→33% with Sap (39% on the full grove route), for you or allies in the circle.

## Design notes

- Shape: Thorn and Rot (enemy-facing: +2 IDT, +1 Damage) forks into Ironwood Lash → Old Growth's Wrath (damage) and Stripped Bark → Blight and Harvest (exposure). Sap of the Grove (self-facing: +2 Heal, +2 Increase Heal) runs Deepening Roots → Everbloom Sanctuary (sustain) and carries the leaf Bramble Warden (+1 Damage, +1 Increase Heal), a fight-inside-the-grove pick, so that root has its own 4 BP build. 2 Foundations, 4 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts; any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Reference reuse: the damage route copies Taiyo Kami verbatim (Ironwood Lash +2 = Crown of Cinders, Old Growth's Wrath +3 = Solar Cataclysm). Departures: the Foundation carries +1 Damage and +2 IDT, not +2 IDG/+2 IDT (no IDG row), so Damage reaches +6 on 2 rows rather than +5 on 3; the Afterburn route becomes exposure (no Afterburn row); the defensive root becomes Bastion sustain (no DDT/DDG rows). The shape fits two Wood strikes, one exposure row and one sustain cast.
- Calibration: IDT is one row but broad exposure (brief §5), so its route is held to Taiyo's IDT maximum, +7 (2+2+3, 35→42%), not the +10 single-row routes Taiyo gives DDT/DDG (2+3+5) or Afterburn (3+7); Stripped Bark sits below the +3 Hidden Art calibration to keep it there. Heal stops at the +5 guardrail (2+3, one 450 HP tick). Increase Heal: 2/3/3 on the Growth chain, +3 on Blight and Harvest, +1 on Bramble Warden (max 9, 39%). Value options (user-owned) are listed in risks.
- Arithmetic and timing: the bloodline's Increase Damage Given passive (20 + 0.15/level on Water, Earth, Wood, None) is fromType bloodline and multiplies the enhanced Wood hits; Fury's exposure is jutsu-sourced and adds power/100 x the staged base (§3). Buffs and debuffs act only in the 2 rounds after their cast round (§3b), so Fury's own hit never benefits from its exposure; a Curse, normal jutsu, weapon or ally hit on that target in those rounds does. All three jutsu have cooldown 7.
- Verdant Bastion (ground circle, range 0, 40 AP): Heal is target SELF, realized on the caster at cast time (§4b, actions.ts 980–1004): one 400 HP tick next round wherever the caster stands. Increase Heal is INHERIT / FRIENDLY: a ground effect re-applied each round to the caster and allies on the tiles (process.ts 321–340), live the 2 rounds after the cast. It raises the holder's heals, lifesteal, vamp and absorb (tags.ts 1110–1195), Bastion's own tick included while the caster is inside.
- Fourth purchases: Burst → Sap of the Grove or Stripped Bark; Exposure → Sap of the Grove or Ironwood Lash; Sustain → Thorn and Rot (exposure 37%) or Bramble Warden (Increase Heal 39%). 12 legal full allocations, 11 non-dominated; 01,04,06,07 loses to 01,04,05,06 (same Increase Heal, more exposure). Row weight: Burst 18, Sustain 17, Exposure 16; the alternatives 01,02,03,04, 01,02,04,05 and 06,07,08,09 are 16; no hybrid exceeds 15. Lowest-value node: Stripped Bark (2).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Wood is not exclusive (Shinseina Ki has 4 Wood rows, 3 damage). Under the current resolver a Wood modifier reaches only the two damage rows; the three None rows fall back to None, which also reaches other non-elemental jutsu. The tree assumes the proposed bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification. Whether NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT jutsu carry Wood is unverified.
- Broad exposure: Blight and Harvest builds put Fury's Increase Damage Taken at 42% on one target for the 2 rounds after the cast; with all four stat types and no element it adds to every non-pierce hit the target takes there (§3, G16), allies' hits included. Same-tag sources all apply and add (process.ts 1109–1117): another 35% exposure makes +77% of the staged base. Cooldown 7 bars self-overlap.
- Value decisions (user-owned): IDT +7 matches Taiyo's IDT maximum; +8 (Stripped Bark +3) or +10 (Draft 1: +3/+5) fit the 10% guardrail. Density: Burst is 18 on 5 rows (3.6/row) vs Taiyo's 31 on 9 (3.44) and named builds 2.2–3.2/row, as Damage takes the +6 ceiling on a B-rank kit; option: Thorn and Rot +0 Damage (route +5, Burst 16). Bramble Warden at +2 Increase Heal: 40%, grove-only Sustain 17.
- Increase Heal leakage (holder side): the buff raises heal_hp, lifesteal_hp, vampRatio and absorb_hp for its holder (tags.ts 1110–1195), so Bastion's up to +9% also lifts Nature's Curse's 30% absorb, the 10% absorb passive and main-tree or item heals, lifesteal and vamp in its window. Maximum 39% is far below the 100 cap; lifesteal and vamp still share the 60% leech budget.
- Ally reach (verified, G16): the Increase Heal row is INHERIT / FRIENDLY on a ground spawn, re-applied each round to the caster and allies on the tiles, never enemies (actions.ts 1006–1016, process.ts 321–340), so every Increase Heal node also lifts allies' heals, lifesteal, vamp and absorb there. The dossier's SELF BUFF label comes from bloodright_lib.recipient_for collapsing area_allies to self.
- Heal timing: Bastion's Heal ticks once the next round (rounds 1, static x10; tags.ts 1721–1775). The ground-derived Increase Heal effect copies its cleared isNew/castThisRound (process.ts 301, 333–339) and heal adjusters run after heals (process.ts 440, 600), so a caster on the tiles gets 450 x 1.38 = 621 HP on the full Sustain route vs 400 x 1.30 = 520 (+19.4%); off them, 450 vs 400 (+12.5%).
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the Wood Increase Damage Given passive, so +6 raw power is not +6 damage; realized value depends on stats and the passive's level scaling. No combat simulation was performed.
- Absorb and drain on Nature's Curse are unsupported and receive no potency; only Increase Heal touches absorb indirectly. Main-tree effects, equipment, AP and uptime are outside this audit.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Nature's Blessing jutsu inherit Wood potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
