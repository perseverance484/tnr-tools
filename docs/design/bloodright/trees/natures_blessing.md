# Nature's Blessing — Root and Thorn

**Bloodline:** Nature's Blessing (BR-049, rank B, `clh4d6qbf000ctb0h7o5amr0o`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance (roster pass) / Wood classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Wood (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Co-primary — Wood Damage on the Fury → Curse one-two (Nature's Fury and Nature's Curse) and Verdant Bastion healing (Increase Heal, with a small Heal lift from Sap of the Grove) · secondary Increase Damage Taken: Nature's Fury's exposure, one row that multiplies every non-pierce hit the marked target takes in its window · tertiary Absorb and drain on Nature's Curse are unsupported and receive nothing.

Verdant Bastion (A-rank, 40 AP, cooldown 7) carries the two healing rows (Heal 40 = one 400 HP tick on the following round; Increase Heal 30% for the 2 rounds after the cast, for the caster and allies in the circle) and the two B-rank 60 AP attacks carry the two Wood Damage rows (40 EP each at level 25) plus Fury's 35% exposure. Thorn and Rot answers "How do I bring down the marked target: hit it harder or open it wider?": the Old Growth's Wrath route adds a controlled +2 Damage to both Wood attacks (40 → 42, Normal tier kept) and the Blight and Harvest route deepens the mark to 43% for every hit on that target. Sap of the Grove answers "How do I keep the grove standing?" through one chain, the Everbloom Sanctuary route's sustained healing (Increase Heal 40% in the circle, Bastion's tick 420 HP). Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Absorb and drain are unsupported and receive nothing directly.

**Director review recommended:** Two items; neither changes the routes. (1) Exposure magnitude: Blight and Harvest holds Nature's Fury's single team-wide row at +8% (35 → 43%; ×1.43 / ×1.35 ≈ ×1.059 on every eligible hit on the marked target from the whole party), above the +5% exposure of Blood-Enchanted Eyes and Shakunetsu Sakura and below the +10% that most of the roster's single-row exposure routes keep. The +8% has a kit reason, so it should hold under a roster +10% rule: Ironwood Lash is Exposure's natural fourth, and at +10% Exposure + Lash tied Burst + Stripped Bark on the kit's own Fury → Curse one-two (≈ 100.5 against ≈ 100.8) and beat it on every other hit into the mark. (2) Structure: the Sudden Greening leaf (+3% Heal, about +30 HP once per 7-round Bastion cast, kept so no node was universal) is removed; eight nodes, Thorn and Rot universal (narrow-kit exception).

- Concern: Healing is the kit's low-leverage side: Sap of the Grove's +2% Heal is +20 HP once per Bastion cast and Increase Heal only multiplies healing in a 2-round, positional window, so Everbloom Sanctuary is the trait-identity pick rather than the power pick; the kit has no Decrease Damage, Reflect or Lifesteal rows to give it more weight. The bloodline's Burst Healing trait has no node of its own: Bastion's single 400 HP tick could only carry a Heal-only leaf of about +30 HP per 7-round cast, which would be glue, not a choice.
- Concern: Thorn and Rot is universal (in all 8 legal 4-BP builds): Everbloom Sanctuary's only fourth purchase is the +2% mark (Fury 37%), so the Sanctuary build is fixed (06, 07, 08 + 01) and every build carries that mark.
- Concern: Exposure (43%) is a single row but multiplies every non-pierce hit the marked target takes from anyone for 2 rounds; in team play Blight and Harvest is the strongest Thorn and Rot route. It is held at +8% so that Burst keeps the kit's own one-two (Burst + Stripped Bark ≈ 100.8 against Exposure + Ironwood Lash ≈ 99.6); at +10% Exposure + Lash tied there and led on every other hit into the mark, leaving Burst only Fury's opener.
- Concern: Both Thorn and Rot capstones are light: Old Growth's Wrath is +1 Damage because Ironwood Lash keeps the route's other +1 as a Hidden-Art commit (any non-Damage setup there would twin Stripped Bark or Deepening Roots or be a Heal-only node, so moving both points to the capstone would need such a node), and Blight and Harvest is +3%. Burst's case is certainty, +2 on each cast of the two cooldown-7 attacks with or without the mark, not size.
- Concern: Damage +2 and Increase Damage Taken +8% reach every Wood jutsu; off-kit Wood coverage is unverified (an off-kit 50 EP Wood Damage row would read 52; the validator checks kit rows only).

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has four supported tags on five rows (Damage x2, Increase Damage Taken x1, Increase Heal x1, Heal x1), and both healing rows sit on the one Verdant Bastion cast. A fourth Advanced Art would either split that single cast into a second healing capstone or repeat a Thorn and Rot route. Universal node accepted: Thorn and Rot is in all 8 legal 4-BP builds because Sap of the Grove roots a single 3-node chain, so Everbloom Sanctuary's fourth purchase is always the +2% mark. Every 1 BP leaf under Sap of the Grove that would avoid this is filler or a twin: a Heal leaf (the roster pass's Sudden Greening, +3% = about +30 HP once per 7-round cast) is Heal glue, an Increase Heal leaf twins Deepening Roots, an Increase Damage Taken leaf twins Stripped Bark, and a Damage leaf would be a Hidden-Art Damage node kept only to avoid a universal node.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

## Design notes

- Shape: Thorn and Rot (+2% Increase Damage Taken) forks into Ironwood Lash → Old Growth's Wrath (Burst) and Stripped Bark → Blight and Harvest (Exposure). Sap of the Grove (+2% Heal, +2% Increase Heal) runs one chain, Deepening Roots → Everbloom Sanctuary (sustained healing). 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts; any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP. The kit has no Increase Damage Given, Decrease Damage, Lifesteal or Afterburn rows.
- 2026-10-04 rebalance: the old Burst route (Ironwood Lash +2, Old Growth's Wrath +3 Damage) lifted both 40 EP attacks to 45, a full tier on the kit's only two Damage rows. The first pass cut it to +3; the roster pass sets Old Growth's Wrath to +1, so Burst is +2 (Lash +1, Wrath +1; 40 → 42), the director payoffs' +2 (Blood-Enchanted Eyes, Shakunetsu Sakura, Arashima). Everbloom Sanctuary trades its +3% Heal / +3% Increase Heal for a single +5% Increase Heal, and Blight and Harvest drops its 3% Increase Heal rider. The final review removes the Sap leaf (Bramble Warden, a +1 Damage / +1% Increase Heal filler hybrid, then Sudden Greening, +3% Heal kept only so no node was universal) and sets Blight and Harvest to +3% (route +8%). Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +2, Increase Damage Taken +8%, Increase Heal +10%, Heal +2%.
- Why Ironwood Lash keeps flat Damage on a Hidden Art: every other tag in the kit is a sibling's Hidden Art (Stripped Bark Increase Damage Taken, Deepening Roots Increase Heal) or Heal, which on Bastion's single tick is glue, so any non-Damage setup there would be a twin or filler, and the route already has its setup in Thorn and Rot (Fury's mark, 37%). The +1 keeps both attacks Normal (41).
- Why Exposure stops at 43%: it is one row, one target and the 2 rounds after a cooldown-7 cast, while Burst's +2 lands on each cast of either attack and any target, Fury's opener and a Curse outside the window included. At 4 BP Burst + Stripped Bark leads the kit's own Fury → Curse one-two (42 + 42 × 1.40 ≈ 100.8 against Exposure + Ironwood Lash's 41 + 41 × 1.43 ≈ 99.6), and Exposure + Lash overtakes once about 39 EP-equivalent of other hits land on the marked target in the window. The two builds differ only in the capstone: +1 Damage (42 / 41 ≈ ×1.024 on each Wood attack) against +3% (×1.43 / ×1.40 ≈ ×1.021 on each hit into the mark). At 45% (route +10%) Exposure + Lash tied the one-two (41 + 41 × 1.45 ≈ 100.5) and led on every other hit. Per hit into the mark the full route is ×1.43 / ×1.35 ≈ ×1.059, against Burst's 42 / 40 = ×1.05 on each Wood attack.
- Arithmetic and timing: Fury's exposure is a jutsu-sourced stage-2 multiplier on each matching hit (×1.35 at base, ×1.43 on the full Exposure route; §3b), applied before the bloodline's Increase Damage Given passive (20 + 0.15/level on Water, Earth, Wood, None), which multiplies last. Buffs and debuffs act only in the 2 rounds after their cast round, so Fury's own hit never benefits from its exposure; a Curse, normal jutsu, weapon or ally hit on that target in those rounds does. All three jutsu have cooldown 7.
- Verdant Bastion (ground circle, range 0, 40 AP): Heal is target SELF, realized on the caster at cast time (§4b, actions.ts 980–1004): one 400 HP tick next round wherever the caster stands. Increase Heal is INHERIT / FRIENDLY: a ground effect re-applied each round to the caster and allies on the tiles (process.ts 321–340), live the 2 rounds after the cast. It raises the holder's heals, lifesteal, vamp and absorb (tags.ts 1110–1195), Bastion's own tick included while the caster is inside.
- Fourth purchases: Burst → Stripped Bark (Curse 42 EP into a 40% mark) or Sap of the Grove; Exposure → Sap of the Grove or Ironwood Lash (41 EP into a 43% mark); Sanctuary → Thorn and Rot only (37% mark). No fourth stacks a capstone's tag past its route: Burst + Stripped Bark reaches only +5% Increase Damage Taken, and Exposure + Ironwood Lash only +1 Damage. Burst + Stripped Bark leads on the one-two and on Wood casts outside the window; Exposure + Lash leads once other hits land on the marked target.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Wood is shared with Shinseina Ki (expected under RUL-2026-10-03-005). 2 of 5 kit rows carry Wood; Fury's exposure row and Bastion's two rows need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Verdant Bastion carries no element on any row, so in-kit it qualifies only through an authored Wood jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Wood coverage is unverified.
- Broad exposure: Blight and Harvest builds put Fury's Increase Damage Taken at 43% on one target for the 2 rounds after the cast; with all four stat types and no element it multiplies every non-pierce hit the target takes there by 1.43 (§3b, G16), allies' hits included. Same-tag sources all apply, each as its own multiplier (process.ts 1692–1825): with another 35% exposure live a hit takes ×1.43 × 1.35 ≈ ×1.93. Cooldown 7 bars self-overlap.
- Increase Heal reach (holder side): the buff raises heal_hp, lifesteal_hp, vampRatio and absorb_hp for its holder (tags.ts 1110–1195), so Bastion's up to +10% also lifts Nature's Curse's 30% absorb, the 10% absorb passive and main-tree or item heals, lifesteal and vamp in its window. Maximum 40% is far below the 100 cap; lifesteal and vamp still share the 60% leech budget.
- Ally reach (verified, G16): the Increase Heal row is INHERIT / FRIENDLY on a ground spawn, re-applied each round to the caster and allies on the tiles, never enemies (actions.ts 1006–1016, process.ts 321–340), so every Increase Heal node also lifts allies' heals, lifesteal, vamp and absorb there. The dossier's SELF BUFF label comes from bloodright_lib.recipient_for collapsing area_allies to self.
- Heal timing: Bastion's Heal ticks once the next round (rounds 1, static x10; tags.ts 1721–1775). The ground-derived Increase Heal effect copies its cleared isNew/castThisRound (process.ts 301, 333–339) and heal adjusters run after heals (process.ts 440, 600), so a caster on the tiles gets 420 × 1.40 = 588 HP on the Sanctuary route (+13.1% over 400 × 1.30 = 520); off the tiles, 420 vs 400.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and then multiplied by the Wood Increase Damage Given passive, so +2 raw EP is not +2 damage; realized value depends on stats and the passive's level scaling. Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Wood jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
