# Nature's Blessing — Root and Thorn

**Bloodline:** Nature's Blessing (BR-049, rank B, `clh4d6qbf000ctb0h7o5amr0o`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Wood classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Wood (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Co-primary — Sustain (Verdant Bastion Heal and Increase Heal) and Wood damage (Nature's Fury, Nature's Curse) · secondary Exposure — Increase Damage Taken (Nature's Fury) · tertiary None — absorb and drain on Nature's Curse are unsupported.

The kit's real rows split evenly between two faces: Verdant Bastion (A-rank, 40 AP, one cast per 7 rounds) carries the two sustain rows (Heal 40 power = 400 HP on the following round; Increase Heal 30% for 2 rounds), and the two B-rank 60 AP attacks carry the two Wood damage rows (Nature's Fury 40, Nature's Curse 40 at jutsu level 25), so sustain and damage are co-primary; the bloodline's Burst Healing / Sustained Healing traits lean the theme toward sustain, but Heal and Increase Heal are held at the +5 / +10 guardrails rather than given more purchases. Increase Damage Taken is a single 35% row on Nature's Fury; it is secondary by row count, and its downstream reach (every non-pierce hit the exposed target takes for 2 rounds from the kit, normal jutsu, weapons and allies alike) is why it gets a full route held to Taiyo Kami's +7 IDT ceiling rather than a larger one. Absorb and drain on Nature's Curse are unsupported and receive nothing directly.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has four supported tags on five rows (Damage x2, Increase Damage Taken x1, Increase Heal x1, Heal x1). The enemy-facing root (Thorn and Rot) forks into a damage route and an exposure route; the self-facing root (Sap of the Grove) is a single Foundation → Hidden → Advanced chain. A second sustain Hidden Art or capstone under Sap of the Grove would breach the +5 Heal / +10 Increase Heal guardrails in a full build. A damage or exposure Hidden Art or capstone under Sap of the Grove is structurally possible (any two Advanced Arts would still cost at least 5 BP) but would duplicate the Thorn and Rot routes and open a second path to the Damage and Increase Damage Taken ceilings; the design declines that by choice so that Old Growth's Wrath and Blight and Harvest remain the only ways to +6 Damage and +7 IDT. Draft 1's Thorned Bloom (+2 Damage under Sap of the Grove, an exact twin of Ironwood Lash whose builds tied Ironwood Lash builds on every bonus vector) was removed for that reason. Three distinct complete builds (Burst, Exposure, Sustain) exist; Burst and Exposure each have two fourth-purchase choices, and Sustain's fourth purchase is always Thorn and Rot (06,07,08 plus 01 is legal and non-dominated).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Wood-classified Nature's Blessing jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Blight and Harvest** — Every blighted thing returns to the soil, and the soil feeds the grove. Fury exposure 42% on the route (Taiyo's +7 IDT ceiling); Bastion Increase Heal 30→33% (35% with Sap) for you or allies in the circle.
- **Sap of the Grove** — Living sap runs where blood once spilled. Verdant Bastion only: Heal 40→42 (400→420 HP next round); Increase Heal 30→32% for 2 rounds, for you or allies in the circle. 40 AP, CD 7.
- **Deepening Roots** — Roots that reach deeper drink more than rain. Verdant Bastion Increase Heal only (one row, 2 rounds, you or allies in the circle): 32→35% with Sap of the Grove. Lifts absorb/lifesteal.
- **Everbloom Sanctuary** — Within the bastion, nothing withers. Verdant Bastion Heal 42→45 (450 HP per tick, the +5 guardrail) and Increase Heal 35→38% for you or allies in the circle. 40 AP, CD 7.

## Design notes

- Node split: the two roots mirror the kit's two faces. Thorn and Rot (enemy-facing) raises Nature's Fury's exposure and both Wood attacks and forks into Ironwood Lash → Old Growth's Wrath (damage) and Stripped Bark → Blight and Harvest (exposure). Sap of the Grove (self-facing) raises both Verdant Bastion rows and runs a single chain through Deepening Roots → Everbloom Sanctuary (sustain). 2 Foundations, 3 Hidden Arts, 3 Advanced Arts; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two Advanced Arts cost 5 BP (shared root) or 6 BP. Every capstone is worth 6 row-weighted (Old Growth's Wrath +3 Damage on 2 rows = raw 3; Blight and Harvest +3 IDT, +3 Increase Heal = raw 6; Everbloom Sanctuary +3 Heal, +3 Increase Heal = raw 6).
- Flats scale by coverage, not rank. Damage reaches only 2 rows (Taiyo Kami: 3), so the route totals +6 power (1+2+3) rather than +5 and sits at the guardrail; the Foundation's +1 is the sliver that lets the Hidden/Advanced keep the Taiyo +2/+3 shape. Increase Damage Taken is a single row but a broad-exposure tag (brief section 5), so its route is held to Taiyo Kami's actual IDT ceiling of +7 (Dawnheart +2 with Dying Light +5; +5 with Eternal Noon +3): Thorn and Rot +2, Stripped Bark +2, Blight and Harvest +3 = +7 (35→42%). Taiyo's 2+3+5 = +10 single-row routes belong to Decrease Damage Taken (a self buff) and Decrease Damage Given (a debuff that suppresses one enemy's output), not to IDT, so they are not the template here; Stripped Bark sits below the +3 single-row Hidden Art calibration for the same reason. Heal is single-row raw power and is capped by the +5 guardrail (2 on the Foundation, 3 on the capstone = 450 HP per tick). Increase Heal is spread 2/3/3 on the Growth chain (maximum 8 in one build, 38%) with +3 on Blight and Harvest as that route's sustain secondary.
- Interaction with the main tree and passives: the bloodline's Increase Damage Given passive (20 + 0.15 per level on Water, Earth, Wood and None) multiplies the enhanced Wood damage rows, so +6 power is worth more than +6 after that passive. Nature's Fury's exposure applies to direct and residual damage the target takes for 2 rounds from the kit, normal jutsu, weapons and allies alike (a Nature's Curse cast in the window is amplified too): the row lists all four stat types and no element, so at the pin (tags.ts 3477–3510, G15) it amplifies every non-pierce hit the target takes in the window, element-less hits matching on None and elemental hits on any stat type, with no captured hit type excluded; pierce resolves after the damage modifiers and is not amplified. Verdant Bastion's Increase Heal adjusts heal_hp, lifesteal_hp, vampRatio and absorb_hp for whoever holds the buff (the caster, and possibly allies standing in the circle, see risks) while active, so it also scales Nature's Curse's unsupported 30% absorb row, the bloodline's 10% absorb passive and any main-tree or item healing inside its 2-round window; it is therefore the kit's broadest sustain lever even though it reaches one row.
- Delivery and uptime: all three jutsu have cooldown 7. Verdant Bastion is a 40 AP ground spawn at range 0 whose Heal row has rounds 1 (one 400 HP tick on the following round, static x10, to the caster still standing in the circle) and whose Increase Heal lasts 2 rounds. Nature's Fury and Nature's Curse are 60 AP single-target casts at range 4, so exposure is live for 2 of every 7 rounds per cast; the one follow-up attack cast inside the window (Nature's Curse) is amplified, and whether Nature's Fury's own hit is amplified by the debuff it applies in the same action is unverified (see risks). Potency changes the percentages and powers only; durations, AP and cooldowns are untouched.
- Fourth purchase choices: Burst → Sap of the Grove (Bastion 420 HP, 32%) or Stripped Bark (exposure 39%); Exposure → Sap of the Grove (IH 35%, 420 HP) or Ironwood Lash (attacks 43, Bastion 400 HP at 33%); Sustain → Thorn and Rot only (exposure 37%, attacks 41), since the Growth chain has no side branch. Legal no-Advanced hybrids: 01,02,06,07 (damage +3, IH +5), 01,02,04,06 (damage +3, IDT +4) and 01,04,06,07 (IDT +4, IH +5). One of the 8 full allocations is dominated: 01,04,06,07 by the Exposure build 01,04,05,06, because Blight and Harvest supplies the same Increase Heal as Deepening Roots on top of a larger exposure. The other seven are numerically non-dominated and every node appears in at least one of them.
- Strongest unadvertised allocation: 01,02,04,05 (damage +3, IDT +7, IH +3), exposure with sharper attacks and no Bastion Heal power. By row weight the Burst build is the strongest full allocation (18; Sustain 17, Exposure 16) because Damage reaches two rows; Taiyo Kami's four named builds range 20–29 on nine rows and its strongest legal allocation (01,02,04,05) reaches 31, roughly 11–17 scaled to five rows. Lowest-value purchase by row weight: Stripped Bark (+2 on one row), accepted because the exposure multiplies every non-pierce hit on the target for 2 rounds rather than one row's own output.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Wood is not exclusive in the census (Shinseina Ki carries 4 Wood rows, 3 of them damage); under the current resolver a Wood modifier reaches only the two damage rows directly and the three None rows fall back to None, which also reaches other non-elemental jutsu. The tree assumes the proposed bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification. Whether NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT jutsu carry Wood is unverified.
- Broad enemy exposure: Blight and Harvest routes put Nature's Fury's Increase Damage Taken at 42% for 2 rounds on one target; the row lists all four stat types and no element, so at the pin (tags.ts 3477–3510, G15) the added percentage multiplies every non-pierce hit from the kit, normal jutsu, weapons and allies alike: element-less hits match on None, elemental hits match on any stat type, and no captured hit type is excluded. Pierce resolves after the damage modifiers and is not amplified. BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is on at the pin, so it stacks with any other exposure source on that target; one cast per 7 rounds limits self-stacking.
- Value decision (user-owned): the IDT route total of +7 matches Taiyo Kami's IDT ceiling; Draft 1 proposed +10 (Stripped Bark +3, Blight and Harvest +5), which the review flagged as above that ceiling and as making Exposure the strongest build by row weight. +8 (Stripped Bark +3, Blight and Harvest +3) or the original +10 remain inside the ≤10% guardrail if a stronger exposure route is preferred.
- Increase Heal leakage (caster side): the buff adjusts heal_hp, lifesteal_hp, vampRatio and absorb_hp for its holder, so +8% on Verdant Bastion also raises Nature's Curse's absorb, the 10% absorb passive and main-tree or item heals, lifesteal and vamp during its window. Maximum 38% is far below the 100 cap. Lifesteal and vamp still share the 60% leech budget.
- Increase Heal leakage (ally side, unverified): in the raw snapshot Verdant Bastion's Increase Heal row is target INHERIT with friendlyFire FRIENDLY on a GROUND / AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN jutsu, so bloodright_lib.recipient_for derives raw recipient area_allies and collapses it to self for the SELF BUFF label. If the buff lands on allies standing in the circle, Sap of the Grove, Deepening Roots, Everbloom Sanctuary and Blight and Harvest also lift allied heals, lifesteal, vamp and absorb for 2 rounds. The Heal row itself is target SELF; whether it also reaches allies in the circle was not verified (the dossier derives its recipient as self).
- Heal timing and delivery: Verdant Bastion's Heal has rounds 1, so it applies once on the following round (static x10). Whether Bastion's own Increase Heal row adjusts that same tick was not verified: if it does, the full Sustain route realizes 450 x 1.38 = 621 HP against a baseline 400 x 1.30 = 520 HP (+19.4%); if not, 450 against 400 (+12.5%). Delivery of the ground-spawned SELF Heal and FRIENDLY Increase Heal is positional and unverified at the pin: ground effects are re-applied each round to whoever stands on the tiles (G16, process.ts 323–340), so the 400/420/450 HP figures assume the caster is still inside the circle on the following round; how a SELF-target row is realized from a ground spawn was not read.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt stat scaling) and multiplied by the bloodline's Wood Increase Damage Given passive, so +6 raw power is not a linear +6 damage; realized value depends on stats and the passive's level scaling. Whether Nature's Fury's own hit is amplified by the Increase Damage Taken debuff it applies in the same action (new-effect / castThisRound ordering in process.ts) is unverified; the exposure figures assume only follow-up hits. No combat simulation was performed.
- Absorb and drain on Nature's Curse are unsupported tags and receive no direct potency; only the Increase Heal interaction above touches absorb indirectly. Main-tree effects, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Nature's Blessing jutsu inherit Wood potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
