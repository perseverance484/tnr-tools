# Shiroi Youso — Confluence of Five

**Bloodline:** Shiroi Youso (BR-067, rank S, `vv45N2En3p8VrNcU2acd8`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Shiroi Youso bloodline-keyed classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Shiroi Youso (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Elemental amplification (self Increase Damage Given on Fire Release and Wind Release; enemy Increase Damage Taken on Fire Release and Element Divine's area) · secondary Burst and burn (Damage on Lightning Release 40 and Element Divine 45; Afterburn on the same two casts, 35% and 30%) · tertiary Defensive control (self Decrease Damage Taken on Wind Release and Earth Release; static Heal on Water Release).

Eleven supported rows sit on six casts and every percentage tag reaches exactly two of them: two 35% five-element self damage buffs (Fire Release, Wind Release), two 35% five-element exposures (Fire Release, Element Divine's area), two 35% all-stat self Decrease Damage Taken rows (Wind, Earth), two Afterburn rows (Lightning Release 35%, Element Divine 30%) and two Damage rows (40, 45) under a 25% + 0.15/level five-element Increase Damage Given passive. The kit's Burst and Combo Playmaking traits ride Lightning Release and Element Divine (60 AP each) after Fire Release's one-cast setup, so the offensive root carries amplification and forks into Damage and Afterburn; the Defensive Control trait lives in the two-round self Decrease Damage Taken buffs and the 25-power Water heal, so the defensive root forks into Decrease Damage Taken and a sustain route that ends in exposure.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Shiroi Youso jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Fivefold Kindling** — Five elements, one white spark. Everything the clan casts begins as kindling. Self damage buffs on Fire Release and Wind Release 35→37%; exposure on Fire Release and Element Divine's area (ally hazard) 35→37%.
- **Pale Lightning** — The bolt arrives white, not blue; the thunder is an afterthought. Lightning Release 40→42 and Element Divine 45→47 power (both 60 AP, cooldown 7); stun, inject and Afterburn rows unchanged.
- **Divine Convergence** — When all five currents meet in one hand, the sky itself gives way. Same two Damage rows; route total +6: Lightning Release 46, Element Divine 51 power. Element Divine's area damage hits enemies only.
- **Charred Current** — Lightning leaves a charred path, and fire remembers the way. Afterburn debuff: Lightning Release 35→38%, Element Divine 30→33% (area, ally hazard); 2 rounds; raises the percent, not the duration.
- **White Immolation** — Not a flame that burns, but a whiteness that consumes what the flame has touched. Afterburn route total +7: Lightning Release 42%, Element Divine 37%; exposure on Fire Release and Element Divine 37→39% with Kindling.
- **Bedrock and Tide** — Stone to stand on, water to mend with. The clan outlasts before it overwhelms. Self DDT on Wind Release and Earth Release 35→37% (2-round buff on the caster); Water Release heal 25→27 power (270 HP per tick, 2 ticks).
- **Gale Bulwark** — The wind does not stop the blade; it carries the blade past you. Both self Decrease Damage Taken rows 37→40% (all four stat types, element-less: every non-pierce hit); realized on the caster at cast time.
- **Eye of the Tempest** — At the storm's heart the air is still, and every strike from it is a hurricane. Self DDT route total +7 (42%) on Wind Release and Earth Release; self damage buffs on Fire Release and Wind Release 37→40% with Kindling.
- **Tidal Mending** — Water closes over every wound as if it had never been opened. Water Release heal 27→30 power: 300 HP per tick on the two following rounds, a 2-round effect on the caster (40 AP, cooldown 7).
- **Pull of the Undertow** — The tide takes the footing from under them and gives it to you. Exposure on Fire Release and Element Divine's area (ally hazard) +4 (41% with Fivefold Kindling); self DDT +2 (39% with Bedrock and Tide).

## Design notes

- Node split: root Fivefold Kindling (+2 self Increase Damage Given, +2 exposure) forks into a Damage route (Pale Lightning → Divine Convergence) and an Afterburn route (Charred Current → White Immolation). Root Bedrock and Tide (+2 self Decrease Damage Taken, +2 Heal) forks into a DDT route (Gale Bulwark → Eye of the Tempest) and a sustain route ending in exposure (Tidal Mending → Pull of the Undertow). 2/4/4 nodes; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP against the 4 BP cap.
- Flats scaled by coverage. Every percentage tag reaches two rows, so each takes the two-row ladder: IDG +2/+3 (max +5, 40%), IDT +2/+2/+4 (max +6, 41%), DDT +2/+3/+2 (max +7, 42%). Afterburn +3/+4 (max +7: 42% and 37%) stays under the single-row +10 because Lightning Release 35% and Element Divine 30% already saturate the 60% per-hit cap together. Damage reaches two rows (40, 45), not Taiyo Kami's three, so it carries +2/+4 (max +6, the guardrail). Heal +2/+3 (max +5: +50 HP per tick on 250).
- Capstone secondaries never out-bid a sibling Hidden Art on its own tag: White Immolation's +2 exposure and Pull of the Undertow's +2 DDT sit below Gale Bulwark's +3; Eye of the Tempest's +3 self buff has no Hidden Art competitor. Row-weighted node values: Foundations 8/6, Hidden Arts 4/6/6/3, Advanced Arts 8/12/10/12; full builds 21–32 against Taiyo Kami's 20–31 on nine rows. Maxima: Damage +6, IDG +5, IDT +6, DDT +7, Afterburn +7, Heal +5; not jointly attainable.
- Main tree and passives: the IDG and IDT rows list the five elements with no stat filter, so (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3) they multiply five-element damage only: the kit's two Damage rows, the five injected children and any other five-element hit; element-less damage (basic attacks, non-elemental jutsu, element-less weapons) gets ratio 0. The DDT rows list all four stat types, no element: every non-pierce hit. The five-element IDG passive (25% + 0.15/level) stacks on top as a bloodline multiplier.
- Delivery and uptime: all six casts are cooldown 7; Lightning Release and Element Divine cost 60 AP, the rest 40. Wind Release's DDT/IDG and Earth Release's DDT are SELF rows realized on the caster at cast time whatever the ground target (SOURCE_MECHANICS §4b); only the unsupported INHERIT rows (Wind's move, Earth's barrier) become ground effects. Water Release's heal is a 2-round effect on the caster, paid on the two following rounds. Fire Release carries a self buff and an exposure.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Bedrock and Tide or Charred Current; Burn pressure Bedrock and Tide or Pale Lightning; Fortified Fivefold Kindling or Tidal Mending; Control Fivefold Kindling or Gale Bulwark. Tidal Mending (+30 HP per tick on the single heal row) is the lowest row-weighted node (3 vs siblings 4/6/6), a gate to Pull of the Undertow. User-owned: (a) keep as a deliberate gate; (b) shift +1 Heal from Bedrock and Tide to it (+1/+4); (c) add a small second tag off the DDT lane.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Ally hazard: Element Divine row 1 (Afterburn 30%) and row 2 (exposure 35%) are AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire none (=ALL): allies inside the circle also receive the debuffs; the caster never does (OTHER_USER target, SOURCE_MECHANICS §4b). Kindling, Charred Current, White Immolation and Pull of the Undertow raise what allies there take; positioning decides. The Damage row is ENEMIES-only.
- Afterburn saturation: Lightning Release 35% and Element Divine 30% stack to 65% on one target, above the 60% per-hit cap, so the +7 route adds value only while a single Afterburn source is active. Downstream coverage (every non-pierce hit by anyone for 2 rounds, including the injected children cast into Lightning Release's 2-round stun) was not simulated; pierce hits take no Afterburn.
- Leakage: with five-element IDG/IDT rows, the +5 self buff and +6 exposure reach only five-element damage: the player's other five-element jutsu (IDG) and anyone's five-element hits on the exposed target, allies included (IDT). Basic attacks, element-less weapons and non-elemental jutsu get ratio 0. Fire and Wind windows overlap to 80% self buff; Fire plus Element Divine exposures to 82%.
- Delivery: Wind Release rows 0-1 and Earth Release row 0 are SELF rows on EMPTY_GROUND casts; Water Release's heal is INHERIT on a SELF-target cast: all four land on the caster at cast time, not positionally (SOURCE_MECHANICS §4b). Their cost is the forgone Damage/Afterburn routes; Eye of the Tempest holds both DDT (42%) and IDG (40%) maxima unconditionally: user-owned check (IDG +3 → +2?).
- Injected children: Lightning, Fire and Water Release inject five SPECIAL children (empty bloodlineId; level = inject power 100/110) with Damage, DDG, IDG, Lifesteal and IDT rows. Inheritance is a gap-register decision; this tree counts none of them. If they inherit, Damage +6 reaches five more hits, IDG +5 Voltari's IDG row, IDT +6 Rising Water Slicer's IDT row; Lifesteal/DDG stay untargeted.
- Classification: Fire and Lightning both sit on Damage rows; neither isolates the kit (5 of 11 rows match either, 5 fall back to None). Collisions: Primal Radiance (INCLUDE) on Fire damage rows, Voltara Divine (INCLUDE) on Lightning damage rows; only the excluded non-public Youso Shiroi carries both. The bloodline-keyed label needs the classification extension; normal-jutsu collision unverified.
- Heal scale: one static row, 25 power = 250 HP per tick on two following rounds; the +5 route adds 100 HP per 40 AP cast, small against high-level pools. Tidal Mending's value rests entirely on that cast, and Increase Heal is absent from the kit, so no node compounds it. Absorb (Water) and barrier (Earth) are unsupported and unchanged.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring; normal-tree potency policy is not approved, so a combined stacking audit precedes implementation. Lightning Release and Element Divine carry Damage and Afterburn and are touched by both offensive routes: the load-bearing casts. No combat simulation was performed; balance values remain user-owned.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Shiroi Youso jutsu inherit Shiroi Youso potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
