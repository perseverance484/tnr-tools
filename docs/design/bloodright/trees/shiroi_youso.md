# Shiroi Youso — Confluence of Five

**Bloodline:** Shiroi Youso (BR-067, rank S, `vv45N2En3p8VrNcU2acd8`) · **Revision:** Fable proposal — 2026-10-04 batch rebalance / Fire + Lightning classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Fire + Lightning (multi-element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Elemental amplification (two compounding 35% five-element self Increase Damage Given buffs on Fire Release and Wind Release, a third on the injected Voltari for Earth, Lightning and Water hits; two 35% five-element exposures on Fire Release and Element Divine's area, raised only at the Foundation) · secondary Burst and burn on the marked target (Damage on Lightning Release 40 and Element Divine 45 EP, held to +2; Afterburn on the same two casts, 35% and 30%) · tertiary Defensive control (self Decrease Damage Taken on Wind Release and Earth Release; static Heal on Water Release).

Eleven supported rows sit on six casts. The highest-leverage rows are the paired five-element percentage rows: two self Increase Damage Given buffs and two exposures, each multiplying every five-element hit in the two rounds after its cast, injected children included, and compounding with one another. The tree therefore splits by who gains. Fivefold Kindling marks the target (+2% exposure, the tree's only exposure bonus) and asks "How do I finish the target I mark: strike it down with my own blows, or burn it so every hit on it feeds the fire?": a self-buff setup with a controlled +2 Damage payoff (Divine Convergence) or Afterburn that allies' hits feed too (White Immolation). Pale Vessel strengthens the caster ("How do I strengthen myself: strike harder or stand firmer?") and forks into the compounding self damage buffs (White Maelstrom) and the compounding self Decrease Damage Taken buffs with Water Release's Heal (Eye of the Tempest); both pairs stop at +8%. Flat Damage stops at +2 because the kit's Damage rows are 40 and 45 EP: +5 would lift Lightning Release to 45 and Element Divine to 50. Potency scope is multi-element pending director review: Fire and Lightning (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

## Design notes

- 2026-10-04 rebalance (BALANCE_REVIEW_METHOD.md). Rewired by recipient: Fivefold Kindling becomes the target root (+2% Increase Damage Taken; its +2% Increase Damage Given moves to the caster root) and Bedrock and Tide becomes Pale Vessel (+2% Increase Damage Given, +2% Decrease Damage Taken). Re-cut: Pale Lightning (+2 Damage) becomes a Hidden-Art setup and Divine Convergence goes +3 → +2 Damage, because +5 lifted Lightning Release 40 → 45 and Element Divine 45 → 50. Replaced: Tidal Mending (+3% Heal) and Pull of the Undertow (+5% Increase Damage Taken, +2% Decrease Damage Taken), a heal-gated exposure that did not fit a defensive Foundation, by Rising Currents (+2% Increase Damage Given) and White Maelstrom (+4%). White Immolation drops its +2% Increase Damage Taken. Bedrock and Tide's +2% Heal and Tidal Mending's +3% Heal are removed; Eye of the Tempest's +3% Increase Damage Given becomes +5% Heal (Heal maximum unchanged at +5%). Second pass: Eye of the Tempest's Decrease Damage Taken goes +5% → +3% (route +10% → +8%; next note). Final review: the setup, first Opened to the Elements (+3% Increase Damage Taken), becomes Gathering Palm (+3% self Increase Damage Given), because White Immolation's natural fourth bought that exposure on Element Divine's area on top of its Afterburn and took over Divine Convergence's niche (R1). Kept: Charred Current, White Immolation's +7% Afterburn and Gale Bulwark's +3%.
- Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +2, Increase Damage Taken +2%, Afterburn +10%, Increase Damage Given +8%, Decrease Damage Taken +8%, Heal +5%; not jointly attainable. Both self pairs compound on one hit, so both caster routes stop at +8%. Increase Damage Given: the double-buff window goes ×1.35² ≈ ×1.82 → ×1.43² ≈ ×2.04, about what Divine Convergence with Pale Vessel gives its Damage rows in the same window (×1.40² × 42/40 ≈ ×2.06). Decrease Damage Taken: incoming with both buffs live goes ×0.65² ≈ ×0.42 → ×0.57² ≈ ×0.32, ×0.769 of unmodified (+10% would reach ×0.55² ≈ ×0.30, ×0.716). Blood-Enchanted Eyes' Deathless Vitality (×0.747) runs 2 + 3 + 5 over two rows, but one is a 15% row on a 60 AP strike; here both are 35% buffs on 40 AP casts, so the tree holds them to the same test as the damage buffs. Exposure stays at the Foundation's +2% (two rows, team-wide) and Afterburn at +10% (the burns do not compound; they share the 60% per-hit cap).
- Filters (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3/§3b): the Increase Damage Given and Increase Damage Taken rows list the five elements with no stat filter, so each multiplies five-element damage only, by 1 + power/100 per matching row: the kit's two Damage rows, the five injected children and any other five-element hit; element-less hits get ratio 0. The Decrease Damage Taken rows list all four stat types and no element: every non-pierce hit. The bloodline passive (25% + 0.15/level, five-element) multiplies last.
- Delivery: all six casts are cooldown 7; Lightning Release and Element Divine cost 60 AP, the rest 40. Wind and Earth Release's rows are SELF rows realized on the caster at cast time (§4b); Water Release's heal lands on the caster. Every buff and debuff acts only in the two rounds after its cast (§3b), so Element Divine never raises its own hit: a strike sees Fire Release's mark and, for Lightning Release, an earlier Element Divine's.
- Fourth purchases: Divine Convergence takes Pale Vessel or Charred Current; White Immolation Pale Vessel or Gathering Palm; White Maelstrom Fivefold Kindling or Gale Bulwark; Eye of the Tempest Rising Currents or Fivefold Kindling. Two pairs share three nodes and differ only in the capstone. On the target, 01+02+03+04 and 01+02+04+05 trade +2 Damage for +7% Afterburn: on the caster's Lightning Release against a target carrying Fire Release's mark and Element Divine's burn they come out even (42 × 1.33 ≈ 56 against 40 × 1.40 = 56); the +2 wins in the opener and while both burns are capped at 60%, the Afterburn on allies' hits. No fourth adds exposure: White Immolation with Pale Vessel or Gathering Palm leaves Element Divine's area at Fivefold Kindling's 37%. On the caster, 06+07+09 then chooses between Eye of the Tempest, about −10% incoming (×0.60² = ×0.36 → ×0.57² ≈ ×0.32 with both buffs live) plus 100 HP per Water Release cast, and White Maelstrom, about +6% output (×1.39² ≈ ×1.93 → ×1.43² ≈ ×2.04). White Maelstrom with Fivefold Kindling and Divine Convergence with Pale Vessel hold the same Foundations, so exposure and Decrease Damage Taken cancel. On hits from Fire and Lightning jutsu (both Damage rows, Voltari, Great Fire Annihilation, Rising Water Slicer) they are even with both self buffs live (42 × 1.40² ≈ 82.3 against 40 × 1.43² ≈ 81.8; 47 × 1.40² ≈ 92.1 against 45 × 1.43² ≈ 92.0), and Divine Convergence leads with one live (×1.47 against ×1.43) or none (×1.05), because the +2 is element-wide. White Maelstrom leads on other five-element hits inside the windows (×1.43² / 1.40² ≈ ×1.04: Planetary Devastation, Great Vacuum Bullet, off-kit Earth, Wind or Water jutsu) and in Voltari's three-buff window. No fourth makes a route automatic.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification (multi-element: director review): the proposal lists both signature elements on the kit's Damage rows, Fire (Element Divine) and Lightning (Lightning Release), which reaches every jutsu of either element; choosing one element, both, or a classification extension is the director's decision. Sharing these elements with other bloodlines is expected (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Water Release and Earth Release carry neither element, so Earth Release's Decrease Damage Taken (Pale Vessel, the fortress) and Water Release's Heal (Eye of the Tempest) need an authored jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Fire/Lightning coverage is unverified.
- Ally hazard: Element Divine row 1 (Afterburn 30%) and row 2 (exposure 35%) are AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire none (=ALL): allies inside the circle also receive the debuffs; the caster never does (§4b). Fivefold Kindling (exposure), Charred Current and White Immolation (Afterburn) raise what allies there take; positioning decides. The Damage row is ENEMIES-only.
- Afterburn saturation: Lightning Release 35% and Element Divine 30% already pass the 60% per-hit cap together (65%), so White Immolation's 45% / 40% adds value only while one burn is active. Downstream coverage (every non-pierce hit by anyone for 2 rounds, including the children cast into Lightning Release's 2-round stun) was not simulated; pierce hits take no Afterburn.
- Compounding through injected children: Voltari, a SPECIAL Lightning jutsu injected by Lightning Release, is in scope under Fire + Lightning, so its own 35% self Increase Damage Given row (Earth, Lightning, Water; 2 rounds) takes the same bonuses: Earth, Lightning and Water hits in the two rounds after Voltari compound three self buffs (×1.43³ ≈ ×2.92 at White Maelstrom's maximum, ×2.46 unmodified; R3 departure in design_review.concerns). Rising Water Slicer's 35% exposure row (Earth, Fire, Lightning) is in scope too: a third exposure at Fivefold Kindling's 37% (×1.37³ ≈ ×2.57). Not simulated.
- Injected children's Damage: under Fire + Lightning, Voltari (40 EP), Great Fire Annihilation (45 EP) and Rising Water Slicer (40 EP) also take Divine Convergence's +2 (42 / 47 / 42: no tier crossed); Planetary Devastation (Earth) and Great Vacuum Bullet (Wind) carry no qualifying element on a supported row. Children are cast at the inject power as level (100/110) and are not counted in this tree's coverage.
- Heal scale: one static row, 25 = 250 HP per tick on two following rounds; Eye of the Tempest's +5% adds 50 HP per tick (100 HP per 40 AP cast), small against high-level pools. Absorb (Water) and barrier (Earth) are unsupported and unchanged.
- Skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Fire and Lightning jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
