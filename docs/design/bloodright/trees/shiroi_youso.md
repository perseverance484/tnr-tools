# Shiroi Youso — Confluence of Five

**Bloodline:** Shiroi Youso (BR-067, rank S, `vv45N2En3p8VrNcU2acd8`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Fire + Lightning multi-element classification (director review) / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Fire + Lightning (multi-element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Elemental amplification (self Increase Damage Given on Fire Release and Wind Release; enemy Increase Damage Taken on Fire Release and Element Divine's area) · secondary Burst and burn (Damage on Lightning Release 40 and Element Divine 45; Afterburn on the same two casts, 35% and 30%) · tertiary Defensive control (self Decrease Damage Taken on Wind Release and Earth Release; static Heal on Water Release).

Eleven supported rows sit on six casts and every percentage tag except Heal reaches exactly two of them: two 35% five-element self damage buffs (Fire Release, Wind Release), two 35% five-element exposures (Fire Release, Element Divine's area), two 35% all-stat self Decrease Damage Taken rows (Wind, Earth), two Afterburn rows (Lightning Release 35%, Element Divine 30%) and two Damage rows (40, 45 EP) under a 25% + 0.15/level five-element Increase Damage Given passive. The kit's Burst and Combo Playmaking traits ride Lightning Release and Element Divine (60 AP each) cast in the round after Fire Release's setup (buffs act only after their cast round, SOURCE_MECHANICS §3b), so the offensive root carries amplification and forks into Damage and Afterburn; the Defensive Control trait lives in the two-round self Decrease Damage Taken buffs and the static 25 Water Heal, so the defensive root forks into Decrease Damage Taken and a sustain route that ends in exposure. Potency scope is multi-element pending director review: the proposal lists both signature elements, Fire and Lightning (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Fire and Lightning jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Eye of the Tempest** — At the storm's heart the air is still, and every strike from it is a hurricane. Fortified: Wind Release and Earth Release 35 → 45% Decrease Damage Taken on the full route (+10%); self damage buffs on Fire Release and Wind Release +3% (40% with Fivefold Kindling).
- **Tidal Mending** — Water closes over every wound as if it had never been opened. Water Release Heal +3% (30 with Bedrock and Tide: 300 HP per tick on the two following rounds; 40 AP, cooldown 7).
- **Pull of the Undertow** — The tide takes the footing from under them and gives it to you. Control: exposure on Fire Release and Element Divine's area (ally hazard) 35 → 40% (+5%; 42% with Fivefold Kindling); self Decrease Damage Taken +2% (39% with Bedrock and Tide).

## Design notes

- Shape: Fivefold Kindling (+2% Increase Damage Given, +2% Increase Damage Taken) forks into Damage (Pale Lightning → Divine Convergence) and Afterburn (Charred Current → White Immolation); Bedrock and Tide (+2% Decrease Damage Taken, +2% Heal) into Decrease Damage Taken (Gale Bulwark → Eye of the Tempest) and sustain-to-exposure (Tidal Mending → Pull of the Undertow). This is Taiyo Kami's topology; six flats match it (Kindling = Dawnheart, Pale Lightning = Crown of Cinders, Divine Convergence = Solar Cataclysm, Charred Current = Emberwake, Gale Bulwark = Golden Mantle, Eye of the Tempest = Sovereign Sun).
- Routes after the RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration: Burst +5 Damage (Pale Lightning +2, Divine Convergence +3; was +2 / +4); Burn pressure +10% Afterburn (Charred Current +3%, White Immolation +7%; was +3% / +4%); Fortified +10% Decrease Damage Taken (Bedrock and Tide +2%, Gale Bulwark +3%, Eye of the Tempest +5%; was +2% / +3% / +2%); Control +5% Increase Damage Taken (Pull of the Undertow +5%, was +4%; 42% with Fivefold Kindling). Maxima over every legal allocation: +5 Damage, +5% Increase Damage Given, +7% Increase Damage Taken, +10% Decrease Damage Taken, +10% Afterburn, +5% Heal; not jointly attainable. Pull of the Undertow's +2% Decrease Damage Taken stays below its sibling Gale Bulwark's +3%. Every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5–6 BP.
- Main tree and passives: the Increase Damage Given and Increase Damage Taken rows list the five elements with no stat filter, so (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3/§3b) each multiplies five-element damage only, by 1 + power/100 per matching row: the kit's two Damage rows, the five injected children and any other five-element hit; element-less damage (basic attacks, non-elemental jutsu, element-less weapons) gets ratio 0. The Decrease Damage Taken rows list all four stat types, no element: every non-pierce hit. The bloodline passive (25% + 0.15/level, five-element) multiplies last.
- Delivery and uptime: all six casts are cooldown 7; Lightning Release and Element Divine cost 60 AP, the rest 40. Wind's Decrease Damage Taken / Increase Damage Given and Earth's Decrease Damage Taken are SELF rows realized on the caster at cast time (§4b); only the unsupported INHERIT rows (Wind's move, Earth's barrier) become ground effects. Water's heal lands on the caster (SELF target, util.ts 2753). Every buff/debuff acts in the two rounds after its cast, never on its own cast's hit (§3b): Fire Release sets up the next round's strikes.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Bedrock and Tide or Charred Current; Burn pressure Bedrock and Tide or Pale Lightning; Fortified Fivefold Kindling or Tidal Mending; Control Fivefold Kindling or Gale Bulwark. Tidal Mending (+3% Heal, +30 HP per tick on the single heal row) is the lowest-value node and serves mainly as the gate to Pull of the Undertow.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification (multi-element: director review): the proposal lists every signature element on the kit's Damage rows, Fire (Element Divine) and Lightning (Lightning Release), as qualifying, which reaches every jutsu of either element; choosing one element, both, or a classification extension is the director's decision. Sharing these elements with other bloodlines is expected (RUL-2026-10-03-005). Water Release and Earth Release carry neither element, so in-kit they qualify only through an authored jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Fire/Lightning coverage (NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN) is unverified.
- Ally hazard: Element Divine row 1 (Afterburn 30%) and row 2 (exposure 35%) are AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire none (=ALL): allies inside the circle also receive the debuffs; the caster never does (OTHER_USER target, SOURCE_MECHANICS §4b). Kindling, Charred Current, White Immolation and Pull of the Undertow raise what allies there take; positioning decides. The Damage row is ENEMIES-only.
- Afterburn saturation: Lightning Release 35% and Element Divine 30% stack to 65% on one target, above the 60% per-hit cap, so the +10% route (45% / 40%) adds value only while a single Afterburn source is active. Downstream coverage (every non-pierce hit by anyone for 2 rounds, including the injected children cast into Lightning Release's 2-round stun) was not simulated; pierce hits take no Afterburn.
- Reach: the +5% self buff and +7% exposure reach only five-element damage: the player's other five-element jutsu (Increase Damage Given) and anyone's five-element hits on the exposed target, allies included (Increase Damage Taken); basic attacks, element-less weapons and non-elemental jutsu get ratio 0. Same-tag rows compound (§3b): overlapping Fire and Wind buffs at 40% give ×1.40 × 1.40 ≈ ×1.96, and Fire plus Element Divine exposures at 42% give ×1.42 × 1.42 ≈ ×2.02.
- Delivery: Wind Release rows 0-1 and Earth Release row 0 are SELF rows on EMPTY_GROUND casts; Water Release's INHERIT heal is on a SELF-target cast (only the caster is valid, util.ts 2753): all four land on the caster at cast time (§4b). Eye of the Tempest carries both the Decrease Damage Taken (45%) and Increase Damage Given (40% with Kindling) maxima; its cost is the forgone Damage and Afterburn routes.
- Injected children: Lightning, Fire and Water Release inject five SPECIAL children (level = inject power 100/110). Provenance is not a selector: a child is in scope only as a jutsu of a qualifying element, and its rows are not counted in this tree's coverage. Under Fire + Lightning, Voltari (Lightning Damage, Increase Damage Given), Great Fire Annihilation (Fire Damage) and Rising Water Slicer (Damage, and an exposure row listing Fire and Lightning) would qualify; Planetary Devastation (Earth) and Great Vacuum Bullet (Wind) carry no qualifying element on a supported row.
- Heal scale: one static row, 25 = 250 HP per tick on two following rounds; +5% Heal at the maximum adds 50 HP per tick (100 HP per 40 AP cast), small against high-level pools. Increase Heal is absent from the kit. Absorb (Water) and barrier (Earth) are unsupported and unchanged.
- Context: Lightning Release and Element Divine carry Damage and Afterburn and are touched by both offensive routes: the load-bearing casts. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Fire and Lightning jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
