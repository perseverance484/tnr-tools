# Godstorm Eclipse — Throne of the Black Sun

**Bloodline:** Godstorm Eclipse (BR-029, rank H, `szai-IgtB7PLubojIIlh-`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Godstorm Eclipse classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Godstorm Eclipse (bloodline-keyed extension) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment_and_classification_extension

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Burst Heavy; five Shadow/Storm damage rows, +5 ceiling) · secondary Lifesteal (Sustain, Tempest, +10 ceiling) and Decrease Damage Taken (Tank, Gate and Bulwark, +7 ceiling) · tertiary Afterburn pressure (Tempest, +10 ceiling); Increase Damage Given (+5 on four rows) and Increase Damage Taken (+5 on three rows) as glue.

Godstorm Eclipse is a rank H kit whose traits read Sustain / Burst Heavy / Tank. Five of its twelve jutsu carry a Damage row (Raijin's Cage 50; Storm-God's Heart, Black Sun Cataract and Corona Devourer Palm 45; Obsidian Tomb 40, all 60 AP on a 7-round cooldown), so burst is the primary emphasis and the deepest raw-power route. Godstorm Mantle: Tempest is the sustain engine (Afterburn 35%, Lifesteal 40% and IDG 35% on one 40 AP cast) and anchors both the lifesteal and the burn routes, while Event Horizon Gate and Thunder-Crowned Bulwark carry the two DDT rows that anchor the tank route. IDG (four self-buff rows) and IDT (three enemy-debuff rows) are confined to Foundation 01 and to capstone secondaries so no capstone duplicates a sibling Hidden Art and every full-budget allocation stays non-dominated.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Godstorm Eclipse jutsu (bloodline-keyed classification, proposed extension) under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Black Sun Rising** — The eclipse crowns the storm, and whatever it looks upon is laid bare. IDG 35% on Wall, Tempest, Bulwark and Marrow Mantle (self, 2 rounds); IDT 35% on Gate (ally hazard), Wall and Storm-God's Heart.
- **Hammer of the Heavens** — Thunder is not a sound. It is the whole weight of the sky coming down. Damage on Raijin's Cage 50, Storm-God's Heart 45, Black Sun Cataract 45, Corona Devourer Palm 45 (ally hazard), Obsidian Tomb 40; all 60 AP.
- **Total Eclipse** — For one breath the sun is gone, and only the storm-god's judgement remains. Same five Damage rows, 40/45/50 -> 45/50/55 on the full route; the Verdicts' pierce rows are unsupported and unchanged.
- **Charged Firmament** — The air itself keeps the lightning's grudge long after the flash. Tempest row 0 Afterburn 35% -> 38% (enemy, 2 rounds, 40 AP); every non-pierce hit that target takes while burning feeds it.
- **Lingering Corona** — The eclipse passes; the burning ring it leaves behind does not forgive. Tempest Afterburn 35% -> 45% on the route (60% per-hit cap); IDT on Gate (ally hazard), Wall, Heart: 35% -> 40% with Black Sun Rising.
- **Stormgod's Marrow** — The storm lives in the bone, and the bone drinks back whatever the storm costs. DDT 35% on Event Horizon Gate (circle, friendlies inside) and Thunder-Crowned Bulwark (ground); Lifesteal 40% on Tempest (self, 2 rounds).
- **Obsidian Skin** — Cooled from godfire, the Eclipse's hide turns blades the way glass turns light. Gate and Bulwark DDT 35% -> 39% with Stormgod's Marrow; Gate only while inside its circle, Bulwark from the round after stepping in.
- **Pillar of Heaven** — Immovable beneath the whole weight of the sky, and the sky answers to it. Gate and Bulwark DDT 35% -> 42% on the route; IDG on Wall, Tempest, Bulwark, Marrow Mantle 35% -> 40% with Black Sun Rising.
- **Devouring Eclipse** — The moon does not merely cover the sun. It feeds. Tempest row 1 Lifesteal 40% -> 45% with Stormgod's Marrow (self, 2 rounds); shares the 60% leech cap with vamp; pierce hits count.
- **Eater of Suns** — What the eclipse swallows, the storm-god keeps for good. All three Tempest rows: Lifesteal 40% -> 50% on the route, Afterburn 35% -> 37%, IDG 35% -> 37% (also Wall, Bulwark, Marrow Mantle).

- **Total Eclipse:** Damage +5 lifts all five Shadow and Storm attacks (Raijin's Cage 50 -> 55; Storm-God's Heart, Black Sun Cataract and Corona Devourer Palm 45 -> 50; Obsidian Tomb 40 -> 45), and the bloodline's own IDG passive (25% + 0.15/level across Shadow, Storm and None) multiplies that raw power downstream. Black Sun Rising is the required ancestor and adds 2% IDG on four self buffs and 2% exposure on three debuffs; Stormgod's Marrow is the fourth purchase for 2% DDT and 2% lifesteal. It is the pure burst option and gives up the deep sustain, the tank route and the burn entirely.
- **Lingering Corona:** Tempest's Afterburn goes from 35% to 45% for its two rounds, so every non-pierce hit the burning target takes (the kit's five damage rows, weapons, normal jutsu, allies) carries up to 45% extra inside the 60% per-hit cap; the capstone's +3 IDT with Black Sun Rising takes Gate, Wall and Storm-God's Heart exposure to 40%. Stormgod's Marrow rounds it out with 2% DDT and 2% lifesteal. This is single-target pressure: the burn follows only the one enemy Tempest struck, and raw damage power is untouched.
- **Pillar of Heaven:** The DDT route takes Event Horizon Gate's and Thunder-Crowned Bulwark's 35% mitigation to 42% while you stand in them, and the capstone's +3 IDG with Black Sun Rising lifts all four self damage buffs (Wall, Tempest, Bulwark, Marrow Mantle) to 40%. It is the tank who hits back: lifesteal stays at 42% and Afterburn at 35%, so it is clearly neither the sustain nor the burn build, and its mitigation is positional (inside the Gate circle, on the Bulwark tiles).
- **Eater of Suns:** The lifesteal route takes Tempest's leech from 40% to 50%, ten points under the 60% cap shared with vamp, and draws from every damage row the caster lands, including the unsupported 60- and 66-power pierce Verdicts. Its small Tempest secondaries plus Black Sun Rising leave IDG at 39% on four rows, Afterburn at 37% and exposure at 37%. The alternative fourth purchase, Obsidian Skin instead of Black Sun Rising, is the tank-sustain variant (DDT 39%, no exposure, IDG 37%).

## Design notes

- Node split: two Foundations each fork into two Hidden -> Advanced routes mapping onto the four non-glue tags and the traits: Damage (Burst Heavy, 5 rows), Afterburn (pressure, Tempest row 0), DDT (Tank, Gate and Bulwark) and Lifesteal (Sustain, Tempest row 1). IDG and IDT appear only on Foundation 01 and as capstone secondaries, never on a Hidden Art, and Eater of Suns' +2 Afterburn sits below Charged Firmament's +3, so all 14 full-budget allocations are non-dominated and every node is used.
- Calibration: Foundations +2/+2; Hidden Arts +2 Damage (5 rows), +2 DDT (2 rows) or +3 on a single-row tag; capstones +3 Damage or +5/+7 primary with +2/+3 secondaries. Per-row gains match Taiyo Kami (Damage +5 is +10-12.5% on 40-50 power rows), but 16 supported rows against Taiyo's 9 put the row-weighted strongest build at 45 (01+02+03+06) against 31. Scaling: two-row DDT capped at +7 (Taiyo +10), four-row IDG and three-row IDT at +5. Lever if burst reads too strong: Total Eclipse to +2 Damage.
- Passives and main tree: the bloodline IDG passive (25% + 0.15/level on Fire, Lightning, None, Shadow, Storm, Water, Wind) multiplies every enhanced Damage row downstream. The four IDG self buffs carry no element and 'Highest' (Bulwark: all four) stat types, so while active they also raise basic attacks, weapons and element-less normal jutsu; the three IDT debuffs amplify allies' non-pierce hits on that enemy. Lifesteal includes the Verdicts' 60/66 pierce rows; Afterburn and IDG/IDT skip pierce.
- Delivery and uptime: all 12 jutsu are item-gated behind two items (Eclipse set: Gate, Tomb, Midnight Verdict, Corona Palm, Marrow Mantle, Cataract; Mantle set: Heart, Heavenbreaker, Cage, Tempest, Wall, Bulwark); cooldowns are 7 rounds, damage rows cost 60 AP, buffs 40 AP for 2 rounds. Gate's DDT reaches only friendlies inside the circle spawned on the enemy, who also take its IDT; Bulwark's rows apply from the round after stepping onto its tiles, while you stay. Ranked modes skip skill effects.
- Fourth-purchase choices: after any 3 BP route the natural fourth is the other Foundation. Unadvertised legal builds include 01+02+04+05 (the strongest burn: +2 Damage, +10 Afterburn, +5 IDT, no mitigation), 01+02+03+04 (burst with 3% Afterburn), 06+07+08+09 (pure fortress: +7 DDT, +5 lifesteal, +3 IDG) and 06+07+09+10 (tank-sustain: +10 lifesteal, +4 DDT). No-capstone hybrids such as 01+02+06+09 (+2 Damage, +5 lifesteal, +2 IDG/IDT/DDT) are legal and non-dominated.
- Four examples are given because each capstone answers a different trait or role (burst, pressure, tank, sustain) with its own Advanced Art; the pressure route's value is downstream on the five non-pierce damage rows and is not measured by its single application row. User-owned tuning left open: Total Eclipse +3 vs +2 Damage; whether the two-row DDT route should match Taiyo's +10 rather than +7; whether Black Sun Rising should carry IDT at all given the Gate ally-hazard row.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Shadow and Storm both sit on the kit's damage rows and neither is exclusive in the census (Shadow: Blood-Enchanted Eyes, Oblivion Seal, Shadow Weaver, Night Parade and deferred/excluded kits; Storm: Arashima, Shinrai Ou, Stormboat Willy), so a bloodline-keyed classification extension is required, not a bare element label. Non-bloodline jutsu carrying Shadow or Storm: unverified.
- Resolver: 11 of the 16 supported rows carry no element and fall back to None under the current resolver; affectedElements=['Shadow'] matches only 3 rows directly and ['Storm'] only 2. Reaching the rest today would need None, which also reaches every non-elemental row on any jutsu the player casts. The tree assumes the proposed whole-kit classification.
- Ally hazard (validator warns): Event Horizon Gate row 1 IDT (circle on the enemy, friendly fire none) is raised by Black Sun Rising and Lingering Corona; Corona Devourer Palm row 0 Damage (ground spiral, friendly fire none) by Hammer of the Heavens and Total Eclipse. Allies and the caster inside those areas take the enhanced effect; positioning decides. Inside the Gate circle, DDT and IDT offset.
- Item gates: every supported row sits on an item-gated jutsu (two item ids gate six jutsu each), so the tree's realized value depends on holding both items. Gating is uniform across tags, so no per-tag down-weighting was applied, but the Eclipse set alone removes Tempest and with it the whole Afterburn and Lifesteal routes; the Mantle set alone leaves two of the five Damage rows and one DDT row.
- Lifesteal: the route tops out at 50%, ten points under the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage leech budget shared with vamp; vamp from the normal tree or items can saturate the cap and waste part of the +10. It needs both combatants alive and is blocked by healprevent on the caster. Pierce hits (Heavenbreaker 60, Midnight Verdict 66) feed lifesteal at full ratio: the sustain route's unlisted upside.
- Afterburn: the +10 route is one application row on a single-target 40 AP cast; its value is downstream on every non-pierce hit that one enemy takes for two rounds, capped at 60% of each hit, so other Afterburn sources saturate quickly, and the kit's own pierce Verdicts never feed it. Not simulated.
- Stacking: BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is true at the pin, so these potency effects stack with any other potency source; the combined budget with a future normal-tree potency policy is unaudited. The bloodline IDG passive multiplies the enhanced Damage rows and four separate 2-round IDG buffs at 40% can be overlapped by alternating 40 AP casts; neither was modelled.
- No combat simulation: non-dominance of the 14 allocations and the row-weighted totals are arithmetic over per-row additions, not evidence of equal combat strength. AoE target counts (Heart and Cataract circles, Corona spiral), the Cage stun, Tomb's buff prevention, uptime, AP and the Bulwark move row were not modelled. No adverse, hidden, mode-restricted or injected rows exist here.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Godstorm Eclipse jutsu inherit Godstorm Eclipse potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
