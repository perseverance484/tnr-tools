# Godstorm Eclipse — Throne of the Black Sun

**Bloodline:** Godstorm Eclipse (BR-029, rank H, `szai-IgtB7PLubojIIlh-`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Shadow + Storm classification (multi-element, director review) / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Shadow + Storm (multi-element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Burst Heavy; five Shadow/Storm Damage rows, +5 Damage) · secondary Decrease Damage Taken (Tank: Gate and Bulwark, +10%) and Lifesteal (Sustain: Tempest, +5%) · tertiary Afterburn pressure (Tempest, +10%); Increase Damage Given (+5% on four rows) and Increase Damage Taken (+5% on three rows) as glue.

Godstorm Eclipse is a rank H kit whose traits read Sustain / Burst Heavy / Tank. Five of its twelve jutsu carry a Damage row (Raijin's Cage 50; Storm-God's Heart, Black Sun Cataract and Corona Devourer Palm 45; Obsidian Tomb 40 EP, all 60 AP on a 7-round cooldown), so burst is the primary emphasis. Godstorm Mantle: Tempest is the sustain engine (Afterburn 35%, Lifesteal 40% and Increase Damage Given 35% on one 40 AP cast) and anchors both the Lifesteal and the burn routes, while Event Horizon Gate and Thunder-Crowned Bulwark carry the two Decrease Damage Taken rows that anchor the Tank route. Increase Damage Given (four self-buff rows) and Increase Damage Taken (three enemy-debuff rows) are confined to Black Sun Rising and capstone secondaries. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Shadow and Storm jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005; multi-element, director review).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Shadow and Storm jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

The tank who hits back: Lifesteal stays at 40%, Afterburn at 35% and Damage is untouched.

## Design notes

- Node split: two Foundations each fork into two Hidden → Advanced routes mapped onto the traits: Damage (Burst Heavy, 5 rows), Afterburn (pressure, Tempest row 0), Decrease Damage Taken (Tank, Gate and Bulwark) and Lifesteal (Sustain, Tempest row 1). Increase Damage Given and Increase Damage Taken appear only on Black Sun Rising and as capstone secondaries, never on a Hidden Art, and Eater of Suns' +2% Afterburn sits below Charged Firmament's +3%, so all 14 full-budget allocations are non-dominated and every node is used.
- Routes (RUL-2026-10-03-005): Burst +5 Damage (Hammer of the Heavens +2, Total Eclipse +3); Burn pressure +10% Afterburn (Charged Firmament +3%, Lingering Corona +7%); Tank +10% Decrease Damage Taken (Stormgod's Marrow +2%, Obsidian Skin +3%, Pillar of Heaven +5%); Sustain +5% Lifesteal (Devouring Eclipse +2%, Eater of Suns +3%). Glue maxima over every legal allocation: Increase Damage Given +5%, Increase Damage Taken +5%; not jointly attainable.
- Recalibration from Draft 2: Lifesteal is hard-capped at +5%, so Stormgod's Marrow drops its +2% Lifesteal (it is now a Decrease Damage Taken gate), Devouring Eclipse goes +3% → +2% and Eater of Suns +5% → +3% (Sustain +10% → +5%; Tempest 45%, not 50%). The Tank route moves from +7% (2/2/3) to the +10% band (Obsidian Skin +2% → +3%, Pillar of Heaven +3% → +5%). Damage, Afterburn and glue values are unchanged.
- Reference reuse: Black Sun Rising, Hammer of the Heavens, Total Eclipse, Charged Firmament, Obsidian Skin and Pillar of Heaven carry Taiyo Kami's Dawnheart, Crown of Cinders, Solar Cataclysm, Emberwake, Golden Mantle and Sovereign Sun values; Lingering Corona is Eternal Noon without its +3% Increase Damage Given; Stormgod's Marrow is Sunward Oath without Decrease Damage Given (the kit has no such row); Lifesteal replaces the Suppression route.
- Passives: the bloodline Increase Damage Given passive (25% + 0.15/level on seven elements incl. None, Shadow, Storm) applies last to the enhanced Damage rows, after the kit IDG/IDT rows multiply the hit in turn and the DDT rows reduce it in sequence (computeDamagePacket, §3b). The four IDG self buffs carry no element ('Highest' stats; Bulwark all four), so they also raise basic attacks, weapons and element-less jutsu; the three IDT debuffs raise allies' hits on that enemy. Lifesteal counts the Verdicts' pierce hits; Afterburn, IDG, IDT and DDT skip pierce.
- Delivery and uptime: cooldowns are 7 rounds, Damage rows cost 60 AP, buff casts 40 AP. Every DDT, IDG and Lifesteal row lands on the caster at cast and is live the two rounds after, wherever it stands (§3b, §4b): Gate's DDT and Bulwark's IDG/DDT are SELF rows, not circle or tile effects (Bulwark's only positional row is its unsupported move). No buff helps a hit in its cast round. Ranked modes skip skill-tree effects.
- Fourth purchases: after any 3 BP route the natural fourth is the other Foundation; four examples, one per capstone and trait. Pillar of Heaven (01+06+07+08) is the only Increase Damage Given +5% build and one of two at Decrease Damage Taken +10% (with 06+07+08+09). Also legal: 01+02+04+05 (burn with +2 Damage), 06+07+09+10 (tank-sustain), 01+02+06+09 (no-capstone hybrid).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification (multi-element, director review): Shadow and Storm both sit on the kit's Damage rows. The proposal lists both signature elements as qualifying, so potency reaches matching supported tags on all Shadow and all Storm jutsu, whatever their source; choosing one element, both, or a classification extension is the director's decision. Sharing either element with other bloodlines is expected. Gate, Tempest, Wall, Bulwark and Marrow Mantle carry no Shadow or Storm row, so in-kit they qualify only through an authored jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit coverage is unverified.
- Resolver: 11 of the 16 supported rows carry no element, so the current row-element resolver cannot reach them (['Shadow'] matches 3 rows directly, ['Storm'] 2); the tree depends on the proposed jutsu-classification resolver.
- Ally hazard (validator warns): Gate row 1 exposure (friendly fire none; raised by Black Sun Rising and Lingering Corona) lands once at cast on every non-caster user in its radius-1 circle, so allies there take it for two rounds; the caster never does, and Gate's SELF DDT offsets nothing (§4b). Corona Palm row 0 Damage (raised by Hammer of the Heavens and Total Eclipse) is a GROUND spiral of radius 4 around the caster, own tile excluded, hitting allies on its tiles.
- Equipment gates castability only: every kit jutsu needs Eclipse Marrow or Godstorm Aegis equipped to be cast, but no node lists an item as a prerequisite and no bonus is limited by one. Which keystone is worn decides which rows are castable: Eclipse Marrow alone leaves no castable Tempest row (no Afterburn or Lifesteal), Godstorm Aegis alone keeps two Damage rows and one Decrease Damage Taken row.
- Lifesteal: the route tops out at 45% (+5%, the hard ceiling), fifteen points under the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage leech budget shared with vamp; vamp from the normal tree or items can still saturate it. It needs both combatants alive and is blocked by healprevent on the caster. Pierce hits (Heavenbreaker 60, Midnight Verdict 66) feed Lifesteal at full ratio.
- Afterburn: the +10% route is one application row on a single-target 40 AP cast (45% at the maximum); its value is downstream on every non-pierce hit that one enemy takes for two rounds, capped at 60% of each hit, so other Afterburn sources saturate quickly, and the kit's own pierce Verdicts never feed it. Not simulated.
- Stacking: BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is true at the pin, so these modifiers stack with other potency sources (budget with a future normal-tree policy unaudited). Same-tag kit effects all apply and compound (§3b): two live 40% IDG buffs give ×1.40 × 1.40 = ×1.96 on a hit both cover, Gate and Bulwark DDT at 45% give ×0.55 × 0.55 ≈ ×0.30 (×0.65² ≈ ×0.42 without the tree), and the bloodline passive applies last. Overlap uptime and AP were not modelled.
- No combat simulation: non-dominance of the 14 allocations and the row-weighted totals are arithmetic over per-row additions, not evidence of equal combat strength. AoE target counts (Heart and Cataract circles, Corona spiral), the Cage stun, Tomb's buff prevention, uptime, AP and the Bulwark move row were not modelled. No adverse, hidden, mode-restricted or injected rows exist here.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Shadow and Storm jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
