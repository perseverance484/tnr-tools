# Teno Yuki — Court of Heaven's Snow

**Bloodline:** Teno Yuki (BR-077, rank A, `clh4d6qjs000gtb0hr357gjvv`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Ice classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Ice (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Control by identity (Increase Damage Taken 35% on Ice Palace's circle; Decrease Damage Given 30% on Ice Coffin) · secondary Burst offense, the broadest coverage and heaviest route (Damage on three Ice rows at 45/40/40; Increase Damage Given 35% on Frostbound Ascendancy) · tertiary Self preservation (Decrease Damage Taken 35% and Heal 25 on Cryostorm Aegis).

Teno Yuki is an A-rank Genjutsu bloodline with the Control and Defensive traits, and its supported rows split the same way: two enemy debuffs on 60 AP attack casts (Ice Palace exposure, Ice Coffin suppression), three Ice Damage rows at 45/40/40 (Imperial Freeze, Ice Coffin, Ice Palace) under an Increase Damage Given passive of 25% + 0.15 per user level, a single 35% self damage buff on Frostbound Ascendancy, and the kit's only guard and heal on the 40 AP Cryostorm Aegis. Control is primary by trait identity, not by weight: exposure and suppression are single rows that make the Genjutsu kit a pressure tool, while Damage is the broadest tag (three rows) and its route gives the strongest row-weighted build (Burst 23 against 19 for Exposure and Bulwark and 18 for Suppression). The Aegis rows are the tertiary sustain route; stun, absorb and recoil are unsupported and untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Ice-classified Teno Yuki jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **First Snow of Heaven** — The first flakes settle without a sound; by morning the court belongs to winter. IDG 35→37% on Frostbound Ascendancy (self); IDT 35→37% on Ice Palace's circle (enemies and allies inside). Both live 2 rounds after cast.
- **Killing Frost** — One cold night is enough. Nothing green survives it. Damage 45→47 on Imperial Freeze; 40→42 on Ice Coffin and Ice Palace (Ice, Genjutsu-scaled, 60 AP). Palace's row hits allies in the circle.
- **Reign of Absolute Zero** — At the bottom of the cold nothing moves, nothing resists, nothing is spared. Route total +5: Imperial Freeze 45→50, Ice Coffin and Ice Palace 40→45 on the same three Ice Damage rows.
- **Hairline Fracture** — Every surface the frost touches is already fractured; it only waits for the blow. Ice Palace IDT 37→40% with First Snow of Heaven (one AOE row, 2 rounds, 60 AP, cooldown 7); the kit's only exposure row.
- **Court of Splintered Ice** — The palace shatters around the condemned; every shard carries the sovereign's will. Ice Palace IDT 45% on the full route (Ice, Water, Wind and element-less hits); Frostbound Ascendancy IDG 40% with First Snow of Heaven.
- **Vigil of Winter** — The watch is kept in silence and frost; the gate does not open. DDT 35→37% on Cryostorm Aegis (self, 40 AP, 2 rounds); DDG 30→32% on Ice Coffin (single enemy, 60 AP, 2 rounds).
- **Permafrost Mantle** — Ground that has not thawed in a thousand years does not give way to a blade. Cryostorm Aegis DDT 37→40% with Vigil of Winter (one self row, all four stat types, no element: every non-pierce hit taken).
- **Still Heart of Winter** — Beneath the snow the heart slows, mends, and endures until the thaw that never comes. Cryostorm Aegis DDT 45% on the full route; its Heal 25→28 power (250→280 HP per tick on the two following rounds), both on one 40 AP cast.
- **Cold Saps the Will** — Numb hands, slow thoughts, a blow that lands without conviction. Ice Coffin DDG 32→35% with Vigil of Winter (one single-target row, 2 rounds, 60 AP); the kit's only suppression row.
- **Silence of Falling Snow** — Snow deadens every sound. The enemy's fury arrives as a whisper; you are already healing. Ice Coffin DDG 40% on the full route (one single-target row); Cryostorm Aegis Heal 25→27 power (270 HP per tick, two ticks per cast).

## Design notes

- Node split: First Snow of Heaven (IDG + IDT) is the attack root, forking into raw power (Killing Frost → Reign of Absolute Zero, three Ice rows) and exposure (Hairline Fracture → Court of Splintered Ice, the Ice Palace row). Vigil of Winter (DDT + DDG) is the defensive root, forking into guard (Permafrost Mantle → Still Heart of Winter, both Aegis rows) and suppression (Cold Saps the Will → Silence of Falling Snow, the Coffin row). 2F/4H/4A; capstones 3 BP deep; any two cost 5-6 BP.
- Calibration by coverage: Damage +2/+3 (max +5) on three rows matches Taiyo's three-row figure (15 row-weighted). IDT, DDT and DDG are single rows at +2/+3/+5 = +10 (45/45/40%), Taiyo's single-row scale; IDT gets a full route as the only exposure row. IDG is glue (+2 root, +3 capstone secondary; max 40%) because the passive (25% + 0.15 per user level, combat.ts 3062: 28.75% at user level 25, 40% at 100) already multiplies matching hits. Heal is a secondary only (+3/+2 = 280 HP per tick).
- Resolver reads (§3): Aegis DDT and Coffin DDG list all four stat types and no element, so the filter is not binding: they act on every element-less or stat-typed non-pierce hit the caster takes or the Coffined enemy deals. Ice Palace IDT and Frostbound IDG list Ice/None/Water/Wind, no stat types: they match Ice, Water, Wind and element-less hits, never pierce. IDT is target-side, raising such hits from caster or allies on the exposed enemy; Frostbound's SELF IDG raises only the caster's own hits.
- Delivery: all five jutsu have cooldown 7; the attacks cost 60 AP, Cryostorm Aegis and Frostbound Ascendancy 40 AP. Percentage rows and Aegis heal ticks are live the two rounds after the cast round (§3b): Ice Palace's IDT never raises its own hit, Frostbound's IDG only later-round attacks. Frostbound's IDG row is target SELF, realized on the caster at cast whatever the spiral (actions.ts 980-1004). Ice Palace (AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN radius 1, OTHER_USER) lands both rows once on each living non-caster in the circle.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Vigil of Winter (37% guard, 32% suppression) or Hairline Fracture (40% exposure); Exposure takes Vigil of Winter or Killing Frost (+2 power); Bulwark takes First Snow of Heaven (37% exposure and self buff) or Cold Saps the Will (35% suppression); Suppression takes First Snow of Heaven or Permafrost Mantle (40% guard). Strongest unadvertised allocation by row weight: Reign of Absolute Zero + Hairline Fracture (+5 power, 40% exposure, no guard).
- Reference reuse: the flats copy Taiyo Kami nearly verbatim (same 2F/4H/4A frame; +2/+2 Foundations, Damage +2→+3, DDT and DDG +3→+5, +3 capstone secondaries) because the kits share five of Taiyo's six tags at similar reach (three Damage rows, single-row IDT/DDT/DDG). Departures: no Afterburn rows, so IDT takes Emberwake's slot as its own route (+3→+5, IDG secondary); Heal replaces IDG/IDT as the defensive capstones' secondary, so no tag crosses roots and all 14 allocations stay non-dominated.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Ice is not exclusive. Blue Blade Eyes and Hyouga Yui (INCLUDE), Blue Edge Eyes (EXCLUDE) and four DEFER records carry Ice rows; the normal-jutsu collision is unverified. Today 5 of 8 supported rows match Ice directly and 3 (Aegis DDT and Heal, Coffin DDG) fall back to None, which also reaches non-elemental rows on any jutsu. The tree needs a bloodline-scoped classification.
- Ally hazard: Ice Palace rows 0 (Damage) and 1 (IDT) are AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN, friendly fire none (= ALL): Killing Frost and Reign of Absolute Zero raise the hit on allies in the circle to 42/45, and the IDT nodes expose allies inside at up to 45%. The caster is never a target (OTHER_USER). Ice Coffin (single, none/ALL) reaches an ally only if aimed at one; Imperial Freeze's row is ENEMIES-only.
- Exposure downstream: a 45% Ice Palace debuff adds 45% of the staged base to every non-pierce Ice, Water, Wind or element-less hit the target takes from caster or allies in the two rounds after cast, so its value scales with party damage and enemies in the circle. Same-tag debuffs from different casters all apply (process.ts 1109-1117): two Palaces add 90%. Flats held to Taiyo's single-row +10.
- Guard and suppression: Aegis DDT on the caster and Coffin DDG on the enemy, live in the same round, each subtract their percentage of base from the Coffined enemy's matching hits on the caster (100 AP cast together). Both routes cost 6 BP, so a build caps at 45% + 35% (Still Heart of Winter + Cold Saps the Will) or 40% + 40% (Silence of Falling Snow + Permafrost Mantle); the Water-only 15% DDT passive multiplies.
- Heal: Cryostorm Aegis heals 250 HP per tick at jutsu level 25 on the two rounds after cast; the route maxima add 30 (Still Heart of Winter) or 20 (Silence of Falling Snow) per tick, a small value against late-game pools, so Heal is only a capstone secondary; healprevent on the caster blocks it. Cooldown 7 allows one Aegis window per caster, and the row is SELF, so an ally's Aegis never lands on the caster.
- Offense stacking: in Frostbound Ascendancy's window (two rounds after cast) a matching caster hit (Ice, Water, Wind or element-less: kit Damage rows, normal jutsu, weapons, basic attacks) gains 40% of the staged base at the route maximum, plus 45% on an Ice Palace-exposed target (85% added); the bloodline IDG passive multiplies. Normal-tree potency policy is unapproved; the combined total is a roster-review item.
- No adverse rows, hidden jutsu, item gates, mode restrictions or injected children exist. Stun (Imperial Freeze), absorb and recoil (Frostbound Ascendancy) are unsupported and unchanged; no pierce, lifesteal, reflect or afterburn rows. Ranked modes suppress skill-tree and bloodline effects. No combat simulation: non-dominance of the 14 allocations is arithmetic over per-row additions only.
- Rank and shape: Teno Yuki is A-rank with an Increase Damage Given passive of 25% + 0.15 per user level (28.75% for a level-25 user, 40% at level 100; combat.ts 3062) and a 100 regen bonus. The tree equalises marginal opportunity against the Taiyo reference, not final strength. Cross-bloodline comparison (including the other Ice kits) is deferred to the roster review.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Teno Yuki jutsu inherit Ice potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
