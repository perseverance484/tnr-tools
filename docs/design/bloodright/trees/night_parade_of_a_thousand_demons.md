# Night Parade of A Thousand Demons — The Lantern Procession

**Bloodline:** Night Parade of A Thousand Demons (BR-051, rank H, `r_99Xg8SIOYw2awCMSR7e`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Shadow classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Shadow (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Otherworldly Conduit self buffs: Decrease Damage Taken (+10 route) and Lifesteal (+8 route), with Increase Damage Given as the +5 glue · secondary Damage on the two Shadow hits, Possessing Yurei and Oni Hammer Swing (+6, the two-row guardrail) · tertiary Enemy debuffs: Herald of the Black Night exposure (Increase Damage Taken, +7 ceiling) with Oni Hammer Swing suppression (Decrease Damage Given, +5, ally hazard).

Three of the seven supported rows ride one 40 AP self cast, Otherworldly Conduit (Increase Damage Given 35%, Decrease Damage Taken 35%, Lifesteal 40% at jutsu level 25, 2 rounds, realized on the caster at cast time), so the Conduit is the kit's engine and root A buffs it: Decrease Damage Taken and Lifesteal each take a route, Increase Damage Given is Foundation-plus-capstone glue. The two Shadow damage rows (Possessing Yurei single target, Oni Hammer Swing radius-1 circle; 45 power, 60 AP, cooldown 7) are the only direct damage and take the raw-power route under root B, which also carries Oni Hammer Swing's 30% Decrease Damage Given rider. Herald of the Black Night's 35% Increase Damage Taken (element-less, all four stat types, friendly fire ENEMIES, radius-1 circle at range 5 for 40 AP) is the kit's pressure tag and the fourth route; it amplifies every non-pierce hit on exposed enemies from any source, the summoned Underworld Gate ally included, so it is held to the Taiyo Kami +7 ceiling. Stun, buffprevent and summon are unsupported and untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Shadow-classified Night Parade of A Thousand Demons jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Oni's Heavy Hand** — The oni does not lift the hammer. The hammer lifts the oni, and comes down. Possessing Yurei and Oni Hammer Swing Damage 45→46 (Shadow, 60 AP); Hammer Decrease Damage Given 30→32% on its radius-1 circle, allies too.
- **Grip of the Yurei** — A yurei's fingers close where the heart used to be, and do not let go. Possessing Yurei (single target) and Oni Hammer Swing (radius-1 circle) 46→48 power with Oni's Heavy Hand; Shadow, range 4, 60 AP, CD 7.
- **March of a Thousand Demons** — The lanterns bob, the drums roll, and the whole procession walks over you. Same two Shadow damage rows; route total +6 (45→51 at jutsu level 25, the two-row guardrail). Yurei's stun and the Hammer's rider unchanged.
- **Marked by the Herald** — The herald names you to the parade, and the parade remembers. Herald of the Black Night exposure only, 35→38%: radius-1 circle at range 5 (friendly fire ENEMIES), 40 AP; every non-pierce hit, 2 rounds.
- **Toll of the Night Bell** — When the night bell tolls, every hand raised against you weakens and every wound deepens. Herald exposure to 42% (route +7) and Oni Hammer Swing Decrease Damage Given +3 (35% with Oni's Heavy Hand; allies in its circle too).

## Design notes

- Node split: two roots, four Hidden Arts, four Advanced Arts, every capstone 3 BP deep, two capstones 5 BP under one root or 6 across roots. Root A (Hour of the Ox, +2 IDG and +2 DDT on the Conduit) forks into Lifesteal (3, then 5 with +3 IDG) and Decrease Damage Taken (3, then 5 with +2 Lifesteal). Root B (Oni's Heavy Hand, +1 Damage on both Shadow hits and +2 DDG on the Hammer) forks into Damage (2, then 3) and Increase Damage Taken (3, then 4 with +3 DDG).
- Flats by coverage: Damage reaches two 45-power rows, so the route totals +6 (1+2+3; 45→51), the two-row guardrail Musashi Ken and Lycanthropy use. Single rows carry the larger flats: Decrease Damage Taken 2+3+5 = +10 (35→45%), Lifesteal 3+5 = +8 (40→48%, 12 points under the leech cap), Increase Damage Taken 3+4 = +7 (35→42%, the Taiyo Kami / Oblivion Seal exposure ceiling; the Herald exposes a whole circle to every source). Decrease Damage Given stays at +5 (30→35%); its row is an ally hazard.
- Capstone secondaries never out-bid a sibling Hidden Art: Barred Gate of Yomi takes +2 Lifesteal, below Hungry Ghost's Draught's +3; Feast of a Thousand Mouths takes +3 Increase Damage Given, a tag no Hidden Art carries (+5 ceiling with Hour of the Ox); Toll of the Night Bell takes +3 Decrease Damage Given, which only the root carries. March of a Thousand Demons is Damage-only, like Solar Cataclysm. Strongest full build by row weight is 19 on 7 rows (Oblivion Seal 23 on 7, Taiyo Kami 31 on 9).
- Interactions: every non-damage supported row is element-less and lists all four stat types, so at the pin the Conduit's buffs, the Herald's exposure and the Hammer's suppression match every non-pierce hit of any stat type (Lifesteal also counts pierce). The bloodline passive (Increase Damage Given 25% + 0.15/level; Earth, None, Shadow, Water, Wind) is not a potency target; it multiplies in its own stage and stacks with the Conduit buff under BATTLE_TAG_STACKING. Damage modifiers skip pierce.
- Delivery and uptime: the Conduit is a SELF cast, so its three rows are realized on the caster at cast time (not positional); 40 AP, cooldown 7, 2 rounds, so at most 2 of 7 rounds. Herald of the Black Night (OTHER_USER, AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN radius 1, range 5, 40 AP, cooldown 7) applies its exposure once to each living enemy on the tiles; the caster is never a target. Oni Hammer Swing (OPPONENT, same method, range 4, 60 AP) damages enemies only; its DDG rider (friendly fire none) reaches allies too.
- Fourth purchases: Sustain (01, 02, 03) takes Hide of the Oni (40% reduction) or Oni's Heavy Hand (46 power, 32% suppression); Fortress (01, 04, 05) takes Oni's Heavy Hand or Hungry Ghost's Draught (45% lifesteal); Burst (06, 07, 08) takes Hour of the Ox (37% given / taken) or Marked by the Herald (38% exposure); Pressure (06, 09, 10) takes Grip of the Yurei (48 power) or Hour of the Ox. Capstone-less hybrids (e.g. 01, 02, 04, 06 or 01, 06, 07, 09) are enumerated by the validator; see the audit.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Shadow is the single signature element but not exclusive in the census (13 collisions, incl. INCLUDE bloodlines Blood-Enchanted Eyes, Godstorm Eclipse, Oblivion Seal and Shadow Weaver); the tree assumes a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification, not a bare Shadow match. The current resolver matches only the 2 damage rows; 5 fall back to None. Normal-jutsu Shadow use: unverified.
- Ally hazard (accepted, small): Oni Hammer Swing row 1 (Decrease Damage Given 30%) is an INHERIT row on an OPPONENT AOE_CIRCLE_SPAWN with friendly fire none, so allies on the radius-1 tiles around the target receive the suppression too; the caster never does. Oni's Heavy Hand (+2) and Toll of the Night Bell (+3) raise it to 35% for allies and enemies alike. Positioning decides; solo it is gain.
- Increase Damage Taken reach: the Herald's exposure (element-less, all four stat types) amplifies every non-pierce hit each exposed enemy takes for 2 rounds from any source (allies, weapons, normal jutsu, the Underworld Gate summon), and one cast can expose several enemies, so the Pressure build's row weight (18) understates it. +7 matches Taiyo Kami's Stellar Inferno ceiling; +8 (3+5) is heavier.
- Lifesteal budget: the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage leech cap is shared with vamp, so 48% leaves 12 points of headroom and any vamp from equipment or the main tree erodes the capstone's value. healprevent on the caster blocks it entirely; the kit itself applies none. Lifesteal includes pierce hits, which the kit does not have, so only weapons or normal jutsu with pierce feed it that way.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: the +5 glue adds statically to the Conduit's 35% self buff, which stacks with the bloodline passive (bloodline-sourced modifiers multiply and honour allowBloodlineDamageIncrease) and any main-tree or gear modifiers; BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is true at the pin. Combined final damage was not simulated.
- Realized value: all five jutsu share cooldown 7 and both damage casts cost 60 AP, so the Conduit's 2-round window rarely covers both hits and the Herald's exposure in one rotation; per-row numbers overstate per-round value. Whether a Conduit buff affects a hit cast in the same round, and whether the exposure lands before the hits, was not verified against combat timing. Nothing was simulated.
- Damage is formula-calculated (sqrt scaling on Highest / Highest) and then multiplied by the Increase Damage Given passive, the Conduit buff and any exposure, so +6 raw power is not a linear +6 damage; March of a Thousand Demons is the lowest-weight capstone (+3 on two rows). Each Damage row fires at most once per 7 rounds.
- Unsupported rows and modes: Possessing Yurei's stun, Herald of the Black Night's buffprevent (itself an ally-hazard row, unchanged by any node) and the Underworld Gate summon receive nothing. Skill-tree and bloodline effects are skipped in ranked PvP and ranked sparring at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Night Parade of A Thousand Demons jutsu inherit Shadow potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
