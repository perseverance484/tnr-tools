# Shinrai Ou — Throne of Thunder

**Bloodline:** Shinrai Ou (BR-065, rank A, `xO4Dycx7FFEqxwvAEXBxY`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Storm classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Storm (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Storm burst on all four attacks, +6 ceiling) · secondary Increase Damage Taken (Izanagi's Hammer exposure, +10 ceiling) and Reflect (Indra's Storm Cloak, +10 ceiling) · tertiary Increase Damage Given (glue across routes, +5 ceiling on a doubled-row cast).

Shinrai Ou is a rank-A Burst kit: four of its five jutsu are Storm damage rows (Daibutsu Thunder 50, Divine Railgun 45, Izanagi's Hammer 40, Strike: Raijin 40, all 60 AP / cooldown 7), so raw Storm power is the primary emphasis and the deepest commitment. Its only enemy debuff is Hammer's 35% Increase Damage Taken on area enemies, which anchors the exposure route; its only defensive row is the Storm Cloak's 40% Reflect, which anchors the bulwark route. Increase Damage Given has three rows (the Cloak carries two, Raijin one) and the bloodline passive already multiplies it, so it is kept as Foundation and capstone-secondary glue capped at +5 rather than given a route of its own.

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has four supported tags on nine rows (Damage x4, Increase Damage Given x3, Increase Damage Taken x1, Reflect x1). The enemy-facing root (Mandate of Thunder) forks into a damage route and an exposure route; the self-facing root (Raiment of Storms) is a single Foundation -> Hidden -> Advanced chain on Reflect. Increase Damage Given is the only tag left for a fourth route, and it is deliberately not given one: two of its three rows sit on the same 40 AP Storm Cloak cast and the bloodline passive multiplies it again, so a dedicated route would either push IDG past the +5 that Taiyo Kami's doubled-row Celestial Ignition tolerates, or need a +3-primary capstone that only mirrors the Reflect capstone's secondary. A second Hidden Art under Raiment of Storms was also rejected: a +2 IDG or +2 IDT support node is dominated by Mandate of Thunder as the bulwark's fourth purchase, and a +2 Damage node would be a twin of Vajra Tempering giving a capstone-free +5 Damage route (01, 02, 06 and it), equal to Taiyo Kami's Damage ceiling and tying the Burst route's first three purchases. The consequence is that the bulwark route's fourth purchase is always Mandate of Thunder (06, 07, 08 plus 01 is legal and non-dominated), while Burst and Exposure each have two fourth-purchase choices. Three distinct complete builds exist.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Storm-classified Shinrai Ou jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Mandate of Thunder** — Heaven names its king in thunder; the storm answers before the title is spoken. Storm Cloak IDG rows 0 and 2 and Raijin IDG (35%, self, 2 rounds); Hammer exposure (35%, area enemies, 2 rounds). No gates, no adverse rows.
- **Vajra Tempering** — The thunderbolt is forged again and again until nothing it strikes stays standing. Damage on Daibutsu Thunder 50, Divine Railgun 45, Izanagi's Hammer 40 and Strike: Raijin 40; all Storm, 60 AP, cooldown 7.
- **Thunder King's Wrath** — When the king's patience ends, the sky itself is the weapon. Same four Storm damage rows; the route total of +5 (+6 with Raiment of Storms) lifts Daibutsu Thunder to 55 or 56 power.
- **Lightning Rod** — Mark the foe as the tallest thing on the field and let the storm choose. Hammer exposure row only (35% Increase Damage Taken, Lightning/None/Storm/Water, area enemies, 2 rounds, range 5 spiral).
- **Judgement from Above** — The exposed are not struck once; every bolt that follows finds them first. Hammer exposure reaches 45% with the full route; +3 IDG also lifts Cloak rows 0 and 2 and Raijin's self buff to 40% with Mandate.
- **Raiment of Storms** — A cloak of living lightning: it bites whoever touches it and sharpens every bolt. Storm Cloak Reflect 40% (self, 2 rounds, all four stat types) and +1 raw power on all four Storm damage rows.
- **Galvanic Rebuke** — Strike the storm and the storm strikes back, charge for charge. Storm Cloak Reflect row only (40% of each hit returned, pierce included, 60% cap); 40 AP self cast, cooldown 7, 2 rounds.
- **Thunder Answers Thunder** — Every blow against the king is a confession, and the sky passes sentence. Storm Cloak Reflect reaches 50% with the route; +3 IDG lifts Cloak rows 0 and 2 and Raijin's buff to 40% with Mandate of Thunder.

- **Judgement from Above:** The exposure route takes Hammer's area Increase Damage Taken from 35% to 45% for its two rounds, so every non-pierce Storm, Lightning, Water or element-less hit the exposed enemies take, from the caster or allies, is amplified by ten more points. The capstone's +3 IDG with Mandate lifts both Cloak rows and Raijin's self buff to 40%, and Vajra Tempering adds +2 power to the four Storm attacks that follow the opener. Raiment of Storms is the alternative fourth purchase for a Reflect-leaning caster.
- **Thunder Answers Thunder:** The bulwark route takes Indra's Storm Cloak from 40% to 50% Reflect for its two-round window, still ten points under the 60% per-hit cap, and its capstone's +3 IDG with Mandate lifts both Cloak IDG rows and Raijin's buff to 40%, so the whole 40 AP cast is improved on both sides. Raiment's +1 power on the four Storm attacks is incidental. Mandate of Thunder is the only closed fourth purchase for this route; it is a good one here because the IDG and exposure it adds are what a Cloak duelist uses next.

## Design notes

- Node split and calibration: Foundations +2/+2 (Mandate: IDG and exposure, 4 rows) and +2/+1 (Raiment: Reflect and raw power, 5 rows); Hidden Arts +2 Damage on four rows or +3 on a single-row percentage tag; capstones +3 Damage or +5 primary with a +3 IDG secondary. IDG sits only on Mandate and the capstone secondaries, so it tops out at +5 (Cloak 35/35 -> 40/40, Raijin 35 -> 40), as on Taiyo's doubled-row Celestial Ignition. Damage tops out at +6 on four rows (50/45/40/40 -> 56/51/46/46).
- Main tree and passives: both Storm Cloak IDG rows match the kit's own Storm hits (row 0 via its Highest stat filter, row 2 via its Storm element), stack additively with Raijin's IDG, and the bloodline passive IDG (25 + 0.15/lvl) multiplies on top. Enhanced IDG also reaches normal jutsu and weapons in the two-round windows (Highest-stat or element-less hits for row 0 and Raijin; Storm, Lightning, Water or element-less for row 2). Hammer's exposure amplifies allies' matching hits too.
- Delivery and uptime: all five jutsu have cooldown 7; the four attacks cost 60 AP, the Cloak 40 AP. Buffs and the exposure last two rounds, so staggered Cloak and Raijin casts give about four of every seven rounds of enhanced IDG, and Hammer exposes whoever its range-5 spiral catches for two rounds. Daibutsu Thunder (OTHER_USER, range 4) and Raijin (EMPTY_GROUND, range 5) are circle spawns; Divine Railgun is a single-target range-4 shot. Realized hits depend on positioning; not simulated.
- Fourth-purchase choices: Burst (01, 02, 03) finishes with Lightning Rod (exposure 40%) or Raiment of Storms (Reflect 42%, Damage +6); Exposure (01, 04, 05) finishes with Vajra Tempering (Damage +2) or Raiment of Storms; Bulwark (06, 07, 08) finishes only with Mandate of Thunder (see the narrow-kit exception). Hybrids without a capstone (for example 01, 02, 04, 06 or 01, 02, 06, 07) spread +2 to +5 across all four tags and stay non-dominated.
- Reflect route reasoning: the Cloak Reflect row lists all four stat types and no element, so under getEfficiencyRatio it matches effectively every hit the caster takes, pierce included, and the returned damage bypasses shield absorption. 40% -> 50% leaves ten points under the 60%-of-hit cap. Reflect only pays when the caster is hit during the window, so the bulwark is a duelist's build rather than a passive defence; the kit has no Decrease Damage Taken row to pair with it.
- Untouched rows: Daibutsu Thunder's recoil 40%, Divine Railgun's stun 100 and the two move rows are unsupported tags and receive nothing; the bloodline passives (IDG and 15% Decrease Damage Taken against Lightning) are not potency targets. Skill-tree effects are suppressed in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING at the pin, so no node applies there.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Storm is not exclusive in the census (Arashima and Godstorm Eclipse are INCLUDE kits with Storm damage rows; Stormboat Willy is deferred), and whether any NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN jutsu carries Storm is unverified. The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped whole-kit classification; a bare element match would leak every node to those kits.
- Resolver: 3 of the 9 supported rows (Storm Cloak IDG row 0, Storm Cloak Reflect, Raijin IDG) have no element and fall back to None under the current resolver, so they are unreachable without the proposed classification change; reaching them with affectedElements including None would also reach every element-less row on any jutsu the player casts.
- IDG stacking: with both Cloak rows, Raijin and the bloodline passive active, the kit already carries roughly 105 additive points of jutsu IDG plus a multiplying passive in a full window; the +5 ceiling adds 15 more additive points across those three rows. Each row stays far below the 100 cap, but the combined per-hit amplification is high and was not simulated against peer kits.
- Exposure breadth (user-owned decision, not applied): +10 lifts Hammer's area Increase Damage Taken to 45% for two rounds and amplifies allies' Storm, Lightning, Water or element-less hits (pierce excluded). It is at the +10 guardrail and above Taiyo Kami's +7 IDT ceiling. Options: Judgement from Above +3 IDT (route +8, Hammer 43%) or accept +10 as a single-row team-play tag (OPEN_DECISIONS D14).
- Reflect cap and uptime: 50% leaves ten points of headroom under the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage cap, but main-tree or item Reflect sources could saturate it and waste part of the +10. Reflect pays only when the caster is hit during the two-round window and, like IDG and IDT, only on rounds after the cast round (tags.ts reflect handler: !isNew && !castThisRound); realized value was not simulated.
- Open balance decision (user-owned, not applied): the Burst build (01, 02, 03, 06) puts +6 Damage on all four damage rows, row-weighted 34 against Taiyo Kami's 31 (Damage +5 on 3 rows). Options: (a) Thunder King's Wrath +2 Damage (ceiling +5, Burst 30); (b) drop Raiment's +1 Damage (a +1 IDT or IDG replacement breaches IDT +10 or IDG +5); (c) keep +6 and record it under OPEN_DECISIONS D8.
- Universal opener: every full-budget allocation contains Mandate of Thunder, because the bulwark chain has no other prerequisite-closed fourth purchase. This is accepted under the narrow-kit exception (Nature's Blessing precedent) rather than fixed with a filler Hidden Art.
- Delivery: Daibutsu Thunder (circle spawn on an OTHER_USER target), Raijin (ground circle spawn) and Hammer (spiral shoot) are area deliveries whose realized hits and exposure coverage depend on positioning; the dossier flags no ally-hazard rows (friendly fire ENEMIES on every harmful row). Daibutsu's recoil and Railgun's stun are unsupported and unchanged.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Shinrai Ou jutsu inherit Storm potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
