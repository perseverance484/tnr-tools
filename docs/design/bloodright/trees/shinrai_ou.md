# Shinrai Ou — Throne of Thunder

**Bloodline:** Shinrai Ou (BR-065, rank A, `xO4Dycx7FFEqxwvAEXBxY`) · **Revision:** Draft 4 / Storm classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Storm (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Damage (Storm burst on all four attacks; +5 Damage route) · secondary Increase Damage Taken (Izanagi's Hammer exposure, +10% route) and Reflect (Indra's Storm Cloak, +10% route) · tertiary Increase Damage Given (glue across routes, +5% at most on a doubled-row cast).

Shinrai Ou is a rank-A Burst kit: four of its five jutsu are Storm damage rows (Daibutsu Thunder 50, Divine Railgun 45, Izanagi's Hammer 40, Strike: Raijin 40 EP, all 60 AP / cooldown 7), so the Damage route is the primary emphasis and the deepest commitment. Its only supported enemy debuff is Hammer's 35% Increase Damage Taken on area enemies, which anchors the Exposure route; its only defensive row is the Storm Cloak's 40% Reflect, which anchors the Bulwark route. Increase Damage Given has three rows (two on the Cloak, one on Raijin) which compound with each other when their windows overlap, with the bloodline's own Increase Damage Given passive multiplying last, so it is kept as Foundation and capstone-secondary glue at +5% at most rather than given a route of its own. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Storm jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

> **Narrow-kit exception:** Eight nodes and three Advanced Arts rather than ten and four. The kit has four supported tags on nine rows (Damage x4, Increase Damage Given x3, Increase Damage Taken x1, Reflect x1). Mandate of Thunder forks into a Damage route and an Exposure route; Raiment of Storms carries a single Reflect chain. Increase Damage Given gets no route of its own: two of its three rows sit on the same 40 AP Storm Cloak cast and the bloodline's Increase Damage Given passive multiplies on top, so it stays glue at +5% at most. Mandate of Thunder is therefore a universal node (in all 8 legal full builds): Raiment's 3-BP chain needs a fourth purchase and Mandate is the only prerequisite-closed one. A Hidden Art leaf under Raiment would be filler; each single-tag version was checked against the legal builds: a Reflect leaf lifts Reflect past +10%; an Increase Damage Given leaf at +1% or +2% leaves 06, 07, 08 plus the leaf dominated by 01, 06, 07, 08, and at +3% or more lifts the stacking buff rows past the +5% glue level; an Increase Damage Taken leaf at +1% or +2% is dominated the same way, at +3% twins Lightning Rod's builds and at +4% or +5% dominates both Lightning Rod hybrids; a Damage leaf at +1 or +2 is dominated or twinned by Vajra Tempering's builds, at +3 twins the Burst build (01, 02, 03, 06) and dominates Vajra Tempering's hybrids, and at +4 or more exceeds +5 Damage. A split leaf would be a one- or two-point sliver of tags other nodes already own, with no role of its own. Burst and Exposure each keep two fourth-purchase choices; Bulwark has one. Three distinct complete builds exist.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Storm jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Mandate of Thunder** — Heaven names its king in thunder; the storm answers before the title is spoken. Storm Cloak Increase Damage Given rows 0 and 2 and Raijin's 35 → 37% (self, 2 rounds); Hammer exposure 35 → 37% (area enemies, 2 rounds).
- **Vajra Tempering** — The thunderbolt is forged again and again until nothing it strikes stays standing. Daibutsu Thunder 50 → 52, Divine Railgun 45 → 47, Izanagi's Hammer and Strike: Raijin 40 → 42 EP; all Storm, 60 AP, cooldown 7.
- **Thunder King's Wrath** — When the king's patience ends, the sky itself is the weapon. Burst: the same four Storm Damage rows +5 on the full route (Daibutsu Thunder 55, Divine Railgun 50, Hammer and Raijin 45 EP).
- **Lightning Rod** — Mark the foe as the tallest thing on the field and let the storm choose. Hammer exposure row only: +3% (40% with Mandate of Thunder) on Lightning/None/Storm/Water hits against enemies on its spiral tiles, 2 rounds after the cast.
- **Judgement from Above** — The exposed are not struck once; every bolt that follows finds them first. Exposure: Hammer 35 → 45% Increase Damage Taken on the full route (+10%); +3% Increase Damage Given lifts Cloak rows 0 and 2 and Raijin's self buff to 40% with Mandate.
- **Raiment of Storms** — A cloak of living lightning: it bites whoever touches it and sharpens every bolt. Storm Cloak Reflect 40 → 42% (self, 2 rounds, all four stat types). No Damage here, so the Damage route stays at +5.
- **Galvanic Rebuke** — Strike the storm and the storm strikes back, charge for charge. Storm Cloak Reflect row only: +3% (45% with Raiment of Storms; pierce included, 60% cap); 40 AP self cast, cooldown 7, 2 rounds.
- **Thunder Answers Thunder** — Every blow against the king is a confession, and the sky passes sentence. Bulwark: Storm Cloak Reflect 40 → 50% on the full route (+10%); +3% Increase Damage Given lifts Cloak rows 0 and 2 and Raijin's buff to 40% with Mandate of Thunder.

## Design notes

- Reference reuse: Mandate, Vajra Tempering and Thunder King's Wrath use Taiyo Kami's Dawnheart, Crown of Cinders and Solar Cataclysm flats (+2% Increase Damage Given / +2% Increase Damage Taken, +2 Damage, +3 Damage); Thunder Answers Thunder mirrors Sovereign Sun (+5% / +3% Increase Damage Given). Routes: Burst +5 Damage; Exposure +10% Increase Damage Taken (Mandate +2%, Lightning Rod +3%, Judgement from Above +5%); Bulwark +10% Reflect (Raiment +2%, Galvanic Rebuke +3%, Thunder Answers Thunder +5%). Raiment of Storms carries no Damage (its former +1 was removed under RUL-2026-10-03-005), so no allocation exceeds +5 Damage; Increase Damage Given peaks at +5%.
- Main tree and passives: both Cloak Increase Damage Given rows match the kit's Storm hits (row 0 via its Highest stat filter, row 2 via Storm) and compound with Raijin's: three 35% rows multiply a hit all three match by ×1.35³ ≈ ×2.46 (×1.40³ ≈ ×2.74 at the +5% maximum), and the bloodline passive (25 + 0.15/lvl) multiplies last. The buffs also reach normal jutsu and weapons (row 0 and Raijin: Highest-stat or element-less hits; row 2: Storm, Lightning, Water or element-less).
- Uptime: cooldown 7 on all five jutsu (attacks 60 AP, Cloak 40 AP) outlasts every two-round window. Each buff and the exposure is live only in the two rounds after its cast, never on its own jutsu's hit, so Raijin's buff and Hammer's exposure feed the other attacks, normal jutsu and weapons, not their own next cast. Staggered Cloak and Raijin casts give about four of seven rounds of enhanced Increase Damage Given. Both buff sources land on the caster at cast time; Hammer's exposure sits on its tiles.
- Fourth-purchase choices: Burst (01, 02, 03) finishes with Lightning Rod (exposure 40%) or Raiment of Storms (Reflect 42%); Exposure (01, 04, 05) finishes with Vajra Tempering (+2 Damage) or Raiment of Storms (Reflect 42%); Bulwark (06, 07, 08) finishes only with Mandate of Thunder (see the narrow-kit exception). Hybrids without a capstone (01, 02, 04, 06 and 01, 02, 06, 07) spread +2 to +5 across four tags and stay non-dominated.
- Reflect and untouched rows: the Cloak Reflect row lists all four stat types and no element, so it matches effectively every hit the caster takes, pierce included; the return bypasses shields, and other Reflect sources add to it up to the 60%-of-hit cap (50% leaves ten points). Daibutsu's recoil 40%, Railgun's stun 100 and the two move rows are unsupported and unchanged; the bloodline passives (Increase Damage Given; 15% Decrease Damage Taken against Lightning) are not potency targets.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Storm is the single qualifying element; sharing it with Arashima, Godstorm Eclipse and Stormboat Willy is expected (RUL-2026-10-03-005). 6 of 9 kit rows carry Storm; the 3 element-less rows (Storm Cloak rows 0 and 1, Raijin's buff) need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver (matching None instead would reach every element-less row on any jutsu). Off-kit Storm coverage (NORMAL/SPECIAL/EVENT/FORBIDDEN) is unverified.
- Increase Damage Given stacking: with both Cloak rows and Raijin live, three 35% rows compound to ×1.35³ ≈ ×2.46 on a hit all three match, before the bloodline passive multiplies; at the +5% maximum it is ×1.40³ ≈ ×2.74. Each row stays far below the 100 cap, but the combined per-hit amplification is high and was not simulated against peer kits.
- Exposure: Hammer's area Increase Damage Taken reaches 45% at the +10% route maximum, and allies' matching non-pierce hits on the exposed enemies gain too. Not simulated.
- Reflect cap and uptime: other Reflect sources (main tree, items) add to the Cloak's 50% and could saturate the 60%-of-pre-shield-damage cap, wasting part of the +10%. Reflect pays only when the caster is hit in the two rounds after the Cloak's cast (tags.ts reflect: !isNew && !castThisRound), so Bulwark is a duelist's build with no Decrease Damage Taken row to pair. Not simulated.
- Universal node: Mandate of Thunder is in all 8 legal full-budget builds because the Bulwark chain has no other prerequisite-closed fourth purchase, so the 4-BP budget carries 3 BP of real choice. Accepted under the narrow-kit exception, which records why every leaf under Raiment of Storms would be filler (dominated, a twin, or past a ceiling or the glue level).
- Delivery: Daibutsu Thunder (radius-1 spawn on an OTHER_USER target), Raijin (ground circle spawn) and Hammer (spiral around the caster) are area deliveries; realized hits and exposure depend on positioning. Every harmful row is friendly fire ENEMIES, so no ally hazards. Skill-tree effects are off in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING; no combat simulation was performed.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Storm jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
