# Hyouga Yui — Nine Hells Frozen

**Bloodline:** Hyouga Yui (BR-034, rank A, `lPcN4q0dtX2muWT2KlXGg`) · **Revision:** Draft 3 / Ice classification / forked tree (RUL-2026-10-03-005 recalibration) · **Classification:** Ice (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_jutsu_classification_resolver

**Emphasis:** primary Area burst (Damage on the three Ice circle strikes: Nine Hell's Spear 45, Glacial Shatter: Purgatory 45, Cerulean Storm: Avalanche 40 EP) · secondary Ground control (Increase Damage Taken 35% on Glacial Descent: Cocytus and Nine Hell's Spear's circle; Decrease Damage Taken 35% on Cocytus and 30% on Glacier's Will's tiles) · tertiary Self amplification (Increase Damage Given 35% on Cerulean Storm: Avalanche and Glacier's Will beside the 25% + 0.15/level bloodline passive); same +10% band as the secondaries, ranked by role, not size.

Damage is the broadest tag: three Ice rows at 40/45 EP on radius-1 circle strikes (Nine Hell's Spear, Glacial Shatter: Purgatory, Cerulean Storm: Avalanche), all ENEMIES-only, so burst is the primary and the AOE trait's home. The Ground Control trait lives in Glacial Descent: Cocytus (35% exposure and 35% guard on one 40 AP cast), Nine Hell's Spear's 35% circle exposure and Glacier's Will's 30% ground guard, so Increase Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Taken form the secondary. Increase Damage Given sits on two 35% self rows (Avalanche, Glacier's Will) beside a bloodline passive of the same tag. All three percentage routes reach +10% on two rows, so below Damage the tiers are co-equal in size: control ranks above IDG because it spans two tags and its exposure lifts allies' hits, while IDG serves the caster alone. Wound, shield and move are unsupported. Potency reaches matching supported tags on all Ice jutsu (RUL-2026-10-03-005).

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Ice jutsu. Bonuses are static additions under the proposed element-wide classification; no row's combat scope, recipient or filters change. Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Permafrost Heart** — The cold settles in the chest first. Everything after it strikes harder. Self damage buffs 35 → 38%: Avalanche's (Ice/Water/Wind/element-less hits), Glacier's Will's (every non-pierce hit); 2 rounds after cast.
- **Weight of the Avalanche** — Snow is light until all of it moves at once. Damage 45 → 47 EP on Nine Hell's Spear and Glacial Shatter: Purgatory, 40 → 42 EP on Cerulean Storm: Avalanche; radius-1 circles, enemies only.
- **Buried in the Avalanche** — No one digs out of the ninth winter. Route total +5 Damage: Spear and Purgatory 45 → 50, Avalanche 40 → 45 EP on every enemy in the circle; 60 AP each, cooldown 7, no ally hazard.
- **Deepening Frost** — Frost does not stop at the skin. It keeps going down. Both self damage buffs 40% with Permafrost Heart; both rows land on the caster at cast, Avalanche's with its 60 AP strike, Glacier's Will's with its 40 AP.
- **The Glacier Advances** — A glacier moves a hand's width a day and nothing in its path survives the year. Amplifier route total +10%: both self buffs 35 → 45%. Guard +2%: Cocytus 37% (self, at cast) and Glacier's Will's tiles 32% (positional).
- **Cocytus Vigil** — Keep watch over the frozen lake. What is held in it cannot strike back. Exposure 35 → 37% on Cocytus's target and Nine Hell's Spear's circle; guard 35 → 37% on Cocytus (self) and 30 → 32% on Glacier's Will's tiles.
- **Cracks in the Ice** — Every surface has a seam. The cold finds it and widens it. Exposure 40% with Cocytus Vigil: Spear's circle vs every non-pierce hit; Cocytus's target vs element-less and caster's-highest-stat hits.
- **Nine Hells Opened** — The spear does not close the wound it opens. It opens eight more. Exposure route total +10% (35 → 45%) on Cocytus's target and Spear's circle; +2 Damage on all three strikes (Spear and Purgatory 47, Avalanche 42 EP).
- **Rime Mantle** — Rime thickens on the shoulders until blades skate off it. Guard 40% with Cocytus Vigil on Cocytus (self, at cast) and 35% on Glacier's Will's circle (caster and allies on the tiles).
- **Locked in Cocytus** — The lake freezes around both of you. Only one of you was ready for it. Guard route total +10%: Cocytus 35 → 45%, Glacier's Will's tiles 30 → 40%; exposure +2% (39% with Cocytus Vigil) on Cocytus's target and Spear's circle.

## Design notes

- Node split: Permafrost Heart (+3% self Increase Damage Given) forks into a Damage route (Weight of the Avalanche → Buried in the Avalanche) and an amplifier route (Deepening Frost → The Glacier Advances). Cocytus Vigil (+2% exposure, +2% guard) forks into exposure (Cracks in the Ice → Nine Hells Opened) and guard (Rime Mantle → Locked in Cocytus). Any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Routes (RUL-2026-10-03-005): Burst +5 Damage (Weight of the Avalanche +2, Buried in the Avalanche +3) on three rows; Amplifier +10% Increase Damage Given (Permafrost Heart +3%, Deepening Frost +2%, The Glacier Advances +5%); Exposure +10% Increase Damage Taken (Cocytus Vigil +2%, Cracks in the Ice +3%, Nine Hells Opened +5%); Lake warden +10% Decrease Damage Taken (Cocytus Vigil +2%, Rime Mantle +3%, Locked in Cocytus +5%). Each percentage tag reaches two rows (45% on the 35% rows, 40% on Glacier's Will's 30% guard). Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +5, IDG +10%, IDT +10%, DDT +10%; not jointly attainable.
- Recalibration from Draft 2: each capstone's primary rises from +2% to +5% (The Glacier Advances IDG, Nine Hells Opened IDT, Locked in Cocytus DDT), moving all three percentage routes from +7% to the +10% band; secondaries and every other node are unchanged. Row-weighted full builds now run 24–36; Burst (29) sits below the percentage-route examples (32–36) because Damage is hard-capped at +5.
- Why a single-tag root: the self side carries only IDG (Avalanche, Glacier's Will) and DDT (Cocytus, Glacier's Will). Guard or exposure on Permafrost Heart would push that tag past +10% in a 4-BP build with the Cocytus route, so Permafrost Heart takes IDG alone at +3% (Deepening Frost +2% is the thin step and chiefly the amplifier route's gate) and the Cocytus root owns both control tags.
- Resolver (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3, §3b): Avalanche's IDG row (Ice/None/Water/Wind) adds to Ice, Water, Wind and element-less hits; Glacier's Will's IDG and both DDT rows (four stat types, no element) act on every non-pierce hit given or taken, as does Spear's exposure; Cocytus's (Highest, no element) covers element-less hits plus elemental hits of the caster's highest offence stat. Kit rows add power/100 × staged base and sum when live; only the passive multiplies. IDG, IDT and DDT skip pierce.
- Delivery: all cooldown 7. Strikes: 60 AP, range 4, radius-1 circle on an OTHER_USER target, friendly fire ENEMIES. Cocytus: 40 AP, OPPONENT, IDT on the target, DDT on the caster. Glacier's Will: 40 AP, EMPTY_GROUND, range 5; IDG row SELF; DDT row an INHERIT FRIENDLY ground circle re-applied each round to caster and allies on the tiles; move row sorts last (§4b). Buff/debuff rows are live in the two rounds after the cast round, never in it (§3b), so no strike takes its own jutsu's buff.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Cocytus Vigil or Deepening Frost; Amplifier, Cocytus Vigil or Weight of the Avalanche; Exposure, Permafrost Heart or Rime Mantle; Lake warden, Cracks in the Ice or Permafrost Heart. 13 of 14 full builds are non-dominated (risk 6). User-owned option: Nine Hells Opened +1 Damage instead of +2 makes all 14 non-dominated.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: element-wide Ice scope (RUL-2026-10-03-005): matching supported tags on all Ice jutsu, whatever their source; sharing Ice with other bloodlines is expected. 4 of the 9 supported rows carry Ice; the rest need the proposed jutsu-classification resolver, and Glacial Descent: Cocytus and Glacier's Will (no Ice row) qualify in-kit only through an authored jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Ice coverage is unverified.
- IDG stacking: both 35% self rows reach 45%. Avalanche (60 AP) and Glacier's Will (40 AP) fit one 100 AP turn (ENGINE_LAWS 61), so both are live for the same two rounds and add 90% of the staged base on hits matching both (§3b) before the 25% + 0.15/level passive multiplies. Glacier's Will's row also reaches basic attacks, weapons and normal jutsu. Not simulated; normal-tree potency unapproved.
- Exposure reach: Spear's exposure (no element) raises every non-pierce hit from the caster, allies and weapons on every enemy in its circle; Cocytus's (Highest, no element) raises element-less hits and elemental hits of the caster's highest offence stat on one target. Spear and Cocytus fit one turn (60 + 40 AP), so one enemy can carry 90% for two rounds. Pierce hits take no exposure or guard.
- Guard scale: Cocytus's 35% self guard and Glacier's Will's 30% ground guard reach 45% and 40%; both fit one turn (40 + 40 AP), so a caster on the circle can hold 85% on non-pierce hits while both are live. The ground guard is positional and covers allies on the tiles; its FRIENDLY re-application and first active round follow the dossier and §4b, not a process.ts read.
- Ally hazard: none on supported rows. All three Damage rows and Spear's exposure are friendly fire ENEMIES; Cocytus is an OPPONENT-target single cast; Glacier's Will's supported rows are SELF or FRIENDLY. The kit's only ally-hazard row is Spear's wound (friendly fire none) and its only enemy-hazard row is Glacier's Will's move; both are unsupported and unchanged.
- Dominated allocation: 01,02,06,07 (+2 Damage, self buff +3%, exposure +5%, guard +2%) is dominated by 01,06,07,08, because Nine Hells Opened carries the same +2 Damage as Weight of the Avalanche; a player wanting both Hidden Arts' effects should buy the exposure capstone instead. Every node still appears in a non-dominated full build.
- Delivery: three 60 AP strikes and two 40 AP supports, all cooldown 7, so a route's full value is realized over a seven-round cycle and the percentage routes pay only inside 2-round windows. Glacial Shatter: Purgatory is a D-rank record with the same 45 EP as the A-rank Spear, so the +5 Damage route raises it equally. No injected children, item gates, hidden jutsu or mode restrictions exist.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and sparring. The 15% Wind Decrease Damage Taken passive is a bloodline effect and is untouched. The IDG passive is 25% + 0.15/level (28.75% if scaled at level 25; level basis unverified). The tree equalises marginal opportunity against the reference, not final strength. No combat simulation; balance values remain user-owned.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Ice jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
