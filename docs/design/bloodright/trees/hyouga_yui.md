# Hyouga Yui — Nine Hells Frozen

**Bloodline:** Hyouga Yui (BR-034, rank A, `lPcN4q0dtX2muWT2KlXGg`) · **Revision:** Draft 2 / Ice classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Ice (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Area burst (Damage on the three Ice circle strikes: Nine Hell's Spear 45, Glacial Shatter: Purgatory 45, Cerulean Storm: Avalanche 40) · secondary Ground control (Increase Damage Taken 35% on Glacial Descent: Cocytus and Nine Hell's Spear's circle; Decrease Damage Taken 35% on Cocytus and 30% on Glacier's Will's tiles) · tertiary Self amplification (Increase Damage Given 35% on Cerulean Storm: Avalanche and Glacier's Will beside the 25% + 0.15/level bloodline passive); same +7 ladder as the secondaries, ranked by role, not size.

Damage is the broadest tag: three Ice rows at 40/45 power on radius-1 circle strikes (Nine Hell's Spear, Glacial Shatter: Purgatory, Cerulean Storm: Avalanche), all ENEMIES-only, so burst is the primary and the AOE trait's home. The Ground Control trait lives in Glacial Descent: Cocytus (35% exposure and 35% guard on one 40 AP cast), Nine Hell's Spear's 35% circle exposure and Glacier's Will's 30% ground guard, so Increase Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Taken form the secondary. Increase Damage Given sits on two 35% self rows (Avalanche, Glacier's Will) beside a bloodline passive of the same tag. All three percentage ladders stop at +7 on two rows, so below Damage the tiers are co-equal in size: control ranks above IDG because it spans two tags and its exposure lifts allies' hits, while IDG serves the caster alone; trimming IDG to +6 so the order is arithmetic is a user-owned option (design notes). Wound, shield and move are unsupported and untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Ice-classified Hyouga Yui jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Permafrost Heart** — The cold settles in the chest first. Everything after it strikes harder. Self damage buffs 35→38%: Avalanche's (Ice/Water/Wind/element-less hits), Glacier's Will's (every non-pierce hit); 2 rounds after cast.
- **Weight of the Avalanche** — Snow is light until all of it moves at once. Damage 45→47 on Nine Hell's Spear and Glacial Shatter: Purgatory, 40→42 on Cerulean Storm: Avalanche; radius-1 circles, enemies only.
- **Buried in the Avalanche** — No one digs out of the ninth winter. Route total +5: Spear and Purgatory 45→50, Avalanche 40→45 on every enemy in the circle; 60 AP each, cooldown 7, no ally hazard.
- **Deepening Frost** — Frost does not stop at the skin. It keeps going down. IDG 38→40% with Permafrost Heart; both rows land on the caster at cast, Avalanche's with its 60 AP strike, Glacier's Will's with its 40 AP.
- **The Glacier Advances** — A glacier moves a hand's width a day and nothing in its path survives the year. IDG route total +7: both self buffs 42%. Guard 35→37% on Cocytus (self, at cast) and 30→32% on Glacier's Will's tiles (positional).
- **Cocytus Vigil** — Keep watch over the frozen lake. What is held in it cannot strike back. Exposure 35→37% on Cocytus's target and Nine Hell's Spear's circle; guard 35→37% on Cocytus (self) and 30→32% on Glacier's Will's tiles.
- **Cracks in the Ice** — Every surface has a seam. The cold finds it and widens it. Exposure 37→40% with Cocytus Vigil: Spear's circle vs every non-pierce hit; Cocytus's target vs element-less and caster's-highest-stat hits.
- **Nine Hells Opened** — The spear does not close the wound it opens. It opens eight more. IDT route total +7 (42%) on Cocytus's target and Spear's circle; Damage +2 on all three strikes: Spear and Purgatory 47, Avalanche 42.
- **Rime Mantle** — Rime thickens on the shoulders until blades skate off it. Guard 37→40% with Cocytus Vigil on Cocytus (self, at cast) and 32→35% on Glacier's Will's circle (caster and allies on the tiles).
- **Locked in Cocytus** — The lake freezes around both of you. Only one of you was ready for it. DDT route total +7: Cocytus 42%, Glacier's Will's tiles 37%; exposure 37→39% with Cocytus Vigil on Cocytus's target and Spear's circle.

## Design notes

- Node split: Permafrost Heart (+3 self Increase Damage Given) forks into a Damage route (Weight of the Avalanche → Buried in the Avalanche) and an amplifier route (Deepening Frost → The Glacier Advances). Cocytus Vigil (+2 exposure, +2 guard) forks into exposure (Cracks in the Ice → Nine Hells Opened) and guard (Rime Mantle → Locked in Cocytus). Taiyo Kami's 2/4/4 fork and +2/+3 Damage route are reused; the IDG-only root and +2/+2 capstones depart because each percentage tag reaches two rows.
- Calibration by coverage: Damage +2/+3 (max +5 on three rows). Each percentage tag reaches two rows, so each ladder stops at +7: IDG 3/2/2, IDT 2/3/2, DDT 2/3/2 (42% on the 35% rows, 37% on Glacier's Will's 30%). Row-weighted: Foundations 6/8, Hidden Arts 4–6, Advanced Arts 8–10; full builds 24–30 (Taiyo 16–31; the higher floor follows from every percentage tag covering two rows). Deepening Frost (+2 on two rows, 4) is deliberately the thin step and exists chiefly as the amplifier route's gate.
- Why a single-tag root: the self side carries only IDG (Avalanche, Glacier's Will) and DDT (Cocytus, Glacier's Will). DDT in both roots would let guard reach +9 on two rows at 4 BP, and IDT in both would do the same for exposure, so Permafrost Heart takes IDG alone at +3 and the Cocytus root owns both control tags. The IDG route reaches +5 at 2 BP and +7 at 3 BP; any two Advanced Arts cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Resolver (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3, §3b): Avalanche's IDG row (Ice/None/Water/Wind) adds to Ice, Water, Wind and element-less hits; Glacier's Will's IDG and both DDT rows (four stat types, no element) act on every non-pierce hit given or taken, as does Spear's exposure; Cocytus's (Highest, no element) covers element-less hits plus elemental hits of the caster's highest offence stat. Kit rows add power/100 × staged base and sum when live; only the passive multiplies. IDG, IDT and DDT skip pierce.
- Delivery: all cooldown 7. Strikes: 60 AP, range 4, radius-1 circle on an OTHER_USER target, friendly fire ENEMIES. Cocytus: 40 AP, OPPONENT, IDT on the target, DDT on the caster. Glacier's Will: 40 AP, EMPTY_GROUND, range 5; IDG row SELF; DDT row an INHERIT FRIENDLY ground circle re-applied each round to caster and allies on the tiles; move row sorts last (§4b). Buff/debuff rows are live in the two rounds after the cast round, never in it (§3b), so no strike takes its own jutsu's buff.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Cocytus Vigil or Deepening Frost; Amplifier, Cocytus Vigil or Weight of the Avalanche; Exposure, Permafrost Heart or Rime Mantle; Lake warden, Cracks in the Ice or Permafrost Heart. 13 of 14 full builds are non-dominated; strongest 06,07,08,09 (30). User-owned value options: IDG 3/2/1 (max +6, tier order arithmetic); IDG 2/3/2 (Deepening Frost 6, Permafrost Heart 4); Nine Hells Opened +1 power, not +2 (14 of 14 non-dominated, full builds 24–29).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Ice is not exclusive. Blue Blade Eyes and Teno Yuki (INCLUDE), Blue Edge Eyes (EXCLUDE) and five DEFER records carry Ice rows; the normal-jutsu collision is unverified. Today 4 of 9 supported rows match Ice directly and 5 fall back to None, which reaches non-elemental rows on any jutsu. The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped classification and is inert or leaky without it.
- IDG stacking: both 35% self rows reach 42%. Avalanche (60 AP) and Glacier's Will (40 AP) fit one 100 AP turn (ENGINE_LAWS 61), so both are live for the same two rounds and add 84% of the staged base on hits matching both (§3b) before the 25% + 0.15/level passive multiplies. Glacier's Will's row also reaches basic attacks, weapons and normal jutsu. Not simulated; normal-tree potency unapproved.
- Exposure leakage: Spear's exposure (no element) raises every non-pierce hit from the caster, allies and weapons on every enemy in its circle; Cocytus's (Highest, no element) raises element-less hits and elemental hits of the caster's highest offence stat on one target. Spear and Cocytus fit one turn (60 + 40 AP), so one enemy can carry 84% for two rounds. Pierce hits take no exposure or guard.
- Guard scale: Cocytus's 35% self guard and Glacier's Will's 30% ground guard reach 42% and 37%; both fit one turn (40 + 40 AP), so a caster on the circle can hold 79% on non-pierce hits while both are live; the ladder stops at +7. The ground guard is positional and covers allies on the tiles; its FRIENDLY re-application and first active round follow the dossier and §4b, not a process.ts read.
- Ally hazard: none on supported rows. All three Damage rows and Spear's exposure are friendly fire ENEMIES; Cocytus is an OPPONENT-target single cast; Glacier's Will's supported rows are SELF or FRIENDLY. The kit's only ally-hazard row is Spear's wound (friendly fire none) and its only enemy-hazard row is Glacier's Will's move; both are unsupported and unchanged.
- Dominated allocation: 01,02,06,07 (power +2, self buff +3, exposure +5, guard +2) is dominated by 01,06,07,08, because Nine Hells Opened carries the same +2 power as Weight of the Avalanche; a player wanting both Hidden Arts' effects should buy the exposure capstone instead. Every node still appears in a non-dominated full build; 13 of 14 are non-dominated.
- Delivery: three 60 AP strikes and two 40 AP supports, all cooldown 7, so a route's full value is realized over a seven-round cycle and the ladders pay only inside 2-round windows. Glacial Shatter: Purgatory is a D-rank record with the same 45 power as the A-rank Spear, so the +5 power route raises it equally. No injected children, item gates, hidden jutsu or mode restrictions exist.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and sparring. The 15% Wind Decrease Damage Taken passive is a bloodline effect and is untouched. The IDG passive is 25% + 0.15/level (28.75% if scaled at level 25; level basis unverified). The tree equalises marginal opportunity against the reference, not final strength. No combat simulation; balance values remain user-owned.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Hyouga Yui jutsu inherit Ice potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
