# Hyouga Yui — Nine Hells Frozen

**Bloodline:** Hyouga Yui (BR-034, rank A, `lPcN4q0dtX2muWT2KlXGg`) · **Revision:** Draft 1 / Ice classification / forked tree · **Classification:** Ice (element) · **Engine status:** proposal_requires_resolver_adjustment

**Emphasis:** primary Area burst (Damage on the three Ice circle strikes: Nine Hell's Spear 45, Glacial Shatter: Purgatory 45, Cerulean Storm: Avalanche 40) · secondary Ground control (Increase Damage Taken 35% on Glacial Descent: Cocytus and Nine Hell's Spear's circle; Decrease Damage Taken 35% on Cocytus and 30% on Glacier's Will's tiles) · tertiary Self amplification (Increase Damage Given 35% on Cerulean Storm: Avalanche and Glacier's Will over the 25% + 0.15/level bloodline passive).

Damage is the broadest tag: three Ice rows at 40/45 power on radius-1 circle strikes (Nine Hell's Spear, Glacial Shatter: Purgatory, Cerulean Storm: Avalanche), all ENEMIES-only, so burst is the primary and the AOE trait's home. The Ground Control trait lives in Glacial Descent: Cocytus (35% exposure and 35% guard on one 40 AP cast), Nine Hell's Spear's 35% circle exposure and Glacier's Will's 30% ground guard, so Increase Damage Taken and Decrease Damage Taken form the secondary. Increase Damage Given sits on two 35% self rows (Avalanche, Glacier's Will) above a bloodline passive of the same tag and is the tertiary amplifier route. Wound, shield and move are unsupported and untouched.

All nodes cost 1 BP; the budget is 4 BP acquired with silver; each skill is bought once; forks only. Bonuses are static additions to existing supported tags of Ice-classified Hyouga Yui jutsu under the proposed classification behavior; no row's combat scope changes. Baselines at jutsu level 25.

- **Permafrost Heart** — The cold settles in the chest first. Everything after it strikes harder. Self damage buffs 35→38% on Cerulean Storm: Avalanche (Ice/Water/Wind/element-less hits) and Glacier's Will (all four stat types); 2 rounds.
- **Weight of the Avalanche** — Snow is light until all of it moves at once. Damage 45→47 on Nine Hell's Spear and Glacial Shatter: Purgatory, 40→42 on Cerulean Storm: Avalanche; radius-1 circles, enemies only.
- **Buried in the Avalanche** — No one digs out of the ninth winter. Route total +5: Spear and Purgatory 45→50, Avalanche 40→45 on every enemy in the circle; 60 AP each, cooldown 7, no ally hazard.
- **Deepening Frost** — Frost does not stop at the skin. It keeps going down. Both self damage buffs 38→40% with Permafrost Heart: Avalanche after its 60 AP strike, Glacier's Will at cast time; 2 rounds each.
- **The Glacier Advances** — A glacier moves a hand's width a day and nothing in its path survives the year. IDG route total +7: both self buffs 42%. Guard 35→37% on Cocytus (self, at cast) and 30→32% on Glacier's Will's tiles from the next round.
- **Cocytus Vigil** — Keep watch over the frozen lake. What is held in it cannot strike back. Exposure 35→37% on Cocytus's target and Nine Hell's Spear's circle; guard 35→37% on Cocytus (self) and 30→32% on Glacier's Will's tiles.
- **Cracks in the Ice** — Every surface has a seam. The cold finds it and widens it. Exposure 37→40% with Cocytus Vigil: Cocytus's single target and every enemy in Spear's circle take more from every non-pierce hit, 2 rounds.
- **Nine Hells Opened** — The spear does not close the wound it opens. It opens eight more. IDT route total +7 (42%) on Cocytus's target and Spear's circle; Damage +2 on all three strikes: Spear and Purgatory 47, Avalanche 42.
- **Rime Mantle** — Rime thickens on the shoulders until blades skate off it. Guard 37→40% with Cocytus Vigil on Cocytus (self, at cast) and 32→35% on Glacier's Will's circle (caster and allies on the tiles).
- **Locked in Cocytus** — The lake freezes around both of you. Only one of you was ready for it. DDT route total +7: Cocytus 42%, Glacier's Will's tiles 37%; exposure 37→39% with Cocytus Vigil on Cocytus's target and Spear's circle.

## Design notes

- Node split: Permafrost Heart (+3 self Increase Damage Given) forks into a Damage route (Weight of the Avalanche → Buried in the Avalanche) and an amplifier route (Deepening Frost → The Glacier Advances). Cocytus Vigil (+2 exposure, +2 guard: both rows of Glacial Descent: Cocytus) forks into exposure (Cracks in the Ice → Nine Hells Opened) and guard (Rime Mantle → Locked in Cocytus). 2/4/4 nodes; every Advanced Art is 3 BP deep; any two cost 5 or 6 BP.
- Calibration by coverage: Damage +2/+3 (max +5 on three rows, as Taiyo Kami). Every percentage tag reaches two rows, so each ladder stops at +7: IDG 3/2/2, IDT 2/3/2, DDT 2/3/2 (42% on the 35% rows, 37% on Glacier's Will's 30%). Capstone secondaries are +2, below the +3 Hidden Arts on IDT and DDT; Nine Hells Opened's +2 power ties Weight of the Avalanche across roots (the one dominated hybrid). Row-weighted: Foundations 6/8, Hidden Arts 4–6, Advanced Arts 8–10; full builds 24–30 (Taiyo 20–31).
- Why a single-tag root: the self side carries only Increase Damage Given (Avalanche, Glacier's Will) and Decrease Damage Taken (Cocytus, Glacier's Will). Putting DDT in both roots would let guard reach +9 on two rows at 4 BP, and IDT in both would do the same for exposure, so Permafrost Heart takes IDG alone at +3 and the Cocytus root owns both control tags. The IDG route reaches +5 at 2 BP and +7 at 3 BP, the same ceiling as the other two percentage ladders.
- Resolver (SOURCE_MECHANICS §3): Avalanche's IDG row lists Ice/None/Water/Wind and multiplies those elements plus element-less hits; Glacier's Will's IDG and both DDT rows list all four stat types, no element, so they act on every non-pierce hit given or taken; Spear's exposure covers every non-pierce hit from anyone; Cocytus's (Highest, no element) covers element-less hits plus elemental hits of the caster's highest stat. All four tags skip pierce. The IDG passive multiplies downstream.
- Delivery: every cast is cooldown 7. Strikes cost 60 AP, range 4, radius-1 circle around an OTHER_USER target, friendly fire ENEMIES. Cocytus is 40 AP, single OPPONENT, range 4: IDT on the target, DDT on the caster at cast. Glacier's Will is 40 AP, EMPTY_GROUND, range 5: its IDG row targets SELF and lands at cast; its DDT row is INHERIT, friendly fire FRIENDLY: a ground circle re-applied each round to caster and allies on the tiles from the round after the cast. Rows last 2 rounds.
- Fourth purchases: Burst takes Cocytus Vigil (37% exposure/guard) or Deepening Frost (40% self buffs); Amplifier takes Cocytus Vigil or Weight of the Avalanche (+2 power); Exposure takes Permafrost Heart (38% self buffs) or Rime Mantle (guard); Lake warden takes Cracks in the Ice or Permafrost Heart. 13 of 14 full allocations are non-dominated; every node appears in one. Strongest unadvertised by row weight: 06,07,08,09 (30: exposure 42%, guard 40%/35%, +2 power); 01,02,03,04 is all-offence (25).

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: Ice is not exclusive. Blue Blade Eyes and Teno Yuki (INCLUDE), Blue Edge Eyes (EXCLUDE) and five DEFER records carry Ice rows; the normal-jutsu collision is unverified. Today 4 of 9 supported rows match Ice directly and 5 fall back to None, which reaches non-elemental rows on any jutsu. The tree assumes a bloodline-scoped classification and is inert or leaky without it.
- IDG stacking: both 35% self rows reach 42% at the maximum, BATTLE_TAG_STACKING is true at the pin and the 25% + 0.15/level passive multiplies on top; Avalanche and Glacier's Will cast on consecutive rounds overlap for a round at 84% plus passive. Glacier's Will's row (all stat types, no element) also multiplies basic attacks, weapons and normal jutsu. Not simulated; normal-tree potency unapproved.
- Exposure leakage: Spear's exposure row (all stat types, no element) raises every non-pierce hit from the caster, allies and weapons on every enemy in its circle for 2 rounds; Cocytus's (Highest, no element) raises element-less hits and elemental hits of the caster's highest-offence stat on one target. Both on one enemy at the maximum reach 84%. Pierce hits take no exposure or guard.
- Guard scale: Cocytus's 35% self guard and Glacier's Will's 30% ground guard reach 42% and 37%; cast on consecutive rounds they overlap for a round at 79% on matching non-pierce hits, so the ladder stops at +7. Glacier's Will's guard is positional (stand on the circle from the round after the cast) and covers allies there; its FRIENDLY re-application follows the dossier, not a new source read.
- Ally hazard: none on supported rows. All three Damage rows and Spear's exposure are friendly fire ENEMIES; Cocytus is an OPPONENT-target single cast; Glacier's Will's supported rows are SELF or FRIENDLY. The kit's only ally-hazard row is Spear's wound (friendly fire none) and its only enemy-hazard row is Glacier's Will's move; both are unsupported and unchanged.
- Dominated allocation: 01,02,06,07 (power +2, self buff +3, exposure +5, guard +2) is dominated by 01,06,07,08, because Nine Hells Opened carries the same +2 power as Weight of the Avalanche; a player wanting both Hidden Arts' effects should buy the exposure capstone instead. Every node still appears in a non-dominated full build; 13 of 14 are non-dominated.
- Delivery: three 60 AP strikes and two 40 AP supports, all cooldown 7, so a route's full value is realized over a seven-round cycle and the ladders pay only inside 2-round windows. Glacial Shatter: Purgatory is a D-rank record with the same 45 power as the A-rank Spear, so the +5 power route raises it equally. No injected children, item gates, hidden jutsu or mode restrictions exist.
- Context: skill-tree and bloodline effects are suppressed in ranked PvP and ranked sparring. The 15% Wind Decrease Damage Taken passive is a bloodline effect, not a jutsu row, and is untouched. Hyouga Yui is A-rank with a 28.75% IDG passive at jutsu level 25; this tree equalises marginal opportunity against the reference, not final strength. No combat simulation; balance values remain user-owned.

## Limits

- Proposed potency classification behavior; not implemented or verified in the live engine.
- All existing supported tags of Hyouga Yui jutsu inherit Ice potency eligibility; original combat elements and target scopes stay intact.
- Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power, not final-damage percentages; percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
