## Design notes

- Structure kept (edges 01→02→03, 01→04→05, 06→07→08, 06→09→10; 2 Foundations, 4 Hidden Arts, 4 Advanced Arts). Routes: burst, a +2% Increase Damage Taken setup (Weight of the Avalanche) then +2 Damage (Buried in the Avalanche); Amplifier +8% Increase Damage Given (+3% + 2% + 3%); Exposure +8% Increase Damage Taken (+2% + 2% + 4%); Fortress +10% Decrease Damage Taken (+2% + 3% + 5%). Every percentage capstone pays more than its Hidden Art; every capstone carries one tag. Maxima over every legal allocation: Damage +2, IDG +8%, IDT +8%, DDT +10%.
- Roster pass: The Glacier Advances +5% → +3% Increase Damage Given and Nine Hells Opened +5% → +4% (Cracks in the Ice +3% → +2%) Increase Damage Taken hold both two-row compounding routes at the roster's +8% (×1.43² ≈ ×2.04, ×1.122 over unbuffed) instead of +10% (×2.10). Consistency pass (R9′): Weight of the Avalanche +1 Damage → +2% Increase Damage Taken setup, Buried in the Avalanche +1 Damage with a +2% IDT rider → +2 Damage; the route's 3- and 4-BP totals are unchanged and no Hidden Art carries flat Damage.
- R9′ test of the burst setup on Weight of the Avalanche (trial edits, near_tie.py): Increase Damage Given fails (a) and (b), a legal fourth for The Glacier Advances on the same two self buffs (+10% at +2%) and 01+02+04 at +7% / +8% against its +8%; Decrease Damage Taken fails (c), it does nothing for the route's hits; Increase Damage Taken +2% passes: Cracks in the Ice hangs from Cocytus Vigil, so neither is a legal fourth of the other's route (not a twin), 01+02+06+07 stops at +6%, 2 under Nine Hells Opened (+3% would read +7%, a near-tie), and the top package stays ×1.172. The bare +2 Damage payoff holds the strikes at the director anchors' +2 (47 / 47 / 42) and an off-kit 50 EP Ice row at 52; the route needs no rider because the capstone is the tree's only flat Damage.
- Opener used in this tree: R1 Avalanche + Glacier's Will, R2 Spear + Cocytus, R3 Purgatory on a target in Spear's circle; the R1 self buffs are live in R2–R3 and both marks in R3. Strike power is EP × the live percentage rows, before the passive (271.5 unbuffed). Full builds: Burst + Deepening Frost 307.0, Amplifier + Cocytus Vigil 304.7 (+ Weight of the Avalanche the same without the guard), Burst + Cocytus Vigil 304.4, Exposure + Permafrost Heart 300.9 for the caster, with allies' hits on the marked target ×1.122 (×1.060 after Burst + Cocytus Vigil). Not simulated.
- Damage tiers: the strikes are 45 / 45 / 40 EP radius-1 area casts. The burst route's +2 gives 47 / 47 / 42 and crosses no tier; +5 would turn both 45s into 50 Nukes and the 40 into a 45. Only Buried in the Avalanche carries flat Damage, so no build without it lifts a strike.
- Fortress keeps +10% on two compounding guard rows (×0.55 × 0.60 = ×0.33 with both live, ×0.725 against the unmodified rows: stronger than Blood-Enchanted Eyes' Deathless Vitality ×0.747, level with Red Pestilence ×0.725), above the roster's +8% two-row default (×0.777), for three kit reasons: Deathless Vitality holds +10% Decrease Damage Taken on two self rows with +5% Decrease Damage Given on top; Glacier's Will's guard is positional, live only on its radius-1 tiles; and guard raises no output.
- Fourth purchases: the burst takes Deepening Frost or Cocytus Vigil; Amplifier, Cocytus Vigil (Weight of the Avalanche is the same +2% exposure without the guard); Exposure, Permafrost Heart or Rime Mantle; Fortress, Cracks in the Ice or Permafrost Heart. No fourth adds a step of the capstone's primary tag. Burst + Cocytus Vigil puts +4% IDT (exposures 39%, ×1.060, against Nine Hells Opened's ×1.122) beside +2 Damage; Fortress + Cracks in the Ice puts the same ×1.060 beside the ×0.33 guard; Exposure + Rime Mantle puts +5% DDT (×0.39) beside 43% exposures. Top package including flat Damage (R14′, all windows live): Exposure + Permafrost Heart ×1.045 × 1.122 ≈ ×1.172, then both burst builds ≈ ×1.163 (Avalanche 40 → 42), under Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.234.

## Risks and unproven interactions

- Classification: element-wide Ice scope (RUL-2026-10-03-005); sharing Ice with other bloodlines is expected. 4 of the 9 supported rows carry Ice; Glacial Descent: Cocytus and Glacier's Will carry no Ice row and qualify in-kit only through an authored jutsu classification (ENGINE_GAP_REGISTER G1). Off-kit Ice coverage is unverified.
- Self-buff stacking: Avalanche and Glacier's Will fit one 100 AP turn, so at 43% both compound to ×1.43 × 1.43 ≈ ×2.04 (×1.82 unbuffed) on hits both cover, before the 25% + 0.15/level passive applies last. Glacier's Will's row also reaches basic attacks, weapons and normal jutsu.
- Exposure reach: Spear's exposure (no element) raises every non-pierce hit on every enemy in its circle, from any attacker; Cocytus's raises element-less hits and hits of the caster's highest offence stat. Spear and Cocytus fit one turn, so one target can carry 43% on both for two rounds, ×1.122 over the unmodified marks, allies' hits included. The burst setup reaches the same two rows (39% with Cocytus Vigil, ×1.060).
- Damage reach: the burst route's +2 reaches every Ice Damage row the owner casts, so an off-kit 45 or 50 EP Ice row would read 47 or 52, the same +2 the director-approved anchors use; off-kit Ice coverage is unverified and the validator checks kit rows only.
- Guard scale: Cocytus 45% and Glacier's Will 40% apply in sequence, ×0.55 × 0.60 = ×0.33 (×0.46 unbuffed) on non-pierce hits while both are live. The ground guard is positional and also covers allies on the tiles.
- Ally hazard: none on supported rows (strikes and Spear's exposure are friendly fire ENEMIES; Glacier's Will's supported rows are SELF or FRIENDLY). Spear's wound and Glacier's Will's move are the kit's only hazard rows; both are unsupported.
- Skill-tree effects are skipped in RANKED_PVP and RANKED_SPARRING. No combat simulation was performed; balance values remain director-owned.

## Limits

- Proposed element-wide potency classification; not implemented or verified in the live engine (needs a jutsu-classification resolver).
- Bonuses apply to matching supported tags on all Ice jutsu. Off-kit jutsu of the element are in scope by rule; their count is unverified. Original combat elements, recipients and stat/general/element filters stay intact.
- Bloodline id, equipment, injected-child provenance and jutsu names are not selectors; equipment only gates castability.
- Coverage counts below are this kit's rows only. Every matching row on a jutsu receives each matching modifier; repeated rows are counted separately.
- Damage values are raw power (EP), not final-damage percentages; every other modifier is shown with %. Percentage-valued tags are capped at 100.
- Afterburn is an enemy debuff: for its existing duration, damage the target takes causes extra Afterburn damage at its percentage (60% cap per hit). It is not itself a damage instance; downstream instances were not simulated.
- Unsupported tags (e.g. wound, shield, stun, pierce, absorb, poison, drain, summon) receive no bonuses.
- No combat simulation. Budget and numerical non-dominance checks do not establish equal combat strength.
- Main-tree effects, bloodline passives, equipment, AP, uptime and modifier delivery are outside this audit.
