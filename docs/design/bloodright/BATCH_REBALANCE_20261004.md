### Roster conventions used by the proposal

These conventions keep similar kits treated alike. They are Fable's proposal, not rulings; each is a candidate for one director decision.

- **Damage above Nuke (R1″).** A flat-Damage route may lift a 50 row above the Nuke tier when the dossier Traits include Burst, or when both prongs hold: *strike-led* (every supported offensive percentage row of the kit sits on a jutsu that also carries a Damage row) and *finisher* (the 50 row's jutsu is the kit's unique highest-rank Damage jutsu). It stops at +2, only on the Advanced-Art payoff, after a percentage setup on the Hidden Art. Otherwise the kit's Damage is left unamplified.
- **Flat Damage cap (R8).** Flat Damage is at most +2 in every legal allocation, because potency is element-wide: a +3 route would lift any off-kit 50 EP row of the same element to 53.
- **The Damage route's commit (R9′).** On a kit with no 50 row the Hidden Art takes a percentage setup (setup → +2 payoff, labelled burst) unless every candidate tag fails one of: (a) it would stack with a sibling route's same-tag Hidden Art as a legal fourth (twin); (b) a near-tie: a legal allocation with no Advanced Art would come within 1 point of a sibling capstone's route total; (c) it does nothing for the route's hits or breaks R12/R14′. Only then is a 1/1 "steady edge" (+1 commit, +1 capstone) used. Heal is never the commit in front of a flat-Damage capstone.
- **Labels (R2).** "Burst" means a percentage setup paid off by flat Damage; a 1/1 flat-Damage route is a "steady edge"; pure Increase Damage Given is "sustained amplification".
- **Magnitude by compounding rows (R3, R10, R11).** One row may go to +10%. Two compounding rows ≤ +8%; +10% there is a DQ-C question for Decrease Damage Taken / Given, DQ-G for Increase Damage Given and DQ-B for exposure. Three or more rows ≤ +5%. Exposure is compared by its compounded factor, with a proposed ceiling of about ×1.12. A bare capstone exceeds its Hidden Art's step (a 1/1 steady edge excepted, recorded under DQ-D); +1% Hidden Arts in three-row routes are accepted.
- **Twins (R4).** No twin Hidden Arts that stack on the same rows, and no anti-universal filler leaves; same-tag setups in different branches that cannot stack are not twins. A universal Foundation is acknowledged in `narrow_kit_exception`.
- **Afterburn (R12, R17).** Exposure reachable beside the Afterburn capstone stays ≤ +5%, matching Ethereal Monarch's Rain of Fallen Stars. Where an exposure route and an Afterburn route pay off the same cast, the tree asks the director to confirm them as distinct identities.
- **Defensive riders (R13).** Fortress and suppression capstones carry no offensive rider.
- **Top-package cap (R14′).** No allocation's offensive factor, flat Damage included, exceeds Blood-Enchanted Eyes' ×1.234 top package.
- **A setup bought as a sibling route's fourth (R16).** Accepted when R14′ holds; Blood-Enchanted Eyes' own top package stacks a setup with a sibling route. Splitting self and enemy roots to prevent it is a structural choice recorded under DQ-H.
- **Route type (R18).** A capstone carrying flat Damage makes a Damage (burst or steady-edge) route whatever its modifier order; a percentage modifier on it is a rider. DQ-B, R17 and the structural lists use this.
- **Exposure beside amplification (R19).** A kit's exposure route may equal or exceed its own amplification route in compounded factor; holding it lower is not a reason on its own.
- **Node count (R7).** The pre-batch node count holds unless a node is filler or a duplicate.
