# Bloodright open user decisions

Decisions reserved to dauntless under `docs/DOCTRINE.md` (D-reserved-dauntless) and `docs/workflows/DIRECTOR_DECISIONS.md`. None is settled by the drafts; every number in the trees is a placeholder proposal. Each entry gives the practical consequence; recommendations are advisory.

| # | Decision | Why it matters | Options | Recommendation | Can defer? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Silver price per BP; acquisition gates/pacing | Sets how fast a player reaches four purchases and whether Bloodright is early or late power. | (a) flat price per BP; (b) rising price per BP; (c) rank-gated. | Decide after reviewing the trees; the designs assume all four BP are eventually reachable. | Yes — planning does not depend on it. |
| D2 | Reset/refund cost and BP retention on bloodline swap | A swap with retained BP makes the new section instantly maxed; no retention punishes experimentation. Either way inactive sections must grant nothing (G4). | (a) BP retained, section re-chosen; (b) BP refunded to silver fraction; (c) BP lost. | (a) with a silver reset fee; implement the gate in G4 regardless. | Yes. |
| D3 | Classification key: bloodline-keyed for all vs element where exclusive | Only Sand, Boil and Metal are exclusive within the census; 17 approved kits have no usable element; shared labels leak between kits and possibly into ordinary jutsu (unverified). | (a) one bloodline-keyed classification for all 44; (b) element labels where exclusive, bloodline-keyed elsewhere. | (a): uniform, no leakage, display element kept as flavour. The Taiyo Kami "Scorch" label then becomes its display element. | Yes for review; no for implementation. |
| D4 | Injected children inheritance | Ethereal Monarch's Stardust (own bloodlineId, rank D SPECIAL at level 110), Shiroi Youso's five generic children (no bloodlineId), Reptilian's summon child. If children inherit, their supported rows gain the nodes at the inject level. | (a) inherit when `bloodlineId` matches the parent bloodline; (b) inherit for every injected child of a bloodline jutsu; (c) never inherit. | (a). The drafts count only rows with a matching bloodlineId and flag the rest. | Yes. |
| D5 | Item-gated jutsu weight | Blood-Enchanted Eyes (6 gated jutsu, two keystones), Blue Blade Eyes (6, two HAND-slot weapons), Godstorm Eclipse (12, two keystones), Tetsugan (9, three weapon types) have coverage that depends on owning items; keystones are exclusive per battle, weapons may not be (G7). | (a) count gated rows at full weight; (b) discount them in tuning; (c) exclude them from coverage. | (b): drafts count them but keep flats on gated-only tags lower and say so. | Yes. |
| D6 | Ranked modes | Skill-tree effects are suppressed in ranked PvP/sparring today; Bloodright inherits that. | (a) inherit the exclusion; (b) allow Bloodright in ranked (engine change). | (a). | Yes. |
| D7 | Normal-tree potency policy | Broad potency on the normal tree is not approved; if added later it stacks with these flats (G9). | (a) keep potency Bloodright-only; (b) later combined budget audit. | (a) for this version. | Yes. |
| D8 | Final numeric tuning | All flats follow the Taiyo Kami scale adjusted by coverage; no combat simulation exists. | Accept, adjust per tree, or request simulation evidence. | Review the balance matrix first; adjust the strongest and weakest outliers it names. Recorded for review here: Blood-Enchanted Eyes Draft 2 re-derives its defensive route from the kit's two DDT rows (DDT +8, DDG +3 secondary, no offensive secondary) and raises its Damage route to +6 on two castable rows; its design note 3 gives per-keystone castable totals (Fortress 24/29, Burst 24/26, Plague 25/20, Sustain 18/28). | No — final acceptance. |
| D9 | Name collisions with existing SkillTree entries | `SkillTree.name` is unique; live names are unknown here. | (a) prefix with the bloodline on collision; (b) rename case by case. | (a). Needs the read-only listing in the gap register. | Yes. |
| D10 | Hidden/variant rows | Namikaze's hidden NORMAL-type jutsu *Soaring Fujin* is linked to the bloodline and counted in coverage; other kits have none. | (a) count hidden rows; (b) exclude hidden rows from coverage. | (a) with a note, since a hidden record can be published later. | Yes. |
| D11 | Adverse rows (Dai Kenja) | An Increase Damage Taken node also raises Overloaded Impact's self-targeted IDT. The draft accepts it with +2/+3 flats on Cracked Vessel and Shattered Vessel (risk 1, design note 6). A second tuning question is recorded in its design note 8: Shattered Vessel's Decrease Damage Given secondary (+3) versus Edict of Silence (+4) weighting (node row-weight 12 vs 9 excluding the adverse row). | (a) accept with a small flat; (b) avoid the IDT tag in that tree; (c) engine opt-out flag (not proposed); for the secondary: keep +3/+4, or move to +2/+5. | (a) as drafted; secondary weighting left at +3/+4 pending review. | Yes. |
| D12 | Afterburn in trees with many damage sources | Afterburn value scales with every hit the target takes; stacked sources saturate at 60% per hit. | Accept the +10% ceiling or lower it for kits with two Afterburn rows. | Keep ≤ +10% total; review Blood-Enchanted Eyes, Shiroi Youso, Bakuhatsu, Vaporia, Godstorm Eclipse. | Yes. |
| D14 | Ceiling for stacking exposure rows | Several kits carry two Increase Damage Taken rows that can sit on one target at once (Aerathiel, Shakunetsu Sakura, Tenohira Musei, Hyouga Yui, Blood-Enchanted Eyes on the Ring side). The drafts hold such routes to +6/+7 total (versus +10 on a single-row tag) so the stacked exposure stays near Taiyo Kami's single-row reach; Shinrai Ou's single-row IDT route reaches +10. | (a) keep the two-row ceiling at +7; (b) allow +10 regardless of row count; (c) set a stacked-percentage ceiling (e.g. 85% combined). | (a) until combat evidence exists. | Yes. |
| D13 | Narrow-kit exceptions | Some kits support fewer than ten meaningful nodes or fewer than three distinct builds; the drafts record each exception. | Accept the smaller trees, or decline Bloodright for those bloodlines. | Accept; filler nodes would be worse than fewer nodes. | No — acceptance. |

Decisions recorded by dauntless go to `docs/RULINGS.md` first (history) and then to the canonical owner (`docs/design/BLOODRIGHT_DESIGN_BRIEF.md` for design contract changes; `roster.json` for membership).
