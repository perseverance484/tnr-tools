## Technical conflict: black braided crossing wires vs immutable pixelwise luminosity

The director-accepted source visual has genuinely **matte-black cables** overlapping reflective silver facets. When the master has a bright pixel, an algorithm keeping **`max(R,G,B)` exactly equal** must leave at least one channel equally bright. That makes a dark cable **look saturated blue** if only its blue channel retains the required bright intensity. To make physically black cables visible, the internal cable overlay must reduce the pixel's brightness in those small existing emblem regions.
