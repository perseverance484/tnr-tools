# Reagent, Armorer's and Field Supply Caches — design draft

**Status:** DRAFT proposal for dauntless. Nothing here has been pushed to the game, turned into
a manifest, or approved. Every chance, quantity, rarity, chassis field and source below is a
**proposal**. Balance and rewards are dauntless's call (`docs/DOCTRINE.md`; item reference §4).

**Inputs:**
- Census: `reports/crafting_recipe_census/craftable_recipes.json`. It is COMPLETE, from capture
  `81c76fef…`, read on 2026-10-13 at source pin `TheNinjaRPG@641040531`.
- Loot-table data: `caches_draft.json`. This is the single editable source.
- Yield report: `CACHE_YIELD_REPORT.md`, generated by `harness/crafting_caches/check_draft.py`.

## 1. What the census says

- **86 recipes are player-craftable today.** By type they are 24 consumables, 17 crystals,
  25 weapons, 14 armor, 4 materials and 2 other.
- **Ingredients fall into three families,** which map directly onto the three caches:
  - **Botanical and beast reagents.** These are COMMON and RARE gathered or hunted herbs, pelts
    and spines (Galecrest Sage, Frostbite Leaf, Murkshade, Brasel Spine and others). They feed the
    consumables, and the crystals together with **Rainbow Cabochon**, the single most-used
    ingredient (14 player recipes, 250 units).
  - **Metals and cores.** The base ores are Iron, Silver, Adamantine and Diamond. The Legendary
    tier is Mithril, Imperial Gold and four hunted cores (Mask, Thread, Parchment, Willful Steel).
    Every weapon and armor recipe draws on these.
  - **Finished COMMON consumables.** These are pills, kunai and the Repair Kit. They are all
    tradable and stack to 100 or more.
- **Unobtanium is the Legendary bottleneck.** 17 recipes need ×10 each, and it is crafted from
  Adamantine, Diamond, Willful Steel and Thread Core. This puts the Armorer's Cache directly
  upstream of every Kaeruun armor piece and six Legendary weapons.

## 2. Design principles (proposed)

1. **Caches supply crafting; they do not replace it.** They contain no finished gear, no crafted
   intermediates (Unobtanium, the Godstorm/Eclipse/Unbroken parts) and no crystals. The Field
   Supply Cache is the one exception, and only for disposable COMMON/RARE consumables (see
   decision D3).
2. **They leave other content's scarcity alone.** Event and quest-exclusive materials stay out:
   Skyglass, the Godstorm 400-unit samples and blueprints, the Veil set, the Rosary beads and the
   Crimson Court pieces. So does Heart of Kaeruun, which has no source other than trading (D5).
   `caches_draft.json` lists every exclusion and the checker enforces it.
3. **Every item is real and visible.** Each line is a census id. The checker rejects hidden items,
   event items and names that don't match.
4. **Engine law 22 (the drop doctrine) holds.** No cache quantity shares a factor with any
   recipe quantity of that item. Recipes use 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 30, 40 and 400, so the draft uses
   3, 7 and 1, and a cache never pays out an exact recipe. The checker enforces this too.
5. **One loot-chest mechanic.** Each cache is one item with a single `noncombatconsumereward` and
   independently rolled lines. A 100% line is the floor; a second line on the same id adds a bonus
   range (item reference §4).

## 3. The three caches (proposed contents)

The full tables, with expected units per open, are in `CACHE_YIELD_REPORT.md`. In summary:

### Reagent Cache — 25 lines, about 7.5 lines hit and 27 units per open
- **Core:** Rainbow Cabochon 7@100%, plus a 7@25% bonus.
- **Common herbs and beast parts (3 each):** Galecrest Sage @60%, and eight more @40%.
- **Uncommon (3 each):** nine reagents at 25% or 15%.
- **Rare (3 each):** Widow's Kiss, Starfire Petal, Zephyr Seed and Solar Petal @10%; Ofuda Moss @5%.
- **What it funds alone:** COMMON pills in about 2–3 opens, EPIC consumables in about 7, COMMON
  crystals in about 17.

### Armorer's Cache — 11 lines, about 3.4 lines hit and 18 units per open
- **Core:** Iron Ore 7@100%, plus a 7@50% bonus.
- **Common:** Silver Ore 7@60%, Adamantine Ore 3@40%.
- **Uncommon:** Diamond Ore 3@30%, Mithril Ore 3@20%, Imperial Gold 3@15%.
- **Rare:** Mask, Thread and Parchment Core and Shard of Willful Steel, each 1@5%.
- **What it funds:** the metal half of every weapon and armor recipe. RARE weapons need one
  reagent from the Reagent Cache as well.
  - At these rates a Legendary recipe needs about 200 opens, because 10 cores at 0.05 per open is
    200 opens. That is deliberately grindy as a placeholder (D6).

### Field Supply Cache — 13 lines, about 4.8 lines hit and 14 units per open
- **Core:** Chakra Pill II 3@100% and Stamina Pill II 3@100%.
- **Common:** Small Health Pill 3@50%, Repair Kit 3@50%, Galecrest Sage 3@25%.
- **Uncommon:** six kunai types plus Robber's Friend and Venom Cask, each 3@20%.
- **Rare:** Bloodthorn Kunai 3@10%.
- **What it funds:** about 1.3 opens per Pill Pack. Mostly it is ready-to-use stock.

**All three together** fully supply the ingredients for 31 of the 86 player recipes. That covers
every RARE weapon except the event Veil pair. Repair Kit and Bloodthorn Kunai aren't craftable from
cache contents, but the Field Supply Cache hands them out finished. The report lists the other 55 recipes
and the ingredient each one is missing.

## 4. Proposed chassis (all three)

This is cloned from the item reference §5 chest chassis:
- `CONSUMABLE` / `ITEM`, rarity `COMMON`;
- `destroyOnUse` and `preventBattleUsage` true;
- `stackSize 999`, `canStack` true;
- `inShop` and `canBeTraded` false;
- `cost 1`, `requiredLevel 1`;
- `battleDescription "Supply cache"`;
- created **hidden**. Publishing is a separate action that dauntless owns.

## 5. Decisions for dauntless

| # | Decision | Draft assumption | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | **How players get each cache.** The options are quest reward, AI drop, shop (ryo, reputation or Seichi Silver), daily login, or event. | Not decided; the tables are sized as "one open per typical reward". | The source sets the economy. A purchasable cache turns the yield table into a price. |
| D2 | **Who it is for.** | Low and mid crafters (Novice to Master). The Legendary tier is present only as a rare tail. | Decides whether Mithril, Imperial Gold and the cores belong in the cache at all. |
| D3 | **Field Supply: finished goods or reagents.** | Finished COMMON/RARE consumables. | Finished pills and kunai compete with crafters selling the same tradable stock. |
| D4 | **Tradability of the caches.** | Not tradable. | Tradable caches become a currency. |
| D5 | **Heart of Kaeruun and Unobtanium.** | Both excluded. | Heart has no source but trading, so adding it creates a new source for 11 armor pieces. Unobtanium is a crafted, event-flagged bottleneck. |
| D6 | **Every chance and quantity**, especially the 5% core lines and the 7-unit cores. | Placeholder values; see the report. | Balance, which is yours by doctrine. |
| D7 | **Cache rarity** (COMMON chest default versus prestige) and **art**. | COMMON; no art yet. | Art direction and rarity are yours. |
| D8 | **Gold Ore, Bloodcore and Orichalcum**, the crystal ores. | Left out; crystals rely on Rainbow Cabochon from the Reagent Cache. | Adding them would complete Offense Crystal 1 and Heal Crystal III directly, and Shield Crystal II through a crafted Shield Crystal (see "Not supplied" in the report). |

## 6. Iterating

1. Edit `caches_draft.json`.
2. Run `python3 harness/crafting_caches/check_draft.py`. It validates the draft against the census
   and regenerates the report, and exit 0 means every hard check passed.
3. Run `python3 harness/crafting_caches/selftest.py`.

Once D1–D7 are settled, the next step is a builder manifest: three `item` creates with the chosen
chassis, hidden, with the loot tables taken from the approved draft. It would be validated with
`validate.py` and pushed by dauntless through the builder. No manifest exists yet.
