**Celestial Dew: new animations**

Celestial Dew keeps its icon (staff scored it 10/10) and its text. What's new is the animation: it now plays the exact
moment the jutsu describes.

- **The cast:** a small crescent moon appears above the player, a single drop of starlight falls from it into their
  portrait and splashes, and ripples run out across it.
- **The ward:** when the ripples reach the edge of the portrait, they charge it into a celestial ward. It glows softly in
  and out for as long as the shield holds.
- **When it ends:** if the shield runs out, breaks, or fails to form, the ward shatters into stardust.

The heal keeps its own heart effect, which still plays alongside as before. Before, nothing showed the shield.
