# Scroll icon roadmap (jutsu and bloodline scrolls)

- **Status:** DRAFT, stage 1 of 2 (2026-10-22). Inventory and the overlap read of the 13 scrolls we made. Stage 2 adds
  the live art of the remaining scrolls (KGK, Forbidden, Demon, Dragon/Tiger, bloodline unlocks) and the
  replacement plan. Art direction is the director's (CLAUDE.md section 10).
- **Goal (director, 2026-10-22):** every scroll reads as a different scroll at icon size, with no visual overlap. The
  KGK scrolls are already high quality: design around them rather than replace them.
- **Sources:** push/211 (`harvests/inbox/tnr_results_1792693531799.json`); push/216 (the 17 other scroll-named
  items, pending); art in `art/village_bloodline_scrolls/` and `art/item_icons/event_scrolls/`.

## Overlap read, our 13 (`sheet_ours_2026-10-22.png`)

Two silhouettes: **village** (long, thin, tight wraps, shallow angle) and **event** (fat, 45 degrees, heavy steel caps,
swirling effect). Silhouette separates the families; at 125 px colour carries most of the identity, and the colour
clusters cross the families:

| Cluster | Scrolls | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Purple / black | Oblivion Seal (village), Shadows (event), Hollow Veil (event) | high: Shadows and Oblivion Seal share black paper, violet glow and purple tassels |
| Teal wind on cream | Aerathiel (village), Cleave (event) | high |
| Fire orange | Taiyo Kami (village), Blazing (event) | medium (sun-gold vs fang-red) |
| Blue / white | Arashima, Hyouga Yui (village), Celestial Dew (event) | medium |
| Green | Shinseina Ki (village), Renewal (event) | low (leaf-green vs teal water) |
| Earth brown | Bastion (event) | none |
