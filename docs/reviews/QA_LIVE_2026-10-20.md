## How this was checked

- **Peer groups for outliers.** Modified z-score (Iglewicz and Hoaglin): `M = 0.6745 * (x - median) / MAD`,
  flagged when `|M| > 3.5` in a group of at least 8; when MAD is 0 the mean absolute deviation stands in
  (`M = (x - median) / (1.253314 * meanAD)`). Groups: visible player jutsu by (jutsuType, jutsuRank);
  visible items by (itemType, rarity); visible quests by (questType, questRank), using quest-level plus
  objective-level reward totals; non-summon AI by 10-level band. An outlier is a question, not a verdict.
- **Rules used.** Field bounds from `45d`, tag caps, companion and runtime-only tags from `45g`, USER_CAPS
  from `45e`, engine laws from `docs/ENGINE_LAWS.md`, quest-engine rules from `references/quest.md`.
  Contracts are stamped `studie-tech/TheNinjaRPG@85cecaeb` (2026-10-20).
- **Deleted records.** Inbox captures of records that are no longer in a live name list are dropped
  before checking (24 quests and 10 assets were in captures but are gone from the game).
- **Prose.** Mechanical checks (placeholders, stubs, build notes, a/an, repeated words, a fixed
  misspelling list, test names, near-duplicate names) plus a read of random samples. The samples read
  as serviceable but thin: most visible jutsu descriptions are one generic sentence ("A sweeping leg
  attack that creates a whirlwind."), and several item families share one copy-pasted line.
- **Not judged.** Whether any outlier is wrong. Whether hidden content should be published.

> ## RUL-2026-10-20-00N — Standing exception: read-only public QA reads of the live game
>
> **Date:** 2026-10-20 (proposed) &nbsp; **Domain:** live-game boundary (CLAUDE.md section 6) / content QA
> **Status:** PROPOSED - awaiting dauntless's approval.
> **Narrows:** CLAUDE.md section 6 ("prefer pinned source, local checkout, fixtures, in-process harnesses and
> committed captures") for content-QA tasks only. It does not touch the user-pushes rule, publishing, or any write path.
>
> **Ruling:** For a task the user labels as a content QA pass, Claude may send unauthenticated, read-only
> requests to `https://www.theninja-rpg.com/api/trpc` for procedures that `forge/src/transport/procedures.mjs`
> marks `kind: "query"` and `auth: "public"`, restricted to the `get`, `getAll` and `getAllNames` reads of
> jutsu, item, quests, gameAsset and bloodline, plus `profile.getAllAiNames`. It may also send GET or HEAD
> requests to the image URLs those records name, to check that they load.
>
> **Conditions:**
> 1. Requests are built with `forge/src/transport/envelope.mjs` (tRPC batch plus superjson) and a fetcher that
>    refuses any procedure not marked query/public in `procedures.mjs`.
> 2. No cookies, sessions, logins, tokens or credentials of any kind; no redirects followed.
> 3. At most 30 requests per minute per procedure (the server allows 60 per 60 s per procedure and user).
> 4. Every response is cached to disk with its fetch time and never re-fetched within the task.
> 5. A smoke test (one `jutsu.getAllNames`) runs first; on failure the task stops and reports what came back.
> 6. A protected read (e.g. `profile.getAi`, `ai.getAiProfile`) is recorded as a gap, never worked around.
> 7. The handoff states the request count, the procedures used, fetch timestamps, and that there were zero
>    mutations and no credentials.
> 8. Raw dumps are committed only in a format `harvest.py` accepts; checks are rerun with `scripts/qa_live.py`.
>
> **Why:** a QA pass needs the whole live catalog, hidden records included, at one moment. Committed captures
> are partial and days old, and the public read surface already serves the same data to any visitor.
>
> **Boundary:** no mutation, upload, publication or protected procedure is authorized. A task that says zero
> live requests still means zero. Stricter task-specific limits win.
