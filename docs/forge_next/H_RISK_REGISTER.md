# H. Risk register

**Status:** planning. Each risk names the invariant at stake, the evidence it rests on, how it would surface in Forge Next, and the mitigation the roadmap (section G) carries. Likelihood/impact are the planning owner's estimates and are marked as such; they are not measurements.

Legend: L/I = likelihood / impact (low, med, high). "Gate" names the roadmap acceptance gate that retires or bounds the risk.
