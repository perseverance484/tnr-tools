# Stolen Stores: full update design

Release Fri Nov 20, 6 PM CT · drafted Nov 16, 2026

## The update at a glance

**Something aboard has been eating the ship's stores, and this weekend players find out what.** The Quartermaster's crew are hauling the stores off the ship, some of them have washed up on the beach, and the only mouth aboard is waiting at the bottom of the galley.

**What's new for players:**

- **A living beach:** sector 210 is remade, and the Quartermaster's crew wander it. Anyone can walk up and fight them alone, at any level from 30, for salvage and a new daily job.
- **The Quartermaster's Stores:** a new solo chart run, open from level 30. He feeds his crew so they can't fall, so you take out the feeder first. Win three fights, then take the stores, or go below and meet what's been eating them.
- **Two new Watch Log pages:** Stolen Stores for 81+ and Stolen Stores (Shallows) for 30 to 80. Both can be finished without a team.
- **The Salvager's Fire:** new topics on the crew on the sand, the new run and the new job, and a new chart in her hand when you meet her.
- **Wreck fixes:** Maw of the Deep on fixed damage, and a looping animation on reflect stances.

**What stays the same:** both Wrecks, their floors and bosses, the four shore dailies, the trades, the ladder up to the Mother of Echoes.

**A solo player's weekend:** take Wreck Sighted, walk the beach and fight the crew, pick up the raider job, run the Quartermaster's Stores, and fill the page. A team does all that and the Wreck too.

## The story

**The answer to "what's eating the stores" is the ship's mascot.** It's already canon that the crew don't eat, that the galley "still stocks a larder for a crew that does not eat", and that the Core tried to remember the ship's mascot and got the Little Horror back. Put those together and there's exactly one mouth aboard.

**What happened, as the weekend reveals it:**

1. The Quartermaster still runs the stores. Every watch he issues rations to a crew that has no use for them, because that was his order.
2. The crew take their rations and carry them down, below the galley, to the one thing aboard that eats.
3. The Core keeps remaking the mascot, and every copy is hungry. Down in the lowest store room, the oldest one has been eating for a generation. It's the size of the room now.
4. Since the ship came down, the stores have been running short, so the Quartermaster sends his crew farther for more. Some go over the side after the crates the sea took, and the tide leaves them on the sand.

**How each piece tells it:**

- **The beach:** crew on the sand, dragging crates back toward the hull. Players meet the looters before they know why they're looting.
- **The page hook:** "Something down there is eating them" asks the question.
- **The Quartermaster's Stores:** fights 1 to 3 show the ration system. The choice at the end, "go below", is the player choosing to find out.
- **The Stores Below:** the answer, the Mascot, and a line from the Salvager afterwards.
- **The page ending (already planned):** she counts the stores twice and comes up one short.

**The Salvager's take:** she's eaten off this shore for years and never once found food in a crate from that ship. Now she knows where it went. She also knows what's in the Little Horror's Jar, and she isn't sure she likes what that says about it.

**Why this is a good reveal:** it reuses the event's own pet, it makes Galley Stores (pet food) suddenly mean something, and the face at the bottom of the stores is one some players already keep in a jar. A player with the Little Horror should feel the click.

## Beach raiders

Two crew records walk the beach, and they're the same records the Quartermaster's Stores uses, so they're built once. They show a red ring and an Attack button, never start a fight, and scale to whoever attacks them.

### The crew

|  | Looting Deckhand | Quartermaster's Mate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role | the common raider, drags crates | the tough one, hands out rations |
| Toughness | a short fight, 3 to 5 rounds | a real fight, 6 to 10 rounds |
| Signature | **Crate Swing**: a steady hit, same damage every time | **Hand Out a Ration**: one ally can't fall this round and heals a little. Alone, he feeds himself every third round, and the log says so a round early |
| Second move | **Pocket a Ration**: once a fight, at half health, he can't fall for one round | **Count the Stores**: a slow hit that grows each round he isn't hit |
| Drops (your call) | Wreck Salvage 10% | Wreck Salvage 30%, Quartermaster's Stores chart 3% |

**Why these kits:** the beach teaches the same lesson the run does, in miniature. A Mate who feeds himself shows players the ration icon before they ever see the Quartermaster.

### Where they walk

- **Deckhands:** 8 groups of 1 to 3, roaming the sand and the ground between the two Wrecks, never on ocean or ice.
- **Mates:** 3, alone, near the crates and the shore path to each Wreck.
- Tiles reroll each day, which reads as the tide moving them. Defeated crew stay, so a player arriving late still finds them.
- These numbers suit about 20 to 30 players a weekend, the size of weekend 2. More copies is one field per placement.

### The raider job: Short Rations

**Recommended: a Logbook job from the Wake Island board, once a day**, like the trades. It doesn't spend one of the 20 daily NPC rolls and doesn't change the odds on her four shore dailies. The fallback, if a board job can't count beach fights, is a fifth daily on her NPC.

> **Short Rations** "The Quartermaster's crew are on my sand again, dragging my crates back to that ship. Thin them out."
>
> 1. Beat 3 Looting Deckhands on the beach in sector 210.
> 2. Beat a Quartermaster's Mate. Reward: 3 Wreck Salvage (your call). Open from level 30, once a day.

**On the page:** both pages count Short Rations, so a solo or 30 to 80 player can finish the page off the beach alone (next sections).

**Note:** sector 210 can't turn PvP off, so a player fighting raiders can still be jumped. That's how the beach already works.

## The Quartermaster's Stores

A rare solo chart run from the Battle Arena, open from level 30, enemies scaled to the player. Three fights teach the ration trick, then a choice, then an optional fourth fight that answers the page's question.

### Fight by fight

1. **The Ration Line:** a Quartermaster's Mate and 2 Looting Deckhands. The Mate feeds one Deckhand each round, so it can't fall that round and heals a little. Hit the Mate first and the room falls over.
2. **The Crate Wall:** a Looting Sentinel and a Mate. From round 2 the Sentinel stacks crates (**Stack the Crates**), shielding the Mate. Break the Sentinel or burn through the wall: both work, one is faster.
3. **The Quartermaster:** the Quartermaster and 2 Looting Blades. On round 1 he casts **Open the Stores** and every looter is fed, which the log calls out. After that he feeds the weakest looter each round. On round 3, two Deckhands come up from the hold. He has about 10k HP and stands behind his crew: one big hit can't drop him, two or three can, and when he falls the rations stop and the room collapses in a round.
4. **The choice:** take the stores and leave with your pick, or go below.
5. **The Stores Below (optional):** the Mascot. Lose here and you leave with nothing.

### The Mascot

The oldest of the Core's copies of the ship's mascot, grown to fill the lowest store room on a generation of rations. Same creature as the Little Horror, much bigger, with too many teeth.

- **Gulp:** every third round, a big bite at fixed damage, telegraphed a round early ("It opens its mouth wider than the room"). Same shape as Maw of the Deep, so players who know the Wreck recognise it.
- **Feeding:** each round between Gulps, it eats from the stores and gains a small damage stack. The fight is a race, and players can see the clock.
- **Spit Up:** at half health it coughs up two Little Horrors. Next round they crawl back into it and heal it, unless you kill them first. That's the feeding trick turned inside out: this time you stop the food going in.
- About 13 rounds for a good solo player, in line with the team median on the Wreck boss.

### Rewards (your call)

- **Take the stores:** choose one: Galley Stores ×3 · Medicine Inro · Skyglass Token. Pays 1 Wreck Salvage.
- **Beat the Mascot:** choose two: Galley Stores ×3 · Skyglass Coffer · Sealed Treasury · Skyglass Token ×2. Pays 3 Wreck Salvage.
- **Something only found below:** my pick is a small chance at the Little Horror's Jar, once per player. Today the Jar only drops at 5% from the 81+ Wreck, so this opens the pet to 30 to 80 players and solo players. If that's too generous, a cosmetic such as a Mascot skin token works instead.
