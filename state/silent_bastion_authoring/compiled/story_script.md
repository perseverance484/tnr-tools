## Quest

Starting this expedition consumes one Skyglass Signal Beacon. Failure or abandonment will not refund it. Retrying a division costs no additional Beacon.

If you still have a reward to choose from a previous expedition, claim it before you set out: an expedition cannot be completed while an earlier reward choice is waiting.

**Success.** The Supreme Commander has fallen, and the Bastion's orders did not fall with him. Choose your reward: a Bastion Arsenal Cache or a Bastion Research Cache.
