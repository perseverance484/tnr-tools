Thanksgiving 2026: The Gobblefather
Seichi Seasonal Event • November 2026
This year's Thanksgiving event introduces the Feathered Family, an organized crime syndicate that has taken control of Seichi's harvest supply.
Instead of another turkey rampage, the players must dismantle an entire criminal operation. There will be three boss encounters, each with a different fighting mechanic, leading to the mysterious Gobblefather.

Event Lore
Prologue: An Offer You Cannot Refuse
Thanksgiving was approaching in Seichi.
Across the villages, preparations had begun. Merchants delivered their harvests, families prepared enormous feasts, and farmers counted the profits of another successful season.
But something was wrong.
Caravans carrying corn vanished along the southern trade roads.
Warehouses were emptied without a single lock being broken.
Merchants who refused to surrender their supplies discovered threatening messages nailed to their doors.
Each message bore the same symbol.
A black feather wrapped around a golden ear of corn.
At first, the villages suspected bandits.
Then the demands arrived.
Every merchant transporting food through the Harvest Plains was required to pay a new fee.
The Feather Tax.
Those who paid were allowed to continue their business.
Those who refused were visited by two enormous turkeys wearing matching black suits.
Nobody knew where they came from.
Nobody knew who employed them.
But every witness remembered the same words.
"Lovely harvest you got here."
"Would be a shame if something happened to it."
Soon, nearly every supply route through the Harvest Plains fell under the control of a mysterious organization known as the Feathered Family.
Its leader was spoken of only in whispers.
The Gobblefather.
Chapter 1: The Drumstick Brothers
The first major warning came when an entire caravan disappeared outside the Harvest Plains.
Its driver returned three days later, shaken and covered in feathers.
When questioned, he described two enormous turkeys blocking the road.
One carried a wooden club fashioned from an oversized drumstick.
The other wore golden rings on every feathered finger.
They called themselves the Drumstick Brothers.
The brothers demanded payment for safe passage.
When the driver refused, they confiscated his shipment.
"Nothing personal," one brother told him.
"Just poultry business," said the other.
The driver was released with a single bag of corn.
Inside the bag was a note.
THE FAMILY ALWAYS EATS FIRST.
Chapter 2: Don Giblet
As merchants searched for ways around the Feather Tax, a second figure emerged.
Don Giblet.
A calculating turkey dressed in an expensive burgundy waistcoat, Don Giblet controlled the syndicate's money, inventory, and protection rackets.
He operated from a converted grain warehouse known as the Golden Silo.
Every stolen shipment passed through his records.
Every merchant owed him something.
He never raised his voice.
He never chased anyone.
He simply adjusted his spectacles and reminded people of their debts.
And those debts always grew.
When several villages attempted to organize a resistance, Don Giblet issued a warning.
"No one steals from the Family."
"Especially when the Family stole it first."
Chapter 3: The Gobblefather
Beyond the Golden Silo stood an enormous estate surrounded by fields of golden corn.
Inside, beneath chandeliers and lavish harvest decorations, sat the leader of the Feathered Family.
The Gobblefather.
An enormous turkey wearing a dark pinstripe suit and a crimson silk tie.
A golden ring decorated one wing.
A tiny chicken rested peacefully on his lap.
For years, the Gobblefather had watched Thanksgiving celebrations come and go.
Every year, people gathered to celebrate their harvests.
Every year, enormous feasts were prepared.
And every year...
The turkeys were never invited.
The Gobblefather had finally decided to correct this injustice.
But his solution was not peace.
It was control.
He intended to seize the entire Thanksgiving harvest and host the greatest feast Seichi had ever witnessed.
A feast where the turkeys sat at the head of the table.
And everyone else paid the bill.
His final declaration was delivered across the Harvest Plains:
"This Thanksgiving, nobody eats until the Family eats."

Quest Descriptions
Description:
A criminal organization known as the Feathered Family has seized control of the Harvest Plains, stealing Thanksgiving supplies and imposing a ridiculous Feather Tax on Seichi's merchants. Investigate the missing caravans, defeat the syndicate's enforcers, and confront the mysterious Gobblefather before he claims the entire harvest.
Success Description:
The Feathered Family's criminal empire has collapsed. The stolen harvests have been recovered, the Feather Tax has been abolished, and Thanksgiving celebrations can continue throughout Seichi. The Gobblefather's reign has ended, but somewhere among the scattered feathers, a familiar voice mutters that the Family never forgets.
Failure Description:
The Feathered Family retains control of the Harvest Plains. The stolen supplies remain locked away, merchants are forced to continue paying the Feather Tax, and the Gobblefather prepares his grand Thanksgiving feast using Seichi's entire harvest.

Background Image List
Harvest Plains Trade Road: A sprawling autumn countryside with golden fields, wooden merchant wagons, scattered corn, and suspicious black feathers along the roadside.
The Drumstick Tollgate: A makeshift checkpoint constructed from stolen caravan supplies, wooden barriers, and enormous signs demanding payment of the Feather Tax.
Golden Silo Warehouse: A towering grain warehouse filled with stolen food, stacked wooden crates, account ledgers, barrels of corn, and heavily guarded storage rooms.
The Feathered Estate: A luxurious criminal mansion surrounded by enormous cornfields, golden fences, and stone turkey statues.
The Gobblefather's Office: A dimly lit office with red curtains, expensive wooden furniture, a grand desk, and a high-backed leather chair beneath a portrait of an enormous turkey.
The Grand Feast Hall: A magnificent banquet hall containing a long dining table, enormous Thanksgiving decorations, chandeliers, and enough stolen food to feed several villages.

Character List
Goah Way (Quest Giver): The wandering Sun Praiser and Kage of Freedom State. While traveling through the Harvest Plains, Goah discovers the missing supply caravans. Rather than allowing the Feathered Family to expand its influence, he asks the player to investigate.
Merchant Hachiro (NPC): A nervous traveling merchant whose food shipments were confiscated by the Drumstick Brothers. Provides information about the Feather Tax.
Farmer Toma (NPC): A hardworking farmer whose entire corn harvest has been seized by Don Giblet. Helps the player locate the Golden Silo.
The Gobblefather (Final Boss): The mysterious leader of the Feathered Family. A sophisticated, ruthless turkey crime lord who believes that every harvest in Seichi should belong to his organization.

NPC List — Boss Encounters
Boss Encounter 1: The Drumstick Brothers
Bosses: Tony Drumstick and Vinny Drumstick
Location: The Drumstick Tollgate
Difficulty: S-Rank
Two enormous turkey brothers employed as the primary enforcers of the Feathered Family.
Tony is loud, reckless, and prefers overwhelming physical attacks. Vinny is quieter and specializes in defensive support and counterattacks.
The brothers coordinate their attacks and become significantly more dangerous when fighting together.
Tony Drumstick — Jutsus
1. Drumstick Release: Concrete Clobber
Rank: A
Type: Physical / Earth
Tony swings an enormous hardened drumstick into an opponent, dealing heavy damage and potentially stunning the target.
2. Feather Barrage: Collection Notice
Rank: B
Type: Wind / Projectile
Tony launches sharpened feathers at multiple enemies, applying Bleed.
3. Family Business
Rank: A
Type: Buff
Tony gains increased attack power while Vinny remains alive.
Vinny Drumstick — Jutsus
1. Wing Guard: Protection Payment
Rank: A
Type: Defensive
Vinny shields Tony or himself, reducing incoming damage for two turns.
2. Back-Alley Counter
Rank: A
Type: Counterattack
Vinny reflects a portion of incoming physical damage toward the attacker.
3. Double Drumstick Beatdown
Rank: S
Type: Combination
Tony and Vinny launch a coordinated attack against a single target, inflicting heavy damage and Stagger.
Unique Boss Mechanic: Family Loyalty
As long as both brothers remain alive, they can use Double Drumstick Beatdown.
When one brother is defeated, the surviving brother gains Family Vengeance, increasing attack power but reducing defense.
Boss Dialogue
Tony: "You got two options. Pay the Feather Tax or become part of the pavement."
Vinny: "Tony, be polite. We're conducting business."
Tony: "Right. Sorry. PLEASE pay the Feather Tax."
Defeat Dialogue:
Tony: "Tell the boss... we tried..."
Vinny: "You just had to hit him with the drumstick, didn't you?"

Boss Encounter 2: Don Giblet
Boss: Don Giblet
Location: Golden Silo Warehouse
Difficulty: S-Rank
Don Giblet handles the Feathered Family's finances and stolen inventory. Unlike the Drumstick Brothers, he prefers controlling a battle through strategy and manipulation rather than brute strength.
Unique Jutsus
1. Corn Release: Golden Avalanche
Rank: A
Type: Area Damage
Don Giblet unleashes a massive wave of hardened corn kernels, striking all enemies and reducing their speed.
2. Feather Tax: Mandatory Collection
Rank: S
Type: Chakra Drain
A forbidden financial sealing technique that steals chakra from enemies and converts it into a temporary shield.
3. Compound Interest
Rank: A
Type: Curse / Debuff
Applies a Debt Mark to an enemy. The mark accumulates each turn, increasing the damage received when Don Giblet activates Collection Day.
4. Collection Day
Rank: S
Type: Burst Damage
Consumes all active Debt Marks to inflict heavy damage based on the number of stacks.
5. Emergency Audit
Rank: A
Type: Defensive / Cleanse
Removes one negative status effect and gains temporary damage resistance.
Unique Boss Mechanic: Outstanding Debt
Don Giblet places Debt Marks on players during the fight.
Players must remove these marks through defensive or cleansing abilities before he activates Collection Day.
Each remaining mark increases its damage.
Boss Dialogue
Don Giblet: "According to my records, you owe the Family a considerable amount of corn."
Player: "I don't owe you anything."
Don Giblet: "Interest has already been applied."
During Battle: "Your chakra has been repossessed."
Defeat Dialogue:
"Impossible... My calculations were perfect..."
"Who... who is going to balance the books?"

Boss Encounter 3: The Gobblefather
Boss: The Gobblefather
Location: The Grand Feast Hall
Difficulty: S-Rank Final Boss
The leader of the Feathered Family.
A massive, powerful turkey whose chakra techniques combine intimidation, sealing arts, and devastating Thanksgiving-themed attacks.
Despite his ridiculous appearance, the Gobblefather is the most dangerous member of the organization.
He fights in three phases.
Unique Jutsus
1. An Offer You Cannot Refuse
Rank: S
Type: Control
The Gobblefather marks an opponent with an Intimidation Seal, temporarily reducing their attack power and increasing their chakra costs.
2. Gravy Release: Drowning Debt
Rank: A
Type: Area Damage
Summons a massive wave of scalding gravy that damages enemies and reduces their movement speed.
3. Thanksgiving Massacre: Flying Drumsticks
Rank: S
Type: Multi-Target Attack
Conjures enormous chakra-infused drumsticks that rain down upon the battlefield, striking enemies repeatedly.
4. Family Protection
Rank: A
Type: Shield / Summoning
The Gobblefather summons two Feathered Enforcers and gains a protective shield while either remains active.
5. The Final Feast
Rank: S
Type: Ultimate Attack
The Gobblefather gathers the chakra of the stolen harvest into an enormous golden corn sphere before detonating it across the battlefield.
6. Omertà: Code of Feathers
Rank: S
Type: Special Buff
The Gobblefather gains increased defense and counterattack damage after falling below 35% health.
Unique Boss Mechanic: Three Phases
Phase 1 — Business as Usual (100%–70% HP)
The Gobblefather remains seated at the head of the banquet table, issuing commands and using sealing techniques. Players must remove his protective enforcers.
Phase 2 — This Is Personal (69%–35% HP)
The Gobblefather rises from his chair, destroys the banquet table, and begins attacking directly. He uses Gravy Release and Flying Drumsticks.
Phase 3 — The Family Never Forgets (34%–0% HP)
The Gobblefather activates Omertà: Code of Feathers and begins preparing The Final Feast. Players must interrupt his ultimate technique before the attack resolves.

The merchants returned.
The caravans moved freely.

Deep within the abandoned Feathered Estate, however, the office of the Gobblefather remained untouched.
The enormous desk stood beneath the portrait.
The red curtains hung motionless.
And on the desk sat a tiny chicken.
Beside it rested the Gobblefather's golden ring.
A shadow moved across the office.
Someone approached the desk.
A quiet voice broke the silence.
"The boss is gone."
