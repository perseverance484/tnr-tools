# Stolen Stores story v2: draft for the director (2026-11-20)

Nothing here is live or built. It asks for the director's yes before any record changes.

## What it has to fit

- The director's words: the crew "are Chakra impressions, essentially like undead. They dont think for themselves or
  notice changes in the environment all that much... They would be indifferent to any kind of change like crashing or
  this new beast."
- Isonade is a plain great white shark yokai with a barbed hook tail: no arms, legs or tentacles. No scene character.
- Rejected before: the ship's dog, Sayo's order or thumbprint, the Quartermaster's ledger as the hook.
- The coast's own folklore: the isonade is a sea yokai that hooks people off boats with its barbed tail.

## The story in five lines

1. When the Ghost Ship came down off Wake Island, the crash split her galley storeroom along the keel. Every tide
   since, stores have spilled out through the crack into the shallows.
2. The crew never noticed. The Quartermaster still issues rations to a crew that doesn't eat, and his deckhands still
   carry crates back to the hold, because that is their watch. A crate on the sand is a crate, so they take the
   Salvager's too.
3. The spilled stores drew Isonade in from deep water. It has wedged itself into the breach and eats whatever reaches
   the storeroom.
4. So the crew feed it without knowing. Every crate they carry in goes down the crack and into the shark. That is
   where the stores go, and why the crew never run out of work.
5. The Salvager doesn't care about the shark. She wants her crates back. The player thins the crew (Short Rations),
   takes the Quartermaster's issue sheet (the chart), and goes down to the stores to drive the shark off.

This removes the earlier idea that the crew raid the beach to appease the beast: they would have to notice it.
