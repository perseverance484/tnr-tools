# The Tenth Name - rebuilt as an episode

Ruling: these should read as short stories, not investigations. That kills the market framing
I proposed last round. It made the economics coherent; it did not make the mission simple,
and simple was the actual problem.

---

## What an episode needs, and what this quest has instead

| an episode | The Tenth Name today |
|---|---|
| one want, stated in the first minute | retrieve a page **and** its route, and do not touch the person |
| one antagonist with a face | nobody. The enemy is a hand that copies things |
| the player does something physical | 5 of 36 nodes are a fight; the rest is reading |
| one turn you can see | the turn is a filing-system fact delivered in a lecture |
| a cost at the end | three off-screen policy outcomes, months later |

36 nodes, five of them dead ends, three interchangeable acquisition routes, three epilogues.
An episode of this would be nineteen minutes of a character reading paper.

## The cut list

Everything below goes. None of it is bad writing; all of it is a second mission living inside
the first.

- **x5 / x5b / x6 / x6b** - the canal stones and gate ledgers dead ends. They exist to make
  the body feel earned. The body can just be the opening.
- **x10's three routes** (x11-x12, x14-x16, x17-x19). Three ways to obtain the same object,
  converging on the same node. Keep **one**.
- **x20 / x21 / x21b** - the three fields, three sources, three lists reveal. This is the
  filing-system lecture and it is the part that reads as a murder mystery.
- **x22 / x23 / x24** - the officer explaining the implications of the filing system.
- **x26 / x27 / x28** - three policy epilogues.

That is 17 of 36 nodes. What survives is the spine that was always the good part.

## What survives

- **The body in the cold room.** Unidentified, which is normal and not a failure.
- **The chalk stub in the coat lining.** The page was carried folded against the body, the
  way you carry something you refer to often. Working stock, not a delivery.
- **The runner chain.** Nobody passes a page. They pass a place.
- **The seller.** "They are not secret. They are just not for you."
- **The tenth line.** A service number, our prefix, a date eight months out.

## The episode, 14 nodes

**Act 1 - the page (3 nodes)**

1. `t1` Cold room. A dead courier nobody has claimed. In his coat, folded twice, a page with
   ten of our people on it. Nine are already gone - deserted, sold, or dead.
2. `t2` The tenth has no name. A service number, our prefix, and a date eight months from now.
   The officer does look it up this time, in front of you, and does not say the name out loud.
   *"Somebody is writing down who breaks before they break. Find me the one holding the pen."*
3. `t3` Choice out: take the chalk, take the street.

**Act 2 - the chain (5 nodes)**

4. `t4` The chalk stub and the stones. You learn the marks are handover points, not messages.
5. `t5` [collect] You follow a runner four streets without closing.
6. `t6` Four handovers, none the same person twice. Nobody passes a page. They pass a place.
   The route runs inward and ends at a reading room off the records annexe.
7. `t7` [fight] The last runner in the chain, standing in the annexe doorway with a copy in
   hand and no idea the four ahead of them are already accounted for.
8. `t8` The copy in their hand is in the same handwriting as the one off the body. The runner
   has never met the writer. Nobody has.

**Act 3 - the writer (4 nodes)**

9. `t9` The reading room. One lamp, one desk, one person, and a wall of our own public
   notices - promotion boards, lending register, discipline dockets, all of it posted by us.
   She is copying tomorrow's page out of them by hand.
10. `t10` She does not run and does not deny it. **She is one of ours** - discharged four years
    ago, name on one of those same dockets. That is the turn, and it is a face, not a fact.
    *"I do not make anybody leave. I write down who is going to."*
11. `t11` [fight, optional] She has one guard on the door who does not know what she does.
12. `t12` The desk. Every page she has written, and a ledger of who paid for them.

**Act 4 - the tenth name (2 nodes)**

13. `t13` The choice, and it is one choice: **the tenth line is a person who has not done
    anything yet.** Hand the page up, and they are on a watch list by morning. Burn the page
    and take only the writer, and the village never learns the name - and neither do you.
14. `t_win` Two closing beats, one per choice, three lines each. The date is still eight
    months out either way.

Two fights, one investigation beat per act, one turn with a face on it, one choice that costs
something the player can see. Fourteen nodes against thirty-six.

## Does this apply to the rest of the set?

**Old Ghost is already this shape** - a manhunt, one quarry, three ways to corner him, and it
ends when you catch him. It is 45 nodes but they are mostly the three routes, and `push/34`
splits the ending so each route lands its own. It does not need this treatment.

**Copies, Not Thefts** is closer to The Tenth Name's problem: a heist wrapped around a
document reveal. Worth reading with the same eye once this one is settled.
