## Director's coaching (2026-11-09)
- High damage reduction: use **pierce** (Stormblade Arc, Night's Testament) or **several 40-AP actions and items**
  rather than one big hit.
- **Clear and cleanse need good timing**: use them right after the enemy stacks buffs/debuffs, not on reflex.
- Strong combos: **Seal of Nullification + a well-timed Clear**; a chain like **Ethereal Rebirth + Elite Smoke Bomb**.

## Action costs
- Jutsu 60 or 40 AP; move 10; basic attack 20 (not yet confirmed); **combat items 20**; weapons 40.
- When stunned (-20 AP): plan 60 + item, or 40 + 40, never 60 + 40.

## Rulings and findings, 2026-11-09 (session 4)
- **Never use Flee** (director). It costs 100 AP for a 20% chance anyway.
- Clear and Cleanse: 60 AP, range 4, **10-round cooldown**. One each per fight.
- **Cleanse does not remove stun**, and debuff-prevent does not block it. The in-game guide's "Cleansable Tags"
  page says cleanse helps against stuns. That is a doc/engine mismatch to report to the game owner.
- Stuns don't stack: the largest reduction applies.
- Prevents are a % chance and only block effects applied in a later round.
- Seal of Nullification: target cannot be buffed 1 round, and I cannot be debuffed 2 rounds.
- Elite Smoke Bomb has a cooldown (can't be thrown on consecutive turns).

## Study of top PvP players' fights (2026-11-09, RUL-2026-11-09-146)
Read: Winta vs Gario and vs Choris1717; DDXXD vs Korin and vs Enduyn; Ziharkk vs Sukuna. Also the account's own

  on self over 1-2 turns, then one 60-AP finisher (Ziharkk: 595 -> 1,084 -> 2,391 + 717 afterburn).
