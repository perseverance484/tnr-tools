## Mechanics learned (for the playbook)
- Battle loop: `combat.getBattle` for version/positions, `combat.performAction {battleId, actionId, longitude, latitude, version}`.
  A call with only `battleId` advances AI turns.
- `usersState` order changes per battle: always match by username.
- Grid is odd-q: from an even column the right-hand neighbours are (x+1, y-1) and (x+1, y);
  from an odd column (x+1, y) and (x+1, y+1). An illegal move returns "Action Move no longer possible" or an empty result.
- Move costs 10 AP; most jutsu 60 AP, self buffs 40 AP; out-of-range jutsu fail with "no longer possible".
- Cooldowns reset each battle.
- Auto combat advances one action per call (no call saving); toggling it off also sets the default off.
- Basic actions include `clear` (strips the target's positive effects): the answer to openers like Sovereign Ascendance.
- Arena opponents: `profile.getAllAiNames {arenaOnly:true}`.

## PvP rules learned
- **Brackets** (`libs/profile.ts`): bracket by experience. I am 7 (3M+ XP); I can attack brackets 6, 7 and 8 only.
  Most players in village sectors are bracket 0-4.
- **Village rules:** own village and allies are protected; neutral and enemy villages can be attacked.
  Protected villages (Freedom State) cannot be attacked at all. Safe zones (Wake Island) neither.
- **Locations:** player locations are not public. Scout by travelling (about 1 minute between sectors here) and
  reading the sector. `stealth.useSensory` reveals stealthed players (30 s cooldown). Stealth lasts 20 minutes.
- **Turns:** 60 s per turn. A turn that times out passes. Poll with background waits, not tight loops.
- **Stun math:** available AP = AP - stun reduction. Plan a 60 + 20 turn when stunned, not 60 + 40.
- **Recoil/wound on me:** every hit I land hurts me. Cleanse or defend first; don't race.
- `flee` was unaffordable at 20 effective AP. Flee early or not at all.
