# The Tenth Name - prose pass proposal

Record from `tnr_results_1788135094828`. 36 nodes, three investigation routes, three endings.
Nothing written yet.

Mechanical sweep is clean: no em dashes, no empty descriptions, the officer reads `she`
through x22-x28 per the ruling, and unlike Old Ghost there is no convergence bleed - x12,
x16 and x19 all land on x20, but x20 is about the page, not about the fight, so it reads
correctly from any route.

This is the stronger of the two quests. The pass is one real problem and two small ones.

---

## 1. Three fields, four words, three sources

The count does not hold across the middle of the quest.

**x20** says:

> The nine known entries all carry the same three fields under the name. A year, a prefix,
> and a single word.
>
> Passed. Debt. Discipline. Passed. Clanless. Debt. Discipline. Passed. Debt.

Three fields. The choice out of it is labelled **"Four words."** Then **x21b** explains the
sources:

> Promotion boards go up on a wall four times a year. The lending register is open to any
> creditor who asks. Discipline dockets are posted so the punishment is seen to be done.

Three sources - board, register, docket - covering Passed, Debt and Discipline. **Clanless
has no source.** Then **x22** opens "She reads the four fields before she reads the tenth
line", and x21b itself says "four public facts".

So: three fields, four words, three sources, four fields, four public facts. A player
counting along - which is exactly what the "Four words." choice asks them to do - finds the
arithmetic changes twice.

### Proposed fix

The cleanest reading is that there are **four** fields (year, prefix, word, and the name
itself is the fourth thing on the line), and that the fourth *source* is a clan register.
Two edits close it:

**x20**, first line:

> The nine known entries all carry the same fields under the name. A year, a prefix, and a
> single word.

Dropping "three" costs nothing and the sentence still scans.

**x21b**, add the missing source:

> Promotion boards go up on a wall four times a year. The lending register is open to any
> creditor who asks. Discipline dockets are posted so the punishment is seen to be done. The
> clan rolls list who belongs to a house and, by omission, who does not.
>
> Nobody infiltrated anything.
>
> This village publishes a list of the people it has disappointed, on purpose, and somebody
> has been walking in and reading it.

"By omission, who does not" is the line that makes Clanless land, and it is the bleakest of
the four sources, which suits the ending.

**Alternative**: cut Clanless from the x20 word list and leave the three sources alone. Costs
one word, and loses the best of the four.

## 2. x25 asks for an opinion the officer has already ruled out

> She has the page and the route, which is what she asked for. What happens next is not a
> field decision, and she says so, and then asks anyway.

"and she says so, and then asks anyway" narrates the beat rather than playing it, and it is
the one place in the quest where the writing explains a character instead of showing her.
Everything around it earns its effect by not doing this - x24's "the date will be right for
reasons we caused", x27's "Be angrier about that than I am able to be in this office".

Proposed:

> She has the page and the route, which is what she asked for.
>
> "What happens next is not a field decision."
>
> *She does not put the page down.*
>
> "You walked that route. Tell me what you think and then I will ignore you if I want to."

---

## 3. x_win is thinner than the endings it follows

x26, x27 and x28 are three genuinely different outcomes - a knock that makes things worse, a
seven-month fight that wins one field of four, a circulation that turns the page into a watch
list. All three then close on:

> Page recovered.

Old Ghost's "Report filed. Entry open." works because both halves stay true on every route.
"Page recovered" is true but says nothing about what the player just chose, and it is the
last line they read.

Proposed:

> Page recovered. Route mapped.
>
> The tenth line is still eight months out.

Route-neutral, holds on all three endings, and puts the date back in front of the player on
the way out.
