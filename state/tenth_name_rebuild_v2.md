# The Tenth Name - travel the names, find what they mean

Two corrections taken: I cut too hard, and the copyist twist was not a twist. Someone sitting
in a room copying pages answers nothing - not what the pages say, not what "going to break"
even means, and not what the nine names have in common. The list was never explained, which
is exactly why the mission never landed.

So make that the mission. **You are handed ten names and no context. You go and find out what
happened to nine of them, and what that means for the tenth.**

---

## The shape

Act 1 hands you the page. Act 2 is travel - each name is a place, each place is a short scene
with one fact in it. The facts look unrelated until the third one. Act 3 is the pattern. Act 4
is the tenth name, still alive, and what you do about it.

Dead ends come back, two of them, because a lead that costs you an afternoon is what makes the
real one feel found rather than handed over. The rule is that a dead end must be **funny or
bleak**, never just empty - Old Ghost's g14, "you spend the afternoon walking in a circle
somebody drew for you," is the standard.

## What the nine names mean - three candidates

Whichever we pick, the structure above is unchanged. This is the only decision that matters.

### A. Burned informants (recommended)

The nine were the village's own informants. Each one died or vanished in a way that looked
like nothing - a fall, a debt collector, a transfer that was never processed. Someone has been
working through the roster, and the ninth was three weeks ago.

- **Why the officer will not say the name out loud**: informants are hers. She reads the
  number, goes quiet, and closes the file drawer with her hand still on it.
- **Why the brief is "the route, not the person"**: she is protecting the tenth, not
  investigating them.
- **What the date means**: the killer works on a schedule. Eight months is when they get to
  the last name. It is not a prophecy, it is a rota.
- **The turn**: the ninth name died in a way only the village could have arranged. The list
  did not leak. It was **issued**.
- **The tenth**: still alive, still reporting, and does not know the other nine are gone.

Grounded, personal, and the twist is a betrayal rather than a filing system.

### B. The contract that eats people

All nine took the same standing bounty contract. Each one failed to return, and the contract
was quietly reissued - nine times. The tenth line is the next issue, dated.

- **The turn**: the contract is bait, and whoever posted it has been feeding this village's
  shinobi to something one at a time for years.
- **The tenth**: the officer is holding that contract when you walk back in. She was about to
  hand it to you.
- Strong ending image, but it makes the antagonist a trap rather than a person, and the
  Forsworn set already has Old Ghost as its faceless-danger quest.

### C. The tenth name is yours

The nine are all people who did what you are doing right now - took an off-books errand for
the same officer, and were written down for it.

- Sharpest hook, cheapest twist. Works once, and it puts the officer under suspicion, which
  breaks her for every other quest in the set.
- Listed for completeness. I would not.

## Node outline, on candidate A - 19 nodes

**Act 1 - the page (3)**

1. `t1` A dead courier nobody has claimed. In his coat, folded twice, a page: ten names, nine
   struck through, one clean. The tenth is a service number and a date eight months out.
2. `t2` The officer reads the number, does not say it, and puts her hand flat on the page.
   *"Nine of these are mine. Find out what happened to them and come back before you tell
   anybody else."*
3. `t3` Three of the nine are close enough to walk to. Choice of where to start.

**Act 2 - the names (8, two of them dead ends)**

4. `t4` **The debt.** A moneylender's back room. The fourth name owed eleven ryo and was
   killed over it, which everyone agrees on and nobody witnessed. His debt was settled a week
   after he died, by someone who left no name.
5. `t5` [dead end] **The transfer.** You spend an afternoon at a records window establishing
   that the sixth name was transferred to a village that has no record of receiving her, and
   that this is nobody's department.
6. `t6` **The fall.** A roof in the eastern quarter. The seventh name fell from it. You stand
   where he stood and the tiles are sound, the rail is sound, and it is not a roof anybody
   falls off.
7. `t7` [dead end] **The widow.** She has said all this before, to two people already, and
   asks you which office you are from. You do not have a good answer.
8. `t8` [collect] The ninth name, three weeks dead. You go through what he left.
9. `t9` A chalk stub, worn to a thumbnail. Handover marks. He was still working the week he
   died.
10. `t10` [fight] The two who come for what you just took off a dead man's shelf.
11. `t11` They are not foreign. Their kit is ours, their marks are ours, and one of them is
    carrying a copy of the same page.

**Act 3 - the pattern (3)**

12. `t12` Nine deaths, nine departments, nine reasons, none of them the same. That is the
    tell: a coincidence does not vary that carefully.
13. `t13` The debt settled after death. The transfer nobody received. The roof nobody falls
    off. Each one closed by a different office, and every one of those offices reports to the
    same desk.
14. `t14` The list was never stolen. **It was issued.** Somebody inside is retiring the
    roster and dressing each one as ordinary.

**Act 4 - the tenth (5)**

15. `t15` The tenth service number belongs to somebody still reporting, who does not know the
    other nine are gone. The date is when they get to her.
16. `t16` [travel] You go to her before you go back to the officer.
17. `t17` She is mid-report when you arrive, writing to the same desk that issued the list.
18. `t18` **The choice.** Bring her in and the desk learns she is warned - the date moves
    closer. Leave her running and she keeps feeding the people hunting her, but she stays
    useful and alive a while longer. Neither is protection.
19. `t_win` Two closing beats, one per choice. The page goes in a drawer. Nine struck through,
    one clean, and now two people know.

Two fights, two dead ends with a joke and a sting in them, three investigation scenes that
each hand over one fact, one pattern, one turn, one choice that costs.

## What this costs

The Tenth Name's current record is 36 nodes and almost none of them survive - the briefing
frame and the chalk stub carry over, everything else is written new. It is a rebuild, not a
pass. `push/35` stays held and will be discarded.
