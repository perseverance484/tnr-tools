# 93_WALKTHROUGH_economy.md: what drops where, how often, and how long [2026-07-27, v2]

Rulings applied: Iron Ore is a 100-unit spine across a full build; the Muster hands over **one** base of the player's choosing; the farm layer is split into lanes so a player can choose what they are farming today.

Read down the middle column and the lanes are obvious: **ore and bases at the Foundry, clan material at the kilns, Ash in the deep, hides and blooms from the professions, and one chase apiece in four different places.**

## 2. What a day yields
Five daily runs for a non-professional (Foundry, Districts, Furnaces, Crucibles, Deep Works):
**about 5 of whichever clan you aimed both kilns at, 3 Concord Ash, 4.5 Kiln Salvage, 2.5 bases of your chosen slot, and roughly half a Kilnheart.** Professionals add 4 hides or blooms on their daily plus 2 more per run on a standing mission that has no cooldown.

## 4. What the numbers say
- **Iron Ore is a gatherer's trade, and the campaign never drops it.** A full build eats 100 units, every one of them gathered by a profession or bought at market. That is the single largest trade pressure in the campaign and the main reason a non-gatherer will visit the market at all. Name it plainly in the launch post.
- **Clan components stopped being the bottleneck** the moment the kilns let you aim: five days of focused farming covers every component in a full build. That is the right outcome. Material identity should decide *what* you build, not how long you wait.
- **Bases are unlimited but not free.** The Foundry never closes, so a determined player can farm any slot they lack, but at 25% a run it costs real time rather than a daily tick. That is the point of an always-open lane: it removes the fear of missing a day without removing the work.
- **Unlimited records pay no currency.** The Foundry and the two profession standing missions are the only uncapped quests in the campaign, and all three pay materials alone. A permanent assert holds that law, along with a ban on chase drops appearing on any uncapped record, since infinite rolls at 1.2% would be a guaranteed drop in an evening.
- **Kilnhearts stay off the critical path**, funding the Glasskissed pity forge, the Vitric Brand, and the First Brick. Optional prestige, never required progress.

## 6. The fittings (rebuilt 2026-07-27)
Three one-time fittings, each offering three finished pieces in three different slots so a player fills the gap they actually have rather than choosing between three of the same boot.

**The First Fitting**, after the Ryokin, rare grade: the Charterbound Cuirass (600 raw pool, the survivability pick), the Ashrisen Gauntlets (damage), the Veilspun Treads (speed with a mitigation cushion). Chest, hands, feet.

**The Second Fitting**, at the Compact, epic grade: the Refined Charterbound Hood (mitigation plus pool), the Refined Veilspun Gauntlets (the largest raw damage row in the campaign), the Refined Ashrisen Greaves (sustained damage). Head, hands, legs.

**The Third Fitting**, at the Veiled Table, Kilnwrought grade: the Charterbound Cuirass (pool, mitigation and a 500 shield), the Veilspun Treads (full debuff prevention), the Ashrisen Hood (hybrid). Chest, feet, head.

Across the ladder every slot appears at least once and no fitting repeats its own slots, so three choices leave a player with three pieces in three places and the forge covering the rest. [P] The third fitting hands over a legendary piece for free, which is the single most generous reward in the campaign and the first number to revisit if the endgame feels cheap.
