# 31_ARC_ashen_concord.md - The Ashen Concord (campaign canon and ruling record)

The current campaign. Everything here is decided; the reasoning is kept because a decision without its reason gets relitigated. Companion documents: `35_ATLAS_farm_layer.md` (what drops where), `36_TREE_unlocks.md` (how content reveals), `37_ECONOMY_ashen.md` (rates and timelines). Live state and the pending list are in `90_STATE_board.md`.

## 1. What it is

A permanent, always-available campaign set in **Haishiro**, a kiln city held together by a treaty between five clans. A player walks one story spine, and the story opens working areas behind it. The whole thing fits in a sentence a player can repeat: **pick how you fight, follow the story, work a clan's fire for what the forge wants, and take the hotter fire when you can survive it.**

Lore lives in `27_LORE_world.md`: the Shirakotsu, Ryokin, Itokage, Hagane and Nemori; the Gravemother and the Debtkeeper; the three fighting lines (Charterbound the wall, Ashrisen the blade, Veilspun the quickening).

## 2. The spine

Ash Road, the Concord Board, the Muster, the Forge Column, **The Ryokin**, the First Fitting, the Kiln Column, **The Red Itokage**, **The Shirakotsu**, **The Hagane**, **The Quiet Nemori**, the Heats, **The Compact**, the Second Fitting, **The Reckoning**, **The Veiled Table**, the Third Fitting, the Deep Doors, **The Ash Settlement** (weekly), **The Glassfire** (weekly).

Five short board visits sit inside that chain rather than one front-loaded manual, each teaching only what just became relevant: the shape of the event, the forge, the kilns and their odds, the heats and how fights behave, then the deep doors and the chase odds.

## 3. The farm layer

- **The Ashen Foundry**, uncapped and always open: bases and salvage, no currency, nothing rare.
- **The Outer Districts**, daily: salvage, odd components, the Founder's Stride.
- **The Clan Furnaces**, daily from the first clan: pick a house, take its material.
- **The Clan Crucibles**, daily from the Compact: the same five houses hotter, and the only daily Kilnhearts.
- **The Deep Works**, daily from the Veiled Table: Concord Ash, the Undervaults gamble, the Debtkeeper's Greaves.
- **Four profession records**, rank-gated: Kilnhide and Cinderbloom, which nobody else can farm.

## 4. The rulings, and why

**The key layer is deleted.** Marks, Seals, oaths and caches are gone. In a linear, prerequisite-chained story the completed quest is the proof, and a key on top of it is ceremony that can be spent wrong, hoarded, or lost. No item in this campaign exists solely to be shown at a door.

**One identity, stated once.** The campaign used to ask for an allegiance at the Muster and a gear line three chapters later, in two vocabularies that never met. Now there is one: how you fight, chosen at the forge and reinforced by the fittings.

**Ten kilns became two grounds.** Five clans times two heats was one idea said thirteen times, counting the Clan Grounds hub and two patrols. Two records with a clan choice at the door say it better, and a player who can aim a run at the material they need finishes faster than one clearing a chore list.

**One common per slot, everything else converted.** The drop table carries six commons. A base plus that slot's clan material converts upward through rare, epic and Kilnwrought. Line identity lives in the recipe, never in the drop.

**Fittings fill gaps.** Three one-time fittings, each offering three finished pieces in three different slots at rare, epic and Kilnwrought grade. Never three of the same slot in three colours: a player who already has good boots gets nothing from that.

**Uncapped means materials only.** The Foundry and the two profession standing missions can be run without limit, so they pay no currency, no experience, and nothing rare.

**Iron Ore is gatherer-only.** A full build eats 100 units the campaign never drops. That is the largest trade pressure in the design and it is deliberate.

**Reveal at most two records per beat.** One story step, and sometimes one area. Nine simultaneous unlocks is how a campaign becomes homework.
